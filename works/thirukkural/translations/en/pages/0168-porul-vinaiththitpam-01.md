# 67. Firmness in Action

**661. Firmness in action is a person's firmness of mind;  
all other strengths are secondary.**

Even if everything else is present, if firmness alone is absent from a person's mind, there will be no firmness in that person's action either.

**662. Preventing an obstacle before it comes and, if it comes, not losing heart—  
the discerning say these two are the proper ways.**

The wise hold to two courses: making an effort to remove an obstacle before it arises, and, if it nevertheless comes, refusing to let the mind lose its firmness.

**663. The strength of action is to keep it concealed until it is completed;  
if it becomes known midway, it may bring obstruction too great to overcome.**

Firmness in carrying out an action means not revealing it until it has been completed. If it becomes known in the middle, obstacles may arise to such an extent that the action cannot be brought to completion.

**664. Speaking is easy for everyone; what is difficult  
is doing exactly as one has spoken.**

It is easy for everyone to speak; the difficult thing is to carry through and complete what has been said.

**665. The firmness in action of those distinguished by ability  
will reach the ruler and be held in high esteem.**

The firmness in action of worthy people distinguished by their ability to act will attract the ruler and be greatly valued and honoured.
