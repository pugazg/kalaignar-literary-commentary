**936. Those trapped by the evil called gambling  
suffer misery and do not eat their fill.**

People who fall into the net of the evil called gambling suffer and languish in distress, without even wishing to eat enough to fill their stomachs.

**937. If one spends one's time entering the gambling hall,  
it destroys inherited wealth and good character.**

If a person spends his time in the gambling place itself, it will destroy both the property sought and accumulated by his ancestors and his good character.

**938. Gambling destroys wealth, makes one embrace falsehood, destroys compassion,  
and makes one wander in suffering.**

Gambling seizes one's wealth, turns one into a liar, changes a compassionate heart, and makes a person wander in the darkness of suffering.

**939. Clothing, wealth, food, fame, and learning—these five  
will not remain with one who takes to gambling.**

Fame, education, wealth, food, and clothing will all move away and keep their distance from people who have become addicted to gambling.

**940. As gambling is loved more with every loss,  
life clings more with every suffering it endures.**

The desire that grows for gambling as wealth is lost again and again is like the desire one has for life as suffering keeps coming upon the body.
