**986. The touchstone of exemplary character is accepting defeat  
even at the hands of those who are not one's equal.**

The maturity of mind to accept even a defeat caused by people who are not one's equals is the touchstone of a person's excellence.

**987. If one does not return good even to those who have done harm,  
what use is the good quality called exemplary character?**

If one fails to do good in return even to people who have done harm to oneself, what use is there in possessing the good quality called exemplary character?

**988. Poverty is no disgrace to one  
who possesses the firmness called exemplary character as wealth.**

For a person who holds the firmness called character as his wealth, poverty is not something that can bring disgrace.

**989. Even if the age itself changes, they themselves do not change—  
those who are called the ocean of exemplary character.**

Worthy people who perform the duties proper to them with dignity will remain like a sea that does not change its state even if an age of upheaval arrives in which all the seas themselves overturn and change.

**990. If the exemplary character of the worthy begins to decline,  
even this wide world cannot bear the burden.**

If the good character of worthy people itself begins to decline, this world will not bear it patiently.
