# 24. Fame

**231. Live by giving and earning fame;  
there is no other gain for life.**

Apart from the quality of giving and undiminishing fame, there is nothing else that can bring true advancement to life.

**232. Whatever the praisers praise  
rests upon the fame of those who give something to those who have nothing.**

Whatever those who praise may praise, it ultimately concerns the fame of those who give something to people who have nothing.

**233. In this world, nothing stands imperishable  
except exalted, unequalled fame.**

In this world, nothing other than fame remains both incomparable and imperishable.

**234. If one earns fame that extends across the earth,  
the new world will not honour only the learned and leave such people aside.**

Even the new world that is to come will not set aside the great people who, in today's world, renounced self-interest and earned fame, and honour only those of intellectual ability.

**235. To make even loss nourish one's fame, and even death sustain it,  
is difficult except for those of natural ability.**

To grow one's fame even amid suffering through the strength to endure it, and to make that fame endure even through one's death, are acts possible only to those who possess such natural ability.
