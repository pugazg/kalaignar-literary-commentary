# 132. Nuances of Lovers' Quarrel

**1316. I said, “I thought of you”; she refused my embrace and sulked, asking,  
“Then why had you forgotten me?”**

No sooner had I told my beloved, “I thought of you,” than she asked, “If you thought of me, does that not mean you had first forgotten me?” and began to sulk, demanding, “Why did you forget me?”

**1317. I sneezed and she blessed me; then she broke into tears, asking,  
“Who was thinking of you and made you sneeze?”**

I sneezed, and as usual she blessed me. At once some suspicion arose in her: “Who was thinking of you and made you sneeze?” she asked, and contrary to the blessing she had just given, she began to cry.

**1318. I suppressed a sneeze; she wept, asking,  
“Are you hiding from me that someone close to you is thinking of you?”**

Afraid that she would begin another quarrel, I held back my sneeze. Seeing that, she cried and asked, “Oh! Are you hiding from me, so that I will not know, that someone close to you is thinking of you?”

**1319. Even when I humble myself and bring her sulking to an end, she grows angry, saying,  
“Do you behave this same way with other women too?”**

Even if I submit to her, remove her sulking and make her happy, she immediately becomes angry again and says, “Oh! Is this how you behave with other women as well?”

**1320. Even when I sit thinking of her and gaze at her without blinking, she grows angry, asking,  
“Whom are you thinking of as you look at me like this?”**

Even when I think only of her incomparable beauty and look at her without so much as blinking, she becomes angry and asks, “With whom are you comparing me as you stare at me so intently?”
