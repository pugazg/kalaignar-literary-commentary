# 129. Longing for Union

**1286. When I see him, I see none of his faults;  
when I do not see him, I see nothing but his faults.**

When I see him, I do not see his faults; when I do not see him, I see nothing except those faults.

**1287. Knowing the flood will sweep them away, who would leap into it?  
Likewise, knowing my sulking will fail, what use is there in it?**

Like one who jumps into water despite knowing that the flood will carry them away, what benefit is there in entering a lovers' quarrel when I already know that I will not win?

**1288. O thief who stole my heart! Even if you cause me humiliating pain,  
your chest intoxicates me like liquor does one who has tasted it.**

O thief who has stolen my heart! Even if you give me suffering capable of bringing humiliation, your chest makes me lose myself just as one intoxicated by liquor desires that same liquor more and more.

**1289. Love is softer than a flower;  
only a few know how to enjoy it with that same delicacy.**

The delight of love is softer than a flower. Only a few are able to experience it with that very softness.

**1290. She showed her sulking with her eyes, yet in embracing  
she hurried even faster than I did and united with me.**

Though she expressed estrangement through her eyes, when it came to joining and embracing she acted more quickly than I did and united with me.
