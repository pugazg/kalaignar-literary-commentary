**506. Do not choose those without guiding bonds;  
they care little for the world and do not feel shame at blame.**

People without moral restraint should not be chosen for a responsibility. They will act without concern for the world and without shame at blame.

**507. Choosing an ignorant person merely out of affection  
brings every form of folly.**

Choosing a person without knowledge because of affection is not merely ignorance; it produces only useless actions.

**508. One who chooses another without examining him  
brings unending suffering even upon future generations.**

If one appoints a person as a companion without examining him, the suffering caused by that choice may continue even to generations that follow.

**509. Trust no one without examination; once examined and chosen,  
trust that person in the matter for which he was chosen.**

Confidence should be placed in a person only after careful examination and clarity. No one should be trusted without such examination.

**510. Choosing without examination, and doubting one after examining and choosing him,  
both bring unending suffering.**

Accepting a person without examining him, and then suspecting a person after examining and accepting him, both cause suffering without end.
