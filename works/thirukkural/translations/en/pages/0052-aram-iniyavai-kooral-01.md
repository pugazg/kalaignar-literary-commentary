# 10. Speaking Pleasant Words

**91. Words from the mouths of those who have discerned truth,  
mingled with love and free from deceit, are sweet speech.**

A word that comes from a person's mouth is called sweet speech when it is mingled with love, free from deceit, and truthful.

**92. Speaking sweetly with a smiling face is better  
than giving something with inward gladness.**

Speaking pleasantly with a blossoming face is a quality greater than giving something with a heart made glad.

**93. To look pleasantly with a smiling face, and from the heart  
to speak sweet words—that is aram.**

Looking with a blossoming face and speaking sweet words with a blossoming heart is a quality established in the path of aram.

**94. For those whose sweet words bring joy to everyone,  
there is no painful want that causes suffering.**

Those who speak sweetly and deal with everyone kindly suffer no misery of poverty in friendship.

**95. For a person, humility and sweet speech are the ornament;  
there is no finer ornament besides them.**

Apart from humility and the nature of speaking sweetly, there can be no finer ornament for a person.
