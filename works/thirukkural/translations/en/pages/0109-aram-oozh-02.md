**376. What is not one's due may not remain however carefully guarded;  
what is one's own will not leave even if cast away.**

Things to which one has no rightful claim may fail to remain however securely they are kept; things to which one has a rightful claim will not go away wherever they are taken and thrown.

**377. Unless one lives by the ordered way that has been laid down,  
even one who gathers crores will find it hard to enjoy them.**

If one does not live in accordance with a properly ordered way of life, even after amassing wealth in crores, enjoying its benefit will be difficult.

**378. Those without anything to enjoy might take up renunciation,  
if the sufferings that should befall them pass without afflicting them.**

If, through the certainty that there is nothing available for enjoyment, the feelings that could torment them pass away without coming to torment them, they too would take up renunciation.

**379. Those who see good as good when it comes—  
why should they be distressed when its opposite comes?**

Good and evil alternate in life. Those who rejoice when they see good—why should they alone become troubled in mind when evil occurs?

**380. What can be mightier than oozh? Even if another condition is devised,  
the natural condition itself comes first.**

Even if one tries to change the natural condition and establish another artificial condition, the natural condition itself comes forward first; what else can therefore be more powerful than it?
