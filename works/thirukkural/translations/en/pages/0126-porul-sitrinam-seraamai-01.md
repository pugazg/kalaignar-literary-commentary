# 46. Avoiding Base Company

**451. Greatness fears base company;  
smallness surrounds itself with it as kin.**

The great will not join the company of base people. But the small-minded will join that company, as like joins with like.

**452. As water changes according to the nature of the soil,  
people's understanding takes on the nature of the company they keep.**

Water changes according to the character of the land it joins and comes to take on that land's nature. In the same way, people's understanding acquires the character of the group with which they associate.

**453. A person's awareness arises from the mind;  
the judgement of what kind of person he is arises from his company.**

A person's awareness depends on the mind. The judgement by which others say what sort of person he is depends on the company he joins.

**454. Though it appears to arise from within the mind,  
a person's understanding is shaped by the company he keeps.**

A person's understanding may appear to be the natural quality of his own mind, but it is revealed through his association with the people among whom he moves.

**455. Purity of mind and purity of action—both  
come from purity of company.**

Only when the associations a person maintains are pure will both his mind and his actions be pure.
