# 55. Just Rule

**541. Justice is to examine the fault, show favour to no side,  
and decide impartially in each case.**

Justice is what is delivered after examining what the offence is, leaning to neither side, and never departing from impartiality.

**542. As all living beings in the world look to rain for life,  
the citizens of a country look to good government for life.**

Just as the living beings of the world need rain in order to live, the citizens of a country need good governance in order to live.

**543. The ruler's just sceptre stands as the foundation  
for the books of the virtuous and for righteous conduct.**

It is the just rule of a government that becomes the foundation for the books of the virtuous and for actions that follow the path of virtue.

**544. The whole world honours the footsteps  
of the good government that embraces its citizens and rules them.**

The fourfold world will honour the footsteps of the good government that embraces its citizens and conducts its rule.

**545. When a government rules without departing from justice,  
it is like timely rain bringing a rich harvest.**

If a government in a country exists without swerving from justice, it is comparable to the rich yield that comes when rain falls unfailingly in its proper season.
