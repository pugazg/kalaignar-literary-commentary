# 57. Avoiding Acts that Cause Fear

**561. The duty of government is to examine offences without losing impartiality  
and impose a fitting punishment so that they do not recur.**

It is the duty of government to investigate offences without departing from neutrality and to ensure that punishment suited to each offence is given so that the offence will not happen again.

**562. Those who wish their influence to endure for long  
may show severity when warning, but should show restraint when punishing.**

The influence of those who display firmness in preventing offences but show moderation when punishment is actually imposed will continue without decline for a long time.

**563. A government that rules by a cruel sceptre and makes its citizens afraid  
will certainly perish quickly.**

A government that carries on tyrannical rule in a way that makes its citizens live in fear will certainly be destroyed swiftly.

**564. A government whose citizens speak of it as harsh and cruel  
will rapidly lose its greatness.**

A government regarded by its citizens as a harsh-speaking tyranny will quickly lose its standing and greatness.

**565. Great wealth gathered in the hands of one who is hard to approach  
and whose face is forbidding is like the frightening sight of a demon.**

Great wealth accumulated with a person whom no one can easily approach and who wears a harsh face becomes a frightening appearance, like the sight called a demon.
