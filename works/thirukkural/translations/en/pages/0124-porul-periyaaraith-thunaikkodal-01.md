# 45. Seeking the Support of the Great

**441. Knowing their worth, choose the friendship  
of mature people of wisdom who understand aram.**

One must understand how to gain the friendship of mature scholars who know aram, and choose that friendship accordingly.

**442. Cherish and hold close those capable of removing present suffering  
and guarding beforehand so that it does not return.**

One must take as one's support great people who can remove suffering that has already come and protect against further suffering before it arises.

**443. Of all things difficult to obtain, the most difficult  
is to cherish the great and make them one's own.**

Honouring and appreciating great people and maintaining a relationship with them is a blessing greater than all other blessings.

**444. To conduct oneself with those greater than oneself as one's own companions  
is foremost among all forms of strength.**

To maintain a relationship with people greater than oneself in knowledge and ability, and to conduct oneself in accordance with them, becomes a very great source of strength.

**445. Because a king must act with counsellors as his eyes,  
he should surround himself with those who can deliberate.**

It brings benefit to a ruler to keep around him learned and distinguished people who can serve as his eyes, seeing matters and telling him what they find.
