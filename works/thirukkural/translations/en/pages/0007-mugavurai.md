# Preface

I am not unaware that attempting to drape the Himalayas in a golden shawl and writing a commentary on the Thirukkural are one and the same. After completing *Kuraloviyam* using three hundred and fifty-four Kurals, I was seized by the urge to write a commentary on all one thousand three hundred and thirty Kurals. Having fulfilled that desire, to my joy, this book is the collection of the Thirukkural commentaries that I wrote every day in the *Murasoli* daily.

Without departing from the beliefs and cultural practices of Valluvar's time, or from his perspective on them; without forcibly imposing any view of my own; and recognizing the fact that a word used by him in a Kural may have, in Tamil, another meaning besides the meaning assigned to it thus far by commentators, I have woven this golden shawl to the extent of my knowledge and ability. My concern has been only to understand what he thought and wrote, rather than to look for whether he thought as I do.

I have designated the chapter title “Invocation to God” as “Worship.” I do not wish to entangle Valluvar in the debate over whether he was one who denied God or one who believed in God. This can be understood from the commentaries I have written for the Kurals contained in the chapter “Worship.”

Dr. Nannan, who has written the Critical Appreciation, has pointed out many of the other explanations that I would otherwise have had to provide. My thanks to him.

My thanks are also due to the eminent Professor Anbazhagan, standard-bearer of the dignity of our people, who has fashioned the Foreword itself as an elegant Tamil poetic exposition.

I make this book an offering to my Tamil and present it to the Tamil people.

Affectionately,  
[signature]
