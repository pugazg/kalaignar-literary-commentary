**1086. If her cruel brows did not bend and hide them,  
her eyes would not cause this trembling pain.**

If her eyebrows remained straight without bending and covered her eyes, those eyes would not inflict the suffering that makes me tremble.

**1087. The cloth upon the head of a rutting elephant  
is like the garment upon a woman's firm breast.**

I saw the face-cloth placed over the forehead of a rutting elephant; it was like the garment swaying over the unyielding breast of a woman.

**1088. My strength, before which enemies tremble in battle,  
has broken before her radiant forehead.**

My strength, which can make enemies on the battlefield tremble, has now bent and yielded before the shining forehead of this beloved.

**1089. She already has the youthful glance of a doe and modesty;  
what use are ornaments given from outside?**

This great beauty naturally possesses the lively, doe-like look and modesty as her ornaments. Why should she need artificial ornaments?

**1090. Liquor brings delight only to those who drink it;  
love, unlike liquor, brings intoxication merely on being seen.**

Intoxication comes from liquor only when it is drunk; love, however, intoxicates merely by being seen.
