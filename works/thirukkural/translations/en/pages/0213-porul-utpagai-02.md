**886. If internal enmity arises among those who once stood united,  
it is always difficult to prevent the destruction that follows.**

When internal enmity arises among people who had been united, preventing the ruin that results from it becomes difficult at any time.

**887. Though they may fit together like the lid on a metal container,  
a household afflicted by internal enmity is not truly united.**

The lid of the small metal container called a *seppu* may appear to fit closely from the outside. In the same way, people with internal enmity may appear outwardly united, but they will not be united in heart.

**888. Like iron worn down by a file,  
a family afflicted by internal enmity loses both form and strength.**

Just as iron loses its shape and strength when rubbed by a file, the strength of a family in which internal enmity has arisen will wear away and diminish.

**889. Though internal enmity may be as small as the split in a sesame seed,  
it contains the power to bring great harm.**

Even when it is as small as the split in a sesame seed, internal enmity can bring enormous harm.

**890. Life among people whose hearts are not united  
is like living with a snake inside a small hut.**

For people who are not united in heart, living together is like living with a snake inside a small hut, bringing fear at every moment.
