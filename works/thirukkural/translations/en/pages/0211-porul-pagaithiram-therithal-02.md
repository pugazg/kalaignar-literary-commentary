**876. Whether one has understood the enemy or not, when danger comes  
neither trust him fully nor break away from him completely.**

Whether one has examined an enemy and understood him or has not, when danger arises it is best neither to become too close to that enemy in friendship nor to separate completely while maintaining hostility.

**877. Do not tell your pain to friends who do not understand it;  
do not reveal your weakness in the presence of enemies.**

One should not speak of one's suffering to friends who do not understand it, and one should not expose one's weakness to enemies.

**878. Know the proper means, strengthen yourself, and secure protection;  
then the enemy's arrogance will subside of itself.**

When a person understands the proper course, strengthens himself, and makes arrangements for his own protection, the pride of the enemy before him will automatically diminish.

**879. Cut down the thorn tree while it is still young; once it has grown strong,  
it wounds the hand of the one who tries to remove it.**

Just as a thorn tree should be plucked out while it is still a small sapling, enmity too should be brought down before it becomes fully grown.

**880. Those who cannot break the pride of hostile men  
cannot truly be called alive merely because they breathe.**

People who cannot shatter the arrogance of enemies cannot with certainty be said to be alive merely because they continue to breathe.
