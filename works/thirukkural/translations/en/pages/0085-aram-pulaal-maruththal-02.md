**256. If the world did not kill for the sake of eating,  
no one would sell flesh for a price.**

If people in the world refrained from killing living beings in order to eat flesh, no one would take up the trade of selling flesh.

**257. One should abstain from flesh, if one understands  
that it is the wound of another living being.**

Those who understand that flesh is the bodily wound of another living being should refrain from eating it.

**258. Those of stainless understanding will not eat  
flesh from which life has been severed.**

People of stainless understanding will not separate a life from its body and eat its flesh.

**259. Better than conducting a thousand sacrifices by pouring offerings into fire  
is not taking one life for the sake of eating.**

Rather than conduct a thousand sacrificial rites by pouring substances such as ghee into fire, it is better not to take a life in order to eat it.

**260. All living beings will join their hands in worship  
of one who neither kills nor eats flesh.**

All living beings will worship and praise those who do not eat flesh and do not kill living beings for that purpose.
