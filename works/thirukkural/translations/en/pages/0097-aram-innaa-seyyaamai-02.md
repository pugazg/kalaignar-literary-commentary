**316. What one has oneself known to be painful  
should not be done to another.**

What a person has learned through his own life to be painful should not be done to another person.

**317. At no time, to anyone, even in the smallest measure,  
let the mind conceive a degrading act—this is foremost.**

The foremost excellence is never, in any measure, at any time, to think even in the mind of an act that would demean anyone.

**318. One who knows the pain that comes to one's own life—  
how could one cause pain to other living beings?**

One who has felt the suffering that another's act can cause to oneself should surely not inflict that same suffering upon other living beings.

**319. If one causes pain to others in the morning,  
pain will come of itself to one in the afternoon.**

Even while a person is rejoicing that he has caused harm to another, a similar harm will strike that very person.

**320. Every suffering comes upon those who cause suffering;  
those who desire a life without suffering should cause none.**

Harm comes back upon those who cause harm. Therefore, those who desire a life free of harm must not inflict harm upon others.
