**896. Even if one is burned by fire, there may be escape; there is no escape  
for those who conduct themselves by offending the great.**

Even if someone is surrounded and burned by fire, it is possible to survive; but those who do wrong toward people of great power cannot escape and survive.

**897. What are a well-appointed life and vast wealth worth  
if worthy people of excellence grow angry?**

However much great wealth one may accumulate and however many kinds of comforts one may enjoy, all of them become useless before the anger of worthy people of excellence.

**898. If those who are like mountains are diminished, those who seemed firmly established  
with their households will perish from the earth.**

Those who think of undermining the greatness of people who are like mountains will be utterly destroyed, even if they possess great and seemingly permanent wealth.

**899. If people who uphold lofty principles rise in anger,  
even the king will be broken and his rule destroyed.**

If people who stand firm in lofty principles rise in anger, an oppressive government will be shaken and destroyed.

**900. Even those with boundless means and powerful support will not survive  
if people of surpassing excellence grow angry.**

However limitless their facilities and opportunities, and however powerful the support they possess, they cannot escape and survive if they face the anger of worthy people distinguished by excellence.
