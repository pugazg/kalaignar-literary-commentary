# 21. Fear of Evil Deeds

**201. Those who do evil do not fear the pride of evil deeds;  
the worthy fear it.**

Evil people do not fear doing evil; even if an evil deed seems to bring happiness, the worthy fear and tremble to commit it.

**202. Because evil deeds produce evil results,  
evil deeds are to be feared more than fire.**

Since evil actions bring only evil, one should regard such actions as more cruel than fire and fear to commit them.

**203. Foremost among all forms of wisdom, they say,  
is not doing evil even to those who have done evil to you.**

Not returning the same evil to one who has done evil to us is praised as the foremost wisdom among all forms of knowledge.

**204. Even in forgetfulness, do not plot another's ruin;  
if you do, aram will plot the ruin of the plotter.**

One must not even accidentally think of causing harm to another; if one does so, aram will surround that person and bring harm upon him.

**205. Do not do evil saying, “I have nothing”;  
if you do, you will become one who has nothing again.**

A person should not engage in evil acts because of poverty; if he does, he will have to suffer in poverty again.
