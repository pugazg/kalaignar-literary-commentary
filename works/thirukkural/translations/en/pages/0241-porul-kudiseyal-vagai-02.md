**1026. Good leadership for a person is to make  
the community into which he was born a source of greatness.**

A person who has the ability to govern well becomes one who brings greatness to the very community into which he was born.

**1027. As in the battlefield the burden rests upon the strong-eyed warriors,  
so among one's own it rests upon those who have the ability.**

Just as the responsibility of withstanding opposition and leading an army on the battlefield lies with those who possess the necessary ability, the responsibility of protecting citizens and enabling them to rise rests with the capable people among them.

**1028. There is no season for those who work for their community;  
if they grow idle and think of honour, it will be ruined.**

If they are shaken by degrading attacks made upon them, or lazily hesitate saying that the time to work may come later, the welfare of the citizens will deteriorate.

**1029. Is the body of one who shields his community from faults  
a vessel made only to receive suffering?**

A person who continually tries to protect the communities connected with him by preventing suffering from reaching them will be praised as though he were born precisely to bear suffering.

**1030. A community without a capable person to stand beside it  
will fall when hardship strikes to destroy it.**

A community without someone capable of standing against and bearing the hardship that comes upon it will be conquered and brought down by that very hardship.
