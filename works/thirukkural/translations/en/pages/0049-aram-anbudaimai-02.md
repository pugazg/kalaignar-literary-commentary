**76. Those who do not know say love supports aram alone;  
the same love also supports heroic deeds.**

Only those who do not know that love stands as a support for heroic deeds say that love supports only deeds of aram.

**77. Aram will scorch the one without love  
as the sun scorches a boneless creature.**

Even when a person knows what aram is, if that person does not follow it, their own conscience will torment them. It will be like the sun's heat tormenting a worm.

**78. A life without love in the heart is like  
a withered tree sprouting on hard barren ground.**

The life of one who has no love in the heart is like a withered tree putting forth shoots in a desert.

**79. What use are all the outer organs of the body  
to those who lack the inner organ called love?**

What benefit is there in having beautiful outer organs for those who lack the inner organ called love?

**80. Life is the state that moves along the path of love;  
without it, the body is only bones wrapped in skin.**

A body becomes a living body only when it moves along the path of a loving heart; otherwise, it is merely a body of bones covered with skin.
