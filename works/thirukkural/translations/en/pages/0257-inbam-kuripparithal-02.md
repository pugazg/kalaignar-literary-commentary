**1096. Though she speaks as one who is not close,  
words without anger are quickly understood for what they are.**

Though she hides her love and outwardly speaks harshly like a stranger, it quickly becomes clear that there is no anger within her and that love remains in her heart.

**1097. Harsh little words without hatred, and a look like that of an enemy—  
these are the signs of one who acts as a stranger while being close.**

Harsh words without hostility and a fierce look like one directed at an enemy are signs that reveal a person who outwardly acts like a stranger while inwardly holding love.

**1098. There is a new beauty in her swaying nature; when I look,  
she, filled with affection, smiles softly.**

When I look at her, she smiles softly with affection toward me; at that moment, that swaying, slender-waisted woman appears with a new radiance.

**1099. Looking with the common look of strangers in public  
is a quality found among lovers.**

Lovers have a certain habit: in a public place, they look at one another as though they were looking at strangers.

**1100. When eye meets eye and the two looks become one,  
spoken words serve no purpose.**

When eyes join with eyes in equal love and become one, spoken words are no longer necessary.
