# 112. Praising Her Beauty

**1111. Long live, tender anicham flower! Yet softer even than you  
is the woman with whom I have fallen in love.**

I praise and celebrate the softness of the anicham flower; but my beloved is softer than even that flower.

**1112. O heart that is dazzled on seeing a flower, look at her eyes:  
do you think they are like a flower that many people see?**

O heart that becomes enchanted on seeing a flower! Look at her eyes; they shine like a flower at which many people look and marvel.

**1113. Tender-shoot body, pearl-like teeth, intoxicating fragrance,  
kohl-lined spear-eyes and bamboo-like shoulders—she has them all.**

A row of pearl-like teeth, bamboo-like shoulders, a complexion like a tender shoot, an intoxicating fragrance and kohl-lined spear-like eyes—she is my beloved!

**1114. If the water-lilies could see her, they would bend and look at the ground,  
saying, “We cannot equal the eyes of this jewel-adorned woman.”**

If the water-lily flowers could see my beloved, they would bow their heads and look at the ground, saying, “We cannot become equal to her eyes!”

**1115. She wore anicham flowers without removing their stalks;  
for her slender waist, no auspicious drum sounded.**

No auspicious drum sounded for her; for her waist broke and she fell. The reason was that she placed anicham flowers upon her head without removing their stalks.
