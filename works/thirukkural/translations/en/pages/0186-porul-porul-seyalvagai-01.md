# 76. The Way of Acquiring Wealth

**751. There is no wealth other than wealth that can make  
even those not regarded as worthy into persons of worth.**

Nothing other than the money accumulated in their hands can raise even people who are not worthy of respect to a level at which they are treated with respect.

**752. Everyone despises those without wealth;  
everyone shows distinction to the wealthy.**

Praising and honouring those who possess wealth while mocking and reviling those who do not is the way this world conducts itself.

**753. The unfailing lamp called wealth goes to the place one has in mind  
and cuts away the darkness there.**

If only the inextinguishable lamp called wealth is in one's hand, one can go to the place one has in mind and drive away the suffering called darkness.

**754. Wealth acquired by knowing the proper means and without evil  
yields aram and also yields happiness.**

Only wealth that has not been accumulated by taking an evil path will show a person the path of aram and also bring that person happiness.

**755. Let wealth that does not come with compassion and love  
be rejected and left to roll among the base.**

Even if it is great wealth, when it has not come by the path of compassion or the way of love, it should be disregarded and left aside.
