# 60. Possession of Initiative

**591. Those who possess initiative alone are truly called possessors;  
what possession do those without it really possess?**

Only those who possess initiative deserve to be called people who possess something. Those without initiative cannot truly be called possessors, whatever else they may have.

**592. Possession of the inner drive is true possession; material possession  
will not remain but will pass away.**

Apart from the one possession called initiative, nothing else can be called a permanent possession.

**593. Those who hold unwavering initiative in their hands  
do not lament, “We have lost our prosperity.”**

People who firmly hold on to initiative will not lose their spirit and become distressed even when they happen to lose prosperity.

**594. Prosperity seeks out and goes to the one  
whose initiative does not waver.**

Advancement itself seeks out people who possess firm, unwavering initiative and goes to join them.

**595. The height of a lotus stalk is the measure of the water;  
the height of human life is the measure of the initiative within.**

The length of a lotus stalk is determined by the depth of the water in which it grows. In the same way, the rise in a person's life will be to the measure of the initiative held in that person's mind.
