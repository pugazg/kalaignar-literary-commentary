**356. Those who learn here and discern the true reality  
attain the path from which there is no return here.**

Those who learn all that makes them fit for renunciant life, realize the true meaning and live according to it will not again desire domestic life.

**357. If the mind, having examined, firmly realizes the truth,  
there is no need to think again of birth.**

Those who inquire into the truth and know it with certainty will not think that there is another birth.

**358. Wisdom is seeing the distinguished true reality  
so that the folly called birth departs.**

Wisdom consists in removing the ignorance spoken of as another birth and establishing the clear truth.

**359. If one understands what suffering clings to and lives so that attachment ceases,  
the suffering that would cling will no longer come and cling.**

If we want sufferings not to attach themselves to us, we must understand what causes those sufferings and remove our attachment to those causes.

**360. When desire, anger and delusion lose even their names,  
the suffering they bring will perish.**

The suffering that approaches those who give no place to desire, hatred and ignorance will itself disappear.
