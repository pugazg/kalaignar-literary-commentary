# 113. Declaring the Excellence of Love

**1126. My beloved never leaves my eyes; even when I blink, he does not suffer—  
so subtle is my beloved.**

My beloved will never go anywhere from within my eyes; even when I close them and blink, he will not suffer, because he is so subtle.

**1127. Since my beloved dwells within my eyes, I do not apply kohl,  
knowing that it might hide him.**

Because my beloved is within my very eyes, I am afraid that applying kohl might somehow hide him; and so I do not apply it.

**1128. Since my beloved dwells within my heart, I fear eating anything hot,  
knowing that the heat might burn him.**

Lovers are those who have mingled heart with heart to such a degree that one fears that eating something hot might burn the beloved who dwells within the heart.

**1129. Knowing that my beloved within my eyes might disappear if I blink, I do not blink;  
yet for this, the town calls him unloving.**

Knowing that the beloved within my eyes may disappear, I keep from blinking; and for this very reason, the town says that he is an unloving man who has given me the suffering of sleeplessness.

**1130. He lives joyfully within my heart forever; yet this town says  
that the unloving one lives apart from me.**

When the beloved is forever living heart within heart with me, it is wrong for the townspeople, who do not understand this, to reproach us as though we lived separated from one another.
