**176. One who seeks compassion and stands upon its path  
will be ruined if, coveting wealth, one plots evil.**

A person who desires compassion and walks on the path toward attaining it will come to ruin if he goes astray, covets another's property and engages in an evil act.

**177. Do not desire the gain that comes through coveting;  
when it bears fruit, its benefit will not be good.**

If a person seeks prosperity by seizing another's property, the benefit of that prosperity will not bring goodness.

**178. What keeps one's wealth from diminishing?  
Not coveting the property in another's hand.**

If one wishes one's own wealth and prosperity not to diminish, one must not desire to possess another person's property as one's own.

**179. Wealth will join those wise people who know aram and do not covet,  
recognizing the measure of their worth.**

Wealth will come to learned and worthy people who understand the path of aram and do not desire to seize another's property, in keeping with their ability.

**180. Coveting without thought brings destruction;  
the proud strength called “not desiring” brings victory.**

If one desires to seize another's property without thinking of the consequences, destruction will follow; if one remains free of such desire, success in life will follow.
