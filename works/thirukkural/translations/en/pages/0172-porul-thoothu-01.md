# 69. The Envoy

**681. A loving nature, birth in an honoured family, and qualities esteemed by rulers  
are the qualities of one who speaks as an envoy.**

A loving disposition, birth in a renowned family, and good qualities that rulers can appreciate are the qualifications required of an envoy.

**682. Love, knowledge, and eloquence that speaks after examination  
are the three indispensable qualities of one who carries a message.**

The three important qualities needed by one who goes as an envoy are love, knowledge, and the eloquence to speak after examining the matter.

**683. Among those versed in books, he must be master of the books—the quality  
of one who speaks of the action that will bring victory among spear-bearing opponents.**

An envoy who delivers a message to another country in a manner that will bring victory to his own country must be a master even among those who have studied and understood books.

**684. Let one who possesses in full these three—intelligence, impressive appearance,  
and learning examined and understood—go on the mission.**

The person who undertakes the task of delivering an envoy's message must be filled with all three: intelligence, distinction of appearance, and learning that has been examined and clearly understood.

**685. To speak concisely, omit what need not be said, speak pleasantly,  
and bring good benefit—that is the work of an envoy.**

It is the mark of a fine envoy to gather the message into suitable form without provoking anger, leave out what is unnecessary, and speak in a manner that is pleasing and produces a good result.
