**1066. Even if one begs for water for a thirsty cow,  
there is nothing more degrading to the tongue than begging.**

Even if one asks by begging for water for a cow suffering from thirst, there is nothing more degrading to the tongue that asks in that way.

**1067. I beg of all who beg: when you must beg,  
do not beg from those who conceal what they have.**

I stretch out my hands and beg of all those who stretch out their hands: do not stretch them toward people who hide what is in their hands and say, “There is nothing.”

**1068. The unprotected boat called begging will split apart  
when it strikes the rock called concealment.**

When the unprotected boat called begging collides with the stone-hearted refusal that hides what is there and says there is nothing, it will break and shatter.

**1069. The heart melts at the thought of begging; at the thought of concealment,  
it perishes without even anything left to melt.**

The heart melts when one thinks of the condition of those who live by begging. When one thinks of those who live hiding what they possess because they have no mind to give, the heart itself disappears, leaving no way even for it to melt.

**1070. Where can the life of those who conceal what they have hide,  
when the beggar's life departs at their very words?**

The moment those who hide what they possess and say “there is nothing” are heard, the very life of the beggar seems to leave; where, then, can the life of the person who speaks those words be hiding?
