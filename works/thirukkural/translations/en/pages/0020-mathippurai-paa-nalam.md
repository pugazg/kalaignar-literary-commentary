## A. Poetic Quality

**1101. All five senses that know through seeing and hearing,  
tasting, breathing in, and touching are found in the bright-bangled woman.**

In this beautiful, bangled woman are gathered all the pleasures of the five senses: the joy of seeing, hearing, touching, smelling, and tasting.

**1098. When I gaze, a fresh beauty comes upon the swaying one;  
with affection for me, she smiles softly.**

When I look at her, she smiles gently with affection for me; at that moment, that slender-waisted woman appears with a new radiance.

Such examples may be seen.

## B. Excellence of Poetic Figure

Valluvar has expressed his thought by using the figure called *pirithu mozhithal*—saying one thing in order to convey another. Kalaignar too follows that figure in the following Kural commentary and produces a fresh effect.

**17. Even the vast sea would lose its own nature  
if the cloud that draws from it should fail to give back.**

Only when seawater becomes vapour, turns into cloud, and falls as rain into that sea will even the sea remain without drying up. In the same way, those who rise to eminence and fame from human society must be of use to that very society if the society is to live.
