**136. Those of firm mind will not weaken in conduct,  
knowing the disgrace that comes from departing from it.**

People of firm mind understand the degradation that results from departing from conduct, and so they will not behave in a way that allows good conduct to decline.

**137. Through good conduct one attains greatness;  
through departure from it one attains degrading blame.**

Good behaviour brings elevation; otherwise degrading blame will come upon a person.

**138. Good conduct becomes the seed of good;  
evil conduct always brings suffering.**

Good conduct becomes a seed of good in life. Evil conduct brings suffering without end.

**139. For those who possess conduct, even by mistake  
unfit words will not be spoken from the mouth.**

Even accidentally speaking improper words with one's own mouth is a quality that is absent from people of good conduct.

**140. Those who have not learned to live in accord with the world  
are without understanding, though they have learned many things.**

Those who have not learned to live by the quality called conduct accepted by the great are without understanding even if they have read many books.
