# 77. Excellence of the Army

**761. An army complete in all its parts and unafraid of obstacles  
is foremost among all the wealth of a ruler.**

An army that is complete in every respect and is able to fight without fear of obstacles is the greatest wealth of a government.

**762. The firm courage that does not fear obstacles even in a time of weakening  
is rare except in an army of ancient standing.**

Even when it has suffered damage in war and its strength has declined, the firmness of heart that fears no obstacle can belong only to an army of long-established greatness and to no other army.

**763. What does it matter if an enemy of rats roars like the sea?  
It perishes when the cobra breathes.**

Even if rats gather together, roar like the sea, and spew hostility, can they stand before the sound of a cobra's breath? In just that way, when a warrior rises in fury, worthless men will fall.

**764. That alone is an army which does not perish, does not fall prey to stratagem,  
and possesses fearless firmness inherited through its line.**

The true army is one that does not perish in any situation, does not become the victim of stratagem, and possesses a fearless firmness handed down through generations.

**765. That is an army which can unite and stand against  
even death itself when it comes to confront them.**

The name army belongs only to a force that has the strength to unite and stand against death without fear even when death comes to take life.
