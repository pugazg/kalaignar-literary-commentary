**666. What has been planned will be attained exactly as planned  
if those who planned it possess firmness.**

If people remain firm in putting what they have planned into action, they will succeed exactly as they intended.

**667. Do not despise anyone on account of small appearance; the great rolling chariot  
depends upon a linchpin that is itself small.**

No one should be mocked or treated with contempt simply for being small in physical form. One must understand that the linchpin that enables a great chariot to move is itself small in form.

**668. Once an action has been clearly decided without confusion, carry it out  
without wavering, cutting away delay.**

An action decided upon clearly and without mental confusion must be completed swiftly, without allowing either slackness or delay to arise in between.

**669. Even when suffering comes, carry through with courage the action  
that will finally yield happiness.**

An action capable of bringing happiness is one that can be completed with courage even when suffering comes, without allowing that suffering to deter it.

**670. However much other strength a person may possess, the world does not value  
one who does not possess firmness in the action undertaken.**

No matter how strong a person may be, if there is no firmness in the action undertaken, the world will not respect that person.
