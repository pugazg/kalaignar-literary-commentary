By service, by sacrifice, by ability,  
and by the merit thereby earned, Kalaignar became leader of the Tamil people;  
to dispel the “maya” that bewitches the life of Tamils,  
and to make the Kural's aram, the path to uplift, shine forth,  
today he has given a commentary displaying the subtle power of his intellect—  
even for us, revealing the full subtlety of that is no easy task!

If the Thirukkural is a honeycomb,  
I would say this commentary is its pressed essence;  
not only the sweetness of honey that delights the tongue,  
but medicinal benefit too is the distinction of this commentary!  
This volume, easy to study and easy to understand,  
is a hand-lamp for Tamils awakening from sleep!  
For the younger generation, in life,  
it is a sacred lamp that dispels darkness and shows the way!

**K. Anbazhagan**
