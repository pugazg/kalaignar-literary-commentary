# 103. Working for the Community

**1021. For one who says, “I will not withdraw my hand from doing my duty,”  
there is no greatness greater than that greatness.**

There is no greatness above that of the person who, without growing weary in carrying out the duty that is rightly his, continues his efforts without giving them up.

**1022. Through the sustained work born of these two—effective action  
and mature understanding—a community rises.**

When a person works tirelessly with deep understanding and perseverance, the greatness of the citizens associated with him rises.

**1023. For one who says, “I will work for my community,” even the divine  
girds itself and comes forward before him.**

For one who labours without rest to raise the citizens associated with him, even the power of nature that is spoken of as divine action will gird itself and come forward to offer support.

**1024. Success comes of itself, without elaborate planning,  
to those who work without delay to raise their community.**

For those who engage without delay in the work of raising the communities connected with them and keep striving, successes will gather of their own accord.

**1025. The world gathers around as kin the one who lives without fault,  
working for the good of his community.**

People will gather around, regarding as their own relative the person who remains blameless and works for the welfare of the citizens.
