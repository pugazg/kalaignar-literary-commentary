# 56. Tyrannical Rule

**551. More cruel than one who takes killing as his trade  
is the ruler who violates the path of virtue and torments the people.**

A government that crosses the path of virtue and causes suffering to its citizens is more cruel than a person who has made killing his occupation.

**552. When one who holds the sceptre uses authority to seize wealth from the people,  
it is like the threat of a robber standing with a spear.**

For those who hold the sceptre of rule to display their authority and take property from their citizens is like the threat of a robber armed with a spear.

**553. The country of a ruler who does not examine each day  
and govern according to justice will decay day by day.**

A country ruled by a government that does not daily examine the good and harm produced by its administration and act accordingly will fall into disorder.

**554. A government that rules tyrannically without studying the condition of the country  
will lose both its financial resources and the regard of its people.**

A government that exercises harsh rule without examining the condition of the country will lose both its financial foundations and the respect of its people.

**555. The tears shed by people unable to bear oppression  
become the weapon that wears away a ruler's wealth.**

The tears shed by people who cannot endure cruelty become a weapon capable of destroying the government.
