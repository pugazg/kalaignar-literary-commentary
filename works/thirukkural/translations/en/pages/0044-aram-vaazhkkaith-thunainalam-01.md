# 6. The Worth of a Life Partner

**51. A life partner is she who possesses the qualities suited to the home  
and manages the household in keeping with her husband's means.**

A woman who possesses the qualities proper to domestic life and manages the family in keeping with its material means will be a great support in her husband's life.

**52. If the excellence proper to the home is absent in the wife,  
however distinguished the life may otherwise be, it has no special excellence.**

A domestic life in which a wife of good character is absent has no distinctive excellence, however distinguished it may otherwise be.

**53. What is lacking when the wife is worthy?  
What is present when the wife is not worthy?**

Everything will be present in a life in which there is a wife of good character. In a life without such a wife, nothing will be present.

**54. What is there greater in womanhood,  
if she possesses the firmness called chastity?**

If a woman possesses the steadfast quality of womanhood called chastity, what else is there that deserves greater honour than that?

**55. She who does not worship a deity, but rises worshipping her husband—  
the rain will fall when she commands, “Fall.”**

A wife who regards her husband's word as greater than the word of God and worships him alone will come to think of herself as a slave, like rain that, trembling in fear, falls as soon as it is ordered, “Fall.”
