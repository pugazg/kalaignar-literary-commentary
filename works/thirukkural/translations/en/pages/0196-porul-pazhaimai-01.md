# 81. Long-Standing Friendship

**801. What is called old friendship? It is friendship that does not diminish  
any right born of long familiarity.**

To honour an old friendship means that friends who have long been close preserve the rights of that friendship without letting them disappear.

**802. The essential part of friendship is the liberty born of closeness;  
accepting that liberty is the duty of the worthy.**

The duty proper to worthy people who honour the rights of long-standing friends is itself a mark of true friendship.

**803. What use is long familiarity if one cannot accept  
what old friends do by right as though one had done it oneself?**

If one does not agree with and accept, as though they were one's own acts, the things old friends do out of the rights of friendship, all the friendship cultivated until then becomes useless.

**804. If an old friend, through the right of friendship, acts without asking,  
a good friend will still accept what was done.**

If, because of the rights of long familiarity, a friend carries out an act without asking, one who is truly a good friend will accept it.

**805. If friends do something that causes pain, understand it  
as ignorance or as the great liberty of friendship.**

When friends do something that causes grief, one should take it as something done either out of ignorance or because of the rights of friendship.
