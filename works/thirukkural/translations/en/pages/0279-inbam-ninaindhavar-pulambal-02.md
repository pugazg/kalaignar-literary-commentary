# 121. Lamenting the Absent Beloved

**1206. I remain alive only by remembering the days  
when I was together with him; what else could keep me alive?**

I stay alive only by remembering the days when I was united with him; what else could I possibly think of and continue living?

**1207. Even while I remember without knowing how to forget, my heart burns;  
what would become of me if I forgot?**

Even while I keep remembering, without there being any such thing as forgetfulness, the suffering of separation burns and scorches me! What would happen if I ceased to think and actually forgot?

**1208. However much I think of him, he does not grow angry;  
is that not the great kindness my beloved shows me?**

No matter how much I think of him, my beloved will not become angry with me for it. Is that not the great help he renders me?

**1209. Thinking of the lovelessness of the one who said, “We are one, not two,”  
my sweet life is slowly fading away.**

Remembering that the beloved who said, “We are one; we are not separate,” has without compassion left me and gone away, my life is departing little by little.

**1210. O moon, live on and do not disappear, so that with my eyes  
I may see the one who was inseparable from me and has now gone away.**

Moon! Long may you live. Do not vanish; remain as my companion so that with my own eyes I may search for and find the beloved who was once inseparable from me and has now gone away.
