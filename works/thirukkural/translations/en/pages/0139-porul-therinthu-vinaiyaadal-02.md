**516. Examine the person who can act, examine the task,  
and act with an understanding of the proper time.**

Choose the person capable of carrying out the work, examine the work that must be done, understand the time, and then put it into action.

**517. Having examined, ‘This person can complete this by these means,’  
entrust that task to him.**

Examine how a person will complete a particular matter and only after that entrust the matter to him.

**518. After examining a person's fitness for a task,  
make him the person responsible for it.**

Only after determining that a person is suited to take part in an action should he be engaged in that work.

**519. Prosperity leaves the one who misjudges the loyalty  
of a person wholly intent on completing the work entrusted to him.**

Greatness will leave the person who wrongly interprets the relationship of one whose eyes and mind are fixed on completing the task he has undertaken.

**520. Let the ruler examine each day whether the worker remains unwearied;  
so long as the worker does not falter, the world will not falter.**

So long as the hearts of working people do not wither, the prosperity of the world will not wither. Therefore, those who govern must examine the condition of working people every day and do what is needed.
