# 79. Friendship

**781. What is harder to achieve than friendship? And what protection  
is harder for hostile action to overcome?**

There is no rare achievement like forming friendship. Likewise, there is no other act so suited to protection.

**782. Friendship with the wise grows like the waxing moon;  
the friendship of fools wanes after reaching fullness.**

Friendship formed with people of understanding begins like the crescent moon and grows into the full moon. Friendship formed with those without understanding sprouts like the full moon and then diminishes like the waning moon until it disappears.

**783. As a good book gives delight each time it is read,  
so does companionship with people of character each time it is cultivated.**

Just as the excellence of a book gives increasing delight the more it is read, friendship with people of character can give increasing delight the more one associates with them.

**784. Friendship is not for laughing together; when a friend goes too far,  
it is for going forward, rebuking, and correcting him.**

Friendship is not merely for laughing and rejoicing; it is for rebuking and correcting friends when they stray from the right path.

**785. Meeting and long familiarity are not necessary; shared feeling itself  
gives the rightful closeness of friendship.**

For the rights of friendship to blossom between two people, prior contact and familiarity are not necessary. A shared feeling of mind between the two is enough.
