**476. One who has climbed to the tip of a branch,  
if he tries to go beyond it, will meet the end of his life.**

A person who overestimates himself and crosses all limits will meet the same fate as one who has climbed to the tip of a branch and tries to climb still farther.

**477. Give after knowing the measure of one's means;  
that is the way to preserve wealth and live by it.**

Knowing the measure of one's income and distributing it accordingly is the way to preserve one's resources in good order and live.

**478. Even if the incoming stream is small, there is no harm  
so long as the outgoing stream does not grow wider.**

If expenditure does not cross its limits, no harm follows merely because income is small.

**479. The life of one who does not live within measure  
will seem to exist, then disappear into nothing.**

If plans are not made with an understanding of what one has, what one can produce, and what one may yet earn, strength or prosperity may appear to exist but will fade away and vanish.

**480. Generosity that does not weigh the measure of what one has  
will quickly destroy the bounds of one's wealth.**

If one keeps giving without examining the measure of one's possessions, one's prosperity will quickly be ruined.
