# 12. Impartiality

**111. The quality called impartiality is indeed good,  
if one can conduct oneself without leaning to one side among different parties.**

The quality called impartiality, which can bring good, lies precisely in not taking a one-sided position after dividing people as enemy, neighbour, or friend.

**112. The prosperity of one who possesses impartiality will not decay;  
it stands as security for those who come after.**

The wealth of one who remains impartial does not perish; it will also bring benefit to successive generations.

**113. Even if it brings good, abandon at once  
the gain that comes by departing from impartiality.**

Even if the benefit that may arise from departing from impartiality is capable of bringing good, that benefit must be given up and impartiality itself must be upheld.

**114. Whether a person was worthy or unworthy  
will be seen from what remains after that person.**

Whether a person was upright, or acted by departing from right conduct and justice, will be determined by the words of praise or the words of blame that remain after that person.

**115. Loss and gain are not absent from life;  
not bending in one's heart is an ornament to the worthy.**

Rise and decline are part of the nature of the world. In both conditions, remaining impartial and showing firmness is what gives beauty to the great.
