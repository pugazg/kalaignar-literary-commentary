# 110. Understanding Signs

**1091. Two looks dwell in her kohl-lined eyes: one look  
brings the sickness, and the other is medicine for that sickness.**

There are two kinds of looks in the kohl-lined eyes of the beloved: one look brings the sickness of love; the other look gives the medicine for that sickness.

**1092. The brief, stolen glance that her eyes take  
is not half of love's pleasure—it is greater.**

That sly sidelong glance is greater than even half the measure of the pleasure of love!

**1093. She looked at me; then, looking, she bowed in modesty—  
that was water poured upon the bond of her love.**

There was deep modesty in the sidelong look with which she looked at me; that act was like water poured upon the crop of the love she bears for me.

**1094. When I look, she looks at the ground; when I do not look,  
she looks at me and smiles softly.**

When I look at her, she bends down and looks at the ground; when I am not looking, she looks at me and smiles inwardly with delight. Is that not a sign revealing the love she bears for me?

**1095. She does not look directly at me; instead, as though narrowing one eye,  
she looks and smiles within herself.**

She does not stare directly at me; yet, as though keeping one eye slightly narrowed, she looks toward me and delights within herself.
