# 73. Fearlessness in the Assembly

**721. Those of purity who know the measure of words and understand the kinds of assemblies  
do not let their speech falter.**

If people of purity who speak with a sense of the proper measure of words also possess the ability to understand the kinds of people in an assembly, they will not speak in a way that gives rise to error.

**722. Among the learned, those are called truly learned who can speak what they have learned  
so that it reaches the minds of the learned before them.**

A person who can speak before the learned in such a way that what has been learned takes hold in their minds will be regarded as superior among all those who are learned.

**723. Those who die in the midst of battle are easy to find; rare are those  
who do not fear in the midst of an assembly.**

For many people, fighting without fear even of death on the battlefield is easy; only a few can speak without fear in an assembly filled with people of knowledge.

**724. Before the learned, speak what you have learned so that it reaches them;  
then receive what is greater from those more learned than yourself.**

In an assembly of scholars, we must present what we have learned in a manner they can accept, and then learn still more from those who have learned more than we have.

**725. Learn according to the proper measure so as to give a reply  
without fearing the assembly.**

To answer interruptions while speaking in an assembly without fear, one must have learned grammar and the skill of measure called logic.
