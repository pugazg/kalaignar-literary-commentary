**966. If it brings no fame and does not save one in the heavenly world, what worth is there  
in following behind those who despise one?**

What fame can be gained by enduring contempt, abandoning honour, and submissively following behind another person? Will some nonexistent heaven be gained?

**967. Rather than live by following one who does not respect you,  
it is better to be said to have died then and there.**

It is far better to die than to live by going behind someone who does not value you.

**968. What medicine is a life that merely protects the flesh  
when the stature of one's great honour has been destroyed?**

There is no medicine by which one can avoid death. When that is so, honour is more enduring than life; to lower one's own greatness for the sake of improving one's life, without cherishing honour, is a degrading act.

**969. Like the kavari deer that cannot live when its hair is removed,  
people of greatness give up life when honour is at stake.**

It is said that the kavari deer cannot live if the hair on its body is removed. In the same way, when honour is about to be destroyed, people of high character will give up life itself.

**970. The world forever honours the fame of people of honour  
who will not live when degradation comes.**

The world will always cherish the fame of those who can give up their lives when degradation comes that would destroy their honour.
