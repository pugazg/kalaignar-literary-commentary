# 124. Loss of Bodily Beauty

**1236. As my shoulders shrink and the bangles slip away, I grieve  
to hear people call him cruel.**

When I hear those who see my shoulders grow thin and my bangles slip off say that my beloved is without compassion, my heart is wounded.

**1237. O heart, will you not win honour by telling the cruel one  
of the suffering of my wasting shoulders?**

O heart! Will you not gain distinction by telling the one who has left me without compassion about the anguish of my shoulders as they waste and wither?

**1238. When the arms that tightly embraced her loosened even a little,  
the forehead of the bangle-wearing maiden turned pale.**

The moment the arms that had held her tightly loosened a little, the beloved woman's forehead could not bear even that small space of separation and took on the colour of pallor.

**1239. When cool air entered between the embrace,  
the maiden's great rain-like eyes grew pale.**

While they were tightly embracing, cool air entered between them; taking even that as a separation, the beloved woman's wide, long eyes turned pale.

**1240. Seeing the bright forehead grow pale,  
the pallor of her eyes itself fell into great sorrow.**

Seeing her crescent-like forehead turn pale through the suffering of separation, even the pallor in her eyes seems to have fallen into deep distress.
