**1036. If the farmers' hands are folded, even those who say,  
“We have renounced all desire,” cannot remain so.**

Even ascetics who say that they have given up every attachment must live looking to the hand of the farmer.

**1037. If a measure of soil is dried by repeated ploughing until it becomes a quarter,  
even a handful of manure will not be needed for the crop to flourish.**

If one palam of soil is repeatedly ploughed until it becomes a quarter-palam, the crop will grow and flourish without even a handful of manure being needed.

**1038. Manuring is better than ploughing; after the weeds are removed,  
protecting the crop is better even than watering it.**

Applying manure is better than ploughing; removing weeds and supplying water are very good; better than all of these is protecting that crop.

**1039. If the farmer stays away, the field will sulk  
like a wife estranged from her husband.**

If a farmer does not go every day to look after his land, it will become like a wife who has withdrawn in resentment from him and will cease to yield a crop.

**1040. When she sees those who sit idle saying, “We have no means,”  
the good woman called the earth laughs.**

Mother Earth mocks those who sit lazily saying that they have no means by which to live.
