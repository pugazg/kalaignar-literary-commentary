**856. The life of one who thinks victory comes easily through opposing in discord  
will quickly suffer and perish.**

The life of a person who thinks it is easy to win by acting in opposition through difference will quickly fall into suffering and ruin.

**857. Those of harmful understanding who cling to discord  
do not see the true means that leads to victory.**

People of bad understanding who take pleasure in hostile feeling will not recognize the true substance that guides one toward victory.

**858. Turning away from discord is gain; if one instead nourishes and enlarges it,  
ruin will grow.**

If one confronts and removes the difference that arises in the mind, good will result; if, on the contrary, one greatly encourages and develops it, harm will result.

**859. When prosperity comes, one sees no discord; when one seeks one's own ruin,  
one magnifies that discord.**

When good comes to a person, he will not even think of differences. But when he is intent on bringing harm upon himself, he will enlarge those differences.

**860. From discord come all things painful; from friendly feeling  
comes the great good called joy.**

Suffering follows those who display hostile feeling through differences of mind. For those who act with a sense of friendship, the good result called great happiness will arise.
