**116. If one's own heart turns away from impartiality and thinks of doing wrong,  
one should know, “I am going to ruin.”**

If the thought arises in a person that one may act by departing from impartiality, that person should understand for himself that he is on the way to ruin.

**117. The world will not regard as decline the low state of one  
who remains impartial and abides in goodness.**

If a person who lives on the path of aram without departing from impartiality becomes poor because wealth does not accumulate for that reason, the world will praise that person rather than regard him as low.

**118. Like a balance poised to weigh evenly,  
not leaning to one side is an ornament to the worthy.**

True impartiality is to stand like the honest needle of a balance, without tilting to one side, and speak what is just.

**119. Straightness is speech without deviation,  
if the mind is wholly free from one-sided bias.**

If a person has uprightness and firmness of heart, there will be justice and fairness in that person's words. That is what is called impartiality.

**120. For those who trade, true trade is to conduct business  
by treating the goods of others as their own.**

Even when something belongs to another, regarding it just as one's own property and conducting trade with honesty is what is called the proper conduct of commerce.
