# 116. Inability to Bear Separation

**1151. If there is a way for you not to leave, tell me; otherwise, speak of your swift return  
to those who will still be alive when you return.**

If you are not going to leave, tell me that happy news. If you must go no matter what, then take your leave now of whoever will still be alive when you return.

**1152. Once, the gaze of the one with sweet eyes brought delight; now even our embrace  
is filled with sorrow through fear of separation.**

Once, merely embracing him with my eyes was delight; but now, even while embracing him bodily in joy, does not suffering rise because of the fear that thinks of separation?

**1153. Assurance is difficult even from one who understands,  
for separation may still arise.**

Even the lover who knows the suffering of separation may have to leave us; therefore, we cannot take his words “I will not leave” as a certainty.

**1154. If the one who said, “Do not fear,” leaves me,  
what fault is there in the one who trusted his assuring words?**

If the one who said, “I will not leave; do not fear,” goes away from me, what fault can there be in my having believed what he said?

**1155. If you would preserve love, guard against the beloved's departure;  
for once he leaves, reunion is difficult.**

Because reunion is not easy once the beloved has gone away, one must guard from the very beginning against his leaving.
