**96. If one seeks what is good and speaks sweetly,  
evil will diminish and aram will grow.**

If evil deeds are to be removed and the path of aram made to flourish, sweet words must be used to show what the good path is.

**97. Words that yield benefit and never depart from good character  
bring delight and goodness to the one who speaks them.**

Words that are capable of bringing good benefit and do not depart from good character can bring both joy and goodness to those who speak them.

**98. Sweet speech free from pettiness  
brings joy both in this life and after it.**

Pleasant words free from pettiness can bring a person fame both while he lives and after he has lived and passed away.

**99. One who sees that sweet words give delight—  
why would such a person use harsh words?**

Why should someone who understands that sweet words give joy use harsh words instead?

**100. To speak harshly when sweet words are available  
is like passing over fruit and plucking unripe ones to eat.**

When sweet words are available, setting them aside and speaking harshly is like putting fruits aside and plucking unripe ones to eat.
