# 120. Intensity of Solitary Longing

**1191. Those who are loved by the very ones they love  
have gained love's fruit without a seed.**

Those fortunate enough to be loved by the beloved whom they themselves desire have gained the fruit of a life of love—a fruit without a seed.

**1192. The affection a beloved gives to the one who loves  
is like the rain the sky gives to those who live.**

When lovers meet one another at the proper time and shower affection upon each other, it is like seasonal rain falling when it is needed for life.

**1193. Only those who are loved by the ones they love  
may rightly boast, “We live in delight.”**

Only those bound together in a love from which neither departs can possess the proud joy of saying, “We live in happiness.”

**1194. One who is loved has no true good fortune  
if the one she loves does not love her in return.**

If a state arises in which one is not loved in return, that beloved must be regarded as one without the feeling of friendship.

**1195. What joy can the one I love give me  
if he does not love me as I love him?**

If he does not desire and love me as I desire and love him, what happiness can I receive from him?
