# 66. Purity in Action

**651. The excellence of good companions brings strength; excellence in action  
brings everything that is desired.**

The companions one gains increase one's strength; the good actions performed together with them bring every kind of benefit.

**652. At every time one must abandon actions  
that yield neither fame nor good.**

Impure actions that bring neither fame nor benefit must be cast aside and never undertaken under any circumstance.

**653. Those who desire to rise ever higher must avoid actions  
that cause their own honour to decline.**

People who wish to rise higher and higher must take care that their own actions do not destroy their reputation.

**654. Those of clear understanding and unwavering firmness will not do a degrading deed  
even when they are placed in suffering.**

People who possess clear intelligence and firmness will not engage in a degrading act even for the sake of escaping suffering.

**655. Do not do anything that will make one lament, “What have I done?”  
If it has once been done, it is good not to do such a thing again.**

One should not do things that later cause the thought, “What wrong have we done?” and bring distress. Even if such an act has once been committed, it is good not to continue or repeat it.
