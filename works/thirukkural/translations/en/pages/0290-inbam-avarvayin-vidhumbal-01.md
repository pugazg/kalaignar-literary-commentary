# 127. Longing for the Beloved

**1261. My eyes have lost their light from watching the road for his return;  
my fingers have worn thin counting the days since he left.**

Looking and looking along the road, thinking “He will come, he will come,” my eyes have lost their brightness; marking the days of his absence on the wall and touching them again and again as I count, my fingers too have worn away.

**1262. Radiant-bangled friend, if today I try to forget him,  
my shoulders will lose their beauty, grow thin, and their bangles will fall.**

My friend, while the suffering of separation torments me, if I try to forget the beloved from whom I am separated, it is certain that my shoulders will lose their beauty, grow thin, and the bangles will slip away.

**1263. He went seeking victory, taking courage as his companion;  
I remain alive only in the hope of his return.**

My beloved went away desiring victory, with courage itself as his firm companion; I remain alive only because I hope that he will return.

**1264. Thinking of when the one who joined me in love and then departed will return,  
my heart climbs to the topmost branch of a tree to look.**

Wondering when the beloved who was united with me in love and then went away will return, my heart climbs to the highest branch of a tree and looks out for him.

**1265. Let me see my husband to my eyes' content; only after I have seen him  
will the pallor leave my soft shoulders.**

May I see my husband until my eyes are full; only after I see him will the pallor spread across my soft shoulders disappear.
