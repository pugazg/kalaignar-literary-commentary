**106. Forget not the friendship of the stainless;  
forsake not the friendship of one who stood by you in distress.**

The relationship of those without stain must not be forgotten; nor should the friendship of one who stood as a support in suffering be abandoned.

**107. Through sevenfold generations and seven births, one will remember  
the friendship of those who removed one's suffering.**

Just as people speak in exaggeration of “seven times seven generations” and “seven times seven births,” there is in truth no limit of time for remembering and honouring the pure friendship of one who removed another person's suffering.

**108. To forget a good done to you is not good;  
to forget harm done to you that very moment is good.**

It is not good to forget a benefit someone has done for us. If that person has done us harm, it is good to forget only that, at that very moment.

**109. Even if one causes suffering as cruel as killing, it will fade  
when a single good once done by that person is remembered.**

If even a very cruel harm done by someone is to leave without wounding our heart, it is enough simply to remember a good that person had done for us earlier.

**110. There is life even for one who forgets any aram;  
there is no life for one who forgets help done to them.**

There is life for one who has forgotten any aram; but there is no life for one who forgets the help another person has done.
