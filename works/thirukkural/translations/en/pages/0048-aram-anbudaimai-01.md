# 8. Love

**71. Can love be shut behind a bolt?  
The tears of those who care will reveal it.**

The love within the heart cannot be locked away with a bolt. When one sees the suffering of a person one loves, that love will reveal itself through drops of tears.

**72. Those without love claim everything for themselves;  
those who possess love regard even their very body as belonging to others.**

Those without love will claim that everything belongs to them. Those with love, however, will think that their body, possessions, and life itself all exist for others.

**73. The union of love and action  
is like the bond that joins life and the body.**

The highest harmony is for love and action to be joined together just as life and the body are joined.

**74. Love gives rise to attachment toward others;  
that gives rise to the matchless distinction called friendship.**

Love causes the heart to become attached to others. That attachment creates the great distinction called friendship.

**75. The distinction attained by those who live happily in the world  
is said to be the fruit of living with a loving heart.**

It may be said that the distinction enjoyed by those who live happily in the world is the benefit that comes from their being people with loving hearts.
