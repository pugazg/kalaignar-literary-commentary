# 124. Loss of Bodily Beauty

**1231. Thinking of the one who has gone far away leaving us this misery,  
my eyes have lost their beauty and are ashamed before fragrant flowers.**

The eyes of the beloved woman, grieving that he has gone far away after leaving her the suffering of separation, have lost their beauty and now lie ashamed before flowers.

**1232. Growing pale and shedding tears, my eyes seem to proclaim  
that the one I love shows me no love.**

The eyes, pale and pouring tears, seem to announce that the beloved whom I desire has not shown me affection.

**1233. The shoulders that swelled on the day of union now seem, by their wasting,  
to proclaim how completely he has withdrawn.**

The shoulders that were full when we lay embracing are now seen to have grown thin, as though for the very purpose of announcing the beloved's separation.

**1234. Having lost their old beauty, my once full shoulders waste away;  
the green-gold bangles loosen and fall as my companion has left.**

My once full, youthful shoulders have lost their former beauty, and the green-gold bangles slip off and fall because I waste away in separation from my beloved.

**1235. My shoulders, with bangles slipping and ancient beauty withered,  
proclaim the cruelty of the cruel one.**

As the bangles loosen and fall, my shoulders, having lost the beauty they once possessed, tell the town of the cruelty of the beloved who has left me.
