# 36. Realizing Truth

**351. Ignoble life comes from the delusion  
that takes what is not real to be reality.**

The life of one who is deluded into believing a false thing to be the true reality will not attain distinction.

**352. Joy arises when darkness departs  
for those whose delusion is gone and whose vision is stainless.**

When confusion clears and spotless truth is understood, ignorance departs and well-being appears.

**353. For those who have removed doubt and gained clarity,  
heaven stands nearer than the earth.**

Those who resolve their doubts through clear inquiry gain the confidence to regard heaven as much nearer than the earth.

**354. Even if one has mastered the five senses, there is no benefit  
for those who have not realized the truth.**

Those who have not discovered and clearly understood the truth gain no benefit even if they have subdued and conquered all five senses.

**355. Whatever a thing may be and whatever its nature,  
wisdom is seeing the true reality of that thing.**

Wisdom consists in not being deceived by outward appearance, but in understanding the truth about a thing.
