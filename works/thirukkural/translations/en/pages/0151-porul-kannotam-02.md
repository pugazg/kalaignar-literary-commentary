**576. Those whose eyes are joined to them but not joined to compassion  
are like trees joined to the earth.**

Even if a person has eyes, if those eyes lack the love and mercy proper to them, that person is no different from a tree.

**577. Those without compassion are without eyes; those who truly have eyes  
cannot be without compassion.**

What people with compassionate hearts possess alone deserves to be called eyes; those without compassion are to be regarded as eyeless.

**578. This world belongs to those who can show compassion  
without allowing their duty to be impaired.**

Those who stand foremost both in not failing in their duty and in showering compassion have a rightful claim upon this whole world.

**579. Even toward those whose nature is to seek one's destruction, showing compassion  
and having the strength to forbear is the highest quality.**

Showing patience even toward those whose nature is to seek one's destruction is a very high virtue.

**580. Those who desire a gracious civilization will knowingly drink poison  
even when they see it being poured.**

People who possess compassionate hearts and culture will drink even poison with gladness if it is given by those with whom they have lived in friendship.
