**266. Those who practise ascetic discipline perform their own duty;  
the others, caught in desire, perform futile deeds.**

Only those who undertake ascetic practice marked by self-control, the path of love and patience to endure suffering are people who perform their duty. Those who act otherwise are tossed about by desire and become engaged in futile deeds.

**267. Like gold that shines brighter as it is heated,  
those who endure suffering in ascetic practice shine as suffering burns them.**

However many sufferings strike those who afflict themselves and undertake an observance for the sake of a goal, they will rise in fame like gold that shines more brightly the more it is heated.

**268. All other living beings will honour  
one who has cast away attachment to one's own life.**

The world will praise and honour those who possess neither attachment to what is called “one's own life” nor the pride of “I.”

**269. Even overcoming death becomes possible  
for those who have attained the strength of steadfast observance.**

Those who possess the strength to endure however many sufferings may come and remain firm in their goal will overcome even death and live.

**270. The reason those without strength are many is this:  
few practise ascetic discipline, while many do not.**

The reason people without strength are many is that only a few possess firmness of mind, while many are without firmness.
