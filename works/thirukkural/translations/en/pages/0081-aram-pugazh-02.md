**236. If one appears, let one appear with fame;  
otherwise, it is better not to appear.**

Whatever field one enters, one should shine in it with fame; those unable to do so would do better not to enter that field.

**237. Those who do not live in a way that earns true fame  
should grieve for themselves; why grieve at those who scorn them?**

Those unable to live with true fame should grieve over themselves for that; why should they instead be hurt by people who speak disparagingly of their actions?

**238. If one does not leave behind the remainder called fame,  
the world will call that a blame upon the life itself.**

If one fails to earn fame that can remain even after one's life, the world will say that this itself is a stain upon that life.

**239. The rich yield of the blameless earth will diminish  
when it bears a body without fame.**

If this earth must bear a mere human body that lacks the life called fame, it will be regarded as land without good yield.

**240. Those who live without blame truly live;  
those who live without fame are as good as not living.**

A life without blame is what may be called life. For those without fame, living and not living are one and the same.
