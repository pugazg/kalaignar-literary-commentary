# Publisher's Note

There is no work equal to the Thirukkural. It alone is its equal! It alone can equal it! Many commentaries have appeared for the Thirukkural to explain the meaning of the Kurals. Each, in its own style, has presented the ideas of the Kural verses with distinction. Each is a work possessing a distinctive merit of its own.

Kalaignar, however, has written a distinctive commentary on the Thirukkural as a treasury of rational thought.

Kalaignar studied for the sake of Tamils. He lives for the sake of Tamils. Throughout his life he labours for Tamil. Therefore Tamil Nadu stands in his heart. He became Tamil Nadu; Tamil Nadu became him. What thought would a book evolved from such a person awaken? It is only natural that it should awaken thoughts of the world.

He has written the commentary in a very simple manner, with distinction and in his own style, and given it to the people so that it may reach Tamils throughout the world. Therefore, with great joy, we are beautifully printing as a refined edition and presenting as a new publication, for all Tamils across the world, the Thirukkural commentary written by the living Valluvar.

— **Poompuhar Pathippagam**
