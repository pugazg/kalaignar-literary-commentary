# 93. Abstaining from Liquor

**921. Those forever attached to liquor lose their radiance  
and will not be feared.**

People addicted to the habit of liquor not only lose their distinction; even their opponents will not be afraid of them.

**922. Do not drink liquor; if it is to be drunk, let those drink  
who do not wish to be esteemed by the worthy.**

Liquor should not be drunk. Those who do not wish to receive the good regard of worthy people may drink it if they want.

**923. If drunkenness is painful even before the face of one's mother,  
what then before the faces of the worthy?**

When even a mother, who is capable of forgiving her son's faults, cannot bear to see her son intoxicated by liquor, how will other worthy people bear him?

**924. The good woman called Shame turns away  
from those guilty of the hateful great fault called liquor.**

Before people who have fallen into the detestable great fault called alcoholic intoxication, the good quality called shame will not remain; it will run away.

**925. To pay money in order to acquire loss of true awareness  
is an indescribable folly.**

Paying a price to buy an intoxicating substance so that a person may forget himself and remain in a stupor is a stupidity beyond description.
