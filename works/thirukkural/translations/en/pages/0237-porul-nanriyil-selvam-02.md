**1006. One who neither enjoys his great wealth  
nor has the nature to give something to the worthy is a disease upon that wealth.**

A person who lives without enjoying it himself and without possessing the nature to help deserving people is a disease that has infected the great wealth he possesses.

**1007. The wealth of one who gives nothing to the needy  
is like a woman of great beauty growing old alone.**

The wealth of a person who gives nothing to help the poor is like a woman of great beauty who remains entirely alone and grows old.

**1008. The wealth of one whom people hate is like  
poisonous fruit ripening on a tree in the middle of town**

There is no difference at all between wealth piled up with a person who is hated and fruit hanging in abundance from a poisonous tree in the middle of a town!

**1009. Wealth amassed by casting aside love, tormenting oneself,  
and ignoring the path of virtue will be taken by others.**

Wealth accumulated by completely removing the quality called love, tormenting oneself, and gathering it in ways outside the path of virtue will later be carried away as plunder by others.

**1010. A brief poverty that comes to a wealthy person of good character  
is like a spell of drought when rain fails.**

Even a small period of poverty that comes upon wealthy people of excellent heart is like the failure of rain itself.
