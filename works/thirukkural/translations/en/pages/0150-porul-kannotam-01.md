# 58. Compassion

**571. This world exists because there truly is  
the surpassing grace called compassion.**

This world gains its greatness only because there are people who possess the great beauty called compassion, in which love and mercy are joined.

**572. The way of the world rests in compassion; those without it  
are, in truth, a burden upon the earth.**

Those who stand opposed to the worldly quality called compassion—the quality of embracing others with love and showing mercy—become a burden upon this earth.

**573. What use is melody if it does not accord with the song?  
What use are eyes that do not accord with compassion?**

Eyes that do not accord with the compassionate regard of mercy and love are as useless as music that does not fit the song.

**574. What can eyes do merely by appearing upon the face  
if they do not show compassion in due measure?**

Eyes that do not cause love and mercy to well up within have no use beyond merely appearing to be present on the face.

**575. Compassion is the ornament of the eye; without it,  
the eye will be understood as a wound.**

Only the eye of one who has a compassionate heart deserves to be called an eye. Otherwise it is not an eye; it is a wound.
