**556. It is just rule that gives a government lasting fame;  
without it, that fame will not endure.**

Only rule that does not depart from the path of justice gives a government fame. Otherwise, that government's fame will lose its stability and collapse.

**557. As the world suffers without rain,  
citizens suffer under a government without compassion.**

Just as the world suffers when there is no rain, citizens will be troubled by a government that has no compassion.

**558. Even life without poverty can become more painful than poverty itself  
when it is lived under tyrannical rule.**

Even when a person lives without poverty, if that life falls under a cruel government, it can bring suffering greater than the suffering of poverty.

**559. If a government acts against proper order and the places that store water fall into ruin,  
even the rain given by the sky cannot be stored and turned into prosperity.**

Under a government that acts without proper order, if the places used to store and make use of water are allowed to decay, even the rain that comes from the sky cannot be collected and converted into prosperity.

**560. If a government fails to protect the country properly,  
productive works will cease and important occupations will decline.**

If a government fails to protect the country in the proper manner, no productive work will take place and important occupations will wither away.
