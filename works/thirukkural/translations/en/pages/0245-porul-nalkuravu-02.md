**1046. Though they understand rare truths well and speak them,  
the meaning of the poor person's words loses its force.**

Even if one has studied and understood the ideas of many rare books and speaks of them, if the speaker is poor those ideas may fail to gain acceptance.

**1047. Even a mother will look upon him as a stranger  
when poverty leads him away from virtue.**

When poverty has come upon a person and, because of it, he departs from the path of virtue, even his mother will regard him as she would a stranger.

**1048. Will it come again today—the poverty  
that tormented me yesterday as though it would kill me?**

The poor man longs for the poverty that tormented him yesterday as though killing him not to continue and return again today.

**1049. One may even sleep within fire;  
amid poverty, sleep of any kind is hard to obtain.**

A human being may even manage to lie down and sleep in fire; but amid the suffering inflicted by poverty, sleep is an impossible thing.

**1050. For those without orderly means, failing to renounce completely  
is death even to salt and gruel.**

Those who have fallen into poverty through lack of order, yet do not renounce themselves completely and continue living, become a burden even upon salt and gruel.
