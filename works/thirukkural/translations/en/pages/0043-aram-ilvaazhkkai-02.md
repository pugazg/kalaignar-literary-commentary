**46. If domestic life is lived by the way of aram,  
what could be gained by going on some other path?**

Can the benefit gained by those who have ordered their domestic life according to the path of aram be gained by going along some other path? It cannot.

**47. The one who lives domestic life according to its proper nature  
is foremost among all who strive.**

Among those who undertake efforts toward a good life, the one who stands foremost is the person who understands the nature of domestic life and lives accordingly.

**48. A domestic life that walks in the path, leads others in it, and does not stray from aram  
possesses a strength greater than the austerity of those who fast.**

The domestic life of those who themselves walk in the path of aram and cause others to walk in that path is greater than the austerity observed by renunciants.

**49. Domestic life itself is called aram;  
it is good when it also leaves no room for another's blame.**

A domestic life that leaves no room for blame is praised as household aram.

**50. One who lives rightly in this world  
will be esteemed alongside the gods said to dwell in heaven.**

Many rare qualities are spoken of as belonging to gods. A person who lives in this world standing in the path of aram by which life ought to be lived will be respected as the equal of a god said to live in heaven.
