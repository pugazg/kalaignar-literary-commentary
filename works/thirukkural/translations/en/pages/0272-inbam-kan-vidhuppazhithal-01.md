# 118. Lamenting the Eyes

**1171. Why do these eyes now weep, asking to see the one  
whom they themselves showed me and thus brought this incurable sickness?**

Was it not through the fault of these eyes that the sickness of love arose? Why then do those very eyes weep, asking that the beloved be shown to them?

**1172. Why do the kohl-lined eyes that looked without understanding the consequence  
now suffer without understanding that the pain is of their own making?**

The kohl-lined eyes that once looked in enchantment without understanding the consequences now suffer because the beloved is away, without realizing that they themselves are the cause of that suffering.

**1173. They themselves rushed to look at him; now they themselves weep—  
there is something laughable in this.**

The eyes that rushed of their own accord to look at him and rejoice are today weeping of their own accord. This is something worthy of laughter.

**1174. My eyes, which fixed upon me an inescapable, incurable sickness,  
have themselves run dry, unable now even to shed tears.**

My eyes, which caused me to suffer an endless sickness of love from which there is no escape, have themselves dried up until they can no longer cry.

**1175. My eyes, which created a sickness of love greater than the sea,  
now suffer, unable even to sleep.**

My eyes, which caused a sickness of love too vast for the sea to contain, now waste away in suffering because they cannot sleep.
