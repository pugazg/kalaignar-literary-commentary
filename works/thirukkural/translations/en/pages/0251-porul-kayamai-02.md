**1076. The base are like an announcing drum, for the secret they hear  
they carry at once and tell to others.**

The moment they hear a secret that ought to be kept hidden, base people run and tell it to others; they may be compared to the instrument called the *thamukku*.

**1077. The base will not shake out a giving hand, except for those  
whose bent fists can break the jaw.**

Base people without the quality of generosity will give only to brutes who fold their hands into a fist and strike them on the cheek; for the poor and simple, they will not even shake out their saliva-wet hand.

**1078. The worthy are useful when told; the base, like sugarcane,  
yield benefit only when crushed.**

The requested benefit can be obtained from worthy people as soon as one's need is stated; with the base, however, one can obtain the desired benefit only after struggling, as sugarcane must be crushed and squeezed.

**1079. On seeing another person clothed and eating, the base  
become skilled at finding faults in that person.**

A base person who grows envious merely on seeing another person dress and eat will be skilled at deliberately making accusations against that person.

**1080. What are the base fit for? When hardship comes,  
they are fit to sell themselves at once.**

When trouble comes, the one qualification possessed by the base is the readiness to sell themselves to someone else in order to escape from it.
