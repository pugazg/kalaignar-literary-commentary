**826. Though enemies speak good words like friends,  
the baseness in their words will soon be known.**

Even if enemies speak sweetly like friends, the mean quality lying within those words will certainly reveal itself.

**827. Do not accept an enemy's bowing words; the bow bends  
because it is aimed at harm.**

The verbal humility shown by an enemy is like the bending of a bow: it can bring harm. Therefore it should not be trusted.

**828. A weapon lies hidden even within the clasped hands of enemies;  
so too within the tears they shed.**

Even while enemies bow in worship, a killing weapon may be hidden in their hands; likewise, even while they shed tears, a plot fills their hearts.

**829. Toward those who outwardly make much of us while inwardly despising us,  
smile in return and make that friendship wither.**

When people outwardly show a smiling friendly face while inwardly despising us with hostility, we too should follow that same method so that the friendship becomes weakened.

**830. When the time comes to be friendly with an enemy, be friendly on the face, not in the heart;  
then let even that friendship go.**

If a time comes when one must associate with an enemy, friendship should be shown only outwardly and not inwardly, and later even that outward friendship should be given up.
