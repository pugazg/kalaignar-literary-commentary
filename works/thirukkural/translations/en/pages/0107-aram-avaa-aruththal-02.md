**366. What must be feared as aram is desire,  
for it is desire that deceives a person.**

Desire is what causes one person to deceive and ruin another. Therefore one must live with the fear that one should not become a slave to desire.

**367. If desire is utterly cut away,  
the unfailing state one seeks will come of itself.**

For the state needed to live without ruin to arise as one desires, one must completely rid oneself of the quality of greed.

**368. Those without desire have no suffering; if desire exists,  
suffering will come on and on without ceasing.**

Those without desire have no suffering. If desire exists, suffering too will follow it and keep coming more and more.

**369. Unbroken joy will touch one  
if desire, the suffering within suffering, is destroyed.**

If the great suffering that greed can bring is removed, joy will continue in life without leaving.

**370. If the naturally arising, unappeased desire is cast off,  
that very state will naturally bring unfailing joy.**

The state of living after removing the naturally arising, uncontrollable greed is one that can naturally give joy that does not depart.
