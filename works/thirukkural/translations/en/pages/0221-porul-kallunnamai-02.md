**926. Those who sleep are no different from the dead;  
those who drink liquor are no different from those who consume poison.**

Since there is no difference between those who drink liquor and those who drink poison, it may also be said that there is no difference between their lying asleep and their lying dead.

**927. Those who hide and drink, their eyes drooping in intoxication,  
will always be laughed at by the people of their own town.**

Even if they drink liquor in hiding, they cannot conceal it: the people of the town will see their eyes rolling in intoxication and will mock and laugh at them.

**928. Give up saying, “I did not drink”;  
what was hidden in the heart will overflow there.**

A person cannot falsely say that he did not drink liquor at all, because when he is intoxicated he will let that truth out himself.

**929. Showing reasons to a drunkard is like  
carrying a torch to search for one submerged under water.**

Advising a person who has become addicted to drunkenness to reform is the same as lighting a torch and going in search of someone who has sunk under water.

**930. When one who is sober sees a drunkard,  
will he not think of the harm caused by what he himself has drunk?**

When a drunkard is himself not drinking and sees another drunkard staggering in intoxication, will he not at least then think about the harm it causes?
