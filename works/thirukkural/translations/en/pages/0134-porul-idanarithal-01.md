# 50. Knowing the Place

**491. Begin no action and despise none  
until the place for completing it has been found.**

One should not treat any undertaking as too small to matter; one must first choose the completely suitable place and only then enter upon the action.

**492. Even for those strong enough to meet opposition,  
the strength of a fortress joined to them brings many gains.**

Even when one has the strength to resist an approaching enemy, great benefit comes if that strength is joined with the opportunity to fight from the protection of a fort.

**493. Even the weak can become strong and conquer  
if they know the place, protect themselves, and act against the enemy.**

If they choose the place suited for attack, protect themselves, and then meet the enemy, even those without strength will gain strength and victory.

**494. The plans of those who expected victory will fail  
if their opponents know the place and press the attack there.**

If one knows the fitting place and continues the attack from there, the enemy will no longer even think of victory.

**495. The crocodile wins while it remains in deep water;  
once it leaves the water, even lesser creatures can drive it away.**

The crocodile has strength only so long as it remains in the water; once it comes out, even an ordinary creature can chase it away.
