# 23. Giving

**221. Giving is giving something to the poor;  
all other giving carries the nature of expecting a return.**

Giving to those who have nothing is the quality of giving. Giving to others is something done with the expectation of some gain in return.

**222. Even if receiving is by a good path, receiving is bad;  
even if there is no higher world, giving is good.**

Even when something is received from another by a good path, that is not greatness; it is littleness. Giving will not necessarily secure what is called the higher world; even so, living by giving to others is the better life.

**223. Giving without saying, “I have nothing,”  
is found in one of noble birth.**

Giving to others without displaying one's own suffering from poverty is a quality found in one born into a noble lineage.

**224. Being moved to pity is painful  
until one sees the smiling face of the one who came asking.**

For a person with the quality of giving, even having to pity the supplicant who comes seeking help seems painful until he sees that person's face blossom into a smile.

**225. The strength of those who endure hunger  
comes after the strength of those who remove another's hunger.**

Giving food to a hungry person is greater than observing a fast by enduring one's own hunger.
