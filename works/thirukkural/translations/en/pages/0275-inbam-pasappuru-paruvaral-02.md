# 119. Suffering from Pallor

**1186. Like darkness waiting for the lamp's light to fade,  
pallor waits for my beloved's embrace to loosen.**

Just as darkness spreads when it sees the light of a lamp begin to weaken, pallor spreads over the beloved woman's body the moment her lover's tight embrace loosens even a little.

**1187. I lay in his embrace; I moved aside only slightly—  
and in that very moment pallor swept over me.**

I lay embracing him; I moved only a little away. That was all—and pallor gathered me up completely!

**1188. People say only, “She has grown pale”;  
no one says, “He is the one who left her.”**

They reproachfully say that pallor has spread over her body, but there is no one to say that the reason for it is that her beloved has gone away.

**1189. If the beloved who persuaded me to consent to his departure is well,  
then let my body remain covered in pallor.**

If the beloved who made me agree to his leaving is safe and well, then let my body continue to shine covered in pallor!

**1190. It is good if I become known as “the pale one,”  
provided no one reproaches the beloved who persuaded me as unloving.**

If no one will reproach the beloved who made me consent to separation as one without love, then it is indeed good that I should earn the name of a woman covered in pallor!
