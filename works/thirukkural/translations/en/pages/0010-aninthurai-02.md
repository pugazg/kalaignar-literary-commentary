Though Valluvar's Muppaal is available as counsel,  
the ten commentators who wrote on this ancient work of aram  
blended into it the colours of their respective sects and religions  
and mixed in Sanskrit aligned with theistic belief;  
they therefore did not make the distinctive worth of the Kural's path fully manifest.

As a result of the renaissance of the twentieth century,  
matchless poets who wrote commentaries were many, many!  
Even if their names were set out at length, more would remain!  
Though some wrote with the eye of a researcher,  
and though some works do kindle new thought,  
some could not wipe away the ingrained attachment to antiquity,  
while attempts to wipe it away could themselves become unacceptable;  
just as the sacred image of Valluvar drawn by an artist is an imagining,  
Valluvar's thought too became the commentator's own “vision.”

Whoever may study whichever commentary,  
in the desire to discern that commentary's true import,  
by critically examining the unfailing words,  
the capacity to see truth has risen day by day!

Because the way of life laid down by the Thirukkural alone  
is the cultural fountainhead of Tiruvidam,  
so that the meaning of the sacred text may be easily understood by all,  
Kalaignar undertook to write a commentary on the Thirukkural.

Though the meaning spoken by a Kural is one,  
the capacity of those who seek clarity in its meaning differs;  
not merely by rearranging the way the Kural's phrases are joined,  
Kalaignar gave a commentary so that the Kural's path would take root in the heart.

> Translation note: the verified Tamil archival text reads `திருவிடம்`. Part 001 editorial review therefore retains **Tiruvidam** rather than silently normalizing it to a different Tamil form. The source-specific form is preserved deliberately.
