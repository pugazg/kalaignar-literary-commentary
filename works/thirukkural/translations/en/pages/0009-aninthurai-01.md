# The Professor's Foreword

Knowing clearly that every thought should aspire to what is lofty,  
examining every thought that soaks into the heart,  
Valluvar discerned that what must be cast aside is imagination born of religion,  
and that what must be embraced is the path of mutual help!

Though distinction may differ because occupations differ,  
he declared, “Birth is alike to all living beings,”  
and rejected the doctrine of Manu, a corruption of aram,  
that lays down a law for the birth-based varna order!

Even amid distinction and wealth, there is no gain greater than aram;  
there is no ruin greater than forgetting it; happiness comes through  
the noble strength of a stainless mind; and aram is to keep outside oneself  
envy, desire, anger, and hurtful speech;  
wealth free of evil is the fruit of effort;  
unblemished joy in love is the bond with a fitting companion;  
guileless inward renunciation is the fruit of restraining the five senses—  
thus Valluvar found the path to a great life without blemish!

For those who praise the Lord, deity, and God,  
what is the “worship” by which fullness of virtue is attained;  
for those who believe in rebirth, the soul, and renunciation,  
what is the “path” by which enduring fame across births is attained;  
for those who believe the birth one has received to be “karma” and predestined fate,  
that “oozh” is the result of the natural environment one encounters;  
so that those endowed with awareness may gain clarity as darkness leaves the mind—  
the Muppaal stands as a steadfast support for the life of the world's people!
