**126. If one can restrain the five, like a tortoise within a single shell,  
it will stand as protection through seven lives.**

Like a tortoise drawing its limbs within one shell, the firmness that restrains and governs the five senses becomes a protecting fortress for life through all time.

**127. Whatever else one may fail to guard, guard the tongue;  
if it is not guarded, one will suffer, caught by the fault of one's own words.**

Even if a person cannot guard anything else, the tongue at least must be restrained and guarded. Otherwise the very words that person speaks will become the cause of their suffering.

**128. If even one evil word produces a harmful result,  
the good in the whole speech will cease to be good.**

Like a drop of poison in a pot of milk, if even one word among the words spoken is an evil word and causes suffering, all the good words in that speech will become evil.

**129. Even a wound burned by fire will heal within;  
the scar burned by the tongue will never heal.**

Even a wound caused by fire will heal. But the suffering caused by words spoken in hatred will never, ever heal.

**130. For one who has learned, guards against anger, and practises self-control,  
aram waits upon the path to reach him.**

Aram itself will wait upon the path to reach the person who learns what must be learned, guards against anger, and possesses the quality called self-control.
