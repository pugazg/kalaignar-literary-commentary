# 33. Non-killing

**321. What is a deed of aram? It is not killing;  
killing brings every other evil deed.**

Not killing any living being is a deed of aram. Killing gives rise to every kind of evil deed.

**322. Sharing what is available and protecting many lives  
is foremost among all that the learned have gathered.**

Without dividing people into those who have and those who do not, sharing what is available and holding to the balanced principle that all living beings should live has no equal.

**323. First among what is good stands non-killing;  
after it, freedom from falsehood is good.**

In the order of aram, non-killing holds the first place and freedom from falsehood comes next.

**324. What is called the good path? It is the way  
that guards against killing any living being.**

The good path of aram is the path that preserves the principle that no living being should be killed.

**325. Among all who renounced in fear of worldly instability,  
foremost is one who fears killing and observes non-killing.**

Greater than all who rejected the worldly state and took up renunciation is the person who rejects killing and follows the principle of non-killing.
