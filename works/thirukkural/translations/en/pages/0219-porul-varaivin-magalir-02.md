**916. Those who guard their own worth will not touch the shoulders  
of women who parade base pleasure with pride.**

No worthy person deserving praise will lie upon the shoulders of pleasure-seeking women who are themselves deserving of contempt.

**917. Only those without steadfast hearts lie upon the shoulders  
of women who embrace while cherishing another purpose in their hearts.**

Only people with unsteady minds will trust and lie upon the shoulders of women available to all who have no love in their hearts and enter into physical relations for self-interest.

**918. For those without discerning intelligence, the embrace  
of deceitful women is called a bewitching possession.**

When a person without understanding becomes infatuated with a deceitful “public woman,” people call it a “Mohini spell.”

**919. The soft shoulders of ornamented women without bounds  
are the mire called hell into which the base sink.**

There is no difference between going behind a woman for hire because one desires her and falling into the mire called “hell.”

**920. Double-minded women, liquor, and gambling  
are the companions of those from whom prosperity has departed.**

The distinction that ought to settle into a person's life will move away from those who keep entangling themselves with double-minded women available to all, liquor, and gambling.
