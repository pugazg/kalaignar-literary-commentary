# 9. Hospitality

**81. All the care taken to sustain household life is for this:  
to welcome guests and render them help.**

Domestic life is cherished and sustained for the very purpose of welcoming guests and giving them the help they need.

**82. Even if it were a medicine against death, it is not fitting  
to eat it oneself while leaving a guest outside.**

Even if the food were a medicine that prevents death, leaving one who has come as a guest outside and eating it alone is not a desirable way of life.

**83. The life of one who welcomes and tends arriving guests every day  
will not, on that account, fall into misery and ruin.**

The life of one who welcomes guests every day with joy will not, for that reason, suffer distress and perish.

**84. The Lady called Wealth will dwell in the house  
of one who welcomes a good guest with a smiling face.**

In the home of one who welcomes guests, showing inner joy through a blossoming face, the Lady called Wealth will sit and dwell.

**85. Would he withhold even the seed meant for his field,  
who feeds guests first and eats what remains?**

Would a person of good character who first feeds guests and lives by eating what remains refrain from using even the seed meant for his field for the sake of hospitality?
