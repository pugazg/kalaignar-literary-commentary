# 111. Delight in Union

**1101. The pleasures known by seeing, hearing, tasting, breathing in fragrance and touching—  
all five senses are present in the bracelet-wearing woman.**

In this beautifully formed woman wearing bangles are gathered all the pleasures of the five senses: delight in seeing, delight in hearing, delight in touching, and delight in smelling and tasting.

**1102. Medicine for one illness is something else;  
for the illness she has caused, the adorned woman herself is the medicine.**

There are many medicines that cure illnesses; but the medicine that cures the illness of love is that beloved herself.

**1103. Is the world of the lotus-eyed one truly sweeter  
than sleeping upon the soft shoulder of the one we love?**

People speak of the world of the lotus-eyed one and such things—but what is that! Is it really as sweet as resting and sleeping upon the shoulder of a beloved filled with love?

**1104. Where did she obtain this strange fire  
that burns when she is away and turns cool when she comes near?**

Where did this woman obtain such a novel fire—one that burns when she moves away, yet becomes cool when she comes close?

**1105. Like the things one desires arriving whenever one desires them,  
so are the shoulders of the flower-adorned woman.**

If something one longs for were to come whenever it is desired and give pleasure, how would that feel? The flower-adorned woman's shoulders give pleasure in just that way.
