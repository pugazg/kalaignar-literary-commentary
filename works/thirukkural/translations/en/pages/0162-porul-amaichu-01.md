# 64. The Minister

**631. One who has mastered the proper means, the right time, the manner of acting,  
and the difficult task to be performed is a minister.**

The best minister is one who examines and understands the proper instrument, the fitting time, the way in which to act and the work that must be carried out, and then acts accordingly.

**632. Courage, protection of citizens, learning and understanding, and tireless action—  
a minister must excel in all five.**

A council of ministers should possess all five qualities: acting with courage, protecting the citizens, learning the books of aram, knowing what must be done, and making unwearied effort.

**633. A minister must be capable of separating, preserving those who stand with the country,  
and bringing back those who had separated.**

The ability proper to a minister is seen in separating those who support the enemy for the good of the country, protecting the welfare of those who support the country, and bringing back those who had gone away and later reformed.

**634. A minister must be able to discern, choose and carry out an action,  
and speak the decision with firmness.**

The special qualities of a minister are the ability to choose an undertaking, examine the means by which it can be completed and engage in it, and, whatever the final decision may be, state it firmly.

**635. One who knows the path of aram, possesses mature speech,  
and understands ability at all times is a discerning adviser.**

Only one who understands the ethical path, possesses eloquence, and has the ability to act can serve as a companion capable of giving counsel.
