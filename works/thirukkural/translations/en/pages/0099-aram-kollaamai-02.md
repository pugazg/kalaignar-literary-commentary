**326. Death that devours life will not approach the days  
of one who lives by observing non-killing.**

Marveling at the greatness of one who has made non-killing the path of aram in life, even death will hesitate to take that person's life.

**327. Even if one's own life must be lost, one must not do  
the deed that takes the life of another living being.**

Even if one's own life itself is at stake, one must not for that reason engage in an act that takes another life.

**328. Even if the gain from killing is great and beneficial,  
to the worthy, gain born of killing is base.**

Even if a killing could bring a benefit great enough to appear good, people of good character will regard that benefit itself as base.

**329. Those whose work is killing are people of base deeds  
in the hearts of those who discern baseness.**

The hearts of the worthy regard killers who act after losing rational discernment as degraded beings.

**330. Those who live a wretched life in bodies afflicted by suffering  
are said to be people who once severed life from bodies.**

The people of old said that those who toil in an evil life filled with poverty and disease are people who have already committed many killings.
