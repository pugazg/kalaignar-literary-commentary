**866. The enmity of one who grows angry without thought and is driven by excessive desire  
may safely be taken up.**

One may take up and confront the enmity of a person who becomes angry without thinking and who is driven by excessive desire.

**867. Even at a price, make an enemy of one who stays near  
and keeps doing what does not befit you.**

Even by giving something of value, one should turn into an enemy a person who stays close while continually doing things that do not befit one.

**868. If a person lacks good qualities and abounds in faults, he loses his allies  
and becomes easy for enemies to bring down.**

If a person is of bad character and filled with many faults, he will lose the support around him and be easily brought down by his enemies.

**869. When one's enemies are fearful cowards and ignorant,  
the joy called victory never leaves those who oppose them.**

If the enemies are cowards who are afraid, and ignorant cowards, then the joy called victory will remain without leaving those who oppose them.

**870. Fame will never approach those who hesitate even to oppose  
enemies untrained in the ways of battle.**

People who hesitate even to oppose enemies who have not learned the ways of warfare will be mocked with the question, “How will they face true warriors?” and fame will stay far away from them.
