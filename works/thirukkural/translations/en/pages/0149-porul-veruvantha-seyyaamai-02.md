**566. If one is harsh in speech and without compassion, great wealth  
will not endure but will perish there and then.**

The great wealth of those who use harsh words and have hearts without compassion will not remain; it will be destroyed.

**567. Harsh speech and punishment beyond measure are a file  
that wears down a ruler's fighting strength.**

Harsh words and punishment that crosses proper limits become a tool like a file, wearing away and weakening the strength of a government.

**568. The prosperity of a ruler who does not deliberate with his associates  
but gives way to anger and rage will shrink.**

A government that does not consult its allies, gives itself over to anger, and proceeds on a crooked path will collapse by itself.

**569. A ruler who has not secured protection before war arrives  
will be seized by fear and quickly perish.**

A ruler who has made no proper provision for protection beforehand will, when war comes, fear it and be brought rapidly to ruin.

**570. Tyrannical rule gathers the unlearned as its supporting force;  
there is no greater burden upon the earth.**

A tyrannical government makes the uneducated its supporting strength; there is no other burden on the earth like that.
