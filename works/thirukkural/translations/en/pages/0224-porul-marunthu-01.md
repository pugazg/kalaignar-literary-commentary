# 95. Medicine

**941. The three that medical texts reckon, beginning with vatham,  
cause disease when they increase or diminish.**

Of the three called vatham, pittham, and silethumam that medical texts have reckoned, disease will arise if even one becomes excessive or deficient.

**942. The body will need no medicine if one eats  
after allowing what was eaten before to digest.**

If people allow an interval of time for the food they have eaten to digest before eating again, their bodies will need no other medicine.

**943. Once it has digested, eat knowing the proper measure;  
that is the way for one with a body to live long.**

Knowing that the food already eaten has digested, and knowing the measure of the food to be eaten, is the way to live a long life.

**944. Know that what was eaten has digested, hold to that discipline, and when hunger is complete,  
eat food that does not conflict with the body.**

One should recognize whether what was eaten has digested and, after becoming properly hungry, eat food that agrees with the body.

**945. If one refuses excess and eats food that does not disagree with the body,  
life will suffer no harm.**

If even food that suits the body is refused when it becomes excessive, and is eaten only in proper measure, there will be no trouble to life.
