# 72. Knowing the Assembly

**711. Let those of purity who know the measure and nature of words know the assembly,  
examine it, and then speak.**

Good scholars who understand the nature of every word will also understand the nature of those gathered in an assembly, examine the situation accordingly, and speak.

**712. Let those of good understanding who know the course of words discern the occasion,  
understand it well, and speak.**

People of sound understanding who know the proper way of using words must understand the time and the situation of the assembly and then deliver their speech.

**713. Those who undertake to speak without knowing the assembly  
know neither the kinds of words nor any skill in speaking.**

People who use words without understanding the nature of the assembly do not know the kinds of words they are using, and they possess no skill in speaking either.

**714. Before the enlightened, be enlightened; before those without understanding,  
take on the colour of white lime.**

Scholars who shine before people of knowledge with a purity like that of milk should, before those without knowledge, show themselves too as if without knowledge, like white lime.

**715. Among all things called good, this is indeed good:  
the restraint not to speak ahead of the elders.**

In a gathering of people of learning, the restraint of not speaking ahead like a cashew nut is the finest of all good qualities.
