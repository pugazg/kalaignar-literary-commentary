# 91. Following a Woman's Lead

**901. Those who desire domestic pleasure do not attain worthy gain; for those who desire action,  
that too is an unwanted thing.**

If people who have set out to carry out duty-bound work come to regard the pleasures of domestic life as the greater thing, they will not attain distinguished fame.

**902. The prosperity of one who fails to preserve what must be preserved and pursues women  
will bring a great shame that makes one bow one's head.**

If one fails to preserve and protect the principle one has accepted, and instead goes after a woman and wanders behind her, one's condition will become one of shame that makes one hang one's head.

**903. The base nature of submitting to one's wife  
will always bring shame among the good.**

A husband who, instead of trying to correct a wife who lacks good character, simply submits to her will be placed in a position of standing ashamed before good people.

**904. One who fears his wife and has not gained the benefit of a new life  
will find no excellence in his capacity for action.**

The capacity for action of one who, without understanding the mind and gaining the benefit of a new life, is afraid to conduct family life will not attain excellence.

**905. One who fears his wife will also always fear  
when doing good for the good.**

One who is always anxious that no mistake should occur while doing good for good people will act with fear before a wife who watches to ensure that no mistake takes place.
