**806. Those who understand the bounds of friendship do not abandon  
the bond of old companions even when it brings them harm.**

Those who understand the nature of friendship will not abandon long-standing friends even when those friends bring harm upon them.

**807. Those whose friendship arose through love do not cease to love  
even if their old friends do deeds that bring ruin.**

Even when people with whom they have long associated act against them in ways that bring ruin, those whose friendship rests on love will not remove that love because of it.

**808. For one whose trusting friendship refuses to accept others' accusations,  
if the friend himself acts wrongly, all those days of friendship are wasted.**

A person may possess such trusting rights of friendship that he refuses to accept accusations made by others against his friend. If that very friend then behaves wrongly toward him, all the days for which he maintained that friendship become wasted days.

**809. The world cherishes those who do not abandon  
a friendship that has come down unbroken from long ago.**

The world praises those who do not give up a relationship of friendship that has continued with rights of closeness from ancient times.

**810. Even enemies desire and praise those who never depart  
from their loving character toward old friends.**

Even enemies will admire and praise those who do not withdraw their love from old friends even when those friends have committed wrongs.
