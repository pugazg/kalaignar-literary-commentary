# 43. Possession of Wisdom

**421. Wisdom is the instrument that guards against destruction  
and a fortress that enemies cannot break from within.**

The one fortress that protects a person from destruction through hostility is wisdom.

**422. Wisdom is restraining the mind from going wherever it would go,  
turning it away from evil and guiding it toward good.**

Not allowing the mind to travel along every path it chooses, casting aside evil paths, and choosing the good path—that is possession of wisdom.

**423. Whatever the matter and whoever may speak of it,  
wisdom is discerning the truth of that matter.**

Whatever the subject and whoever says whatever about it, wisdom is not believing and accepting it just as it is said, but examining and becoming clear about what the truth is.

**424. Wisdom is expressing one's meaning in a form easily understood  
and discerning the subtle meaning in the words of others.**

Wisdom is saying what we have to say in a simple way that will lodge in the hearts of listeners, while also examining and clearly understanding the subtle ideas expressed by others.

**425. Wisdom is a relationship that embraces the world  
without blooming in delight or closing up in distress.**

Since the truly elevated are the people of the world, wisdom is forming friendship with them and treating both pleasure and suffering as one and the same state.
