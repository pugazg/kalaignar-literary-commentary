# 98. Greatness

**971. For one person, the light within is zeal; for another, baseness is  
to say, “Let us live having cast that away.”**

What gives light to a person's life is enthusiasm. To live without enthusiasm is degrading.

**972. Birth is equal for all living beings; distinction differs  
only through differences in the work they perform.**

Everyone is equal by birth. Difference can be seen only in the skill they show in the work they do.

**973. Those above are not truly above if they lack worth; those below  
are not below if they do not engage in base acts.**

People without good character are not elevated people even if they hold high office; people who do not engage in degrading acts are indeed elevated even if they live in a lowly position.

**974. As with women of steadfast chastity, greatness exists  
when a person guards himself and does not stray.**

If a person protects himself and lives without departing from his proper state, he will receive the fame and greatness granted to queens of chastity.

**975. People of greatness accomplish, in the proper way,  
deeds that are difficult to accomplish.**

Those who possess the ability to complete rare deeds by following the method proper to them are people worthy of greatness.
