# 70. Conduct in the Presence of Kings

**691. Those who live in the company of contentious kings should be like people warming themselves by fire:  
neither moving too far away nor drawing too near.**

Those who associate with crowned kings will keep themselves from coming too close and also from moving too far away, just as one warms oneself by a fire.

**692. Not desiring what the king desires  
brings lasting prosperity through that king.**

If one does not begin to desire for oneself the things that the king desires, that restraint will bring lasting advancement through the king.

**693. If one wishes to guard oneself, guard against faults that are hard to remedy;  
once displeasure has arisen, clearing suspicion is difficult for anyone.**

Those who wish to protect themselves in relation to those above them must avoid committing faults that cannot be tolerated. Once such a fault has been committed, removing the suspicion that has arisen against them is not an easy matter.

**694. In the presence of powerful great people, avoid whispering into ears and joining in laughter;  
conduct yourself with restraint.**

Before powerful and eminent people, one must avoid speaking into another person's ear and laughing together with others, and must preserve the quality of self-restraint.

**695. Do not listen in on any confidential matter or pursue it;  
hear it only when they themselves choose to disclose it.**

When others are speaking privately, one must neither eavesdrop nor ask what they were speaking about. One should hear it only if they themselves tell it.
