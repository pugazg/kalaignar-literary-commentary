**426. Wisdom is to live in accord with the way  
the world itself moves and lives.**

Wisdom is conducting oneself in keeping with the way the world proceeds along the path of the elevated.

**427. The wise know what is to come; the unwise  
are those who cannot know it.**

Only people of wisdom think about what reaction will arise from a result; people without wisdom do not think about it.

**428. Not fearing what ought to be feared is foolishness;  
fearing what ought to be feared is the work of the wise.**

Only those without wisdom fail to fear what must be feared. The wise alone fear what ought to be feared.

**429. Those with the wisdom to guard beforehand  
have no suffering that comes upon them as a shock.**

For those who possess the ability to know beforehand and protect themselves, no suffering capable of causing shock will arise.

**430. The wise possess everything; those without wisdom,  
whatever else they may possess, possess nothing.**

People without wisdom have no greatness whatever else they may possess; people with wisdom have no smallness even if they possess nothing else.
