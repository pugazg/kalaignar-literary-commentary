# 63. Not Losing Heart in Adversity

**621. When adversity comes, smile at it;  
there is nothing equal to that for driving it away.**

What can resist and defeat trials is the mind that does not become shaken on seeing those trials but remains joyful.

**622. Suffering vast as a flood will vanish  
within the thought of one who possesses wisdom.**

Even if suffering comes like a flood, the moment wise people think of the way by which it can be overcome, that suffering will withdraw and run away.

**623. Those who do not themselves suffer under suffering  
will make suffering itself suffer.**

When suffering surrounds them, those who do not become dispirited will plunge that very suffering into suffering and cause it to be defeated.

**624. Adversity itself meets adversity before one who is like the bull  
that drags a heavy load through every obstructed path.**

Like an ox pulling a great burden along a rough road filled with obstacles, if one acts with unceasing effort, troubles will come to an end and victory will be gained.

**625. Even if hardships come one upon another, they themselves will suffer  
before the person who does not collapse.**

The hardships that keep pursuing a person who does not become shaken by suffering will themselves suffer and perish.
