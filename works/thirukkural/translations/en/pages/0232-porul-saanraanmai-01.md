# 99. Exemplary Character

**981. For those who know their duties and undertake exemplary character,  
all good things are duties.**

All efforts undertaken after recognizing the duties that must be performed and setting out to fulfil them in a cultured manner may be regarded as good duties.

**982. Good character alone is the beauty of the worthy;  
no other beauty, whatever it may be, is beauty.**

Good character alone is beauty for worthy people. No other beauty is beauty.

**983. Love, modesty, helpfulness, compassion, and truthfulness—  
these five are the pillars supporting exemplary character.**

Showing love, feeling shame at doing what is blameworthy, upholding the conduct of the world, acting with compassion, and holding fast to truthfulness—these five are the pillars that support exemplary character.

**984. Fasting rests on the virtue of not killing;  
character rests on not speaking of the evil done by others.**

Fasting is founded upon the virtue of not killing life. The quality called character refers to not pointing out and speaking of the evil done by others.

**985. Humility is the strength of the capable; for the worthy,  
it is the weapon that turns enemies**

Conducting oneself humbly without arrogance is the strength of the capable; for that reason, it becomes for worthy people a weapon that transforms enmity.
