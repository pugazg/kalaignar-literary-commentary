# 39. Excellence of Kingship

**381. Army, people, resources, ministers, allies and fortress—  
the ruler who possesses all six is a lion among kings.**

A powerful army, knowledgeable citizens, resources that do not run short, a ministry without deficiency, friendship that cannot be broken, and a fortress that cannot be destroyed by assault—an administration possessing all six distinctions is like a male lion among rulers.

**382. Fearlessness, generosity, knowledge and resolve—  
these four, without deficiency, are the nature of a ruler.**

Courage, compassionate thought, intellectual strength, and effort to reach a lofty goal—these four qualities are qualifications proper to government.

**383. Wakefulness, learning and courage—these three  
must never leave one who governs the land.**

Swift action without delaying the time, possession of knowledge, and courage are qualities needed by those who govern a country and must remain with them without departing.

**384. A government that does not stray from aram, removes what is wrong,  
and does not fail in valour has honour.**

Those who govern without straying from the path of aram, without committing any wrongdoing, and with valour and honour are the finest rulers.

**385. The government capable of creating revenue, gathering it, preserving it,  
and distributing what has been preserved is true government.**

Properly arranging sources of finance, increasing revenue for the government treasury, safeguarding it, and spending it according to plan—this is the mark of capable good governance.
