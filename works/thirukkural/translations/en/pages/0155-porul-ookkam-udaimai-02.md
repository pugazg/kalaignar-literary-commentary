**596. Let every thought be a thought of rising high;  
even if it is pushed aside, do not push that thought away.**

Everything one thinks should be a lofty thought. Even if it does not come to fruition, that is no reason to abandon the thought.

**597. The resolute do not shrink in ruin, like an elephant  
that stands firm though its body is pierced by arrows.**

Just as an elephant does not lose its firmness even when its whole body is pierced by arrows, people with initiative do not become distressed even when destruction itself comes upon them.

**598. Those without the urge to give from the heart  
will never attain the proud name of a generous giver in this world.**

A person who has no desire to give freely has no way to take pride in calling himself a generous benefactor.

**599. Though huge and armed with sharp tusks, the elephant trembles  
when a tiger comes upon it.**

An example that initiative is stronger than physical size is this: an elephant with a massive body and sharp tusks trembles in fear when it sees a tiger coming to attack it.

**600. Firm initiative in the mind is a person's true strength; without it,  
people differ from trees only in outward form.**

Those who lack firm initiative in their minds may appear in human form, but there is no difference between them and trees.
