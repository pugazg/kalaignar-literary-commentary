**816. A crore times better is the enmity of the wise  
than close friendship with a fool.**

Rather than maintaining intimate friendship with a person without understanding, it is a crore times better to have enmity with a person of understanding.

**817. The suffering brought by enemies is ten crore times better  
than the friendship of those who merely smile and pretend.**

Compared with the friendship of people who smile, speak, and merely act as friends, the suffering that comes from enemies is regarded as ten crore times better.

**818. Let the friendship of those who spoil an achievable task  
quietly fall away without speaking of it.**

The relationship of one who ruins a task that could have been completed should be given up little by little without letting that person know.

**819. Even in a dream, the company of those whose deeds differ from their words  
brings only pain.**

The friendship of one whose words and actions have no connection gives nothing but suffering even in a dream.

**820. Guard against even drawing near to the friendship of those who are sweet in private  
but revile you in the public assembly.**

The friendship of those who behave pleasantly when meeting one in private and then speak disparagingly in a public assembly should be kept from coming near us.
