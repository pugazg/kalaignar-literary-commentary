# 105. Poverty

**1041. If one asks what suffering is like the suffering of want,  
the suffering of want itself is the answer.**

There is no suffering that can be offered as a comparison for the suffering of poverty except the suffering of poverty itself.

**1042. The sinner called poverty comes, taking away  
both the world to come and this world.**

If the cruelty of poverty, reviled as a sinner, befalls a person, there will be no peace for that person either in the present or in the future.

**1043. The craving born of poverty destroys together  
one's ancient heritage and one's fame.**

If poverty gives rise to greed in a person, it will destroy at one stroke both the greatness inherited through one's lineage and one's fame.

**1044. Even among those born in a good house, poverty brings weariness  
that gives birth to degrading words.**

The cruelty called want can create in even those born into a good family a weariness that causes degrading words to emerge.

**1045. Within the affliction called poverty,  
many kinds of suffering gather and arise.**

From within the suffering called poverty, many different kinds of suffering will rise up.
