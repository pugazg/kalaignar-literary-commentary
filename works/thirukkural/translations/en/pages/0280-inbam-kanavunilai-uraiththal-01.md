# 122. Describing the State of Dreams

**1211. What feast can I offer in return to the dream  
that came bearing a message from my beloved?**

The dream that came to me arrived bearing the message sent by my beloved; what feast can I prepare and offer in gratitude to honour that dream?

**1212. If my kohl-lined, fish-like eyes will sleep when I plead with them,  
I shall tell the one who joins me in dreams that I am still alive.**

If my kohl-lined, fish-like eyes agree to my plea and fall asleep, then I shall tell the beloved who comes in my dream that I am still alive.

**1213. My life still endures because in dreams I see  
the one who does not come and show me love while I am awake.**

My life remains with me only because, at least in dreams, I am able to see the one who does not come to me and show affection in waking life.

**1214. Love's delight comes through dreams,  
for they seek out and bring the beloved who gives no love in waking life.**

The dream seeks out the beloved who does not come to me in person and show love, brings him to me and shows him to me; through that dream I receive the delight of love.

**1215. The joy of seeing him while awake was sweet at that moment;  
so too the dream is sweet at the very moment I see him there.**

Just as the joy of seeing the beloved in person gave sweetness then, the joy of seeing him now in a dream gives sweetness too!
