# 90. Not Offending the Great

**891. Not despising the power of those capable of accomplishing a task  
is foremost among all protections that guard a person.**

If one does not belittle the ability of people capable of completing a task, that itself becomes a protection greater than all the safeguards that can protect oneself.

**892. If one acts without respecting the great,  
the great can bring suffering that does not depart.**

If one behaves without honouring great people, one will have to undergo immense suffering that does not leave.

**893. If one wishes to be ruined, act without listening; if one wishes to be destroyed,  
show contempt toward those who possess the power to destroy.**

If a person wishes to ruin himself, he may speak disparagingly, without listening to anyone, about people who possess the power to destroy an enemy the moment they resolve to do so.

**894. For those without such strength to do harm to the powerful  
is like beckoning Death with one's own hand.**

When people who cannot endure even a small suffering clash with people who possess the strength to endure any suffering, it is as though they themselves are waving their hands and calling their own final hour toward them.

**895. Those who incur the anger of a ruler of immense strength  
cannot remain alive wherever they flee.**

People who become the object of the anger of a government possessing immense strength cannot survive wherever they escape and go.
