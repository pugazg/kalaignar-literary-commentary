**636. Before those who possess both subtle natural intelligence and knowledge from books,  
what subtle stratagem can stand?**

For those who possess natural subtle intelligence along with knowledge from books, what scheme can stand against them? None can.

**637. Even after learning the principles of action from books,  
act only after understanding the ways of the world.**

Even when one has acquired book knowledge about how to act, one must understand the practices of the world and carry out actions in a way suited to them.

**638. Even if one does not know and refuses to accept what is explained,  
it is the duty of the minister nearby to speak what is sound.**

When a person neither listens to what is said nor possesses understanding of their own, the ministers nearby must still courageously offer good counsel.

**639. Better seventy crore enemies at one's side  
than a single minister nearby who plots what is wrong.**

Having seventy crore enemies close by is far better than having one minister nearby who thinks only of wrong courses and acts upon them.

**640. Even plans properly devised remain unfinished  
when they fall into the hands of those without the ability to execute them.**

Even plans framed in the proper manner will remain stalled and incomplete if they become trapped in the hands of people who lack the capacity to carry them out.
