# 107. Dread of Begging

**1061. Even before those dear as one's own eyes, who gladly give without concealment,  
not begging is worth a crore times more.**

Even from those whose compassionate minds give without hiding what they have, remaining without begging is a crore times more exalted.

**1062. If some must live only by begging, let the one said to have made this world  
perish and wander without rest.**

If there exists a condition in which some people must live only by taking alms, then let even the one who is said to have created this world perish and wander about.

**1063. There is no cruelty harsher than the hard thought  
that the suffering of poverty can be ended by begging.**

There is no other cruelty like the cruelty of thinking that the torment of poverty can be removed by begging from others.

**1064. The quality that refuses to beg even when there is no means to live  
is so great that the whole world cannot contain its worth.**

Even in a condition where there is no way to make a living, the character that refuses to think of stretching out a hand to others is beyond the worth of this whole world.

**1065. Even if it is only thin, clear gruel, nothing is sweeter  
than eating what one's own labour has brought.**

Even if one's condition permits only gruel to drink, if that too has been earned by one's own labour and one drinks it, nothing else can be sweeter.
