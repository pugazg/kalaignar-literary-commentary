# 52. Choosing and Employing

**511. One who examines what is good and what is harmful  
and is disposed toward the good is fit to be employed.**

Those who examine and understand what is good and what is harmful, and are interested only in good action, are qualified to undertake any responsibility.

**512. Let the work be done by one who enlarges the sources of income,  
increases resources, and examines and removes obstacles.**

The person capable of widening the ways by which income can come, increasing resources, and studying and removing obstacles is the one who has the ability to carry out action.

**513. Love, knowledge, competence, and freedom from greed —  
choose the one who firmly possesses all four.**

It is best to choose the person who steadily possesses these four qualities: love, knowledge, the ability to act, and a character free from greed.

**514. However thoroughly people may be examined and chosen,  
many turn out differently when it comes to the actual work.**

However carefully methods are examined and people are chosen with clarity, many people prove different when they actually begin to act.

**515. A task should not be entrusted merely because someone is considered distinguished;  
it belongs to one who knows, is capable, and can complete it.**

A task should be given only to those who possess the ability to understand it and carry it through to completion, not to someone merely because he is considered a distinguished person.
