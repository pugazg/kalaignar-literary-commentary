# 101. Wealth Without Benefit

**1001. What can be done with great wealth amassed and kept by one  
who dies without enjoying it?**

What use is the wealth amassed by a person who, driven by unrestrained desire, piles up wealth beyond what a house can contain, keeps it stored, and dies without enjoying it?

**1002. One who clings to wealth saying, “Wealth is everything,” and gives nothing  
is, through that delusion, an ignoble birth.**

A person who gives nothing to anyone, believes that everything can be accomplished by the wealth he possesses, and clings to it without letting go is a base birth without any distinction.

**1003. People who desire only accumulation and do not desire good name  
are a burden upon the earth by their very birth.**

People whose sole aim is the accumulation of wealth, without desiring fame, become a great burden upon this earth merely by being born and living upon it.

**1004. What remainder after death can one unloved by anyone  
imagine will remain?**

What can a person whom no one loves imagine will remain behind after his death?

**1005. For those who neither give nor enjoy,  
even accumulated crores amount to nothing.**

For people who do not possess the quality of finding joy through giving help to others, even if wealth accumulates in crores upon crores, it brings no benefit.
