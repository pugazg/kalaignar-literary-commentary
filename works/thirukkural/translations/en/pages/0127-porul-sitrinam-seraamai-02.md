**456. For those pure in mind, what remains after them is good;  
for those pure in company, no action turns out badly.**

Purity of mind gives rise to fame, and purity in the company one keeps gives rise to good actions.

**457. Goodness of mind brings advancement to living beings;  
goodness of company brings every kind of fame.**

Goodness of mind becomes a source of advancement in life. Goodness in the company one keeps brings every kind of fame.

**458. Even if they possess great goodness of mind, the worthy  
have goodness of company as an added safeguard.**

Even when a person is a worthy one of great mental strength, the strength that comes to him depends also on the company with which he is associated.

**459. What follows from goodness of mind is strengthened further  
by the safeguard that comes from goodness of company.**

Even if a person is elevated and possesses a firm and good mind, the firmness of the group to which he belongs can become a powerful support to him.

**460. There is no support greater than good company,  
and nothing causes suffering more than bad company.**

There is nothing that can serve as a greater support than good company, and nothing that can bring greater suffering than bad company.
