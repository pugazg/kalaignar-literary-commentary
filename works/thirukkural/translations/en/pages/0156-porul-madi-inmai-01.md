# 61. Freedom from Laziness

**601. The unfailing lamp called family distinction will dim and perish  
when covered by the stain called laziness.**

However bright the greatness of the family into which one is born may be, once laziness takes up residence there, that brightness will fade and turn dark.

**602. Those who wish their family to remain worthy of its name  
must reject laziness and conduct themselves without it.**

If a family is to flourish, laziness must be removed and efforts must be undertaken with initiative.

**603. The family into which a foolish man is born, living lazily upon laziness,  
will perish even before he does.**

The family into which a lazy person without understanding or concern is born will be ruined before that person himself is ruined.

**604. Family standing declines and faults multiply  
for those who, overcome by laziness, make no worthy effort.**

In the lives of lazy people, faults will multiply; the greatness of their family too will fall into decay.

**605. Delay, forgetfulness, laziness and excessive sleep—these four  
are the boats eagerly boarded by one headed toward ruin.**

Putting things off, forgetfulness, laziness and sleep beyond measure—these four are boats that a person going to ruin climbs aboard by choice!
