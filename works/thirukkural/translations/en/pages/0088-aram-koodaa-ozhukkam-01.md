# 28. Improper Conduct

**271. The five elements within will laugh  
at the false conduct of one with a deceitful mind.**

At the sight of a deceiver who fools the world by appearing like a person of good conduct, the five elements within that person's body—earth, water, fire, air and space—will laugh among themselves.

**272. What use is an appearance rising as high as the sky  
if one's own heart knows the fault one commits?**

If a person knowingly does something that his own mind recognizes as wrong, there is no benefit merely in wearing the outward garb of a renunciant.

**273. The imposing appearance of one without the strength to control the mind  
is like a cow grazing while covered in a tiger's skin.**

For a person unable to control the mind to assume the garb of a renunciant is like a cow covering itself in a tiger's skin and grazing on crops.

**274. Doing what is wrong while hiding behind ascetic practice  
is like a hunter hiding in a thicket and snaring birds.**

There is no difference between a hunter hiding in a bush and trapping birds with snares, and people who wear the garb of ascetic practice while engaging in improper acts.

**275. The false conduct of those who say, “We are free of attachment,”  
will bring many sorrows of crying, “What have we done!”**

The suffering of having to grieve over what one has done will come upon those who falsely claim to have given up attachments while deceiving the world.
