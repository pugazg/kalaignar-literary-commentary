**526. If one is greatly generous and keeps anger away,  
none in the world will have kindred gathered around as he does.**

If a person is large-hearted in giving and has put away erupting anger, it may be said that no one in the world will have relatives surrounding him as closely as that person.

**527. The crow does not hide what it finds; it calls its kind and eats.  
Prosperity belongs only to those of such a nature.**

A crow does not conceal what it has obtained; it calls its own circle and eats with them. Advancement in the world belongs only to those who possess that quality.

**528. Though all people are equal, if a ruler regards each according to ability,  
many will gather around and live under that protection.**

Even though all people are equal, if each is employed according to his or her ability, everyone will stand around that government as around a fortress.

**529. Those who were once one's own and then departed  
will return when the cause of separation is no longer valid.**

People who were once relatives and left after giving some reason will come back into relationship when they realize that the reason no longer holds.

**530. When one who left after inventing a cause returns for a proper reason,  
the leader should examine him carefully and then accept him.**

A person who went away after putting forward some reason, and later returns to the leader for a fitting reason, should be carefully examined before being accepted.
