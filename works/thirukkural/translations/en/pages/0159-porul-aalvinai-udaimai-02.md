**616. Effort creates prosperous achievement; lack of effort  
brings a person into want.**

Without effort there is nothing. Effort itself becomes the cause of excellent accomplishment.

**617. “Moodevi” is said to dwell in laziness;  
“Thirumagal” dwells at the feet of the tireless.**

The words Thirumagal and Moodevi are used respectively to indicate a person who possesses initiative in effort and a lazy person who lacks initiative in effort.

**618. Lack of fortune is no blame; the blame is failing  
to act with effort after knowing what must be known.**

It is wrong to say that blame arises from the result of fate; the greater blame is failing to act after learning what one ought to know.

**619. Even if something cannot be achieved by divine agency,  
effort will give the reward earned by bodily toil.**

A task that does not happen merely by crying out, “God!” will, when a person works at it with effort, bring success in proportion to that labour.

**620. Those who strive without weariness and without lowering their effort  
will overcome even Oozh.**

They say that “Oozh” is something that cannot be defeated. Those who make an effort without growing weary will make even that Oozh suffer defeat.
