**296. There is no fame like freedom from falsehood;  
it gives every aram without fail.**

There is no other life of fame equal to living without falsehood; it is that life which gives the benefits of the path of aram without ever leaving.

**297. If one can hold firmly to not lying,  
it is good even without doing other deeds of aram.**

Steadfastly observing freedom from falsehood yields the good of the path of aram more surely than merely refraining from deeds that should not be done.

**298. Outward purity comes through water;  
inward purity is seen through truthfulness.**

Bathing in water removes only the dirt of the body; for the mind to remain unstained and pure, there must be truthfulness in word and deed.

**299. Not every lamp is a lamp for the worthy;  
the lamp of freedom from falsehood alone is the lamp.**

More than lamps that dispel the darkness outside, the lamp called freedom from falsehood, which dispels the darkness within, is the radiant lamp that reveals a person as worthy.

**300. Among all that we have examined and known as true,  
there is nothing better than truthfulness.**

The truth arrived at through inquiry and understanding is that there is no quality better than truthfulness.
