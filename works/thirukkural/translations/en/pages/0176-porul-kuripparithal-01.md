# 71. Understanding Signs

**701. One who, by looking, understands the unspoken sign  
is at all times an ornament to the world girdled by unfailing waters.**

A person who can understand from facial signs what another is thinking even before anything is spoken becomes an ornament to the world itself.

**702. One who understands without doubt what lies within another's mind  
may be held equal to a god.**

If it is said that only a god possesses the power to understand clearly what is in a person's mind, then a human being who possesses that ability may also be compared with that god.

**703. One who understands the inner sign from an outward sign  
should be secured as an aide, whatever responsibility must be given.**

A person capable of knowing another's inner intention merely from that person's facial sign should be made an aide by entrusting that person with whatever responsibility is required.

**704. Those who understand what is intended without its being spoken and those who do not  
may look alike in bodily form, yet they are different.**

Although they may appear no different from one another in bodily features, the person who can understand what is in another's mind without being told and the person who cannot are different from one another in knowledge.

**705. If the eyes cannot understand the sign from a sign,  
what use are those eyes among the organs?**

If a person's facial expression reveals what is within the mind, what use is there in possessing eyes that cannot understand that sign?
