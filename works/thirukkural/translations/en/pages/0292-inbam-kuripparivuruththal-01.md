# 128. Communicating by Signs

**1271. Though you try to hide it and hold it back, there is something  
your kohl-lined eyes cannot help but tell.**

Even if you try not to say it openly and conceal it, there is one message the eyes cross every barrier to reveal: a love that does not wish for separation.

**1272. In the maiden of full, beautiful eyes and bamboo-like shoulders,  
the fullness of womanly grace is her greatest beauty.**

For my beloved, with beauty filling her eyes and shoulders like bamboo, the richness of her womanly qualities is itself her greatest beauty.

**1273. Like the thread that gleams within a string of gems,  
there is something within this maiden's beauty that shines as a sign.**

Like the thread hidden inside a necklace of gems, within this maiden's beauty there is a sign that enchants me.

**1274. As fragrance lies within an unopened bud,  
there is something within the bud of this maiden's smile.**

Just as fragrance is contained within a flower bud that has not yet opened, the thought of her love is filled within the bud that is a woman's smile.

**1275. The secret glance of my beauty with close-set, colourful bangles  
contains a medicine that can cure my piercing sorrow.**

In the mischievous glance of my beautiful beloved, who wears richly coloured bangles, there is also a medicine capable of curing the suffering that pierces me.
