# 100. Good Character

**991. It is said that the way to attain the quality called good character  
is to be easy of approach with everyone.**

Whoever people may be, if one moves among them with simplicity, that itself becomes the easy way to attain the excellent conduct called good character.

**992. Possessing love and bearing the marks of worthy birth—  
these two are called the way of good character.**

Being a person who possesses love, and being worthy of the characteristics of one born into a high lineage, together form the excellent path called good character.

**993. Resemblance in bodily members is not resemblance among people;  
true resemblance lies in matching one another in worthy character.**

It is not right to compare people who lack good character merely by matching their bodily parts and then speak of them as belonging among humankind; those who resemble one another in good qualities are the ones called people.

**994. The world praises the good character of those  
who do useful good without swerving from justice.**

The world will praise the good character of people who, without departing from the path of justice, do good and work in ways useful to others.

**995. Even in jest, ridicule causes pain; those who know the proper way  
preserve character even toward enemies.**

Even speaking in ridicule as a joke can cause harm. People of mature understanding will conduct themselves without losing good character even toward enemies.
