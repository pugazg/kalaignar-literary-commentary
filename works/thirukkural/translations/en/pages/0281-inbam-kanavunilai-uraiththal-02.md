# 122. Describing the State of Dreams

**1216. If there were no such thing as waking,  
the beloved met in dreams would never leave.**

If waking alone did not suddenly come and spoil everything, the beloved met in dreams could remain without ever parting from me.

**1217. Why does the cruel one who gives me no love while I am awake  
come in dreams only to deepen my suffering?**

Why does the hard-hearted one who does not come in person and show me affection come in dreams and make the suffering of separation still greater?

**1218. While I sleep he rests upon my shoulder; when I awake,  
he swiftly takes his place within my heart.**

The one who came in my dream during sleep, leaned upon my shoulder and gave me delight has not gone anywhere when I awake; he leaps into my heart and settles there.

**1219. Only those who do not see their beloved in dreams  
will grieve that he gives them no love while awake.**

Only those who do not see their beloved in dreams will grieve, saying that he has not come to see them in waking life.

**1220. The townspeople say my beloved has left me;  
do they never see their own absent beloved in dreams?**

They accuse my beloved of being separated from me; do these townspeople never see in dreams the beloved who has gone away from them?
