# 53. Embracing Kindred

**521. Even when one's possessions are gone,  
it is one's kindred who still honour the old relationship.**

Those who continue to value an old relationship even when poverty comes upon a person are the ones who are truly his kindred.

**522. If one gains kindred whose affection never diminishes,  
it will bring unfailing prosperity and growth.**

If a person has relatives whose love does not lessen under any circumstance, that relationship can bring him advancement and growth.

**523. The life of one who does not mingle affectionately with those around him  
is like water filling a tank without banks.**

The life of one who does not joyfully mingle in affection with those around him as kinsmen and relatives becomes useless like water collected in a tank without banks.

**524. To live so that one's kindred lovingly gather around  
is the true benefit obtained from wealth.**

The benefit a person gains from the wealth he has acquired is a life in which his own people stand around him in affection.

**525. One who can give and speak with sweetness  
will be surrounded, layer upon layer, by kindred.**

A person possessed of generosity and words filled with affection will continually be surrounded by relatives drawing near to him.
