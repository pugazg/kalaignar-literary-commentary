# 83. False Friendship

**821. The friendship of those whose hearts are not with us, though they appear as friends,  
is like an anvil waiting for the right moment to strike.**

The friendship of one who is not sincere at heart but outwardly behaves like a friend is like the anvil block that waits for the proper opportunity to support a blow that breaks iron apart.

**822. The friendship of those who seem like kin but are not  
changes like the mind of women for hire.**

The friendship of those who are not truly close to us but act as though they are is like the mind of women for hire who, without the good qualities expected of women, act as if they possess them: one thing within and another without.

**823. Though they have learned many rare and good books,  
it is hard for those filled with enmity to become good at heart.**

Even after learning many rare books, it is difficult for those who carry a hostile mind to reform their minds and conduct themselves well.

**824. Fear the deceiver who smiles sweetly in the face  
while inwardly intending harm.**

One should fear and keep away from the friendship of deceivers who smile and speak pleasantly while thinking inwardly of ruining us.

**825. No clear decision can be made from the words  
of those whose mind and action do not agree.**

No definite conclusion can be reached by trusting the words of people whose mind is one thing and their action another.
