# 109. The Torment of Beauty

**1081. Is she a goddess? A splendid peacock? A woman with heavy earrings?  
My heart is bewildered.**

What is this beauty that torments me? Is it a colourful peacock? My heart is bewildered at the sight of this maiden.

**1082. She looked; when I looked back, her answering look  
was like a goddess attacking with an army.**

Against the spear of the glance she cast, I looked at her; at once she looked back at me. It was as though she felt that attacking me by herself was not enough and came with an army to attack me.

**1083. I did not know before what Death was; now I know:  
it has a woman's form and great warring eyes.**

I had never before known the cruel Yama called the taker of life; now I understand. The truth is that this Yama comes in the form of a woman, armed with arrow-like eyes capable of waging war.

**1084. Though she is the very mould of womanly beauty,  
this maiden's eyes alone appear to consume the life of those who see them.**

This maiden shines as the very mould of womanhood, yet her eyes alone seem as though they will take life away! What a contradiction!

**1085. Is it Death? An eye? A startled doe?  
The young woman's look contains all three.**

Is it Death that takes life? Is it an affectionate eye? Is it a startled doe? The young woman's gaze raises all three questions.
