# 117. Languishing in Longing

**1166. The joy of love is a sea; when that love is checked,  
the sorrow is greater than the sea.**

The joy of love is like the sea. But the suffering caused by separation from the beloved is greater than the sea.

**1167. I swim the fierce flood of love and see no shore;  
even at midnight, I am alone.**

Even at midnight I am alone without my companion; so I struggle, swimming in the fierce flood of love's delight and unable to see its shore.

**1168. O night, after putting every living being to sleep,  
you have no companion now but me.**

“O night! You have put every living being in the world to sleep; poor thing, now you too have no companion other than me.”

**1169. These nights that pass stretching endlessly  
are crueller than the cruelty of the cruel beloved.**

The cruelty of these nights seeming to grow longer and longer is greater than the cruelty brought by separation from the beloved.

**1170. If my eyes could travel inward as my heart does to where my beloved is,  
they would not have to swim through this flood of tears.**

If they could go to the place where my beloved is, as my heart does, my dark pupils would not have been forced to swim through a flood of tears merely to see him.
