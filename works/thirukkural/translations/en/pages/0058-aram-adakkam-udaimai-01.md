# 13. Self-Control

**121. Self-control raises one among the deathless;  
lack of self-control drives one into deep darkness.**

Self-control gives imperishable fame. Lack of self-control makes life itself dark.

**122. Guard self-control as a treasure;  
for life there is no gain greater than it.**

What must be guarded with the greatest firmness is self-control. There is nothing else that can bring greater advancement than self-control.

**123. If one understands what must be understood and restrains oneself accordingly,  
recognition of that character will bring distinction.**

Praise will gather around a person whose character is recognized and appreciated because they know what must be known and conduct themselves with self-control accordingly.

**124. The stature of one who remains restrained without departing from his state  
is greater in worth than a mountain.**

The elevation of one who has a firm heart together with an unostentatious sense of self-control will be praised as greater than a mountain.

**125. Humility is good for everyone;  
among the wealthy, it is itself another wealth.**

The quality called humility brings good to everyone. For those who are already wealthy, that quality becomes an additional wealth.
