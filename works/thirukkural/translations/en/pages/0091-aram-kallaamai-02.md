**286. Those who cannot live within measure  
are those deeply attached to stealing.**

Those who cannot shape their lives properly within a limit will become people drawn to stealing and taking the property of others.

**287. The dark cunning called stealing is not found  
in those who possess the strength of living within measure.**

Those who have the strength to conduct life with a sense of measure do not possess the cunning mind called stealing.

**288. As aram stands in the hearts of those who know measure,  
deceit stands in the hearts of those who know theft.**

The hearts of honest people travel on the path of aram; the hearts of those who rob travel instead on the crooked path of deceit.

**289. Those who know nothing other than theft  
will fall through deeds that cross all bounds.**

Those who seek no good path other than stealing will lose their lives and fall through actions that go beyond all limits.

**290. For those who steal, even life may fail;  
for those who do not steal, the higher world will not fail.**

For those who steal, even living one's life may slip away; for those who do not even think of stealing, life in the world of fame will never fail them.
