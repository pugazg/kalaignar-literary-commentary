**336. This world has the greatness of saying:  
“One who was here yesterday is not here today.”**

This world takes pride in the arrogance of saying that it has made one who was alive yesterday no longer exist today.

**337. Those who do not know how to live even for a moment  
think not merely a crore of thoughts, but many more.**

It is those who do not spend even a moment thinking about and understanding the truth of life who build castles in the mind with desires beyond measure.

**338. The bond between body and life is like  
a bird flying away and leaving the shell behind.**

The relationship between body and life is like the relationship between an egg and the young bird that emerges from it.

**339. Death is like sleep; birth  
is like waking from sleep.**

In this impermanent life, birth is like waking after sleep; death is the irreversible sleep from which one cannot wake again.

**340. Has life, lodging within the body as in a temporary shelter,  
no settled refuge of its own?**

The life that stays with the body has no other refuge once it is separated from that body.
