# 30. Truthfulness

**291. What is called truthfulness? It is speaking words  
that cause no harm whatever.**

Truthfulness means speaking a word that causes not even the slightest harm to another person.

**292. Even a false word may take the place of truth  
if it yields faultless good.**

If it can bring about a good that is free of fault, even a false statement may earn a place where it can be called truth.

**293. Do not lie against what your own heart knows;  
after lying, your own heart will burn you.**

One must not lie against one's conscience; if one does, the speaker's own mind will punish that person.

**294. If one lives without falsehood even in the heart,  
one will live in the hearts of all people.**

Those who live without even thinking falsehood in their minds will gain an enduring place in the hearts of people.

**295. Those who speak truth with the heart  
stand above those who practise asceticism and charity.**

Those who speak truth sincerely from the heart, and not merely with their lips, are greater than those who practise asceticism and give in charity.
