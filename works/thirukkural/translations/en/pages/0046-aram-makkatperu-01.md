# 7. The Blessing of Children

**61. Among all gains, we know of none greater  
than gaining children endowed with understanding.**

In domestic life there is no blessing greater than good children who excel in understanding.

**62. If one gains children of character untouched by blame,  
evil will not touch them for seven births.**

If the children one has brought forth are people of blameless character, no evil will touch them through the whole span expressed as seven times seven generations.

**63. One's children are said to be one's wealth;  
their wealth will come from their own deeds.**

One's wealth is one's own children. The wealth of those children will come as the result of each one's actions.

**64. Sweeter even than nectar is the food  
stirred by the little hands of one's children.**

Even if the finest substance is called nectar, the food stirred by the tender little hands of one's own children becomes tastier than that nectar.

**65. The touch of one's children brings delight to the body;  
hearing their words brings delight to the ear.**

Embracing one's children brings delight to the body, and hearing those children's prattling speech brings delight to the ear.
