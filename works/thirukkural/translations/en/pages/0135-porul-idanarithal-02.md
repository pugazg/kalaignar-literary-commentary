**496. A strong-wheeled chariot does not move upon the sea,  
and a ship that sails the sea does not move upon the land.**

One who chooses the place suited to an action must at least know that ‘a chariot does not run upon the sea’ and ‘a ship does not go upon the land.’

**497. If one thinks through every detail and acts in the proper place,  
no support is needed except fearlessness.**

When the means appropriate to an action have been thought through without deficiency and the act is done in the right place, no support other than courage is needed.

**498. If a small army reaches the place suited to it,  
the confidence of a great army will be destroyed.**

Even a small army, if it fights from the place that suits it, can defeat a much larger army.

**499. Even if an enemy lacks fortification and other distinctions,  
it is difficult to attack him in the land where he lives.**

Even if enemies possess neither a protective fort nor many other military advantages, it is not easy to march into the stable ground where they live and attack them there.

**500. The elephant that fears no spear-bearing warrior  
can be killed even by foxes if its feet sink into mire.**

An elephant powerful enough to bring down spear-bearing warriors can, if trapped in mud, be killed even by foxes.
