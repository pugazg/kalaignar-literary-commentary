# 118. Lamenting the Eyes

**1176. Ah, how sweet! The eyes that gave me this sickness  
have themselves fallen into the same suffering.**

Oh! The eyes that caused my sickness of love are wasting away and suffering just as I am. This gives me joy!

**1177. Let the inner moisture of those eyes dry up through suffering—  
those eyes that once yearned and yearned to see him.**

O eyes that once softened and melted in desire as you looked upon him! Today, thinking of him who has gone away, suffer without sleep until even the last drop of tears is gone.

**1178. There is one who loves me without any thought of cherishing me;  
yet my eyes find no peace without seeing him.**

There is someone who loves me without any intention of embracing and caring for me; yet my eyes have no peace when they do not see him!

**1179. When he does not come, they do not sleep; when he comes, they do not sleep—  
between the two, what suffering these eyes endure!**

They do not sleep because he has not yet come; even after he comes, they still do not sleep. Surely it is the eyes of lovers that must endure suffering like this.

**1180. It is not hard for the townspeople to learn our secret,  
when eyes like ours proclaim it like a beaten drum.**

When our very eyes are there to beat the drum and proclaim the anguish of love, it is not difficult for the townspeople to discover what we try to conceal.
