**786. Friendship that smiles only on the face is not friendship;  
friendship that smiles within the heart is friendship.**

Merely showing a pleasant face is not a sign of friendship; loving wholeheartedly is true friendship.

**787. Friendship removes what leads to ruin, guides one onto the right path,  
and shares the suffering when harm comes.**

True friendship prevents a friend from going down an evil path and being ruined, makes him walk on the right path, and shares the pain of the harm when trouble comes to him.

**788. Like the hand that acts at once when clothing slips from the body,  
friendship hastens to remove distress.**

Just as the hands act immediately to set right clothing when it slips from the body, the mark of friendship is to hurry to remove the suffering that comes to a friend.

**789. What is friendship's seat of honour? Without change of mind,  
it is the state of standing firm in every way one can.**

The excellence of friendship lies in standing by a friend and supporting him in every way one can, without allowing differences of mind to arise.

**790. Though friends say, “He is thus to us; we are thus to him,”  
friendship becomes mean when it is dressed up in such praise.**

Even if friends artificially praise one another by saying, “He is of this nature to us; we are of this nature to him,” the greatness of that friendship will diminish.
