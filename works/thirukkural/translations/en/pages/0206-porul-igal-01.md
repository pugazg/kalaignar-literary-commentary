# 86. Discord

**851. Discord is the disease that spreads among all living beings  
the evil quality called separation.**

The hostile feeling that arises from differences of mind is an evil quality that prevents people from living together in unity.

**852. Even if another acts hatefully because of difference,  
it is best not to cause him pain out of discord.**

Even if a person engages in hateful acts because of difference, it is the better quality not to do anything that causes him suffering merely because of one's disagreement with him.

**853. If one removes the painful disease called discord, it gives  
a stainless and enduring radiance of fame.**

Those who remove from their minds the disease called discord will gain an enduring fame without stain.

**854. If the greatest pain among pains—the pain called discord—is destroyed,  
it yields the greatest joy among joys.**

Hostile feeling born of discord is the greatest suffering among sufferings. If a person removes that feeling, it becomes the greatest joy among joys.

**855. Who can overcome those who know how to turn away  
the thought of discord when it rises in the mind?**

No one can defeat people who have the strength to deny a place in their minds to thoughts of difference when those thoughts arise.
