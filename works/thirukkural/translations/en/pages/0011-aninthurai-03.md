Clear in thought, accomplished in learning,  
possessed of a mind endowed with subtle, penetrating scholarship,  
deep in conception, unwavering in resolve,  
master of victorious speech, never weary,  
one who made the convictions he held his very doctrine,  
firm enough to attain what he resolved exactly as resolved,  
a force of rational thought, one who never forgets refinement,  
who in surging style gave the essence of *Sangathamizh*,  
who fashioned *Kuraloviyam* like a crystal maiden,  
Kalaignar, master of many modes of lucid Tamil,  
who resolved, “Even if it is we who fall,  
let it be Tamil that lives,”  
and held that Tamil was his very breath of life  
and the life of Tamils was his own life,  
the noble one who accepted as the debt of his birth  
the service of guarding the welfare of his people and the welfare of the language!

Of Periyar, the Father of Self-Respect—  
**the son who guards his spirit!**  
Of Anna, the standard-bearer of the dignity of our people—  
**the younger brother sought by his heart!**  
To those who strive to wipe away the degradation of our people—  
**a companion!**  
In the work of the ideal, Kalaignar—  
**a friend who is my pride!**
