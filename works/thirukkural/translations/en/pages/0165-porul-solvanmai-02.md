**646. Speak in a way that makes others wish to listen,  
and draw out the benefit from what others say; this is the way of the faultless wise.**

It is the conduct of the wise to express ideas so that others willingly listen and understand, and also to examine what others say and accept the benefit contained in their words.

**647. One who is skilled in speech, tireless and unafraid  
is difficult for anyone to defeat in opposition.**

No one can easily defeat a person who possesses eloquence, does not know weariness, and has a fearless heart.

**648. The world will quickly undertake the work shown by those  
who can arrange their ideas and express them pleasantly.**

People of the world will immediately come forward to carry out the work pointed out by those who possess the ability to classify their ideas and express them attractively.

**649. Those who cannot make matters clear in a few flawless words  
are the ones who desire to speak many words.**

It is those who cannot give a clear explanation through a few faultless words who keep repeating many words again and again.

**650. Those who have learned but cannot explain so others may understand  
are like a cluster of flowers in bloom that gives no fragrance.**

A person who cannot explain what has been learned in a way that allows others to understand is like a flower that blooms in a cluster but gives no fragrance.
