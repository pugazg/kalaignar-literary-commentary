# 104. Agriculture

**1031. Though the world turns through many pursuits, it follows the plough;  
therefore, despite the toil, agriculture is foremost.**

This world, moving round through many occupations, must ultimately revolve behind the work of the plough. Therefore, however much hardship it involves, agriculture is the finest occupation.

**1032. Farmers are the axle-pin of the world,  
supporting all those who rise without being able to farm.**

Because agriculture is the occupation that relieves the hunger of people engaged in many different kinds of work, it is called the axle-pin that bears the people of the world.

**1033. They alone truly live who plough and eat; all others  
live by bowing to them and following behind.**

Those who live by ploughing and eating are the ones who live the higher life; for all others must depend upon them, bow to them and eat.

**1034. Those beneath the shade of grain-bearing stalks will see  
the shade of many royal umbrellas beneath their own.**

Farmers possess the strength to bring the shelter of many governments beneath the shade of their own umbrella.

**1035. Those whose hands work for their food will not beg;  
they give without hiding anything to those who beg of them.**

Those whose nature is to work themselves, earn wages and eat will not go to others and stretch out their hands. Nor will they hide what they have from those who stand before them asking; they will give.
