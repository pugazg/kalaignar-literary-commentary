# 32. Not Causing Pain

**311. Even if great prosperity may be gained,  
the stainless hold to not causing pain to others.**

Even if it were possible to obtain wealth that brings great prosperity, the principle of the stainless is not to cause harm to others for its sake.

**312. Even toward one who angrily causes pain,  
the stainless hold to not returning pain.**

When someone causes suffering through angry words or actions, the principle of the finest people is to bear that suffering without inflicting it back upon that person.

**313. If one causes pain even to those who hate one without cause,  
it will bring suffering from which there is no escape.**

If enemies cause harm to a person who has harmed no one, the harm that comes back upon those enemies in return can bring them suffering from which there is no recovery.

**314. The proper punishment for those who cause pain  
is to do them good so that they bow their heads in shame.**

The right way to punish a person who has harmed us is to do good to that person in such a way that he becomes ashamed and bows his head.

**315. What use is intelligence if one does not regard another being's pain  
as one's own pain and move to protect it?**

For those who do not regard the suffering that comes to other living beings as their own suffering and come forward to protect them, what use is intelligence even if they possess it?
