**906. Even if they live as though divine, they have no distinction—those who fear  
the bamboo-like shoulders of their wife.**

Those who submit to a wife who lacks knowledge and culture merely because she is beautiful have no true greatness, even if they imaginatively present themselves as people endowed with a divine nature.

**907. Greater is the womanhood of a woman with a sense of shame  
than the manliness of a man who lives at a woman's bidding.**

The womanhood of a woman who possesses a sense of honour is more worthy of greatness than the manliness of a man who lies wrapped around a woman's feet.

**908. Those who conduct themselves as a beautiful-browed woman desires  
will not settle the troubles of friends or perform good deeds.**

Those who, captivated by a woman's beauty, become infatuated with her and lose their reason will neither care about their friends nor carry out good works.

**909. Virtuous action, worthy understanding, and other deeds  
are absent in those who live at a woman's command.**

Ethical deeds or excellent intelligence cannot be expected from woman-mad men who submit themselves to the commands given by arrogant women.

**910. For those whose minds possess the strength of thought and firmness of heart,  
there is never the folly of circling around women.**

People who possess the capacity to think and firmness of heart will not become blinded by lust and keep circling around women.
