**706. As a crystal shows what stands beside it,  
the face reveals what is held in the heart.**

Just as a mirror shows what stands before it, a person's face reveals what is within that person's mind.

**707. Is there anything more knowing than the face? Whether there is delight or hurt,  
it comes forward and reveals it first.**

There is nothing as knowing as the face in coming forward and revealing the likes and dislikes held within the mind.

**708. It is enough to stand looking at the face,  
if one has the ability to look within and understand what is there.**

If one has the ability to understand what lies within another person, it is enough simply to stand facing that person's face.

**709. The eyes will tell both enmity and friendship,  
if there are those who can understand the different kinds of looks.**

Those who are able to understand differences in a person's gaze can tell merely by looking at the eyes whether what lies in that person's mind is friendship or enmity.

**710. For those called subtle in understanding, the measuring rod, when examined,  
is none other than the eye.**

For those described as people of subtle understanding, there is no instrument other than the eye for measuring and knowing what is within another person's mind.
