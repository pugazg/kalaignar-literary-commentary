In keeping with Tolkappiyar's statement that the range runs from the letter **அ** at the beginning to **ன** at the end, it is delightful to recognize that, just as the Thirukkural begins with **அ** and ends with **ன**, Kalaignar's commentary too begins with **அ** and ends with **ன**.

Instead of proceeding through methods such as traditional meaning, word meaning, explanatory meaning, and concise meaning, he seems to have made it his method to separate out precisely what a Tamil reader of today needs to understand on reading a Kural and to show it with clarity. There is not the slightest difference of opinion that the commentaries of worthy scholars such as Parimelazhagar and Thiru. Vi. Ka. have produced benefit in ways suited to them, can continue to produce benefit, and deserve to be preserved and honoured by us always. In the same way, there is no room for disagreement about the need for Kalaignar's commentary. The Revolutionary Poet praised Father Periyar with the expression `மக்கள் நெஞ்சின் மலிவுப் பதிப்பு`, conveying the image of an edition readily accessible to the people's hearts. I wish to apply that expression here as well. *Thirukkural — Kalaignar's Commentary* too appears as such an accessible edition for the people's hearts. I take great pride, again and again, in having received even a tiny share in this service of bringing out this special edition. I express my gratitude to Kalaignar for giving me this rare opportunity.

I lovingly request the Tamil people not only to buy and read this book and impress it upon their minds, but also, as far as possible, to follow it wherever necessary. That alone is the benefit Kalaignar expects from having written these thoughts in the dailies *Tamizhan* and *Murasoli* and from now collecting and publishing them as this book. My request is that Tamil Nadu render that gratitude.
