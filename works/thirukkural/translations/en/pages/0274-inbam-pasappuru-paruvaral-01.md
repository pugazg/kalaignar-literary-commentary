# 119. Suffering from Pallor

**1181. I consented to separation from the one I love; now that I have grown pale,  
to whom can I tell what has become of me?**

I gave my beloved consent to leave me; but now, as pallor spreads over my body through the suffering of separation, to whom shall I go and speak of it?

**1182. Proudly saying, “He gave me this,”  
the pallor he caused creeps and spreads over my body.**

As though swelling with pride that it was caused by the beloved through separation, the colour of pallor climbs upon my body, creeps over it and spreads!

**1183. He took away my beauty and modesty,  
giving me sickness and pallor in return.**

Giving me the sickness of love and the colour of pallor in exchange, he took my beauty and modesty with him and went away.

**1184. I think only of him and speak only of his worthy nature;  
then by what deceit, or from where else, has this pallor come?**

When everything I think and say concerns only his upright qualities, how has this pallor come upon me—without my knowing it, or by some other way?

**1185. Look there—my beloved has barely gone away; look here—  
pallor is already spreading over my body.**

My beloved has not gone even a little distance away from me; yet within that brief moment the colour of pallor has already spread across my body.
