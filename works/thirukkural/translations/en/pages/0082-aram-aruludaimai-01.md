# 25. Possession of Compassion

**241. The wealth of compassion is the wealth among all wealth;  
material wealth is found even among the base.**

Even among people of cruel heart and low character, wealth worth crores may be accumulated; yet that wealth cannot equal the wealth of compassion.

**242. Seek by the good path and live by compassion;  
however many ways one examines, that alone is support.**

However many ways one examines, possession of compassion should be taken as the good path that stands as support for life.

**243. Those whose hearts are joined with compassion  
do not enter the painful world joined with darkness.**

Those whose minds are filled with compassion will not toil in a world of suffering surrounded by the darkness called ignorance.

**244. Those who protect all living beings and live by compassion  
have no deed that makes them fear for their own life.**

The worthy who regard it as their duty to protect all living beings with compassion do not become anxious about their own lives.

**245. Those who live by compassion have no suffering;  
the mighty world moved by the wind stands witness.**

That one can perform one's duty without feeling suffering through the movement of compassion that wells up within is witnessed by this great world, which stands in strength through the movement of the wind.
