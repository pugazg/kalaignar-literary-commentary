# 133. Joy of Lovers' Quarrel

**1321. Even when the beloved has done no wrong,  
sulking has the power to increase the love he gives.**

Even when there is no fault at all, the lovers' quarrel that arises between two lovers can make their love grow still greater.

**1322. Though good love may wither a little through a lovers' quarrel,  
the small suffering it brings has a greatness of its own.**

Though sulking may cause the good love blossoming between lovers to fade a little, the small suffering produced by it still possesses its own greatness.

**1323. For lovers joined in affection like water mingled with earth,  
is there any new world greater than lovers' quarrelling?**

For lovers united in love like water mingling with earth, can there be any other new world greater than the delight of a lovers' quarrel?

**1324. Within the quarrel that makes us cling together without letting go  
there arises a weapon that breaks the firmness of my heart.**

A lovers' quarrel becomes the very reason for embracing tightly and remaining inseparable; and within that quarrel itself lies the weapon that shatters the firmness of my heart.

**1325. Even if he has done no wrong, there is a certain delight  
in being parted from the soft shoulders of the woman he loves.**

Even when he has committed no fault, there is a delight in being subjected to the sulking of the woman who has captured his heart and remaining apart from her soft shoulders.
