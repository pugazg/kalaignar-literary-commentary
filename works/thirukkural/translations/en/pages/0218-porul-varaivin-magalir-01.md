# 92. Women Beyond Bounds

**911. The sweet words of bangled women who desire not love but wealth  
bring suffering.**

Women available to all who have no love and make the accumulation of wealth alone their goal speak sweetly; those who trust those words and are deceived will meet suffering in the end.

**912. Weighing the gain, they speak sugar-sweet words to suit it;  
weigh their intent and do not join with such women.**

The relationship of women available to all who calculate the profit and speak sugar-sweet words accordingly should never be trusted in a way that leaves one deceived.

**913. The false embrace of women who seek wealth  
is like embracing an unknown corpse in a dark room.**

Women for hire embrace a person only for money and act out false love; it is like lying in a dark room embracing the corpse of a stranger.

**914. Those of understanding who examine the wealth of grace  
will not touch the base pleasure of women who desire material wealth alone.**

People of understanding who seek and examine grace will regard the pleasure offered by women for hire who desire only wealth as base.

**915. Those whose mature intelligence rests on sound understanding  
will not immerse themselves in the base pleasure of women available to all.**

People who possess natural intelligence as well as the understanding gained through learning will not immerse themselves in the pleasure offered by women available to all.
