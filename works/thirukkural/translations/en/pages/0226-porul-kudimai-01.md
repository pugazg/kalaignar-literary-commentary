# 96. Nobility

**951. Rectitude and modesty are naturally found together  
only among those of worthy lineage.**

Only people who possess the quality of not departing from impartiality together with an unostentatious sense of restraint can be regarded as people born into a high lineage.

**952. Those of worthy birth do not stray from these three:  
conduct, truthfulness, and honour.**

Those who do not depart from right conduct in these three—conduct, truthfulness, and honour—will be regarded as people born into a high lineage.

**953. A smiling face, generosity, pleasant speech, and not despising others—  
these four are said to mark truthful citizens.**

Only those who possess the four distinctions of a smiling face, generosity, pleasant speech, and the cultivated quality of not despising others can be classified as truthful citizens.

**954. Even if offered crores piled upon crores, those of worthy birth  
will not do deeds that diminish their standing.**

Even if many crores of wealth are piled up and offered to them, people born into a distinguished lineage will not make room for actions that destroy that distinction.

**955. Even when their means of giving have fallen away, those of an ancient lineage  
do not depart from their habit of generosity.**

People born into a lineage of ancient distinction will not lose the quality of giving to others even when poverty strikes them.
