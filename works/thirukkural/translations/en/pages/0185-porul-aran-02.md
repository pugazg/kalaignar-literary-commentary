**746. A fortress possesses every required resource  
and good, capable people who can help where needed.**

A fortress is one that contains all the resources required for war and possesses strong warriors capable of entering the field of battle.

**747. A fortress is hard to seize by siege, by attack without siege,  
or by treacherous stratagem.**

A fortress is one whose strength makes it impossible for an enemy to capture it whether through siege, without siege, or through a deceitful stratagem.

**748. A fortress enables those who hold it to withstand the powerful force laying siege  
and defeat those who have surrounded it.**

A fortress must be arranged so that those inside can fight from within and defeat a powerful army that has laid siege to it.

**749. A fortress attains distinction through action  
by making the enemy fall at the battlefront.**

A fortress is one that stands out through the special capacity to launch attacks from within strongly enough to bring down the enemy at the battlefront.

**750. However many excellences a fortress may possess,  
it is no fortress in the hands of those without excellence in action.**

Even if a fortress has every kind of excellence required of it, there is no benefit if those operating from within it lack ability.
