**56. A woman is one who guards herself, protects the man who has taken her as wife,  
guards the good name that brings them honour, and does not falter.**

A woman is one who protects both herself and her husband in the path of chastity and remains firm in safeguarding the good name that brings them honour.

**57. What can confinement do as protection?  
The protection by which women guard their own integrity is best.**

It is ignorance to think of treating as slaves women who guard themselves and live with excellent character.

**58. If women obtain a husband worthy of being obtained,  
they will gain the great distinction of a new world.**

If a woman obtains as her husband a man endowed with good character, domestic life will become for her a new world of great distinction.

**59. Those whose household lacks a life worthy of praise  
have no bull-like proud gait before those who speak scornfully of them.**

Those who do not have a domestic life worthy of praise will shrink before those who speak ill of them, unable to walk with their heads held high.

**60. The excellence of the household is called auspiciousness;  
its finest ornament is the blessing of good children.**

The culture of the family is the distinction of domestic life; an even greater distinction is having good children.
