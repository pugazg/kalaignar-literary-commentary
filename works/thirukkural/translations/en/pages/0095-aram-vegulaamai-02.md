**306. The fire called anger kills those who embrace it  
and burns the protecting raft called kin.**

Because the fire called anger is capable of destroying the person who becomes angry, it destroys not only that person but also the circle around him that protects him like a boat.

**307. The ruin of one who takes anger as something of worth  
is as certain as the pain in the hand that strikes the ground.**

When a person strikes the ground with his hand, it is his own hand that hurts. Such too is the condition of one who treats anger as a quality worth possessing.

**308. Even if one causes suffering like being burned by blazing fire,  
if one returns to reconcile, it is good not to be angry.**

Even if a person has repeatedly caused suffering like the pain of being burned by fire, when he regrets it and comes seeking renewed relations, it is good to remain without anger.

**309. If one holds no anger in the heart,  
all that one thinks of will be attained at once.**

If a person remains free of anger in his heart, he will be able to attain immediately all that he has thought of.

**310. Those consumed by boundless anger are like the dead;  
those who have wholly renounced anger are like renunciants.**

Those who give themselves to anger without limit are like the dead. Those who have completely renounced anger are like renunciants.
