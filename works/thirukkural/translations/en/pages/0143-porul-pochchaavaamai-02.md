**536. If the quality of never forgetting remains unfailing in a person,  
there is nothing equal to it.**

If the quality called freedom from forgetfulness is unfailingly present in a person, nothing else can bring that person greater good.

**537. Nothing is impossible  
if one acts with unfailing attention and without forgetfulness.**

If one acts without forgetfulness and with care, there is nothing that can be called impossible.

**538. One must cherish and perform duties worthy of praise;  
those who neglect them will find no rise in life.**

Duties deserving praise must be valued and carried out. Those who fail to do so and neglect them will have no advancement in life.

**539. When pride swells in the joy of success, remember those  
who forgot their duties through such pride and came to ruin.**

Those who become swollen with conceit and forget their duties should remember people who similarly forgot themselves and were destroyed, and correct their course.

**540. For those who remain intent upon the goal they have set,  
reaching that goal becomes easy.**

For those who remain energetic in the goal they have adopted and keep their interest fixed on succeeding in it, attaining that goal becomes easy.
