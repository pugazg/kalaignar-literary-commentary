# 128. Communicating by Signs

**1276. His embracing me with overflowing love seems itself to carry  
the painful sign that he is planning to leave me again.**

When he joins me, embracing me fully and showing boundless love, it seems to signal that he is going to separate from me once again.

**1277. My bangles seem to have understood before I did  
that my beloved of the cool waters had already begun to withdraw.**

Though my beloved of the cool waters was still united with me in body, he had begun to think of separation in his heart; it seems my bangles understood this before I did and loosened from me.

**1278. Our beloved left only yesterday,  
yet pallor has seized us as though seven days had passed.**

Our beloved went away only yesterday; yet pallor has taken hold of us as though many days had already gone by.

**1279. She looked at the bangle, then at her soft shoulder, then at her feet—  
and by that sign showed what she intended to do.**

She looked at the bangle that would slip away through separation, then at the soft shoulder that would grow thin, and by finally looking at her feet she signalled her decision to follow the beloved.

**1280. They say a woman gains womanhood beyond womanhood  
when, with her eyes, she tells her sickness of love and pleads to go with him.**

When she reveals her desire of love through her eyes and pleads to go with her beloved, it is as though womanliness itself has been added to her womanhood.
