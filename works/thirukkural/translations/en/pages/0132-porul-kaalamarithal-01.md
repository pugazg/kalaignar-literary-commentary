# 49. Knowing the Time

**481. In daylight the crow defeats the owl;  
so rulers need the proper time to defeat an enemy.**

If it is daytime, a crow will defeat an owl. Therefore, one must choose the fitting time to bring down an enemy.

**482. Acting in accord with the season  
is the rope that binds success so that it does not slip away.**

Understanding the time and acting accordingly becomes a rope that ties down the success of that good action and does not let it escape.

**483. What deed can be called impossible  
if one acts with the needed means and knows the proper time?**

If one acts with the necessary instruments and with knowledge of the proper time, there is nothing that can be called impossible.

**484. Even the world may come within one's grasp  
if one acts after considering the proper time and place.**

If one studies and understands the fitting time and place and then acts, even the whole world may come within one's hands.

**485. Those who seek to win the world  
wait without confusion for the proper time.**

Those who give no room to confusion and patiently wait for the right time will show that they can win even this world.
