# 120. Intensity of Solitary Longing

**1196. One-sided love is painful; like a carrying-pole balanced at both ends,  
love is sweet when it rests on two.**

Just as both ends of a carrying-pole must bear equal weight, love too must blossom in both the man and the woman; love arising on only one side brings no benefit and instead creates suffering.

**1197. Does the god of love not see my anguish and pallor,  
as he stands and acts on only one side?**

Because the god of love remains only on one side, it seems he does not see either the sickness of love tormenting me or the pallor spreading over my body!

**1198. There are none more hard-hearted in this world than those who live on  
without receiving even one sweet word from the beloved who has gone away.**

When not even a single sweet word comes from the beloved who has departed, there can be no one more stone-hearted than the person who nevertheless continues to live in this world.

**1199. Even if the one I desire gives me no love,  
words of praise about him are sweet to my ears.**

Even if the one dear to my love remains away without showing me affection, words spoken in his praise are like pure honey to my ears.

**1200. O heart, live on! You tell your little grief to one who has no love for you;  
it would be easier to empty the sea.**

O heart, long may you live! Rather than tell your suffering to one who has no love for you and seek consolation there, it would be easier to drain the sea.
