**406. Apart from merely being counted as existing, the unlearned  
are like barren land that yields no benefit.**

It is fitting to compare the unlearned to barren land. The reason is that they are to be regarded as mere walking corpses.

**407. The beauty of one without subtle and penetrating knowledge  
is like a finely adorned clay doll.**

Those who possess only an attractive appearance but no deep and clear knowledge will be valued only like a clay doll that catches the eye.

**408. Wealth that has come to the unlearned is more painful  
than poverty that has come to good people.**

Wealth accumulated in the hands of fools brings greater suffering than the poverty that afflicts good people.

**409. Though born above, the unlearned do not equal  
the learned even when the learned are born below.**

The distinction of being learned removes the difference called high and low.

**410. The difference between the learned in shining books and others  
is like the difference between human beings and animals.**

Whatever difference exists between human beings and animals, that same degree of difference exists between those who have studied books of knowledge and those who have not.
