**396. As water springs in a sandy well according to the depth dug,  
knowledge springs in people according to the extent they learn.**

Just as spring water is found the more one digs, knowledge continues to grow the more one keeps studying.

**397. When every country and every town can become one's own through learning,  
why should a person remain unlearned until death?**

When learning brings distinction in every country and every town, why should a person spend life without learning until the day of death?

**398. The learning acquired in one generation  
becomes a safeguard for seven generations.**

The knowledge gained through education in one generation becomes a protection for seven times seven generations.

**399. Seeing that what gives them joy also gives joy to the world,  
the learned desire to learn still more.**

Seeing that the knowledge which gives them pleasure also gives pleasure to the people of the world, the wise wish to learn more and more things.

**400. Learning is the imperishable and distinguished wealth of a person;  
all other possessions are not true wealth.**

Learning alone is wealth that cannot be destroyed. There is no other excellent wealth equal to it.
