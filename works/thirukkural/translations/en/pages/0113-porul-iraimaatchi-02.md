**386. If a king is easy to approach and does not speak harshly,  
his land will be spoken of highly.**

The world will praise only a government that is simple and approachable in appearance and possesses the pleasant quality of not speaking harsh words.

**387. To one who can give and protect with pleasant speech,  
this world will come under the sway of his own word.**

For one who has sweetness in speech and the nature of giving to others and protecting them, the whole world will come within reach.

**388. The king who rules by proper justice and protects the people  
will be regarded by them as their leader.**

The ruler who conducts government according to justice and protects the people is the one who will be honoured by the people as their leader.

**389. The world will remain beneath the umbrella of the ruler  
who has the quality of enduring words bitter to the ear.**

Only the government of a person of character who can bear even harsh words that pierce the ear will command respect among the people.

**390. Giving, compassion, just rule and care of the people—  
the ruler who has these four is a light among kings.**

Providing what is needed for good living, showing compassion with an understanding of circumstances, governing without departing from impartiality, and nurturing and protecting the people—these bring the light of fame to a government.
