# 62. Effective Effort

**611. Do not lose heart saying, “This is too difficult”;  
greatness is what effort will give.**

Instead of becoming discouraged and wondering whether something is beyond us, we should make the effort with confidence that it can be done; that itself becomes a great strength.

**612. Guard against allowing an undertaking to fail midway;  
the world abandons those who abandon a task unfinished.**

Whatever action one undertakes, it must be carried through completely. Otherwise it becomes like the tale of having crossed only half a well.

**613. The proud quality called helping others rests  
in the excellence called perseverance.**

The proud feeling that comes from helping others is found in people of elevated character who are capable of sustained effort.

**614. The help of one who lacks perseverance will fail  
like a sword wielded in the hand of a coward.**

There is no difference between depending on a person without initiative as a helper and watching a coward raise and swing a sword in his hand.

**615. One who does not seek personal pleasure but seeks to complete the task  
becomes the pillar that removes the suffering of those around him.**

A person who does not seek self-interest but desires to complete the undertaking he has begun becomes a supporting pillar, removing the suffering of his relatives, friends and all the people of his country.
