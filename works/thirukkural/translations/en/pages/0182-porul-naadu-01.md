# 74. The Country

**731. A country is where unfailing produce, worthy people,  
and wealthy people who do not sink low come together.**

A good country is one blessed with produce whose abundance does not diminish, eminent people, and people of wealth who do not spend their wealth along evil paths.

**732. A country is one rich in great resources, worthy of being desired by others,  
free from grievous ruin, and abundantly productive.**

The finest country is one filled with material wealth, worthy of others' praise, free from ruin, and possessing good agricultural yield.

**733. A country bears the full burden when it comes upon it  
and renders in full the revenue due to the ruler.**

The finest country is one that bears new burdens even when they come together upon it, pays the kinds of tax due to the government, and possesses wealth to that measure.

> Source-check note: the audited Tamil commentary prints `மளவுக்கு வளம்`. The source-checked English retains the source's unusual construction rather than silently reconstructing a smoother Tamil phrase.

**734. A country is one where severe hunger, unceasing disease,  
and hostile enemies do not gather.**

A country free from hunger, disease, and enmity is the country that will be praised as the finest.

**735. A country is one without many factions, ruinous internal enmity,  
and murderous bands that trouble the ruler.**

The finest country is one without the internal hostility of many factions dividing and laying it waste, and without the evil caused by killers who exercise dominance in the government.
