# 17. Freedom from Envy

**161. Let one take as the rule of conduct  
the nature of having no envy in one's heart.**

One should make the nature of living without envy in the mind the guiding rule of one's conduct.

**162. There is no attainment equal to this:  
to gain the quality of envying no one.**

If a person gains the quality of feeling no envy toward anyone, there is no greater blessing for that person.

**163. The one who does not cherish another's prosperity through envy  
is said not to desire aram or prosperity for oneself.**

It is the person who neither desires nor honours the path of aram and prosperity who fails to honour another person's greatness and becomes a storehouse of envy.

**164. Those who know the suffering born of a crooked path  
will not do evil through envy.**

Those who know that suffering results from taking an evil path will not engage in evil deeds because of envy.

**165. For those possessed by envy, envy itself is enough;  
even without an enemy, it will bring them ruin.**

Those with an envious nature need no other enemy to bring them down. That very quality will destroy them.
