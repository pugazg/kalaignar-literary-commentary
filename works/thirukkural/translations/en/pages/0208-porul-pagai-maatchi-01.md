# 87. The Character of Enmity

**861. Leave the weak aside and desire to stand against the strong;  
such enmity is praised as greatness in enmity.**

To leave the weak alone and prefer to fight against the strong is what is praised as greatness in enmity.

**862. Without love for those beside him, without a strong ally, and himself without strength,  
how can a man overcome his enemy?**

When a person has no affection for those who are with him, has no powerful ally, and is himself without strength, how can he defeat an enemy?

**863. Fearful and ignorant, unable to live in harmony, and without compassionate thought—  
such a person is easy for the enemy to defeat.**

If a person is fearful and foolish, lacks the nature needed to live together with others, and has no compassionate thought, he will be easily defeated by his enemies.

**864. One who cannot restrain anger or the mind  
is easy for anyone, at any time, in any place.**

Those who cannot control their anger and their mind can be easily defeated by anyone, at any time, and anywhere.

**865. One who seeks no right path, does not do what fits the occasion, fears no blame, and has no character  
is easy for his enemies to defeat.**

If a person does not seek the right path, does not do what is appropriate, does not fear blame, and lacks good qualities, his enemies will defeat him easily.
