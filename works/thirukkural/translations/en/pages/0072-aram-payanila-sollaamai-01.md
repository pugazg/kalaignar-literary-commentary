# 20. Not Speaking Useless Words

**191. One who speaks useless words that make many people resent him  
will be despised by everyone.**

Everyone will speak contemptuously of a person who utters useless words that many people come to dislike.

**192. Speaking useless words before many people  
is worse than acting contrary to friendship toward one's friends.**

Speaking words without benefit before many people is more harmful than acting against the obligations of friendship.

**193. A discourse that expansively speaks of useless things  
will itself declare that the speaker lacks worth.**

The very fact that a person keeps speaking at length about useless matters is enough to reveal that he is himself without usefulness.

**194. Before many people, words without benefit and without good quality  
destroy goodness and remove what is good.**

Speaking before many people in words that are both useless and lacking in quality will disturb happiness and wipe away good.

**195. If people of good character speak useless words,  
their distinction and the respect due to them will depart.**

If a person of good character speaks useless words, that person's standing will disappear along with the respect that belongs to it.
