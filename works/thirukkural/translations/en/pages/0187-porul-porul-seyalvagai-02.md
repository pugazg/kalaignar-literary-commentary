**756. Tax revenue, customs revenue, and wealth obtained from defeated enemies  
are the ruler's wealth.**

Taxes, customs duties, and the tribute paid by enemy countries that have been defeated are resources belonging to the government.

**757. The child called compassion, born of the mother called love,  
is nurtured by the wealthy nurse called material resources.**

The child called compassion, born to the mother called love, can be raised by the nurse called material resources.

**758. Undertaking an enterprise with one's own capital is like  
climbing a hill and watching elephants fight.**

Carrying out an enterprise with one's own capital is as easy as standing on a hill and watching elephants fight one another without becoming trapped between them.

**759. Accumulate wealth; there is no weapon sharper than it  
for cutting down the pride of enemies.**

Since there is no instrument other than wealth that is capable of destroying the arrogance of enemies, wealth must be accumulated.

**760. For those who have amassed abundant good wealth in a fitting way,  
the other two come together easily.**

Among the three—aram, wealth, and happiness—those who have accumulated wealth abundantly in a fitting way will find that the other two come to them together with ease.
