**996. The world rests upon people of good character; without them,  
it would sink into the earth and perish.**

The ways of the world must function by depending upon people of good character. Otherwise, those ways themselves will be destroyed.

**997. Even if they are sharp in intelligence like a file,  
those without the character proper to people are like trees.**

Even if a person is a genius with intelligence sharp as a file, one who lacks the character proper to human beings is equal to a tree.

**998. Even toward those unfit for friendship who do harmful things,  
it is base not to conduct oneself with patience and character.**

If, toward people who are unfit for friendship and keep doing harmful things, we fail to show patience and conduct ourselves with good character, that will be regarded as a degrading act.

**999. For those who cannot mingle and rejoice with friends,  
this vast world is darkness even in daylight.**

For people who do not know how to live among friends and rejoice with them, the world remains dark even in the daytime.

**1000. The great wealth gained by one without character  
is like good milk spoiled by contact with impurity.**

If a vessel is coated with grime, the milk poured into it will spoil; in the same way, the wealth acquired by people without good character becomes useless.
