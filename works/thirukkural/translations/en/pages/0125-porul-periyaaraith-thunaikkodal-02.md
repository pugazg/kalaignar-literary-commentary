**446. What can enemies do to one who knows how to conduct himself  
while belonging to a company of worthy people?**

If a person of knowledge and ability surrounds himself with others of the same kind, enemies will not be able to cause him any harm.

**447. Who possesses the power to destroy one  
who has as his support people who rebuke him and set him right?**

Who has the power to ruin those who live with the support of people who rebuke them and show them the right path?

**448. An unguarded king who has no one to rebuke him  
will perish even when there is no enemy to destroy him.**

A government without people who point out its faults will destroy itself.

**449. There is no return for one without capital;  
there is no stability for one without supporting pillars.**

The state of people who have no support capable of holding them up like a pillar holding up a building is like trade that brings no income because no capital has been invested.

**450. Giving up the companionship of good people  
brings ten times the harm of making many enemies.**

Abandoning one's relationship with good people can bring greater harm than seeking out the enmity of many people.
