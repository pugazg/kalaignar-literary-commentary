# 125. Speaking with the Heart

**1241. O heart, can you think of and tell me some remedy—  
anything at all—to cure this painful sickness?**

O heart! Can you think of some remedy and tell me one that might cure my sickness of love, which no medicine seems able to cure?

**1242. O my heart, live on! When he has no love for us,  
your grieving for him is foolishness.**

When he does not value our love and come to us, O heart, it is ignorance for you alone to keep thinking of him and suffering. Live on!

**1243. O heart, what use is there in sitting here and thinking of him,  
when the one who caused this wasting sickness has no compassionate thought for us?**

When the beloved who gave us the suffering of separation has no compassion for us, O heart, what benefit is there in your remaining here, thinking of him and becoming distressed?

**1244. O heart, when you go to him, take my eyes with you;  
otherwise they will devour me in their longing to see him.**

O heart! When you go to the beloved, take the eyes along with you too; otherwise, in their desire to see him, they seem as though they will eat me alive.

**1245. O heart, can we give him up as one who hates us,  
merely because he does not come to love us though we love him?**

O heart! Even though we desire him and he does not desire us and come to us, can we think that he has come to hate us and abandon the love we bear for him?
