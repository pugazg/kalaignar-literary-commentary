## Editorial principle applied

The controlling editorial rule is:

> **Readable English may improve syntax; it may not replace Kalaignar's language, imagery, emphases or interpretive direction.**

This rule also applies to Professor Ma. Nannan's Critical Appreciation, the Publisher's Note, the chapter index and this edition's own explanatory glossary.

The review therefore did **not**:

- substitute familiar published English Kural wording;
- normalize Kalaignar's readings toward another commentator;
- flatten Nannan's metaphors into smoother but different images;
- replace this edition's chapter glosses with a standard glossary;
- import a conventional 133-chapter English title list.

### `புத்தேளிர் / புத்தேள் உலகு`

The source-specific treatment ***puththelir* / new world** is retained.

Nannan explicitly explains Kalaignar's reading by deriving `புத்தேளிர்` from `புதுமை`—newness. The English therefore follows that source argument instead of importing an external traditional gloss.

### `மக்கள் நெஞ்சின் மலிவுப் பதிப்பு`

The source-close metaphor **“the low-priced edition of the people's hearts”** is retained on scan 26.

A smoother phrase such as “an edition readily accessible to the people's hearts” conveys an implication but changes the image. Because the user's project requirement is to preserve the source author's language, the literal metaphor remains intentional.
