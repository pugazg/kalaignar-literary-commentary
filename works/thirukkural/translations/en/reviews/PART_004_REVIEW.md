### Deliberate main-body refinements from the Part 002 index-local forms

Four Part 004 titles intentionally differ from the already released index-local renderings. The index files remain unchanged because they are preserved as their own reviewed translation layer.

1. `புறங்கூறாமை` — index-local **Not Slandering** → main body **Not Speaking Ill Behind Another's Back**. The main-body verses and Kalaignar's commentary repeatedly centre speech about a person who is absent; “slandering” can also imply falsity that the chapter title itself does not require.
2. `தீவினையச்சம்` — index-local **Dread of Evil Deeds** → main body **Fear of Evil Deeds**. The main-body commentary repeatedly uses the ordinary semantic field of fearing / being afraid of evil action; **Fear** is clear and source-close without intensifying the register.
3. `ஒப்புரவறிதல்` — index-local **Understanding Mutual Help** → main body **Understanding Helpfulness**. The project glossary already records `ஒப்புரவு` as helpfulness / helping others, and Kural 211 explicitly frames the act as giving without expectation of repayment. “Mutual” would build reciprocity into the main-body title where this source stresses the opposite.
4. `புலால் மறுத்தல்` — index-local **Abstaining from Meat** → main body **Abstaining from Flesh**. The supplied main body repeatedly speaks of another living being's body / flesh and ties that imagery to compassion; **Flesh** better preserves that source texture.

The other Part 004 headings either match the released index-local form or were already directly supported by the project's controlled glossary.

## Editorial changes made in this review

Only small readability / consistency refinements were made after source-check:

1. **Kural 160 commentary** — the awkward `honoured only in the place after` was tightened to **honoured only after**.
2. **Kural 183** — removed the repetitive `live ... living falsely` construction while retaining the same death-versus-false-life contrast.
3. **Kural 184** — pronouns were made internally consistent without changing the generic source referent.
4. **Kural 186** — generic pronouns were made consistent rather than inserting an unnecessary masculine referent.
5. **Kural 200** — the mechanically repetitive `words among words` construction was smoothed to **“Speak words that carry benefit; / do not speak words that carry no benefit.”**
6. **Kural 238** — `a blame upon the life itself` was smoothed to **a reproach upon that life**.
7. **Kural 251** — generic `his own flesh` was reconciled to **one's own flesh**, while the explicit image of eating another being's flesh remains unchanged.
8. **Kural 254** — the awkward `no right thing` was smoothed to **not right**; Kalaignar's commentary still separately states that eating flesh is not aram.

No source-sensitive metaphor, relationship, social category, doctrinal qualification, or Kalaignar interpretation was softened by these edits.
