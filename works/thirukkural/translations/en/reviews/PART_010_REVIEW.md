## Structural-term reconciliation

### Chapter 87 refinement

The one heading refinement made at this gate is chapter 87:

- source-checked provisional form: **The Character of Enmity**;
- controlled main-body form: **Excellence in Enmity**.

The refinement restores the force of `மாட்சி` and is consistent with the project's existing treatment of `மாட்சி` in headings such as `படை மாட்சி` → **Excellence of the Army**. More importantly, Kalaignar's own Kural 861 commentary explicitly says that leaving the weak and preferring to fight the strong is what is praised as `பகைமாட்சி`. **Excellence in Enmity** therefore better preserves the supplied main-body semantic direction than the generic “character” wording.

Scans 208–209 were updated consistently in heading/section metadata only. No Kural or commentary body text was changed for this refinement.

## Recurring terminology reconciliation

- `நட்பு / கேண்மை` → **friendship / friendship bond / companionship** according to grammar and immediate context. Do not force one English noun mechanically into every sentence.
- `பகை` → **enmity** when the Tamil denotes the abstract relationship/feeling; **enemy / enemies** when the syntax refers to persons. Preserve the distinction rather than flattening both senses.
- `இகல்` → **Discord** as chapter 86; in prose use **discord / difference / hostile feeling** according to Kalaignar's sentence.
- `உட்பகை` → **Internal Enmity** as chapter 89; retain the source's close-relative/within-the-group danger framing.
- `விலைமகளிர்` remains **women for hire**, consistent with the project's earlier index/glossary treatment and the explicit Part 010 commentary. Do not neutralize the source's social/gender specificity.
- Existing project controls for `அரசு` → **government** in institutional commentary and ruler/king language for `வேந்து / மன்னர்` remain binding. Kural 895 deliberately retains this distinction.
