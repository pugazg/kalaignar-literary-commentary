## Recurring terminology reconciliation

- `நாணம் / நாணுடைமை` → **modesty / shame / honour** according to sentence-level context; chapter 102 remains **Modesty**. Preserve Kalaignar's distinction between inward shame over blameworthy conduct and honour where his commentary explicitly uses that register.
- `குடிசெயல் / குடிமக்கள்` → **working for the community / community / citizens** according to grammar and Kalaignar's civic framing; chapter 103 remains **Working for the Community**.
- `உழவு / உழவர்` → **Agriculture / agriculture / farmers**. Preserve Kalaignar's productive-labour and dependence-on-farming imagery.
- `நல்குரவு / வறுமை` → **Poverty / poverty / want** according to syntax; do not soften the repeated suffering and deprivation language.
- `இரவு / இரத்தல்` → **Begging / begging / to beg**. Keep distinct from chapter 107's fear/refusal of begging.
- `இரவச்சம்` → **Dread of Begging**. Preserve the chapter's dignity-of-labour and refusal-to-beg framing.
- `கயமை / கயவர்` → **Baseness / base people / the base** according to grammar. Preserve Kalaignar's satirical and direct register.
- `குறிப்பு` → **sign / indication**; chapter 110 remains **Understanding Signs**.
- `புணர்ச்சி` → **union / embrace** according to context; chapter 111 remains **Delight in Union**.
- `அனிச்சம் / அனிச்சப்பூ` → **anicham flower**. Retain the flower name rather than replacing the source image with a generic flower.
