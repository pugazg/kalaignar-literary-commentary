## Structural term — `அமைச்சியல்`

The source-visible running-header transition at scan **162 / printed page 129 / Kural 631** is:

`பொருள் — அரசியல்` → `பொருள் — அமைச்சியல்`.

The controlled English metadata therefore remains:

`Porul — Statecraft` → **`Porul — Ministerial Affairs`**.

**Ministerial Affairs** is retained deliberately because it:

- preserves the source's explicit distinction from `அரசியல்` / **Statecraft**;
- accurately names the section centred on ministers, the council of ministers, counsel, eloquence and the conduct of action;
- avoids importing an external conventional section title not supplied by this edition;
- remains broad enough for the chapters that follow `அமைச்சு` rather than reducing the whole section to the single office of “The Minister.”

This is now the controlled section-level rendering for `அமைச்சியல்` in the supplied main body.

## Recurring terminology reconciliation

- `கண்ணோட்டம்` is controlled as **Compassion** at chapter level. In running prose, **compassion / compassionate regard / love and mercy** may be used according to Kalaignar's own explanation.
- `ஒற்று / ஒற்றர் / ஒற்றாடல்` are handled through **spy / spies / intelligence-gathering / Espionage**. Kalaignar's government and intelligence framing is retained.
- `ஊக்கம்` is handled through **initiative / inner drive / firmness of initiative** according to context. Chapter 60 remains **Possession of Initiative**.
- `மடி` remains **laziness**; chapter 61 is **Freedom from Laziness**.
- `முயற்சி / தாளாண்மை / ஆள்வினை` are translated contextually through **effort / sustained effort / perseverance / effective effort** rather than forced into one word. Chapter 62 is **Effective Effort**.
- `இடுக்கண் / இடும்பை / துன்பம்` are handled through **adversity / hardship / suffering** according to the immediate sentence. Chapter 63 is **Not Losing Heart in Adversity**.
- `அமைச்சு / அமைச்சர் / அமைச்சரவை` are **The Minister / minister / council of ministers** according to grammatical function.
- `சொல்வன்மை` is **Eloquence** at chapter level and **eloquence / power of speech** in prose as context requires.
- `வினைத் தூய்மை` is **Purity in Action**; running prose retains **good / pure / improper / degrading action** according to Kalaignar's wording.
- `வினைத்திட்பம்` is **Firmness in Action**; running prose uses **firmness / resolve in carrying out an action** according to context.
- `அரசு` remains **government** where Kalaignar speaks institutionally, while compressed Kural terms such as `வேந்தன்` remain contextual as **ruler** where appropriate.

## Editorial body-text changes made in this review

The source-checked translation was already substantially faithful and readable. Three small readability refinements were made, each preserving the source-checked meaning:

1. **scan 156 / Kural 602 verse**
   - before: “must conduct themselves by casting laziness away as laziness”
   - after: **“must reject laziness and conduct themselves without it”**
   - reason: removes awkward English repetition while remaining within Kalaignar's commentary that laziness must be removed if the family is to flourish;
2. **scan 161 / Kural 627 verse**
   - before: “do not let distress become their way of meeting it”
   - after: **“do not respond to it with distress”**
   - reason: preserves the source-check correction while expressing Kalaignar's explanation more naturally;
3. **scan 164 / Kural 641 verse**
   - before: “The excellence called excellence of speech ...”
   - after: **“The excellence called eloquence ...”**
   - reason: removes unnecessary English repetition while aligning with the controlled chapter term `சொல்வன்மை` → **Eloquence**.

No other substantive body-text change was made during this editorial pass.
