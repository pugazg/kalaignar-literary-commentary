## Recurring terminology reconciliation

- `தூது / தூதர்` → **envoy / message / mission** according to grammar and context. Chapter 69 remains **The Envoy**.
- `குறிப்பு` → **sign / indication / intention** according to the immediate sentence; chapter 71 remains **Understanding Signs**.
- `அவை` → **assembly**. Chapters 72–73 remain **Knowing the Assembly** and **Fearlessness in the Assembly**.
- `நாடு` → **country** in chapter 74, including Kalaignar's government, public welfare and resource framing.
- `அரண்` → **fortress**; retain siege, defence, stored food, soldiers/people and battlefront vocabulary.
- `பொருள்` in chapter 76 → **wealth / material resources / resources** according to context; do not force one equivalent where Kalaignar distinguishes money, capital and public revenue.
- `வரி` → **tax / taxes**; `சுங்கம்` → **customs duties**; `கப்பம்` → **tribute**.
- `படை` → **army**; preserve military organization, formation, leadership and battlefield vocabulary.
- `நடுகல்` → **memorial stone** in the Kural 771 commentary image.
- `வீரம்` → **valour / courage** according to context; `மானம்` → **sense of honour** where explicitly used.
- `அரசு` remains **government** where Kalaignar speaks institutionally; `வேந்தன் / மன்னர் / தலைவன்` remain contextual as **ruler / king / leader** rather than being flattened into government.
