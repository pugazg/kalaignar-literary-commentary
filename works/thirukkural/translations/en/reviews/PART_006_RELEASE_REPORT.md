## Protected Kalaignar-language decisions

The release deliberately retains Kalaignar's own language, imagery, emphases and interpretive direction rather than substituting conventional external Thirukkural readings.

Most importantly:

- `ஊழ்` remains **Oozh**;
- Kalaignar's repeated explanation `இயற்கை நிலை` remains **natural condition**;
- the translation does **not** replace Kalaignar's explanation with a generalized fate/destiny doctrine.

Other protected source-sensitive features released in Part 006 include:

- government / administration, treasury, revenue and planned-expenditure language in chapter 39;
- direct eye/sores, breast, barren-land, walking-corpse and clay-doll comparisons in chapter 41;
- ear-food and walking-stick imagery in chapter 42;
- Kural 423 commentary's insistence on examining what is true rather than accepting a statement merely because someone says it;
- millet/palmyra and haystack/fire imagery in chapter 44;
- rebuke/correction, pillar/building and capital/trade language in chapter 45;
- chapter 46's context-controlled rendering of `இனம்` through **company / group / association**, following Kalaignar's supplied commentary rather than importing a racial or biological interpretation.
