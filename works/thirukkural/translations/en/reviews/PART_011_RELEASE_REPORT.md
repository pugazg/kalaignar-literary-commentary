All six Part 011 direct source-check corrections remain intact:

1. **Kural 911** retains **bring suffering**, not the intensified first-pass **bring ruin**.
2. **Kural 926** retains the source's line relationship: sleepers/dead in line one and liquor/poison in line two.
3. **Kural 953** retains **truthful citizens**, following Kalaignar's explicit `வாய்மையுள்ள குடிமக்கள்` explanation.
