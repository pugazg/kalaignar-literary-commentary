## Recurring terminology and imagery reconciliation

- `காதல் / காதலர்` → **love / beloved / lover** according to grammar and speaker context. Preserve source gender where Kalaignar makes it explicit.
- `நாணம்` → **modesty / shame** according to context; chapter 114 remains **Abandoning Modesty**.
- `மடல்` remains the source's **madal** image/concept where it occurs; do not replace it with a generic act of public courtship that erases the Tamil image.
- `அலர்` in chapter 115 is retained through **Rumour**, with Kalaignar's public/town gossip framing preserved in prose.
- `பிரிவு` → **separation**; retain the direct emotional and bodily suffering Kalaignar attaches to it.
- `பசலை / பசப்பு` → **pallor** in this context; do not dilute the repeated bodily image into generic sadness.
- `காவடி` imagery in Kural 1196 remains a **carrying-pole** balanced at both ends, following Kalaignar's explicit explanation.
- `கள்` in Kural 1201 remains **liquor**, preserving Kalaignar's comparison between remembered love and pleasure available only while drinking.
- dream/waking (`கனவு / நனவு`) contrasts remain explicit throughout chapter 122.
- evening (`மாலை`) in chapter 123 retains Kalaignar's personified and weapon-like force rather than being reduced to a neutral time-of-day description.
