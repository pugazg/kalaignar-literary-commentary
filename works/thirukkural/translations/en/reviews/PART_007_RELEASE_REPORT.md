## Protected Kalaignar-language decisions

The release deliberately retains Kalaignar's own language, imagery, emphases and political/governance framing rather than substituting conventional external Thirukkural readings.

Most importantly:

- **Kural 543** retains **“the books of the virtuous”** because Kalaignar explains `அந்தணர் நூற்கும்` through `அறவோர் நூல்களுக்கும்`; no caste-specific conventional gloss is imported;
- **Kural 520** retains Kalaignar's explicit focus on **working people** and the daily responsibility of those who govern to examine their condition and act;
- **Kural 464** retains the source-check correction centred on `களங்கம்` through **“the blemish of disgrace”**, without reintroducing the unsupported first-pass idea of “failure”;
- chapters **55–57** retain Kalaignar's institutional vocabulary through **government, citizens, good governance, justice, tyranny, public resources, productive work, occupations, punishment and fear** where his commentary explicitly uses that framing.

- citizens' tears becoming a weapon against oppressive rule;
