## Editorial principles applied

The review treated Part 001 as one English editorial set rather than as isolated pages. It checked:

- work, section and contributor titles;
- repeated treatment of `அறம்` / aram;
- transliterated source concepts and work titles;
- names and honorifics;
- quoted source titles `கடவுள் வாழ்த்து` / **Invocation to God** and `வழிபாடு` / **Worship**;
- poetic line handling in K. Anbazhagan's foreword;
- analytical prose register in Professor Ma. Nannan's Critical Appreciation;
- punctuation, quotation marks and emphasis;
- literary terminology on scan 20;
- explicit retention of source-specific or unresolved Tamil where English certainty would otherwise be overstated.

No external English Kural wording or outside commentary was imported to improve fluency or settle an uncertain reading.

## Controlled decisions after editorial review

### Work and front-matter titles

The following are retained as the Part 001 controlled English forms:

- `திருக்குறள்` → **Thirukkural**;
- `கலைஞர் உரை` → **Kalaignar's Commentary**;
- `முகவுரை` → **Preface**;
- `பேராசிரியரின் அணிந்துரை` → **The Professor's Foreword**;
- `மதிப்புரை` → **Critical Appreciation**;
- `கடவுள் வாழ்த்து` → **Invocation to God** when the source title itself is being named;
- `வழிபாடு` → **Worship** for Kalaignar's adopted chapter title.

The distinction between **Invocation to God** and **Worship** is intentional and must not be collapsed.

### `அறம்`

Part 001 continues the context-aware policy:

- **Aram** in structural/book-level labels where appropriate;
- `aram` in running English where the Tamil concept itself is being retained;
- contextual English such as virtue/right conduct only where the sentence requires it.

No single English equivalent is forced across every occurrence.

### `முப்பால்`

**Muppaal** is retained as the project transliteration in the Professor's Foreword. It is not silently replaced with another title or expanded from an external tradition.

### `திருவிடம்`

The verified Tamil archival source reads `திருவிடம்`. The English therefore deliberately retains **Tiruvidam**. Editorial review treats this as a source-specific form, not an error to be normalized.

### `ஊழ்`

The project treatment is now settled for Part 001:

- **Oozh** when used as a section/chapter/concept heading;
- *oozh* in running prose or poetic discussion where the Tamil term itself is being named;
- **natural condition** only where Professor Nannan explicitly states that Kalaignar gives `ஊழ்` that meaning.

The project does not redefine `ஊழ்` globally as “natural condition.”

### `வாயுறை`

The scan-10 rendering **counsel** is retained. Editorial review found it readable and source-compatible without requiring a more interpretive English expansion.

### `பெண்வழிச் சேறல்`

**Following a Woman's Lead** is retained as the Part 001 controlled rendering. This preserves the chapter reference while remaining consistent with Nannan's explicit argument that earlier interpretations had wrongly placed blame upon women.

### `பிறிது மொழிதல்`

The technical term remains ***pirithu mozhithal*** with the explanatory gloss **“saying one thing in order to convey another.”** No external rhetorical label is substituted.

### `அடுத்தூர்வது அஃதொப்பதில்`

The phrase remains **untranslated and preserved exactly in Tamil** on scan 19. The earlier speculative English expansion was withdrawn during source-check. Editorial review confirms that preserving the source phrase is safer than inventing certainty or importing an external Kural/commentary reading.

This is an intentionally documented unresolved English rendering, not an omitted review task.

### Literary headings on scan 20

The following are retained as the Part 001 controlled forms:

- `பா நலம்` → **Poetic Quality**;
- `அணி நலம்` → **Excellence of Poetic Figure**;
- `அடை நலம்` → **Excellence of Epithets**.

These are adequate for the present source context. If later pages provide a broader technical usage that justifies revising the English terminology, any change must be deliberate, recorded in the glossary, and propagated consistently.
