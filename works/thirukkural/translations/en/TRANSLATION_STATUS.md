## Source-check corrections

The direct Tamil-to-English comparison made seven source-fidelity corrections/refinements across six English page files:

1. **Kural 813** — replaced the first-pass Kural phrase **“women who sell intimacy”** with **“women for hire”**, following Kalaignar's explicit commentary `விலைமகளிரும்` and removing an added idea not stated by him.
2. **Kural 822** — removed the first-pass Kural expansion **“women who merely appear to possess virtue”** and used **“women for hire”**; the commentary continues to carry Kalaignar's explicit explanation about appearing to possess the good qualities expected of women.
3. **Kural 842 commentary** — removed unsupported **“earned”** from the recipient's good fortune.
4. **Kural 849** — corrected the Kural rendering so the one who tries to make an unseeing person see becomes unseeing himself, while the unseeing person thinks he has seen in his own way; this follows Kalaignar's adjacent commentary rather than the first-pass subject drift.
5. **Kural 850** — removed the commentary-only evidence clause from the Kural translation. The Kural now states the denial of what the world says exists; Kalaignar's evidence/truth framing remains in the separate commentary where the Tamil places it.
6. **Kural 867 commentary** — removed the unsupported added phrase **“and harmful to us”** from `தனக்குப் பொருந்தாத காரியங்களை`; the source-checked wording keeps only the sense of acts that do not befit one.
7. **Kural 887** — removed the unsupported material qualifier **“metal”** from `செப்பு`; the commentary retains the source term *seppu* without inventing a material not supplied by Kalaignar.
