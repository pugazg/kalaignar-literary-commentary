Both physical Tamil records explicitly carry `இன்பம் — கற்பியல் — ஊடலுவகை`; the released English records use `Inbam — Wedded Love — Joy of Lovers' Quarrel`. Kalaignar's commentary preserves the chapter's characteristic paradoxes of lovers' quarrelling: the quarrel can deepen love, the one defeated in the quarrel becomes the victor when union follows, and Kural 1326 explicitly compares the pleasure of quarrelling with the pleasure of digestion over eating and with lovers' bodily union.
