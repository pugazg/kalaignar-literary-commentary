## What not to do

- Do not perform source-scan lexical correction against Gemini.
- Do not silently modernize or normalize Gemini wording.
- Do not substitute a more familiar spelling.
- Do not alter a Gemini word because another edition or the scan seems preferable.
- If a suspected issue would require changing Gemini wording, leave the wording unchanged unless the user explicitly authorizes a lexical change.

## Omission handling

If the scan reveals a structural block or paragraph that is entirely absent from the supplied Gemini transcription, record the structural omission for follow-up rather than inventing or silently source-transcribing lexical content, unless the user explicitly asks for that recovery.
