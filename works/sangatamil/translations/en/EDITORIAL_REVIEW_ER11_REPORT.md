## Source-faithful editorial refinements

ER11 made editorial refinements in **11 English page files** while preserving the maintained Tamil meaning and the closed SC11/GR11 decisions.

1. scan **372** — replaced the unidiomatic **“There was no one there; the thief himself alone”** with **“There was no one there—only the thief himself.”**
2. scan **374** — recast the awkward **“compares one simile through two scenes”** as **“sets one simile across two scenes.”**
3. scans **377/379** — repaired the cross-page nocchi-garland sentence so that the poet's reflection reads continuously and grammatically across the intervening illustration while preserving the source's causal image.
4. scan **383** — replaced **“keep proclaiming long counsels”** with the more natural **“keep loudly delivering lengthy counsel.”**
5. scan **384** — changed **“stood shaking him with her two buds”** to **“stood stirring him with her two buds,”** retaining the source metaphor while removing an unidiomatic English verb choice.
6. scan **387** — recast **“fragrant gentle breezes drift and move”** as **“the fragrant gentle breeze drifts through.”**
7. scan **390** — repaired the opening refrain's broken **“Me— / I”** syntax while preserving its repeated question and song structure.
8. scan **395** — changed **“I have come to pluck this veena”** to the idiomatic **“I have come to play this veena,”** preserving `வீணையை மீட்டிட`.
9. scan **399** — replaced **“flared up at me with a sharp ‘sul!’”** with **“snapped at me with a sharp ‘sul!’”**
10. scan **402** — replaced **“praised in caressing Tamil”** with **“praised in tender Tamil,”** retaining the sense of `கொஞ்சுதமிழில்`.

The scans **377** and **379** form one cross-page editorial repair but are counted as two modified page files, yielding **11 refined page files** in total.
