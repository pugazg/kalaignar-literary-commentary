## Source-faithful editorial refinements

ER13 made editorial refinements in **10 English page files** while preserving the maintained Tamil meaning and the closed SC13/GR13 decisions.

1. scan **445** — replaced the literal but awkward **“beating the tongue-drum”** with **“beating the drum of boastful words,”** preserving the `நாப்பறை` martial metaphor.
2. scan **448** — reconciled **Karantai → Karandhai** for the already controlled battle-category term.
3. scan **458** — replaced **“a wife who had lost her auspicious married state”** with the readable source-faithful **“a wife widowed by battle.”**
4. scan **460** — replaced **“a Purananuru-like song”** with **“a *puram* song,”** preserving `புறப்பாட்டாய்` without implying a specific anthology imitation.
5. scan **464** — tightened **“Which of these two have you come seeking to give me?”** to **“Which of these two have you come to give me?”**
6. scan **467** — repaired the unintended grammatical image in which the chariot itself **“heard Narkilli's command”**; the sequence now reads that the chariot begins moving and, **at Narkilli's command, the horses break into a gallop**.
7. scan **470** — recast the opaque **“give no answer to pain”** as **“bid farewell to your sorrow,”** preserving the consoling sense of `வேதனைக்கு விடையளிப்பீர்`.
8. scan **473** — removed the non-idiomatic **“today itself”** while preserving the immediate command: **“Go and tell your lord today!”**
9. scan **474** — clarified Kaavarp Penn's invitation from **“ordered her to stay in her house before leaving”** to **“asked her to stay at her house before setting out,”** matching the subsequent instruction to eat, rest and sleep.
10. scan **476** — recast the principle about life and honour into readable English: **“when life inevitably leaves this body, honour must not climb the funeral pyre with it.”**

No quoted Sangam verse block required structural presentation repair in ER13.
