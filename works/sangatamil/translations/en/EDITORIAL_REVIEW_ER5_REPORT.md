## Source-faithful wording refinements

ER5 made source-faithful readability refinements in **8 English page files**:

1. scan **154** — changed **“must I ... become useless?”** to **“must I ... go to waste?”** for the heroine's `பயனற்றுப் போய்விடவோ?`, retaining the self-description as a young parrot in the bloom of youth.
2. scan **161** — smoothed `தலை வைத்தும் படுக்க மாட்டார்` to **“would not even dare lie down with their heads pointing toward the mountain he ruled,”** preserving the source's directional image.

4. scan **167** — changed **“a segment set within a fruit”** to **“a segment nestled inside a fruit”** for `கனியிடையேறிய சுளை`.
5. scan **168** — replaced **“hoping to topple her anger”** with natural **“hoping to win her over”** for `அவளைக் கவிழ்க்கப் பார்த்தான்`.

7. scan **175** — replaced the awkward **“battlefront where wounds are won”** with **“battlefront, face wounds”**, retaining the source's contrast between ordinary life and battle-fame.
8. scan **185** — rendered the idiomatic `காரி உமிழ்ந்தனர்` naturally as **“spitting in contempt”** rather than the literal **“spitting blackness.”**
