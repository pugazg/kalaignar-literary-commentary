## Source-faithful editorial refinements

ER9 made editorial refinements in **10 English page files** while preserving the maintained Tamil meaning and the closed SC9/GR9 decisions.

1. scan **298** — tightened `இறப்பதுதான் உயிர் இயற்கை` from **“death itself would be the nature of life”** to **“death is simply the nature of life,”** and recast the next-birth sentence for natural English without changing its meaning.
2. scan **306** — clarified that the female elephant caught the **young elephant's** eye, rather than the ambiguous **“newcomer's eye.”**
3. scan **310** — reconciled **“warrior's drum”** to the controlled military-context form **“war-drum.”**
4. scan **312** — corrected the awkward **“You have come into my house as Tamil itself in bodily form”** to **“You have entered my heart as Tamil itself embodied,”** reflecting the maintained Tamil `என்னகத்தே`.
5. scan **314** — aligned `அணுப்பொழுதும்` with the established maintained-English rendering **“even for an instant”** instead of **“even for an atom of time.”**
6. scan **320** — recast **“to root itself in memory and make it stagger”** as **“to take root in the mind and make it reel.”**
7. scan **321** — reconciled **“self-made theral”** to the source/glossary sense **“naturally formed theral.”**
8. scan **323** — made the numerical phrase **“sixteen hundred-thousand ... in gold”** readable as **“1.6 million in gold.”**
9. scan **330** — replaced the unidiomatic **“accustomed to pridefully claim”** with **“given to claiming with pride.”**
10. scan **331** — replaced **“received javelins, spears and swords upon himself”** with **“bore the blows of javelins, spears and swords.”**

No quoted-verse block required presentation repair in ER9, and no other literary wording in scans **297–333** required editorial revision.
