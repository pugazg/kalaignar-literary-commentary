> —has died,
> did you too bloom,
> mullai,
> in the land of Ollaiyur?”

<div align="center">*</div>

<div align="right">

(*Purananuru* — Poem 242  
Poet: Kudavayil Keerathanar)

</div>

## Explanation of meaning:

**Aadavar-k kadandha** = defeated warriors.  
**Valvel** = powerful spear.  
**Pinrai** = after.  
**Midimai** = poverty.
