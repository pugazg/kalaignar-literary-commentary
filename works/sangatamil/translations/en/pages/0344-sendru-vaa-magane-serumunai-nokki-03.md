The young woman burst into laughter and said, “Go on, dear one!”

“Whether I return or not, for anything that may happen—give me one kiss now. Here—turn toward me,” he said.

He gave the kiss—and then loosed his arrows upon the enemy.

War! War! War! The war-drum thundered.

*

“Look! Look! Look at the valour with which the master of that green parrot leaps upon the enemy and kills!” all his comrades in the army exclaimed in wonder. That firm, pearl-like woman smiled through her eyes as she heard of victory after victory piling up for her beloved on the battlefield.

*

Fortresses were freed! The enemy horses' legs were broken! The elephant force was shattered! Spears were ground to powder! “The great drums beaten by our victorious king's forces sound like summer thunder! We hear them! We hear them!” she cried, leaping with joy and setting a new melody.

*

Warriors came: “Victory belongs to your husband!” they said. Messengers of the king came and offered congratulations. Women friends from around the house came and rejoiced as they read the long victory message. The beauty shed tears of joy.

Then—why did that man come?
To tell news of loss!

“My precious woman! My dear one—do not be shaken! Hear the news from the field, the final news,” he said.
