> “The bright fire of desire,
> born from what my eyes beheld,
> torments me to the bone.
>
> Yet it is hard for us
to go to him,
> cherish him,
> and embrace.
>
> Nor is he able
to come here
> and remove the suffering
> that has come upon me.
>
> No one set us on,
> no one releases us,
> no one comes between
> to separate and end it.
>
> Like a solitary fight
> between rubbish-heap chickens,
>
> there is no one
to end this illness
> that has come upon me,
>
> except when it reaches
> its own destruction
> and dies by itself.”

<div align="center">*</div>

<div align="right">

(*Kuruntokai* — Poem 305  
Poet: Kuppaik Kozhiyaar)

</div>

## Explanation of meaning:

**Kaama ol-eri** = the bright, shining fire called desire.  
**Muyangal** = intimate union / embracing.  
**Arungkaatchiyam** = I have become one who cannot easily be seen / reached.  
**Vandhu anjar kalaidhal** = his coming to where we are and removing the suffering.  
**Vilivaangu viliyin alladhu** = except by perishing of itself.
