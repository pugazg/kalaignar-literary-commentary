1.

> “For the young bull with warrior anklets bound upon his feet
> and a beard black as collyrium, I fear my mother,
> because my bangles slip loose and fall away;
> I am shy before the assembly to embrace his warring shoulders...”

*

(Purananuru: poem 83  
Poet: Narkannaiyar)

*

2.

> “In the cool, fragrant mountain slopes,
> where the yali, coming with a hunger for victory,
> makes the striped tiger tremble,
> strikes the mottled face of the high-browed elephant
> and tears away its white tusk,
> he too fears not to come alone, bearing a single spear;
> but I, with eyes that shed dew, afflicted by this sickness,
> cannot endure being alone.
> What shall we do, friend!...”

*

(Akananuru: poem 252  
Poet: Narkannaiyar)

## Explanation of meaning:

1. Mai anal = a beard like collyrium. Todi = bangle.
Kazhiththiduthal = slipping from the hand and falling away. Yaai = mother.
Muyangal = embracing.

2. Valampadu = powerful. Vaalvari = tiger.
Pugalvandha yali = a kind of creature called yali, coming with the desire for victory.
Otri = striking and killing.
