—stood manfully at a narrow turn in the path,  
ready to strike down the hunter coming next.

The hunter saw that fierce resolve like a riddle  
and thrilled with wonder!

When soldiers on the battlefront  
are forced to turn their backs on an enemy,

a hill-country chieftain may stand alone  
like a lion that does not lower its head

and gather fame that reaches the sky.

‘Is this pig not showing that same great valour?’  
the hunter wondered.

He praised it inwardly  
and wished that it might live well in the forest.

Not wanting to hurl his spear and kill it,  
he returned.

In the story that hunter told,  
he compared the resolve

<div align="right">of the enraged pig,</div>

which did not fear even death,  
with the resolve of our hero,

<div align="right">whose heart is heroic!</div>

I told your mother all this with enthusiasm  
and asked, ‘Who else possesses a heart of steel like your daughter's lover?’

With joy your mother embraced me to her breast,  
kissed the crown of my head,

and said fondly, ‘You are a good girl!  
You have chosen a truly capable man for my daughter.

Live long!’ she blessed me.

At that very moment the king who captured your heart arrived.

The flowers in the garland around that warrior's shoulders  
were falling as they brushed the bushes;

he comes now seeking the honey  
that wells from your lips, to rest from his weariness!

Young woman, relieve that sweet man's fatigue at once!

Will you thank me or not?  
Surely you will not take revenge  
because I told a pig-story!

This is the path by which he comes.

Neither of you close your eyes until dawn!  
I am leaving,” said the friend.

Before the heroine could move her tongue to thank her,  
she had already run away!
