# When Will He Come to Light the Lamp of Joy?

Song  
Raga: Shanmukhapriya  
Tala: Adi

(Opening)

He parted from me to gather wealth—has he
lost all grace toward me, my friend?
Before darkness covers my life,
when will he come and light the lamp of joy, my friend?

(Verse 1)

“Before the rainy season begins to show its face,
straight-bangled one, I will return,” he said,
kissing my forehead and drawing close; then
he mounted his chariot and went—why has he still not returned?

(Opening)

(Verse 2)

“We need wealth if life is to flourish,” he said,
and parted from me to conduct trade. Yet here
a woman in the pulse of youth pines for him—how strange
that this state awakens no compassion in his heart!
How strange!
