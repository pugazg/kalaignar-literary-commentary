The next day  
he came again  
to the waterfall,

seeking the life  
he had forgotten  
and left behind!

Did he find  
only his life there?

He also caught sight  
of the woman  
who was his very life!

After the previous day's experience,  
that beautiful rose

had brought for protection  
a thorn called her friend.

At first the warrior feared  
the friend standing guard beside her.

Then he calculated  
that through this clever companion

he might gain  
the maiden.

<div align="center">*</div>

He discovered  
where the friend lived

and went to her house  
before the rooster crowed.

“Why have you come?”  
the friend asked,

a little sharply.

“I have come seeking your help

so that the parents  
of your heroine,

a flower no one else has plucked,

will give her to me  
without refusing.

I wander without food or sleep,”  
he said.

“Parents' wishes come later.

First, my tender-creeper heroine  
does not want you!

It is best  
that you turn back  
along the path you came.

Find somewhere else  
to cast your net of love!” she said.

“Allow me  
to go near her just once

and ask the crescent-browed woman  
why she hates me, friend!

I am not a tiger  
that swallows deer.

Live long, friend—live!”

he pleaded.

“To tease a woman  
bathing alone

is not proper conduct.

That is the heroine's view,”  
she replied.

“I looked at her  
only as one looks

at a flower bathing  
in drops of dew.

I shall not do wrong again.

Ask her to show mercy!”  
he said.

“What are you talking  
to him about?”

the heroine cried angrily.

Her golden feet reddening  
as she walked over flowers,

that lightning-waisted figure  
went away

with the pride  
of a king's daughter!
