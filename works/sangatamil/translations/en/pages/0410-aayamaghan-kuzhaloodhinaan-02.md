She pressed her upper body upon the cut wounds
and applied warmth to them—bound in love,
he forgot the pain and blessed the moment, saying,
“This is a delight I have found nowhere else...”

The cowherd youth played his flute; that
cowherd maiden swayed and danced!

(Thogaiyara)

“Have the shoulders of a cowherd maiden
become so cheap as to be garlanded
by men who run to save
a life that can fly away like the wind,
fearing a bull's horn?”
So cried out those women—
the eyes of Tamil Nadu in that age!
She came in their line, a picture over which beauty flowed;
she said her arms yearned to embrace the warrior
who had made a bull that would not submit even to an elephant
as tame as a cat; and as he embraced her and rejoiced with her...

The cowherd youth played his flute; that
cowherd maiden swayed and danced!
