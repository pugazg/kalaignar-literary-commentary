# The Pig Praised by the Maiden

The young woman, lovely as a tender coconut spathe,  
quarrelling with the breeze as she covered her beauty,

opened her nectar-dripping mouth and urgently asked her friend,  
“What did mother say?”

Her mischievous friend flashed her beautiful eyes:

“I have the skill to turn even a hill into an umbrella!  
Does not even stone wear away when ants keep crawling over it?

Just so, I have finished my task with your mother!”

<div align="right">—she said.</div>

“How did my mother ever agree?  
Sit here! Tell me a little of that sweet news!”  
the maiden pleaded.

Her friend said:

“You owe me a thousand thanks.  
There is no reward in this land equal to what I went through!

I told her that your lover has not even a kunrimani-seed's worth of defect,  
and compared him to the deed of a pig.

At once she said, ‘What a simile, sweet as a lump of jaggery!  
There is no obstacle now to my daughter's marrying him,’

patted me on the back,  
and even stuffed a whole plateful of sweets into my mouth—your mother!”

“Are you teasing me, friend?  
You have seen my arms grow thin enough for my bangles to slip  
because I was parted from him—are you teasing me?

Instead of removing the fetter around my mother's heart  
and turning it from poison into something harmless,

you compared the male lion  
who embraced me saying we would live like anril birds

to a pig, and shattered my dreams of desire!  
How could you do this?”
