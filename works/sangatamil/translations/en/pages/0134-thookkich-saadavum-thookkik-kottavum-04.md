He bent my waist  
and wrapped me around his shoulders  
like a creeper.

“I will never leave you  
even for an instant,”

I said and clung to him.

**Friend:**

Enough, golden creeper!

Do not intoxicate me too.

Until the lover  
who has gone away seeking wealth

returns to me,

do not roast and ruin  
the heart that is holding on in patience.

Do not leave me to wither  
and then stand watching for amusement!

**Heroine:**

I became a forest peahen  
dancing in delight

and told you everything  
without hiding a thing.

Because I was flying  
sky-high with him,

I did not realise  
that you had lain awake  
all night without sleep!

We have never lived  
dividing ourselves by rank

as “heroine” and “friend.”

Is not our friendship  
wide as the sea?

So forgive me.

You asked why  
only last night

the game of pleasure  
continued until dawn.

I too was amazed  
because I had no answer.

Yet when the feast  
kept coming forcefully,

I had no heart  
to push it aside without tasting it.

I blessed the night  
that would not stop,

gave myself  
to the union,

and found delight.

Only after the sun  
rose in the east

did I understand  
the explanation

for what happened  
so unusually in the night.

Then I stood  
tormented by confusion.

**Friend:**

You keep speaking in riddles, heroine.

Why are you worried  
after daylight came?

You stood before him all night  
and matched him fully.

What did you discover now  
that makes you tremble so?

**Heroine:**

Look there—  
look at him!

He is wiping the point  
of his spear  
and sharpening it—
