The fruit segments given to me to eat
do not pass beyond my lips!

*

## Kuruntokai poems

> “Is this then love-sickness, friend?
> In the sweet-shaded punnai where the resident *anril* bird sleeps,
> where the fine water blossoms with droplets from breaking waves,
> because the lord of the gentle shore has left,
> my many-petalled, dark-lined eyes cannot sleep?”

(Poet: Nariverooth Thalaiyar  
Poem: 5)

> “Midnight has sounded; speech has ceased;
> people have settled down sweetly, free of anger;
> the wide world too sleeps;
> I alone do not sleep.”

(Poet: Padumanar  
Poem: 6)

## Explanation of meaning:

*Vadhi* = staying there. *Kuruku* = *anril* bird.  
*Udai thiraith thivalai arumbum theeneer* = a pleasant water-surface blossoming with droplets scattered by breaking waves.  
*Mellam pulamban* = the hero of the neithal seashore landscape.  
*Paadu olla* = becoming unable to sleep.  
*Nallen ratre yaamam* = midnight with the sound “nal.”  
*Sol avindhu inidhu adanginar maakkal* = the people who had gathered and spoken against her also settled into peaceful sleep.  
*Munivindru* = having let go of words of anger.  
*Or yaan mandra* = I alone.
