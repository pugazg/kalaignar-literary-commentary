...in hard Tamil.
Sweet one! My friend Porkodi!
Listen—I shall tell its meaning in simple speech!

The lord of my life who stole my heart
belongs neither to this town nor to this land. Therefore,
one group said a parrot of victory would perch upon his shoulder,
while another said victory would go to the wrestler
and not to this warrior.
Though they uttered two different views,
my heart found joy in the fact
that both groups were thinking only of him.
Certain, certain of my beloved's victory,
I ran with my anklets ringing and, my heart thrilled,
awaited the battle's end—standing hidden
behind the palmyra tree
that had grown tall in the grounds of my home,
letting every thought of mine fly toward him.
At last I saw the man who had captured me win.”

Thus,
after reciting the poem and its meaning,
Narkannai performed a penance before her friend,
asking for a boon: “Tell me a way
for this tormenting love to be fulfilled.”
Taking her hand, the friend said:

“O red-lotus blossom! Do not think the red sun you seek
is far away in the crimson sky!
The warrior who felled the brutal wrestler the day before yesterday
is staying with the aged poet Saathanthaiyar—
only three houses away from here.
Come with me and I shall show you,” she said.
