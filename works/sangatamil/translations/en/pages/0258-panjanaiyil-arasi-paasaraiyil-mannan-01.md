# The Queen on the Soft Bed; the King in the War-Camp!

The literature
created by our learned ancestors,
which has come down to us
like a crop of a thousand years,

bears witness
that our precious Tamil people
have lived from ancient times
by beautifully ordering life
as *agam* and *puram*.

There are many examples,

but Nakkirar
strung one like a garland:

the Tamil nectar
called *Nedunalvaadai*,

a pearl-song
among the *Pattuppattu* ten!

It speaks
of the valour
of Pandya Nedunchezhiyan,

and of the mind
of the straight-bangled queen
Kopperunthevi,

burning like fire
as she longs
through his long absence.

It is a work
that shows how,
in the Tamil land of that day,

the life called
*agam* and *puram*
stood almost as a counterpoint
to the division
called this world
and the next.

On the battlefield
of Thalaiyalanganam,

seven kings
miscalculated,
thinking
a lone young man
had been trapped.

Their broad shoulders
fell into dust
defeated!

After that,
Nedunchezhiyan—
who had vowed
they would live
like an inseparable pair—
