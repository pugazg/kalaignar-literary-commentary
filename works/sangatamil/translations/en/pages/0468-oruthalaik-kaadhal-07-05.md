> “Thazhai with thorny leaves like the horn of a shark,
> its broad rough base like the body of the ira-fish;
> its buds ripening like the tusk of a great elephant,
> appearing bent apart like the fine doe among deer,
> O lord of the strong sea where festival grounds spread fragrance!
> If, as the charioteer drives your long chariot with its many bells,
> you set out to go,
> know well that she,
> though you say you will return after a few days,
> will not live!”

*

(Natrinai: poem 19  
Poet: Narkannaiyar)

## Explanation of meaning:

Iravu = ira-fish. Suravu = shark. Pinar = roughness.
Maruppu = elephant tusk. Nanmaan uzhai = a fine doe.
Uravuneer = sea. Seliya seriyaayin = if you set out driving onward.
