# Why Did the Bee Come, Girl?

Like a seed kept safely  
inside a fruit,

the mother guarded  
her maiden daughter inside the house.

The girl whispered into the old woman's ear  
that she was going only

to the nearby pond.

Walking like the very image  
of modest restraint

until her mother's face disappeared,

she would then run  
to the pool fed by the mountain waterfall!

Like a warrior's sword  
waiting to kiss the shield  
that comes onto the field,

the young bull would wait there  
for the arrival of his jewel-eyed beloved.

She would cling to him  
like a garland around his shoulders.

“Secret love is dangerous.

Your mother will oppose it;  
the village gossips will slander.

If word reaches their ears,  
fierce resistance will rise.

So keep it within limits,”

her friend would interrupt them  
again and again,

a thousand times.

Would the heroine listen?

One day she told her mother  
she was going to the waterfall pool,

and then she and he became  
like two clouds joining beside the hill.

As the poet Vazhuthiyar engraved in words,

“her shoulders joined his,  
her beautiful breasts pressed close”—

they embraced until both lovely breasts were crushed together,

and shared fruit-sweet kisses  
by tens, hundreds, thousands!
