# Is There an Eyewitness?

“Fragrant, fragrant, the *mullai* blooms—yet the man who said he had married me has still not come!

On the sandy stretch, hidden among shrubs and creepers, he was in my lap; ‘Do not leave me even for a moment, my dear,’ he said, and I was in his grasp! Embracing in delight, pouring out boundless love, our two bodies joined as we lay there—he the eyelid, I the eye—we drank a feast of pleasure impossible to describe. Forgetting the thousand wonders we performed, can the hero who now scorches me in the fire of anguish go on testing me still, my friend!

Was it false that we became one life and forgot the world? Was his assurance, ‘My very life, I have married you,’ false?

‘Is there a witness to the pledge he gave? Tell me!’ you ask, my friend. The man who held me there is himself the witness. While I rested my face upon his palmyra-like, diamond-hard shoulder, a heron with legs like millet stalks stood gazing intently at the running water, wondering whether an *aarai* fish might come as food! Unless, by chance, it had looked at us, there is no other eyewitness, my friend, to the splendour of our clandestine union!”
