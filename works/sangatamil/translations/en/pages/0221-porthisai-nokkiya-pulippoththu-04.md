> —wearing a finely threaded garland,
> the warrior of an ancient martial clan
> came alone,
>
> stood at the front,
> and bore
> the terrible battle.
>
> When the sword
> in his hand
> bent out of shape,
>
> he used
> the tusk
> of the iron-bodied elephant
> he had cut down
>
> to straighten
> the twisted blade.
>
> Then he would laugh
> at the back
> of the man
> who had fled from him.”

<div align="center">*</div>

<div align="right">

(*Purananuru* — Poem 284  
Poet: Orampogiyar)

</div>

## Explanation of meaning:

**Varugathil val varuga** = come running quickly.  
**Thamiyan vandha muthilaalan** = the warrior of an ancient martial clan who came alone.  
**Arun-samam** = great battle.  
**Oru-kai irumpinaththu eyiru** = tusk of the elephant that was cut down.  
**Miraiyaaga thirindha** = bent and twisted.  
**Irindhaan** = one who fled in fear.  
**Puram nagam** = laughs on seeing the back / after the other turns away.
