# The Male Monkey That Drank Liquor!

A *kaduvan* means a male monkey;
a *mandhi* is a female monkey.
One day, beside a rocky spring,
a *kaduvan* drank illicit liquor and fell asleep.
Kapilar, passing that way,
is said to have seen him.

“A male monkey? Drinking liquor?
And stolen liquor without government permission at that?
Drag him away this instant
and put him in prison!”—
it was not an age in which a government
made such a law in fury
and then bent it according to its own wishes.

Even so, Kapilar had a desire:
he wanted to know where this *kaduvan*
had stolen the liquor.
Could he wake the monkey and simply ask him?
He thought of it, but that was impossible.
For the troublesome *kaduvan*
lay there utterly senseless,
having forgotten that a world even existed,
on a lovely flower-bed
built high in eye-catching colour
by golden blossoms falling and falling
through countless days.

On the mountain beside a murmuring waterfall,
Kapilar had seen *kaduvan* and *mandhi*
play ‘sadugudu’ among sandalwood branches
and then mate.
But today he wondered why
they had forgotten the leaping, caressing play
they usually performed at dusk.
From morning onward the male and female monkeys
had wandered through the whole forest making love—
