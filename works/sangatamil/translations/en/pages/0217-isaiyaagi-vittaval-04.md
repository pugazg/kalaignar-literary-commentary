—what could she do
without yielding?

She became
music itself!

<div align="center">*</div>

> * “In the cool,
> lovely pond
> spread thick
> with ayirai fish,
>
> those who enter
> to pluck
> the tall,
> beautiful aambal flowers
>
> stand in the water
> and still thirst for water.
>
> So too,
> lying between
> this woman's breasts,
> you tremble.
>
> Yet in the days
> when you appeared
> only like the crescent
> one worships
> and rarely sees,
>
> and were hard
> for us to meet,
>
> you endured
> great suffering.
>
> I think of it now
> and grieve.”

<div align="center">*</div>

<div align="right">

(*Kuruntokai* — Poem 178  
Poet: Nedumpalliyaththai, a woman poet)

</div>

## Explanation of meaning:

**Than pazhanam** = cool pond.  
**Kurunar** = those who pluck.  
**Neer-vettaangu** = as though suffering thirst.  
**Nadungal** = the confusion / trembling of love-longing.  
**Thozhuthu-kaan pirai** = Karttikai crescent.  
**Ariyamaagiya kaalai** = the secret-love period in which meeting was difficult.  
**Periya nonranir** = you endured great suffering.  
**Nogo yaane** = I grieve, thinking of it.
