# A Heart Cooler Than the Waters of the Blue Mountain!

In the fruit-laden grove
called *Pathitrupathu*—

a collection
of poems shaped by poets
in tens upon tens—

the thief called Time
entered
and showed his handiwork.

Two tens disappeared;

only eight tens
remained!

What do those
eighty poems say?

Life is not merely
eating and sleeping.

Life is to go
to the battlefront
where wounds are won,

and stand in glory
for victory
or a warrior's death!

Life is to say,

“To those who have nothing,
and to those good people
who are masters of Tamil—

take what you need,”

and to give
without ever saying
“There is none.”

Life is to cherish
the household

in inseparable love
with the parrot-like woman

who came as one's companion,
cooed beside one,
and clung to one's shoulder!

Thus,
speaking the greatness
of the sacred land
of the refined Tamil people,

and singing
the inexpressible valour

of the Cheras,
the Cholas,
the Pandyas,

and the many courageous
Tamil clans like them—

these works of literature
are the wealth
of our peerless land.

Among them,
one radiant pearl
is *Pathitrupathu*!
