But seeing
the flood of affection
in a monkey clan
that might ask,
“What price is beauty?”

the poet's heart melted!

<div align="center">*</div>

> “Thinking that the dark-eyed male monkey
> had met with a great calamity,
>
> the loving female,
> unable to survive widowhood,
>
> entrusted her strong young one,
> still untrained in life,
> to her kin,
>
> then leapt
> from the slopes
> of a lofty mountain
> and ended her life…”

<div align="center">*</div>

<div align="right">

(*Kuruntokai* — Poem 69  
Poet: Kadunthol Karaveeran)

</div>

## Explanation of meaning:

**Karungkan thaakkalai perum piridhu utrena** = the dark-eyed, leaping male monkey had met with death.  
**Mandhi** = female monkey.  
**Kallaa vanparazh kilaimudhal serththi** = leaving the strong young one, still untrained in how to live, with its kin.  
**Onguvarai** = high mountain.  
**Segukkum** = will end / take away life.

<div align="center">*</div>

A note by Dr. U. V. Swaminatha Iyer says that **Karaveeram** was a village in the Chola country near Thiruvarur.
