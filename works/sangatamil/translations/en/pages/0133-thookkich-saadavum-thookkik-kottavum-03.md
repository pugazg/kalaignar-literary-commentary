The human body  
is not cast from iron.

So even if sensual pleasure  
is sugarcane,

if man and woman  
taste it however they please

whenever desire rises,

both bodies will grow thin  
like splinters,

bend, and become stooped!

My grandmother  
is an expert on the body.

Every day I used to hide  
and listen

to the way of life  
she explained to others.

What I learned as truth from her  
is what I have now told you,

my closest friend, heroine!

Your man has remained beside you  
without leaving

for more than six months.

So what came over you  
only last night, friend?

Did even the rooster  
have to cry out urgently

to build a dam  
and keep the two of you apart?

**Heroine:**

He understood very well  
that it certainly was not a rooster.

“That is your friend  
changing her voice

and playing mischief,”  
he said.

Once I am caught  
inside the grasp  
of that strong warrior,

I feel we must remain joined  
like the two lines  
of a Kural couplet!

And when it is  
the section on pleasure,

after passing beyond  
virtue and wealth,

can I push him away  
and come apart?

“There is still time, my jewel,  
before the sun's rays bloom,”

he said,

and with the tip of his finger  
drew patterns

from the crown of my head  
to the soles of my feet!

When a finger touches  
the strings of a yaazh,

do they not tremble into music?

When his hand touched me,  
I flew on a tour  
through the world of pleasure!

Until then  
I lost myself

inside a joy  
I had never known!

Only after the morning sun rose  
did I understand

the reason behind  
everything that thief had done!

He caught my four eyelids  
like flower-petals,

and every flavour-filled kiss  
he gave me

was rock candy  
dripping honey!
