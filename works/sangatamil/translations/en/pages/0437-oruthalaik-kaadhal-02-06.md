## Explanation of meaning:

*Ennaikku* = to my lord.  
*Anmaiyaanum* = even if it is not. *Aadu* = victory.  
*Ponthai* = palmyra tree. *Kudamalai* = western mountain.  
*Gunakadal* = eastern sea.
