# Here Is the Sword—Where Is His Tongue?

A hut!

On one side,
sharp spears and swords
stand in rows,

flashing and gleaming
like weapons forged
to seize the world
and end fierce enmity.

There is no beauty
inside a tiger's cave—
that is nothing new!

But this warrior's house,
a dwelling-place of honour,

shows no trace
of fear,
shivering,
or lowering itself.

At the doorway,
an old woman
with toothless mouth
opened it wide,

mixed a little jaggery
with rice in a bowl,

and sent handful after handful
down into her stomach.

A warrior
came running.

“Grandmother,
a message!”
he said.

“You are gasping
like a little girl
who came dancing!

Are you a man,
younger brother?

Let there be
a pause
between one breath
and the next.

Then begin your speech,”
said that aged Tamil woman,
famous for her teasing!

“This is no time
for jokes, grandmother.

Leave aside
your usual mockery.

Your son has died
on the battlefield,”
he said—

her heart—
