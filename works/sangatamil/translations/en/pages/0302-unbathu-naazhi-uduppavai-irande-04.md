> the true use of wealth is giving.
> If we say, ‘We alone shall enjoy it,’
> many are the errors that follow.”

*

(*Purananuru*: poem 189  
Poet: Nakkiranar)

## Explanation of Meaning:

*then-kadal valagam* = the world surrounded by clear sea water.  
*orumaiyor* = one who seeks exclusive ownership.  
*thunjaan* = one who does not sleep. *kadumaa* = a swiftly running animal.  
*thuyppem enin* = if we say, “we ourselves shall enjoy it.”  
*thappuna palavē* = it gives room for many errors.  
*nāzhi* = a measure of grain.
