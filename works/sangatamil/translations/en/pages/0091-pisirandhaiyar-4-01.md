# Pisiranthaiyar 4

Those who choose to write  
a concluding statement to life

sit facing north, fast without food,  
and give up their lives.

Tamil historical literature records  
this ancient practice called *vadakkiruthal*  
as a custom.

The Chola who had ruled  
the fine Tamil land for many years

felt fog close over his heart  
when he thought of the war kindled by his sons.

He desired to die by sitting facing north  
and chose it as the path

by which the torment of his mind  
might be completely ended.

Some poets living in Tamilakam  
heard of it.

Saying that their own lives were no nectar,  
they went and sat with him.

The king had dared to leave life  
without regarding it as sweet as jaggery.

As his life slowly melted  
and his whole body weakened,

some looked at the great Kopperuncholan and asked:

“King of kings!

You say that Pisiranthaiyar,  
great teacher of fine Tamil verse,

loves you with a friendship  
that has never turned away, though he has never seen you.

Your span of life is about to end;  
why has he still not come?”

Mocking voices too were heard:

“When he left, he swore  
he would sit near you and give up life.

He said, ‘I shall return.’  
Was there no truth in that word?”

Even in a place where he had come to die,  
the king grew angry

at those who questioned  
the integrity of his friend.
