> —and showed her  
> the burning sandalwood,
>
> saying, ‘That is why  
> the bees have gathered here.’”

<div align="center">*</div>

<div align="right">

(*Natrinai* — Poem 55  
Poet: Pandiyan Peruvazhuthi)

</div>

## Explanation of meaning:

**Kaambu-thalai manandha** = where bamboo grows close together.  
**Kal-athar siruneri** = a narrow path full of rocks.  
**Urupagai** = here, the danger/enmity of the vengai.  
**Pori** = beautiful skin-mark / mottling.  
**Aagam** = chest.  
**Arukaar-paravai** = bee.  
**Kankolaaga nokki** = looking as though to kill.  
**Inaiyaiyo** = is this the cause? / what cause is this?  
**Allaandhu** = suffering.  
**Saantham** = sandalwood.  
**Nyeghizhi** = burning firewood.
