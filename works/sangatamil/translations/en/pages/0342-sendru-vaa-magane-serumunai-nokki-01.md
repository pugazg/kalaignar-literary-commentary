# Go, My Son; Toward the Battlefront!

It was a time when the Chera, Chola and Pandya kings had made the fresh sands of the Tamil land fed by the Kaveri into a battlefield, waging war to decide whose flag would fly upon the finial of the tower.

On one such field, a father leapt forward to defend the motherland and died. Hearing the news, a young woman of Tamil land became like wax fallen into fire. Her husband, who had been away in a neighbouring town, returned with eyes like flame. Through eyes pouring water, she gave him the news of the battle.

“You suffered because you heard that your father fell on the field,” he said.

“No, my love! If one warrior is lost from the army of this mullai-covered country, is it not like a tear appearing in a fine garment? I thought the enemy would now enter through it without obstruction; the tears held back within me broke their dam—that is all,” she said.

He rose like a tiger leaving its cave, like a volcano erupting against the foe. He took up the sword that would cut the enemy down. He swore an oath and lifted his radiant face.

“The enemy that surrounds us will be smashed to powder,” he said.

“I shall not return home without recovering the land!” he said.
