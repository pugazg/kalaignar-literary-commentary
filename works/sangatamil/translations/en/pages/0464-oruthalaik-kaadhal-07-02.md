Thinking, “What work have we in this world now?”
her friend quietly moved away, leaving the two alone!

And Narkannai's state here—
every nerve in her body had become a hall of music!

“Has compassion for me been born?” her eyes seemed to write and ask!
“Has tenderness flowed toward me?” her lips throbbed and yearned to ask!

“Son of a king who wears one crown upon his head!
Though I stand bearing two crowns upon my bosom,
why refuse to crown me?”
The slender-waisted woman did not ask it aloud!
It was a silent question hidden inside her smile!

Though he saw the graceful woman standing shaken,
without attempting to calm the turmoil of that sea,
he asked, “Do you understand why I have come?”

She:

“Honey is sweet! A scorpion's venom burns!
Which of these two have you come seeking
to give me?
For the farmer who looks to the sky and yearns,
is the reward only a dry wind,
or the nectar called rain that brings abundance?
Will the bent crescent grow into a full moon?
Or has utter darkness been allotted as the fate of my life?”

He:

“I may gladly ask of you, maiden,
for Tamil that is like mango juice!
I have no desire for your mango-hued body!
I do have a heart that honours your command of great Tamil!”

She:

“Many thanks for praising my talent;
but is it right to set fire to the love
of the maiden who possesses that talent?”

He:

“The exchange of two hearts is the grammar of love!”

She:

“Is that also the grammar for desire on only one side?”
