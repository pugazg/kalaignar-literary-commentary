# Moss and Love-Pallor!

Song  
Raga: Shankarabharanam  
Tala: Tisra Adi

(Opening)

“Sweet Tamil, my treasure, my life, listen;
do not, with a troubled mind, cast your eyes like swords.
I go today and return tomorrow—do not grieve in vain,”
he said. Why has he not returned for so many days?

(Sweet Tamil)

(Verse 1)

A new light would be born in my eyes if he had come;
my worries would fly away in a moment if he had brought it.
Countless pleasures would give fragrance like flowers;
they would resound like verses of an unwritten epic!

(Sweet Tamil)

(Conclusion 1)

He will call me “Love”; my heart will cool!
He will feast to his fill; dawn will bloom!
If the one equal to my life were near, he would be my medicine;
when he leaps to embrace me, I shall become his feast!

(Sweet Tamil)

(Conclusion 2)

If the ruinous illness called love-pallor spreads,
what suffering this maiden endures! Would cotton
suffer so in a fierce storm? For me,
when will worry finally leave?

(Sweet Tamil)
