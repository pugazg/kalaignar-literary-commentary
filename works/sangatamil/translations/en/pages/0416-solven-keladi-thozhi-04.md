> ...with a sidelong glance as though he would kill me,
> broke into a delighted smile—
> that rogue's son!”

*

(Kalithogai: poem 51  
Poet: Kapilar)

## Explanation of meaning:

*Sittril* = little sand house.  
*Adaichiya kothai* = flower placed in the hair.  
*Parindhu* = plucking away.  
*Sirupatti* = an unruly youth in the bull-like stage of adolescence.  
*Siragam* = a vessel for drinking water. *Vaakki* = pouring.  
*Therumarundhittu* = becoming frightened. *Neeva* = to stroke.  
*Nagaikkoottam* = giving a delighted smile.
