Kapilar the great,
carrying deep worry
in his heart,

wandered
to light the lamps
of life

for the daughters
of Pari,

the king
who had ruled the earth
with his banner
at the summit
of the mountain of fame.

At last,
he entrusted
Pari's two daughters

to Avvai,
daughter of Tamil.

Then he said:

“My work
is now complete.

Without Pari,
I cannot live
like a lone tree.”

To restrain
the unbearable sorrow
of separation,

Kapilar sat facing north
and gave up his life.

In this living world
there are base-hearted people
who do not understand
the meaning of friendship.

But the friendship
of Kapilar and Pari

stands like
a memorial pillar

rising into the sky
through which
the sun wanders!

## Explanation of meaning:

**Vethiri** = bamboo.  
**Ori paaythal** = the colour that appears when a honeycomb reaches maturity.  
**Kadandadu thaanai** = an army that kills those who stand against it.  
**Udanranir aayinum** = even if you wage war.  
**Paadinar selin** = come as singers.
