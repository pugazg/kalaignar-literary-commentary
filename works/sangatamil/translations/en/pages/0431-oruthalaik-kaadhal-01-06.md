Thus,
when Narkilli bowed his head and gave thanks
both for the garland of verse from the master of Muthamizh
and the flower-garlands of the people of Mukkaval Nadu,
a maiden there, a picture painted in artful colours,
asked her friend with eyes and a heart tossing in love:
could affection ever purchase him?
Unable to steady herself, that maiden sighed
as though climbing and descending a mountain peak—
the young woman called Narkannai!

## Explanation of meaning:

*Saaru* = festival. *Thalaikkondana* = had begun.  
*Eetru utrena* = she had reached the time of childbirth.  
*Pattamaari* = rain. *Nyaandra nyaayiru* = the time the sun sets.  
*Ninakkum* = binding. *Pozhthoondhu oosi* = a needle that threads a leather strap.
