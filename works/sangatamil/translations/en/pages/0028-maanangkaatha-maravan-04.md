Like small kendai fish floating dead  
in the water of a pond,

horses sank and stood embedded  
in the bloody mire churned by hooves!

Among the sword-bearing infantry,  
when wounds fell upon the backs of a few,

shame drove them on—  
they fell and died!

Cries of resolve, streams of blood,  
and final journeys filled the field everywhere!

It was a battlefield where none could determine  
which of the two combatants would win.

Did they seize lightning, one bolt each,  
and hurl it instead of swords?

Under the sword whirled by the Chola,  
a thousand warriors' heads rolled!

As the result of the Chera's swinging blade,  
the earth reddened like a crimson sky!

To blink at the rush of a spear  
was the definition of a coward—

both men had engraved that rule  
upon their hearts.

Yet, like an elephant trapped in mud,  
Cheraman Irumporai was trapped at last!

He displayed all his strength;  
even so, he stood defeated before the Chola!

On the battlefield called Tiruppor-puram,  
when the strong-shouldered Chera fell,

alas—the Chola king  
confined him in the prison of Kudavayil fort.

Unable to let his drawn sword flash,  
unable to die in battle and add fame to his name,

he withered in pain, every atom of his mind aching,  
that he had come to live as a prisoner.
