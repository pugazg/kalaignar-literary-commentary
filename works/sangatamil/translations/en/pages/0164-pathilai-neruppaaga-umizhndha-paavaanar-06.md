> * “‘What is done in this birth
> will benefit the next’—
>
> Ay is not
> a merchant who trades in virtue.
>
> His open-handed generosity
> belongs instead
> to the path
>
> that other noble people
> have followed before him.”

<div align="right">

(*Purananuru* — Poem 134)

</div>

## Explanation of meaning:

**Palavin maachchinai mandhi** = the monkey on the branch of a jackfruit tree.  
**Iravalar naatriya** = hung up by those who came seeking gifts.  
**Visikoodu muzhavu** = a tightly bound drum.  
**Kani-seththu** = mistaking it for a jackfruit.  
**Kurugin** = if one approaches.  
**Thalaippeyarththitta** = cast away from themselves.  
**Aangup pattandru avan kaivanmai** = his generosity does not belong to that kind of calculation.
