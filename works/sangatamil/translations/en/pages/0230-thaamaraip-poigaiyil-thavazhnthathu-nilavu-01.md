# The Moon Glided in the Lotus Pond (?)!

In the land
where the cool-watered Vaigai flows,

Perungoppendu,
jewel among women,

made as her husband
Bootha Pandiyan,
a cultured Tamil warrior
whose chest wore noble wounds
like a garland.

She became
the lamp of his household,
performed faultless domestic duties,

and shone too
with learning
that never strayed.

If the king
was the rising sun,
she was the full moon!

The name
“Bootha Pandiyan who gave Ollaiyur”
spread like a flowering creeper
and filled Tamil's garden
with fragrance.

He had as friends
Tithiyan of Podhigai hill,

Maiyalur Maavan,
who never failed,

the famed Aandhai,
Andhuvan Saathan,
Aathan Azhisi,

and Iyakkan—
a warrior like a vengai tree,
who rose in anger
and brought valour to life.

The world heard
of Bootha Pandiyan's generosity
and praised it!

Our Tamil people
have one old habit—
within ourselves,
jealousy!
Conflict!

And because of that,
on the battlefield,
a red river!

History tells us at length:
Cheras,
Cholas,
Pandya kings,
warriors,
brilliant poets—

but it does not say
that they all lived together
in unity even for one day.

Our hearts grow weary;
there is no use
lamenting that now.

Hearing Bootha Pandiyan's fame,
the Chera and Chola kings
grew jealous
and beat their war-drums!
