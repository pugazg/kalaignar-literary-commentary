> “In an old green field,
> tangled creepers were cleared,
> red soil was ploughed
> with many yoked oxen,
> manure was laid in due measure,
> and the crop grew thick.
>
> A cow entered
> the field of broad-leaved pulse.
>
> Without mercy,
> the old Kosars of the village
gouged out the eyes
> of her truthful father
> for the wrong they said he had done.
>
> Because of that cruelty,
> she would not eat from a vessel,
> would not wear clean clothing,
> and would not abandon
the form taken by her anger.
>
> She told Thithiyan,
> possessor of a valour-rich army
> and battle-trained good horses.
>
> When she saw him destroy
the sweet lives of those men,
> Anni Minjili's anger changed.”

<div align="center">*</div>

<div align="right">

(*Akananuru* — Poem 262  
Poet: Paranar)

</div>

## Explanation of meaning:

**Muthaipadu pasungkaadu** = an old green tract.  
**Arilpavar mayakki** = clearing tangled creepers.  
**Pagadu pala poonda** = yoked with many oxen.  
**Idumurai** = manuring.  
**Paasilai amarndha payaru aa pukku** = a cow entering and grazing among green pulse creepers.  
**Navaitha** = acted cruelly.  
**Vaalidhu** = clean clothing.
