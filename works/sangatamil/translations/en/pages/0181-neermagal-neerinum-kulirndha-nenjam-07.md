## Explanation of meaning:

**Ural uru** = to the extent that it soaks into the earth.  
**Pulava** = to give off the smell of flesh.  
**Venvel** = victorious spear.  
**Poraiyan** = the Chera.  
**Veppudai aaduu** = a man filled with harshness.  
**Seththanenman** = what I once thought is now no longer so.  
**KazhAI** = boatman's pole.  
**Kuttam** = depth.  
**Saanthu-varu Vaani neer** = the water of the Vaani river along which sandalwood trees float.  
**Theenthan saayalan** = one of clear, cool gentleness.

**Note:** The Vaani river is also known as the **Bhavani** river.
