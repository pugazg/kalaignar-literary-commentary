# Rule and Greatness!

Nedungkilli,
the Chola of Uraiyur,

said his great army
needed food
from the battlefield,

and ordered
the war-drum beaten

against Nalangilli,
the Chola of Poompuhar,

roaring like a tiger!

<div align="center">*</div>

“You think
the realm you already rule
is not enough.

Swollen with arrogance,
you march with an army,

making Tamil blood
spill on both sides.

Why this desire
to seize another country?

If you came
directly before me,

bowed your head,

and asked
for this land
as a gift,

I would even say,
‘Take my very life!’

Why abandon
such an easy path,

think of us
as mice or rabbits,

despise us,

and stand against us
in battle?

I shall say one thing
for Nedungkilli to understand.

Listen!

<div align="right">Before</div>

the point
of my cruel sword,

playing games
is not good.

Tell him that!”

Thus spoke Nalangilli.

Nedungkilli heard it,
laughed,

and said,
“What a fine piece
of humour!”

Gathering
all the strength
he possessed,

he came roaring
with his army
to meet the enemy.

The Poompuhar Chola,
who did not tremble
at the sight of war,

stood upright,
his garlanded chest
held high.
