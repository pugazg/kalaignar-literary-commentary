If the mountain alone  
were always to own it,

what value  
would there be  
in its fragrance, mother?

Pearls are born  
in the sea,

but their fresh radiance  
shines on the bodies  
of those who wear them.

If the deep sea alone  
were to own them,

what greatness  
would there be

either for the sea  
or for the pearl?

Music is born  
in the yaazh,

but delight rises  
only when it is played.

If music belonged  
only to the yaazh,

what use  
would that bring  
to the yaazh itself?

If a daughter belonged  
only to her parents,

learned woman,  
understand one thing.

Should your daughter remain

like sandalwood  
never applied,

like a pearl  
never adorned,

like a yaazh  
never played?

Therefore—

let the chaste maiden live  
with the lover

whose mind has become one  
with hers.

Sound out  
a blessing from your heart

and return  
this very moment!”

The nurse heard the truthful words  
of the travellers,

and her motherly heart  
became very clear.

“May the household life  
chosen by that beautiful girl

flourish  
like flowers in a grove,”

she blessed.

Then she thanked respectfully  
the elders travelling  
through the wasteland,

bowed,  
and went away.

See now how the poet Perunkadungo  
gives this episode

in fruit-juice Tamil  
in *Paalai Kali*:
