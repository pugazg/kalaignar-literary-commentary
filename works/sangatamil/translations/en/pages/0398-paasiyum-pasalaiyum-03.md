(Thogaiyara)

When thirst rises and I reach with my hands
to scoop water from the well and drink,
the moss swiftly parts and moves away; then
it quickly gathers together again!
So too, love-pallor leaves when he is near!
The moment he parts even a little, it spreads and embraces the body!

(Sweet Tamil)

*

> “Like moss gathered at the bathing-place
> of a village drinking-well is love-pallor—
> whenever the lover touches, it withdraws;
> whenever he lets go, it spreads.”

*

(Kuruntokai: poem 399  
Poet: Paranar)

## Explanation of meaning:

*Thoduvuzhith thoduvuzhi* = each time he touches.  
*Viduvuzhi viduvuzhi* = each time he parts.  
*Paasi atru* = like moss.
