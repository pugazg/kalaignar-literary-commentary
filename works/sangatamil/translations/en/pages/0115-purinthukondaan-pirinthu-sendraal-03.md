> ‘Move away!
>
> When a calf has joined its herd,  
> its mother rushes in anger.
>
> You fierce-eyed one, take care!
>
> Let your mother come if she will;  
> let others come too.
>
> Let even your father come here.  
> I shall not weaken,
>
> if only I may receive  
> your grace and favour.’
>
> ‘You do not heed even what I tell you.
>
> Thinking of me,  
> you stand like a bull
>
> that lowers its head  
> under a pounding downpour,
>
> answering every word  
> and bewildering me!
>
> When we go with the milking vessel  
> to the grazing ground,
>
> will you come there too—  
> shameless one?’”

<div align="right">

(*Kalithogai* — Poem 116  
*Mullai Kali*  
Poet: Cholan Nalluruthiran)

</div>

## Explanation of meaning:

**Paangu** = nearby. **Paattangkaal** = in the garden.  
**Mutraai** = do not block. **Thodeeiya** = to touch.  
**Thumiththu** = to spoil / disrupt. **Mandum** = rushes swiftly.  
**Kadu** = very. **Vayam** = strength. **Naagu** = female buffalo.  
**Kathaeetru aa** = an angry mother cow.  
**Kanaippeyal eru** = a bull that stands even when heavy rain pours.
