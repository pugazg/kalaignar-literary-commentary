> —for he possessed
> nothing greater
> than himself to give.
>
> I have come
> overflowing
> with victorious joy,
>
> after seeing
> your elder brother,
> firm in courage
> that never turns its back.”

<div align="center">*</div>

<div align="right">

(*Purananuru* — Poem 165  
Poet: Perunthalai Sathanar)

</div>

## Explanation of meaning:

**Parisilan** = a poet who receives gifts.  
**Aadumali uvagai** = triumphant joy.  
**Odaap-pootkai nin kizhamaiyon** = your elder brother, firm in courage that does not show its back.
