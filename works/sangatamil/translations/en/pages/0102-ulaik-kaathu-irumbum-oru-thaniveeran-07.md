## Explanation of meaning:

**Pulavarai irandha** = passing beyond the limit of the knowledge of those who sing.  
**Nilavarai** = the depth of the earth.  
**Purisai** = fort wall.  
**Nyaayil** = the arrow-emplacement at the top of the fort.  
**Milai** = guard-forest.  
**Kurumbu** = a small fort.  
**Aadukola** = to win victory.
