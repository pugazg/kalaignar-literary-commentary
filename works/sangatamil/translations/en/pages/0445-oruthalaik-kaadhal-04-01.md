# One-Sided Love

## 4

People of Aamur, rise roaring for battle!
Let the fortress drums of Mukkaval country thunder!

Against the enemy who plants a foot upon our soil beating the tongue-drum,
let us beat the war-drum and reduce them to a mound of dust!

We took form as embryos in a woman's womb;
we shall take the form of graves in this earth's womb!

A fulfilled life does not mean living a hundred years!
Even if one perishes young, if it is for a purpose,
for people of honour there is no life
more fulfilled than that!

A jewelled crown, a gem-studded throne, a couch and caresses,
hundreds waiting to serve, even honey standing with folded hands
and pleading—

all these are easy to obtain in this world!
By flattering, bending, exalting and praising,

calling one who knows no refinement an incomparable lord,
and one who has never learned gentle speech a teacher of the good path;

calling one who has forgotten culture a world-renowned sage,
and one bent on vengeance a broad-hearted nobleman;

calling one who values others lower than a dog
one far greater even than a mother;

calling an unlearned fool a learned great man,
and a wicked scoundrel a good man of the earth;

by writing and speaking of virtues that do not exist,
everything is easy to obtain in this world! But—
