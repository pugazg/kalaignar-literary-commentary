2.
> “At the frontier of the Vadugar, wearing kullai garlands,
> beyond Katti's good land of many spears........”

(Kuruntokai: poem 11  
Poet: Mamulanar)

3.
> “Of the young vengai tree,
> the many fine grains of pollen shining with radiant light,
> with dark eyes, her breasts blossomed........”

(Purananuru: poem 352  
Poet: Paranar)

## Explanation of meaning

**Ikandhanavaayinum** = even though it is beyond the limits, far away.  
**Pagaiyar ombinar uraiyum koozhkezhu kurumbu** = a food-rich stronghold where enemies dwell while guarding the cattle.  
**Kuvai imil vidaiya vetru aa oyyum** = seizing the enemy's cattle together with bulls having massed humps.  
**Surunai** = an ornament/fitting.  
**Kanikazh** = the shaft of a ghee-rich spear.  
**Ezhaa Paanan** = Paanan who does not turn his back.  
**Naagila vengai** = a young vengai tree blooming in clusters.  
**Nunpal sunangu** = many fine grains of flower pollen.
