## Explanation of meaning:

**Nugam** = yoke.  
**Eendiya** = rich in wealth.  
**Unakki** = drying.  
**Indhanam** = firewood.  
**Paadu** = falling.  
**Thaalam** = eating vessel / plate.  
**Parivu** = distress.  
**Peeral** = tear in a sari.

**Note:** This poem is said to be one that was left out of the collected anthologies of Sangam literature, and that Tamil scholar Devaneya Pavanar searched for and recovered it. Dr. Tamilkudimagan, Principal of Madurai Yadava College, gave this poem to me and asked me to write *Sangatamil*. My thanks to him. — M.K.
