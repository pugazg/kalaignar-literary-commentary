—and praise
with wonder
the greatness
of that choice!

On a branch
of a tall-grown tree,

a pair of monkeys,
male and female,

sat joyfully,
their feelings mingled
in united love.

Their young baby,
born to them,
slept there
in its mother's lap.

The father monkey
gave his mate a kiss,

then leapt
toward another branch.

Its course slipped;
it lost balance,

and fell
with a thud
to the ground!

The mother monkey
rushed swiftly,
baby in her arms,

to where
her mate had fallen.

The male,
fallen from the branch,
had struck the mountainside
and split his skull,

and lay sunk
in death's lap.

The female monkey's heart
became blazing fire.

Unable to imagine
living even a moment
apart from the male
she had never left
even in dreams,

she poured tears,
wailed,
trembled,

and struck
her own face
with her hands!

“My child
has not yet learned
how to live
in the forest.

But what of that?
My kin and relatives
will teach it.

I shall entrust it
to them now.”

She ran to them,
asked:

“Be a support
to my child,”

and gave the little one
a kiss.

Then—

unable to desire
a life of anguish
separated from the mate
who had taken her as wife,

built their bridal bed
upon the branch,

and placed a child
in her arms,

that animal decided
to join her companion.

She swiftly climbed
to the top
of a high mountain,

fell from it
until her form
could no longer be known,

and gave up her life!

There are human beings
overflowing with beauty
who do not possess
even the shadow of love.
