—she will run here at once,

and like a cow charging in fury  
at those who surround its calf in the street,

she will drive you away!” she said.

He laughed.

“Let your mother come!  
Let this whole village come!

Let even your father,  
who rules the village,

come with a drawn sword!

I need fear no one  
if only you say

that I am the charioteer  
of this little chariot of beauty!”

She shook her body,  
twisted her face,

made a great show of resistance  
and pretended to speak harshly:

“Sir, even when heavy rain pours,  
you have become like a bull

that stands still  
without even noticing!

You keep answering every word  
with another word

and have shown me clearly  
what lies in your heart!”

Then she mocked him:

“You have lost even  
a sesame-seed's worth of shame!”

Her reply snapped and crackled—

yet in words soft as silk  
she asked one thing:

“Tomorrow I am going  
with the milking vessel

to the place where the cattle graze.

Young bull—  
will you come there too?”

That was her question.

He understood her heart.

Then they parted,  
both smiling.
