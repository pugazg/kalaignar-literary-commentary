(Conclusion)

Coming toddling step by step,
making mischief and delighting in affection,
stretching both little hands, putting and touching,
spinning dizzily, clinging and holding,
biting with the lips, pulling and nibbling,
rummaging and grasping with young fingers,
scattering ghee-rich rice all along,
clapping little hands and laughing with a gurgle,
playing in my lap and loosening my hair,
burying the face in a pomegranate fruit—
until a child can blossom in my womb
and call this woman “Mother,”
there is no peace in my heart;
there is no way to dance in joy!

*

> “Though they create many kinds of wealth and eat with many,
> though they are possessors of great riches,
> if, amid it all, little ones do not toddle,
> stretch their small hands, put, touch, bite and rummage,
> scattering ghee-rich rice over their bodies,
> for those without children who bring such bewildering delight,
> the days they live lack that essential fruit.”

*

(Purananuru: poem 188  
Poet: Pandiyan Arivudai Nambi)

## Explanation of meaning:

*Padaippu* = wealth. *Padaiththu* = creating.  
*Udaiperum selvar* = those possessing abundant wealth.  
*Idaipada* = in between. *Thuzhandhum* = rummaging.  
*Adisil* = rice. *Vidhirththal* = scattering.  
*Payakkurai* = the indispensable thing called joy.
