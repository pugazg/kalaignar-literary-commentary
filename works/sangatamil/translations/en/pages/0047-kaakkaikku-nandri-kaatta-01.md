# Showing Gratitude to the Crow...

Because the man who had gone away to join the war  
had not returned for her to rest upon his shoulder,

the maiden, like a jackfruit ripened at the root,  
filled her eyes with tears and suffered!

Her friend tried to remove her sorrow  
and comfort the heroine. She could not!

You know, do you not, the belief in our land  
that if a crow calls while one sits at home, a guest will come?

Fortunately, just such a crow cried there!

“Put away your worry, dear! Because the crow has called,  
the young bull in his prime will come bounding back  
to taste your coral lips,”

<div align="right">said</div>

the friend.  
The heroine's grief eased a little!

When the returning lover embraced her body,  
the two drank sweetness like sugarcane!

“You lived beside the heroine through all the days I was away  
and helped ease her great suffering, friend!

Live long! Live long!” said the hero.

to the heroine who was tormented by thirst!
