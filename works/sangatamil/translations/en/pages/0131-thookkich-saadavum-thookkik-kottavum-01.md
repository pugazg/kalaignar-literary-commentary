# It Drips Like Honey; It Stings Like a Scorpion!

**Friend:**

Girl, what was that sound of bangles  
all night until dawn?

How many did he give you—  
a hundred kisses? A thousand?

Was the torment I endured,  
unable to close my eyes, only a little?

What thunder you raised—  
was that really just a bed?

Is your house so poor  
that you cannot buy a cot  
that makes no noise?

Tell me the truth, heroine.

Do you bear some grudge against me?

Did you not know  
that another woman lay on the veranda?

Did you not understand  
that she is an unmarried young woman?

As though this world  
would last only one day,

you completed an entire festival of pleasure  
in a single night.

Long live! Long live!

Still, my life-like maiden,  
will you listen to one thing?

In the happiness  
of last night's feast,

will you spare  
a little ear for me too?

Because a mango is sweet,  
can one uproot the whole tree?

Because a flower smells sweet,  
can one tear away the creeper with it?

Wise people scoop water, drink,  
and quench their thirst.

Do they plunge into a river flood,  
sink beneath it, and drift away?

Do not fling at me the words,  
“What do you know about pleasure?”

Think ahead instead  
about the suffering that rises

when anything exceeds its measure.

Take it this way:

your friend speaks only  
for the good of you both.
