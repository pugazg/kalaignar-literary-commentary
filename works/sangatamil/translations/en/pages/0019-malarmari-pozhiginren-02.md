> urithu-naaru avizh-thoththu undhoozh, koovilam,  
> fire-bright eruzham, sulli, kooviram,  
> vadavanam, vaagai, sky-flowered kudasam,  
> eruvai, seruvilai, jewel-flowered karuvilai,  
> payini, vaani, many-clustered kuravam,  
> pasumpidi, vagulam, many-clustered kaayaa,  
> wide-flowered aavirai, veral, sooral,  
> kureei-poolai, small fragrant kanni,  
> kurugilai, marudham, broad-flowered kongam,  
> pongam, thilagam, honey-fragrant paathiri,  
> serundhi, athiral, great cool shanbagam,  
> karandhai, kulavi, strongly fragrant kalimaa,  
> thillai, paalai, rock-climbing mullai,  
> kullai, pidavam, small maa-rodam,  
> vaazhai, valli, long-fragrant neydhal,  
> thaazhai, thalavam, thorn-stemmed thaamarai,  
> nyaazhal, mauval, fragrant cool kogudi,  
> sedal, semmal, small red kurali,  
> kodal, kaidhai, mature-fragrant vazhai,  
> kaanchi, jewel-clustered intoxicating neydhal,  
> paangar, maraam, many-flowered thanakkam,  
> eengai, ilavam, hanging-clustered kondrai,  
> arumbu, stately aathi, long-vined avarai,  
> paganrai, palaasam, many-flowered pindi,  
> vanji, pithigam, sindhuvaaram,  
> thumbai, thuzhaai, flame-flowered thondri,  
> nandhi, naravam, fragrant punnaagam,  
> paaram, peeram, green kurukkathi,  
> aaram, kaazhvai, dense fragrant punnai,  
> narandham, naagam, midnight-naari,  
> great dark kurundham, vengai, and others…”

<div align="center">*</div>

<div align="right">

(*Pattuppattu* — *Kurinjippattu*  
lines 61 through 95  
Poet: Kapilar)

</div>

In the *Kurinjippattu* of the *Pattuppattu*,  
the very manner in which the flowers are listed

is arranged like a garland strung by maidens!  
Is it easy to tell the greatness of this Sangam Tamil?
