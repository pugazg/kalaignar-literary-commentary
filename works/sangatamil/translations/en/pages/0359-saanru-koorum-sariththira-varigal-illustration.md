—functioned well through the *kudavolai* method and performed good public work. Poet Maruthan Ilanaganar unfolds the dignity of people's rule, and we can savour its beauty in a poem of the *akam* tradition.

The sorrow of separation;
a young woman's lament;
public gossip and reproach;
riding the *madal*;
love-sickness spreading pallor;

the hero who has gone to the courtesan's house pleading with the bard and sending him as messenger to cool his wife's anger;
telling the friend of the anguish of love;
telling the loving confidante the story of a dream that broke off halfway;
lovers swelling their tears into a sea;
lying without eating and without sleeping;
yearning at the night tryst and saying,
“Why has he still not come?”—
