# One-Sided Love

## 10

Kaavarp Penn—the mother who had raised him
with even greater tenderness than the mother who bore him for ten months—
was fluttering and trembling like a bird inside a cage
in the enemy war-camp.
Unaware of this, her beloved son Narkilli
entered the gold-roofed mansion to see Aiyai.
The moment he saw Narkannai there,
he stopped short in shock!

“Brother, welcome!” cried the good Aiyai.
“See the queen of poetry
who regards you as her very eye and life!”

“King Katti, who rules the land,
has laid siege so that he may take you as his bride!
With their hearts wounded,
all the people of Uraiyur are setting out for war.
Tell Narkannai to write a song
for the soldiers as a battle-tune by tomorrow morning!”

At those words Narkannai fell at Killi's feet.

“Have you decided that my life may turn to dust?
Was I born a woman for this?” she asked,
shedding tears.

“I came to see my sister.
Must I suffer here too
by hearing this maiden's tale of infatuation?
Aiyai, I am leaving.
At daybreak tomorrow I go to the battle-front!
