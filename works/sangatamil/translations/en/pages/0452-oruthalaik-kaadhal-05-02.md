“Whether to forget, or to give up life—we can think about that again.
First, let us leave aside what is wrong and think only of what is good!
You keep thinking, every moment, of Killi battling the army of the ignoble foes,
and pour tears enough to plough furrows down your cheeks!
You tremble that your mother is stopping you;
do not think she is breaking your heart!
All the blocks placed before temple chariots rushing along—
are they meant to stop the chariot from moving? No, no!
They are there so it may move properly through the street!
The pulling and loosening of the reins of a racing horse
are meant to make the journey go rightly,
not to make it collapse and lie still somewhere!

Think it through, beautiful maiden!
Even when your mother grows heated, angry and scolds,
she warns you only so that your love will not become a waning moon!

If your love grows like the waxing moon
and pours a rain of nectar as a cool full moon,
woman of a noble house, would your mother's heart
fail to bless you and become
iron dug out of the earth?

When, during pregnancy, you troubled her
and made her tongue bitter,
that blessed mother drove away the bitterness with sour tastes
and performed her penance only to see your smile!
Even when you turned her beautiful belly, like a banyan leaf,
into the shape of a great trough and left it distorted,
she lived only to see your form!
