—a *puram* poem blossomed!  
And forever it will shine as a new poem!

<div align="center">*</div>

> * “Rice is not life; water too  
> is not life.  
> The wide-flowering world lives by the life of the king.  
> Therefore, to know ‘I am their life’  
> is the duty of the king who commands a spear-rich army.”

<div align="center">*</div>

<div align="right">

(*Purananuru* — Poem 186  
Poet: Mosikeeranar)

</div>

## Explanation of meaning:

**Mannan uyirthe** = the polity has the king as its life.  
**Malar-thalai ulagam** = the broad-spread country.  
**Vel-migu thaanai** = an army rich in spear-forces.  
**Manpadhai** = the place where people live.
