# One-Sided Love

## 6

Having cut clean through the long tail of the formidable enemy,
and gathered victory in a battle fought for two days,
with the soil of Mukkaval country trembling to the drums
and trumpet-calls ringing through all eight directions,
the people of Aamur welcomed him with blessings:
“Thiththan's son is returning!”

There was no sand upon the streets—only flowers!
There were no clouds across the sky—only flags!
Everyone who had remained inside their houses
ran out like rainwater fallen in a forest
to see the man who had won victory for the land!
Would Narkannai, the parrot, alone remain inside the cage?

She came running, asking, “Who won?”
“Who else could it be?” cried Porkodi, embracing her
and whirling around and around!
But that beauty staggered,
unable to bear the weight called joy!

Keeping a majestic stride to the beat of the great drum,
upon a white horse advancing proudly,
Perunarkilli sat like a portrait of valour
that no brush had painted!

Because he had entered the field and fought without rest,
it seemed he had had no time to shave his face.
His black hair had grown a little and looked like a short beard;
even that poured a new intoxication into Narkannai's eyes
and made every vein and sinew play the music of love!
