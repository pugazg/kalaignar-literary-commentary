This is a casket
of heroic history
praising the prowess
of the Chera kings!

Even smelled from afar,
it is a screw-pine
fragrant with pure Tamil!

Within it,

the flower-cluster
called the Ninth Ten

was woven
by the resounding-drum poet-king
of rising fame,
Perungunrur Kizhar.

In its lofty fragrance,
let us feel
the spirit of living Tamil!

To Kuttuvan Irumporai
and his queen,
Kopperunthevi
Andhuvan Chellai,

a lamp of limitless fame
was born
as their son!

They named him
Ilanjeral Irumporai

and rejoiced
in delight!

He—

carried strength
upon his shoulders,

truth
within his heart,

and the banner
of ancient Vanji
in his hand,

and fulfilled
many vows of vengeance!

He defeated
the mountain-land
of Vichchikkon.

Then,
standing beneath
the bow-banner,

he defeated
Pazhaiyan Maran
and the Chola,

like a tiger-cub,
like a lion-bull,
upon the battlefield!

The Chera roared
like a storm wind.

He played
with the heads
of enemies

as though they were flower-balls,
using both hands!

Seeing the firmness
of that heart,

Perungunrur Kizhar
marvelled greatly

and met him
in a military camp.

He told the king
of the suffering
of his spotless-gold wife,

whose heart was deeply pained
by separation from him.

“Wearing iruvatchi
and champak flowers,

before the maidens
who conduct
their splendid rule,

she suffers
like a deer
without sheltering shade.

Like a peahen
in its season,
she wastes away.

The pallor
that spreads
across her body

is hidden;
on her face
she shines

<div align="right">like</div>
<div align="right">your</div>

radiant golden light—
