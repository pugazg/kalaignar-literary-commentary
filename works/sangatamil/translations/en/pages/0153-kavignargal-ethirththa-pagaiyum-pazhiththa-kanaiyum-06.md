## Explanation of meaning:

**Setranru aayinum** = even by holding anger in the mind.  
**Seyirthanru aayinum** = even by standing openly and issuing a battle-cry.  
**Utranru aayinum** = even by closing in, grappling hand-to-hand, and touching the body.  
**Uyvu anru** = there is no way to escape.  
**Polandhaar** = golden garland.  
**Mandamar** = an army that has gathered in force for war.  
**Kootru** = death.
