# I Shower a Rain of Flowers!

The Tamil sung by the Sangam poets—  
for a hundred weeks in the magazine *Kungumam*,

I set out to write it in an accessible style  
and completed the task that lay within my power.

When one dives into the sea and gathers pearls,  
when one cuts into mineral wealth and heaps up gold,

the heart leaps with delight; just so—  
this Kalaignar's heart too truly leaps!

Even treasure in a treasury diminishes as one scoops and scoops;  
the imagination of the great Sangam poets, however, fills like a spring the more one digs!

The great poets of old gathered into song  
Tamil valour, love, culture,

and the ways of life that honour them;  
thus they made even people of other lands praise us!

I salute those eminent elders  
who live on as palm leaves and letters;

and I shall pluck all the flowers  
that poet Kapilar found in forests and mountain groves,

and marked out in the *Kurinjippattu*,  
and shower them like rain upon the great Tamil records that captured my heart!

Here—behold the flowers Kapilar shows us:
