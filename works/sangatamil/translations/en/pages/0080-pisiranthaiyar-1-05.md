## Explanation of meaning:

**Kaaynel** = ripened paddy.  
**Maa** = a unit of land measure. **Seru** = cultivated field.  
**Thamiththu** = alone. **Nandhum** = flourishes.  
**Melliyan kizhavanaagi** = the king becoming weak in wisdom.  
**Varisai ariyaak kall-en suttram** = a noisy circle of attendants who, without knowing what is proper, merely repeat whatever the king desires.  
**Parivu-thaba** = with affection destroyed / without compassion.  
**Pindam nachchin** = if wealth is desired greedily. **Irai** = tax.
