# One-Sided Love

## 7

As her eyelids slowly opened and her dark pupils blossomed,
Narkannai realized that she was lying on the bed in her home!
Like sunlight hidden inside a mass of clouds,
she sent her gaze out from the depths of sorrow,
looked closely at Porkodi bending beside her,
and asked, “Where are my mother and father?”

“Your mother leapt about as though her head had split,
scolding your father: ‘You have put a price on our honour!’
She declared that not even a moment more should be spent in this town,
and, boiling with anger, left for Poompuhar!
Your father, crushed and ashamed by the humiliation here,
followed your mother,
to mend the heart that had broken apart.”

Hearing Porkodi's news, Narkannai
raised her bow-like brows and said,
“Why should I alone go on living for nothing?”

“To keep alive the Tamil language dearer than life,
people like you must live for a thousand years!”
At that voice the graceful maiden started!
When she saw that the owner of the voice was Narkilli,
her beautiful eyelids fluttered,
wondering whether the sight before her was real.
“What world am I in?” she asked,
unable to trust her own eyes!
