—accepted as his queen
a beautiful jewel-dove.

Though the noble wounds
made in his youth
by the spears and swords
of the seven-army battlefront
had healed
and become scars,

his beautiful queen
thought they shone
upon the Pandya's chest
like golden medals,

and without counting them
gave them kisses
every day!

After marriage,
time gave them no room
to remain upon the bridal bed,

holding close
without loosening their embrace
through the whole night,

never leaving
the heart-stealing woman
even for a moment.

The calls
of battlefields
were a great torment!

Even after
the battle of Thalaiyalanganam,
he led armies
and conquered
Mizhalai Kootram,
ruled by the king
Vel Evvi.

He seized
the crown
of the sword-warrior Muthuveilir
and took
their Muthu Kootram as well.

He destroyed
the army
of the great warrior-king
Cheraman Yanaikkat-sey Irumporai
and cast him
into prison!

Thus,
as he piled
victory garlands
mountain upon mountain

and marched out
with armies
roaring wave upon wave,

the Pandya queen trembled
like a bird
surrounded by clusters
of ripe fruit
yet unable to eat.

Unable to make
the hours of day and night pass,
she poured tears!

When the north wind blows,
does not cold air strike?

Even without garments,
a woman may wrap herself
in the body of her man,

and a man
in the body of his woman,

and thus defeat
that cold wind—
