The old man
then reshaped
the Chola child's name:

“Let Thirumavalavan
become Karikal Peruvalathan!”

The plotting kinsmen
heard that the heir
of the old line
was growing.

Hiding fire
in their eyes

and mixing honey
into their speech,

they came
and stood before him

like sages
who grant boons,

speaking lies
from the heart.

“This royal elephant
should not remain
hidden here
like a fox

under your care,
grandfather,

for so many days.

We speak
with the grace
of the Supreme.

There is no need
for fear.

<div align="right">None.</div>

No army
is needed.

Place faith in us.

Send our trusted
Karikal Valavan
with us.

The day
when he rules the country

is not far away!”

Thus the poisonous-hearted
kinsmen acted
with great skill.

The disguise
of holy men

can make
even warriors
lose their discernment!

Unable to perceive
the disastrous plan

hidden
inside their poisonous speech,

the very old man

<div align="right">therefore</div>

gave permission
and sent him away
with blessings.

Karikalan
touched the old man's feet,

bowed,

shed tears
like flowers,

received a kiss,
and took leave.

Then,
head held high,

sword in hand,

he followed
those saffron-robed villains
disguised as sages.

All along the road
they told stories
about God,

gave many kinds
of moral instruction,

and drew the Chola heir
Karikalan

under the spell
of their words.

He fell
into their net.

“We are all tired.

Karikal Valava,
let us rest
our eyes for a while,”
they said.

He lay down
on a raised patch
of field

and fell asleep.

In one moment,
they drugged him
into unconsciousness
and carried him away.

When the stupefying effect
of the herb
wore off,

he looked around
in confusion:

“Where are the sages?”
