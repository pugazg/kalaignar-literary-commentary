He was standing at the front of the house, my friend!—and
he caught the hand I stretched out, my friend!
I too began to tremble, my friend;
what would happen if four people saw us, my friend!

(I'll tell you—listen, my friend...)

(Thogaiyara)

Without knowing why, I cried out, “Mother!” my friend!
She came at once, within half a moment, my friend!
Before then I managed the situation, my friend!
“He has hiccups, Mother,” I said, my friend!
“Oh, poor thing!” Mother too ran over, my friend,
and began stroking his back, my friend!

(I'll tell you—listen, my friend...)

(Conclusion)

Because Mother stood behind him, my friend,
he understood the love I held within, my friend!
With his eyes he spoke of marriage, my friend;
that rogue's son broke into a delighted smile, my friend!
Long may he live; live long!
