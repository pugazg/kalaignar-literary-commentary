Poet: Thondaiman Ilanthiraiyan)

## Explanation of meaning:

**Kaal-paar** = the cart-wheel and its load.  
**Saagaadu** = cart.  
**Ugaippon maanin** = if the driver is competent / proper.  
**Aaru** = the road / way.  
**Uyththal thetraan** = one who does not know how to drive.  
**Pagai-kkoozh allal** = deep hostile mire.  
**Vaikal** = every day.  
**Thalai-thalai** = increasingly / one after another.
