“Tell the Chola who has gone to sit facing north:  
Pisiranthaiyar will soon come  
to sit beside him!”

Having said this, the great poet  
set out for the Pandya country,

tears brimming in both eyes  
like dew falling from a leaf,

his heart trembling,

to take leave of his beloved wife, children,  
grandson and granddaughter.

<div align="center">*</div>

> * “If you ask,  
> ‘How have so many years passed  
> without grey hair appearing?’
>
> My worthy wife and my children are rich in wisdom;  
> my younger attendants are as I would wish them to be.
>
> My king does no wrong and protects us.
>
> Above all, in the town where I live  
> there are many mature, disciplined, principled wise men.”

<div align="center">*</div>

<div align="right">

(*Purananuru* — Poem 191  
Poet: Pisiranthaiyar)

</div>

## Explanation of meaning:

**Yaandu** = year, here referring to age.  
**Maanda** = possessing greatness.  
**Nirambinar** = filled with wisdom.  
**Ilaiyar** = attendants.  
**Adhan-thalai** = above even that.
