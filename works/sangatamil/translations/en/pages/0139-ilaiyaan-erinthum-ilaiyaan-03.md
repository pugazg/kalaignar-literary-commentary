—the Pandya, pure gold of the highest fineness,  
who possessed the distinction

of composing poetry  
in pictorial Tamil,

entered the battlefield!

Calling the Pandya  
young in age,

the two crowned kings  
of Chera and Chola,

joined by five powerful Velir chiefs,

sent a palm-leaf challenge  
and leapt into war.

The armies  
of five Velir rulers

and the armies  
of the two kings

whose banners bore  
bow and tiger

joined together  
and attacked.

Did the young Pandya tremble,  
thinking,

“How can I oppose seven?”

Not at all!

“Submit and stand down!

Otherwise  
your lives will be lost!”

said the message.

He read it  
and laughed aloud.

“Battle is not a tradition  
for hiding,” he said.

“They mock me  
because I am young.

I shall show them this very moment  
what their age and armies

are going to suffer  
before me!

You who brought the palm leaf—

run at once  
to your seven kings!

Tell them:

this young Chezhiyan  
has taken the heroic spear  
into his hand.

That is my answer!

Let the southern Pandya army  
prepare to enter

the battlefield  
of Thalaiyalanganam!”

At that moment  
Mangudi Marudhanar intervened:

“Sprout of the Pandya royal line!

Do not think  
this poet opposes you.

When I look  
at the strength of the enemy,

I fear the final conclusion  
will be that we have lost.

Because it is my duty  
to warn you,

I say it may still be better  
to think again  
and avoid the war.”

heard Mangudi Marudhanar,

walked toward him, and said:
