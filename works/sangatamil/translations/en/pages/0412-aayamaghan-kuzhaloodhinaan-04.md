(Kalithogai: Mullai Kali  
From poems 101, 103 and 106  
Poet: Nalluthiranar)

## Explanation of meaning:

*Kadaa kaliru* = a rutting male elephant.  
*Kannanjaa* = not trembling in fear. *Padaagai* = victory banner.  
*Kollerruk kodu* = the horn of a bull that comes to kill.  
*Vali* = wind. *Nalivaai maruppu* = closely set horns.  
*Thoythal* = embracing.  
*Muyangip pothivem* = we shall clasp and embrace.
