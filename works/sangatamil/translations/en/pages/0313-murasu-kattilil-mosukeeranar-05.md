> you spared the sword-edge that could have split me in two.
> Even that alone would suffice to show your full knowledge of good Tamil;
> yet you did not stop there. You came near,
> raised your mighty, strength-filled shoulder,
> and fanned me with cooling air until the wide place grew fragrant.
> Was it because you had heard clearly
> that, except for those who possess fame here,
> there is no dwelling in the exalted world beyond?
> O victorious lord—was that why you did this here?”

*

(*Purananuru*: poem 50  
Poet: Mosukeeranar)

## Explanation of Meaning:

*visitha* = tightly bound. *vaarpuruv alpin* = having a strapped band.  
*maipadumarungul* = the firm part of the drum made of dark wood.  
*manjnai* = peacock. *manni vaaraa alavai* = before returning from bathing.  
*menpoom sekkai* = cot of soft flowers. *theru* = anger.  
*irupaarpadukkum* = to split into two parts. *mathanudai* = possessed of strength.  
*thannena veesiyoy* = you fanned coolly with a *chamaram*.  
*valampadu kurusil* = O victorious leader.
