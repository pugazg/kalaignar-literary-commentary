## Explanation of meaning:

*Panai* fish = a kind of large fish. *Valai* = conch.  
*Vingupini* = tight binding. *Nonkayiru arii* = cutting the strong rope. *Kalporuthu* = striking against the anchor-stone.  
*Navai* = ship. *Panilam* = conch.  
*Kolor* = goad-bearer. *Melor* = elephant-mahout.  
*Vantodar* = chain fastened to the leg. *Kazh* = tethering post.  
*Kandhu neethu* = moving away from the post.  
*Uzhitharum kadaa yanai* = a rutting elephant that whirls and roams.
