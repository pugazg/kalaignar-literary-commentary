> “…Great one,
> to us you are sweet;
> but, like that elephant
> when hard-to-approach rut seizes it,
> you are terrible,
> great one,
> to those who oppose you.”

<div align="center">*</div>

<div align="right">

(*Purananuru* — Poem 94  
Poet: Avvaiyar)

</div>

## Explanation of meaning:

**Kurumakkal** = young children.  
**Kodu** = tusk.  
**Kazhaalin** = because of washing.  
**Kadaam** = the state of rut that seizes an elephant.  
**Thun** = closeness / approach.  
**Onnaathor** = enemies.
