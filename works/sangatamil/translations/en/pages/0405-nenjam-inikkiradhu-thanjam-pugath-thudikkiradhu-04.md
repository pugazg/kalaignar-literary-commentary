## Explanation of meaning:

*Kurundhaal irukkai mudavan* = a cripple with short legs who cannot walk or stand and remains in one place.  
*Koothali aadiya neduvarai* = the mountain region where the koothali creeper sways.  
*Utkai sirukudai kolik keezh irundhu* = sitting below with the palm cupped like an eating bowl.  
*Suttubu nakkiyaangu* = like pointing toward the honeycomb while licking the empty hand.  
*Nalkaar* = one who does not desire.  
*Nayavaar* = one who does not show affection.  
*Palkal kaandal* = seeing many times.
