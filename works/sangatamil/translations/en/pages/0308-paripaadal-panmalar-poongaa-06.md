> seeing the painted pale *vengai*, she was terrified;
> the dark-hued young female made the gentle women tremble.
> Losing judgement, unable to do what she had been doing,
> she scattered in fear. The rut-maddened elephant,
> stretching its trunk beyond the cruel goad,
> thundered and moved away;
> then, drawing near and embracing, it joined legs with the strong female.
> The great ones' trembling was removed, their distress relieved—
> as rope and binding tear and a damaged vessel splits,
> and a sailor who knows the directions repairs it with resin.”

*

(*Paripadal*: poem 10  
Poet: Karumpillai Puthanar  
Musical mode set by: Maruthuvan Nallachuthanar)

## Explanation of Meaning:

*pasumpidi* = green leaf. *ilamugizh* = young bud.  
*mauval* = jasmine. *kazhuneer* = blue water-lily.  
*aravindam* = lotus. *kullai* = *vetchi*; tulasi.  
*vagulam* = *magizham* flower. *naravam* = fragrance. *vangam* = cart.  
*pandi* = bullock. *unkan* = eye lined with kohl.  
*ayithazh* = beautiful petal. *thamaniyam* = gold.  
*maalutru* = having fallen in love. *sithaithara* = to scatter.  
*thotti* = elephant goad. *ithai* = a ship's sail.  
*iriya* = to become damaged. *payin* = resin used for adhesion.  
*neekaan* = sailor. *madappidi* = female elephant.  
*vayakkari* = male elephant.
