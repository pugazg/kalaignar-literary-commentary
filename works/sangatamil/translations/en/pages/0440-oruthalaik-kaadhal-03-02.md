There are those who, when the longing that torments the heart all day
comes to pierce them upon the bed as though testing its strength,
feel their hearts cool when evening arrives;
delighted that the monkey-like day has vanished,
they praise the night that arrives conveniently for a lovers' meeting!

But here the heroine Narkannai's state was different!
Her long eyes, lofty as a peak, remained open.

All night upon the bed, without sleep,
sitting upright, leaning, rolling over, rising again,
her every thought fixed upon Killi, she lost all composure!

Those lying within an embrace sometimes startle and ask,
“Has the night ended already?”
But she fretted, “Why does this Aamur night alone
move like a tortoise, like a snail?”

“The chance to welcome my Killi, dearer than life—
I can have it only if the dawn-sun rises quickly!
What is wrong with this town's rooster—cough, cold, fever?
Or is there some blockage in its throat?
Why has the town-rooster still not crowed for dawn?
If these idlers do not crow, will daybreak itself refuse to wake?
Where has my friend gone? If she were here,
I could ask her to crow like a rooster and wake the sun!”

Lightning-creeper Narkannai, greatly distressed,
would loosen her braided hair and then tie it again!
“What has happened to this eastern flower?
Why has it still not bloomed?” she would run about asking!

“Clear nectar! Goddess of fortune! Rare painting!
Why do you alone languish without sleep at midnight?”

Her guileless-hearted mother came near
and poured out love like a flood as she asked.

“If a thorn pierces the foot, we can easily pull it out;
but if a thorn pierces the heart, Mother,
is removing it such an easy task?”
