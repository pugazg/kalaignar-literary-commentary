# My Heart Grows Sweet; It Longs to Seek Refuge!

(Song  
Tala: Adi  
Raga: Misra Pilu)

(Opening)

My heart grows sweet—it longs
to seek refuge in you!
Enemies fear your broad shoulders that win in fierce battle,
so great poets say; hearing your triumph
praised in caressing Tamil,
my love overflows; and so
my heart grows sweet—it longs
to seek refuge in you!

(Verse)

I, this *vanji* maiden, beg and ask you
to grant me one boon. Only one thing
remains with me—my life; yet even that
I will give you without deceit, dear one! Make me
the peacock of your bed, jewel-hued one!
At the very thought of it—if in a dream you come
and embrace me—
my heart grows sweet—it longs
to seek refuge in you!
