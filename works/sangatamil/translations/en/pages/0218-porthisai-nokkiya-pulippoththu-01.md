# The Tiger-Cub Who Turned Toward War!

“Come quickly,
warriors!”

the drum thundered.

The fighters
who would give up life
to protect the land

flew in
and gathered
with the army.

The lovers,
lying joined
in their natural bodies,

had said:

“Never again,
not even by mistake,
shall we part,
flower,
jewel!”

Then the sound
of the war-drum
struck the lover's ear.

Knowing
he would leave her,

<div align="right">him</div>

the young woman
held tightly!

He pushed aside
the pleasure
of her breasts
pressed against him

and rose—

a young bull!

He stood upright
like a lion,
looked ahead,

and took up
his sword!

“Before you go
to take the lives
of enemies,

you are taking away
this maiden's life.

Is that justice?”

she asked
in a trembling voice,

holding
his feet,

while all
her palmyra-blossom smiles
ran away in fear.

Like flower-petals
unable to hold
drops of dew

and shedding them
upon the earth,

her golden eyelids
poured tears.

He embraced
the peahen
who had wilted
beneath them

and said:

“Though we joined
as body and life,

when the war-drum
calls us
to give our lives,

is it right
to remain
guarding one woman

instead of guarding
our soil
and our honour,
my dear?

Keep looking
at the sky.

Rejoice
when you see
our flag of victory

flying so high
that it steals
your eyes!
