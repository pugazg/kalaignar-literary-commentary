## Explanation of meaning:

**Maduppa** = to make someone eat.  
**Seraadhu** = to show affectionate anger.  
**Uyavodu** = with anxiety.  
**Manane** = O heart.  
**Kalirattu aanaan** = one who has killed elephants.  
**Uravor** = elders, here the father.  
**Unni** = to notice or attend to.  
**Maan ulai anna kudumi** = a thick topknot like a horse's mane.  
**Thol** = shield.  
**Pul** = young / tender.  
**Analon** = one who has a beard.
