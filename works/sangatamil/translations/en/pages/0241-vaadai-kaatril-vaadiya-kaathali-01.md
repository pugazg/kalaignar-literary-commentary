# The Lover Who Withered in the North Wind!

“Are you not my eye,
my fruit,
my unwritten melody?

Is my heart not the sky,
and are you not
the white moon within it?”

When he called me so,
the words he shaped
were clusters of beautiful flowers
in a grove
where waterfalls glide!

When his jewelled hands
played mischief
upon my breasts—
those jars of nectar—

and gave my lips
a new taste,

I saw the whole earth
spin upside down.

“From the crown of your head
to the soles of your feet,
may there be any obstacle,
my love,

to tasting you
with my lips
and the sound
‘ich’?

Is that obstacle
called clothing?
Then dismiss it at once!”

So spoke
that great warrior
of the battlefront.

And I too
gave no answering speech.

That is why
the bed itself
became soft as cotton!

They say *igal*
means enmity.

When daylight came,
was not *pagal*
itself our enemy then!

“My little lamp,
my precious life,
listen,
flower-cuckoo!

At the war-front
I shall yoke bow,
spear,
and sword

and reap
a harvest of victory.

Before the season
of the cold north wind begins,
beautiful one,

I shall run back
to see you,
flower of the stream!”

He fixed the day,
made me rejoice,

and swore his promise
upon his sword.
