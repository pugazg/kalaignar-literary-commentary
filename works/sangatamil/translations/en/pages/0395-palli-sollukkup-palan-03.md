For that very reason, it raised a call of love!

(The lizard's call)

(Thogaiyara)

When a hunter rubs the sharp point of an arrow
with his fingernail to test and set it right,
a “tik, tik” sound rises. Like that, the lizard's call
falls upon the ear. The moment your hero hears it,
desire will be born in him; he will twirl his young moustache and say,
“I have come to pluck this veena!”

(The lizard's call)

*

> “Could he fail to think of you, friend—he who crossed
> that harsh *kalli* forest, where a red-legged lizard
> calls to its mate with a sound like bandits
> rolling the tips of their nails over iron-made arrows
> to set them right?”

*

(Kuruntokai: poem 16  
Poet: Paalaipaadiya Perunkadungo)

## Explanation of meaning:

*Ullaar kollo* = would he not be thinking?  
*Ponpunai* = made of iron. *Pagazhi* = arrow. *Seppam konmaar* = in order to set it right. *Ugarnudhi* = tip of the fingernail.  
*Payir* = a call or sound. *Kaadu irandhor* = those who crossed the forest.
