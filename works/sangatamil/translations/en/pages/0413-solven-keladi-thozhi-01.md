# I'll Tell You—Listen, My Friend!

Song — Sindhu Bhairavi  
Tala: Adi

(Opening)

I'll tell you—listen, my friend—a piece of news
sweet as jaggery!
That young bull with his sprouting moustache—
he plays his mischief in so many ways!

(I'll tell you—listen, my friend...)

(Verse)

We would build little sand houses in the street—and there
pretend to cook vegetables in play;
he would come halfway through and wreck them—we
would start in alarm, while he would laugh!

(I'll tell you—listen, my friend...)

I would place jasmine and *mullai* in my hair—he
would appear as though by magic and pluck them away!
We would play happily with balls—he
would steal them and disappear before we knew he had come!

(I'll tell you—listen, my friend...)

One day he came and stood outside the doorway,
saying, “My mouth is dry—water.”
Mother called me near and said, “Daughter!”
She ordered me, my friend, to take the golden vessel,
scoop up water, and quench the thirst
of that suffering young man!

(I'll tell you—listen, my friend...)

I too had the desire—but
without showing it outwardly, I carried the water to him!
