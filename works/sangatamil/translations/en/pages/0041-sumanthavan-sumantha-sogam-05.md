> —but has he not been laid upon the bier without legs,  
> and covered with a pure white cloth?”

<div align="center">*</div>

<div align="right">

(*Purananuru* — Poem 286  
Poet: Avvaiyar)

</div>

## Explanation of meaning:

**Velyaattuch checchai** = a white male goat.

**Mandai** = the liquor called *theral*.

**Kaal-kazhi kattil** = the bier described as a cot without legs.

**Thoovel aruvai** = the pure white cloth that covers the body.
