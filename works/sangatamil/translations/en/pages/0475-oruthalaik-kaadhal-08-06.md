3.
> “With anklets filled with sounding pellets,
> an ambal-flower garland,
> fine forearms bright with gold bangles cut by the file,
> ornamented bamboo-like shoulders—
> Aiyai's father is Maaven Tittan,
> who gives bounty like rain...”

(Akananuru: poem 6  
Poet: Paranar)

4.
> “Even if he gives fine, renowned ornaments
> like Uranthai, fenced by white-paddy fields,
> of greatly generous Tittan,
> the noble one will not accept them...”

(Purananuru: poem 352  
Poet: Paranar)

5.
> “Holding the good pillar of the little house,
> you ask, ‘Where is your son?’
> I do not know where my son is.
> This is the womb that bore him—
> like the mountain cave from which a tiger has gone.
> He will appear, surely,
> on the battlefield!”

(Purananuru: poem 86  
Poet: Kaavarp Penn)

## Explanation of meaning:

Venkodu = white conch. Sellaa nallisai = undying good fame.
Aripei = fitted with pellets. Todalai = a garland that has been strung.
Arampozh = cut with a file. Panai = bamboo.
Maavan = one possessed of great generosity.
Vennel veli = having paddy-growing fields as a fence.
Nankalam = ornament. Kallalai = mountain cave.
