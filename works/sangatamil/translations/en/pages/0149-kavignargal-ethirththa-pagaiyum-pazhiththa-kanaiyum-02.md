—Karur recovered  
like a worm that had fallen into fire  
and somehow escaped.

After Alathur Kizhar,  
another rare poem

was sung about him  
by Avur Moolankizhar.

“O mighty fighting bull!  
King among kings!

If you become angry,  
sparks fly!

But if you look with grace,  
then in that very place

radiant golden gems  
pour out light!

To cool the summer sun,  
and to make the cool moon

burn hot like flame—  
victorious king,

only you can do such things!”

Thus the poet praised him  
in fine Tamil!

<div align="right">The king!</div>

“Even Death waits for its time  
before carrying off a life.

Guardian of the Chola line!

But you, on the battlefield,  
take the enemy’s life

in a single instant  
the moment you decide!”

So Kovur Kizhar marvels  
at the speed with which

he completed his deeds.

And he says:

“Just as someone  
who goes to cut firewood

may find gold in the forest,

all who come  
to King Killi

receive gifts and wealth.”

Another poet, Idaikkadanar, says:

“You are the king  
who guards the Tamil people

as a mother tiger  
guards her cub!

Honey among sugarcane,  
vaalai fish at the sluice,

red kazhuneer flowers  
plucked by maidens,

tortoises found  
by those who till the fields,

and, wherever the floodwaters  
of the Kaveri turn,

shining grains of paddy—

O king who rules  
the fertile Chola land!”

Thus he heaps praise  
upon Killivalavan.

“Your parasol, king,  
is not one that merely shields  
from the sun.

It is the white victory parasol  
that gives good rule

so that your people  
do not suffer.
