—and that her golden daughter  
would do no wrong.

Her whole body filling with relief,  
she kissed the crown of the girl's head!

The friend later went to the hero,  
as the heroine had asked,

and told him  
what had happened at home.

“O lord of the hill-country,  
whose fame rises high!

Every day you come,  
unafraid of the rocky paths

thick with bamboo  
and the movement of vengai beasts,

and you ease  
the heroine's longing.

That is good.

But do you not know  
what she suffers from her mother?

I, her friend, ask you one question.  
Do not panic.

Every day you say  
you will fix the wedding day.

Does honey become sweet  
merely because one says ‘honey’?

Choose a day.

Let the two of you join  
as companions in life

with the blessings  
of parents and relatives!

Otherwise this hill-country warrior  
will lose his claim to truth—

only heroic speech  
will remain!” she said.

“Tell my mullai-flower:

tomorrow my parents will come  
to ask for her hand.

There will be no more trouble  
of meeting along the field-path,”

he said and walked away.

And, just as he said,  
the marriage too must have taken place!
