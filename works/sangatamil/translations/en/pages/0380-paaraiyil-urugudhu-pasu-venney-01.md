# Cow's Butter Melts on the Rock!

“Kind-hearted one! Dear friend! I am wasting away until my very bones melt,” I say; yet, with a smile blooming on your face, you scold and tease me without knowing the illness that torments me! Because of the green-bangled maiden whose rows of teeth are like clustered *mullai* flowers bursting with fragrance, I tremble and wilt, friend,” Vanangamudi, the great warrior who stirs the world with his sword, told his friend Thuyan, who had put an arm around his shoulder and teased him.

“The thumbai-flower maiden, sweet at a touch, stood shaking him with her two buds! He watched without blinking as she hurled stones from her sling and drove away the birds, and he stood dazed!

The fish-eyed girl paid him no heed, intent on guarding the millet field! The hero had come with so many thoughts, but the pearl-like woman would neither face him nor speak. He had come like a devotee merely to taste those sugarcane-sweet lips; it was no use!

Her friend too, who was driving away the birds, came near and said to him with tact, ‘Hero who comes wishing to deepen this bond! Come after the sun has set; then you may meet her.’ Unable to embrace her tender body the moment he wished, the steadfast hero bowed his head like a millet ear!
