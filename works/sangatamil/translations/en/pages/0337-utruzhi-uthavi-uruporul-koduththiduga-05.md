> even among the four divisions distinguished by difference,
> if a man of the lower division learns,
> a man of the upper division too will come under him.”

*

(*Purananuru*, poem 183  
Poet: Pandya Nedunchezhiyan who crossed the Aryan army)

## Explanation of meaning:

**utruzhi** = when suffering or hardship occurs.  
**uruporul** = abundant or substantial material wealth.  
**pirrai nilai** = a later/backward condition, or a condition of worship.  
**muniyaathu** = without feeling aversion.  
**thiriyum** = will differ / turn.  
**avan-kat-padume** = will have to be subject to him.
