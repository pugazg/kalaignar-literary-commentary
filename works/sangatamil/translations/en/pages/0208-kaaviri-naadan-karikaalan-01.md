# Karikalan of the Kaveri Land!

In flower-filled Poompuhar,

the sea-beauty
touches again and again,
with his wave-tongue,

the tip of the tongue
of maiden Kaveri,

tastes her sweetness,
rejoices,
and dances.

There,
the fame-rich Chola,
Karikal Peruvalathan,
Thirumavalavan—

while still
inside his mother's womb,

lost his father,
whose life Time
took away.

His wicked kinsmen
sought to roll up
the Chola land
for themselves.

To give birth
to the lion-cub
leaping in her womb

and raise him safely,

the Chola queen
found a way.

She left the palace

and, with the help
of an old man
with a diamond heart,

found a place
where the plotters
could not see her

and lived there
for some days.

Her full term ended.

The moon-like queen
gave birth
to a child
like the crescent moon—

a golden infant
of flawless,
stolen beauty,

a Tamil lion
shining like
an unsheathed sword!

“Let his name
shine as Thirumavalavan!”

said the mother,

giving him
endless kisses
like fruit-segments,

embracing
the lamp of her lineage,

the very image
of her husband
born again.

“My son,
you are surely destined
to rule the world!”

she blessed him.

The royal relatives,
whose hearts
held no straightness,

learned
that the hidden queen
had given birth
to a son.

They decided
there was no task
but to destroy him,

and circled
like wolves
closing around a hare.

Near Uraiyur,
in a small village,

they caught the scent
of the enemy
growing there

and rushed
to that place—
