# The Painted Maiden Sought by the Nurse!

“Like the milk-white moon  
burning over the wasteland,

O sharp-eyed man  
whose spear rises  
and shakes the sky—

must I, a young parrot  
in the bloom of youth,

go to waste?

Lift your eyes  
and see my state!

My eyes have become ponds.

They say even a demon  
would pity a woman  
in such a state!

What answer  
will you give me?

Once I tasted sweet to you  
like sugarcane.

Why have I become bitter  
to you today?

Tell me!”

Letting her two fish-like eyes plead,  
the thread-waisted woman

caught his feet  
and cried aloud.

He lifted her into an embrace,  
stroked her banyan-leaf belly,

and said:

“Foolish girl,  
do not lose heart!

My precious life!  
My jewelled chariot!

I cannot bear  
to be apart from you

even for the space  
of a single moment.

But your parents  
raise a thunderous cry

and forbid our union.

That is why I hesitate,  
tender creeper! Gold!

I am bewildered,  
not knowing what to do!”

Hearing her lover’s words,  
the beautiful woman

clung to him,  
pressed against his chest,

and said:

“You take the honey  
from my lips

before even asking  
whether I will give it.

Why, then,  
must you still ask my parents  
about me?

My love!

Free me  
from this prison

where I am caught  
like sugarcane in a crushing mill.

I will fly with you  
across the wasteland!”
