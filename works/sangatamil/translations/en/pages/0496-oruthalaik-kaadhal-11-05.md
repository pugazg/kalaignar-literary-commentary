Having received that one thing alone,
her life flew away at that very moment.
The man of Uraiyur fainted and fell!

(The End)

1.
> “......the lotus—
> as cold flowers loosen from their stalks and scatter,
> while the rain that sustains the world's labours
> pours in the darkness of midnight,
> like the guard of a great pond with little banks
> upon which the spray of striking waves is flung,
> my hard-guarding mother too forgot sleep!”

*

(Akananuru: poem 252  
Poet: Nakkannaiyar)

## Explanation of meaning

**Eengai** = lotus.  
**Thuy** = stalk.  
**Thozhil-mazhai** = rain, foremost for the world's labours.  
**Paanat-kangul** = the darkness of midnight.  
**Eri-thirai-thivalai** = droplets from a flung/striking wave.  
**Sirukottu** = having a small bank.
