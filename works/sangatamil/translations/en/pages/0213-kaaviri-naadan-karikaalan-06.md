Let us live
singing the fame

of Karikal Peruvalathan,
the warrior,
the tiger-cub,

the great man
who ruled
the Tamil earth.

Let us honour
the dignity
of our people

and triumph
for ever!

<div align="center">*</div>

### (1)

> “…
> A tiger-cub
> with sharp claws
> and cruel stripes,
>
> raised inside a cage,
>
> grows in strength
> while held
> under another's restraint.
>
> Like a great elephant
> that falls into a pit,
>
> drives at the difficult bank
> with its tusks,
> destroys the pit,
>
> and goes
> to join
> its female mate,
>
> he carefully perceived
> his moment,
>
> cut through
> the enemies'
> strong confinement
>
> with the sword,
>
> and by rightful succession
> attained
> the kingship
> that stirred awe…”

### (2)

> “…
> The forest path
> is fiercer
> than the spear
> Thirumavalavan
> hurled
> at his enemies;
>
> yet her broad,
> soft shoulders
> are cooler
> than his sceptre.”

<div align="center">*</div>

<div align="right">

(*Pattinappaalai*  
Poet: Kadiyalur Uruthirankannanar)

</div>

## Explanation of meaning:

**Koor ugir** = sharp claw.  
**Kurulai** = tiger-cub.  
**Pirar-pini** = the enemy's custody / restraint.  
**Peedu-kaazh** = a mind strengthened by greatness.  
**Kuzhi-kondru** = filling / destroying the pit.  
**Pidi** = female elephant.  
**Nannaar** = enemies.  
**Thaayam oozhin eythi** = duly attaining one's royal inheritance.  
**Thaayaththaar** = those who claim rights of inheritance.  
**Thevvar** = enemies.  
**Ookkiya** = hurled.  
**Veyya** = fierce / severe.
