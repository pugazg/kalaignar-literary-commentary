—I found a message  
that filled my heart with pride.

I write it here  
and invite all of you with delight:

“May the joy I received  
grow in you too!”

Come and see  
the ancient distinction  
of glorious Tamilakam!

The Uraiyur poet Mudhukannan Sathanar,  
singing the fame

of crowned Chola Nalangilli,

said that this world  
regularly undergoes

growth and decline,  
birth and death.

He praised as true royal greatness  
the life of a generous king

who gives to those  
who come in distress,

and declared in golden words:

“Help the poor  
like an overflowing spring!”

And further—

countless are those  
who have lived and died

generation after generation  
upon this earth.

A red lotus  
growing out of mud

may have many rich petals,

yet those petals shine  
with such sameness

that it is hard  
to distinguish one from another.

I shall say one thing  
that seems true to me:

among the many born in this world,  
only a few become worthy

of the true fame  
sung by poets.

Many others vanish  
without lustre

like lotus leaves.

The poet who fitted  
such a simile

to the beauty of the petals  
and the lack of lasting worth  
of the leaves

then showed in poetry  
the height of fame

reached in this world  
by kings whom poets praise.

That is—

for those who earn  
the chance to travel

in a vehicle  
flying through the sky

without a *valavan*,  
a driver,

the height of life itself  
is the height of that sky!
