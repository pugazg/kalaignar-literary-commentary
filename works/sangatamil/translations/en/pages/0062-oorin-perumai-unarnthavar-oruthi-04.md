Then—

beneath the jackfruit tree heavy with fruit,  
behind the leaves of spreading vines,

very near the sleeping hunter,  
a stag embraced a doe

that had at first resisted him.

Unwilling to lose the rare pleasure  
the stag was giving her,

the doe made no further futile protest.

The two deer floated at the summit of delight  
in the cool shade,

forgetting themselves  
like ascetics absorbed in penance to win a boon.

The lady of the house watched  
that sacred dance of nature

without blinking  
until her eyes grew tired!

## At once

Fear flashed within her heart.

She hesitated even to take  
one step farther.

If the slightest sound  
of her foot touching

the flowers shed from the vines  
over the leaf-litter beneath her jackfruit tree

were to wake the young hunter  
who slept with eyes closed, forgetting the world—

then at the sight of him rising with a roar,  
the stag and doe competing for victory

on love's battlefield  
would lose all the pleasure they were about to attain.

Like a cooking pot overturned  
at the very moment the food boils,

they might have to flee merely to save their lives,  
or else separate and wither.

And if the hunter, on waking,  
seized and hurled the spear beside him,

the two lives now learning  
the lesson of love through action

might have to fall as victims  
midway through their union.

At the thought of this  
she became deeply anxious!
