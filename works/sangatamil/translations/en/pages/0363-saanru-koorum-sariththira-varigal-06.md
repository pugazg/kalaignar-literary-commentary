## Explanation of meaning:

**சுட்டுடை முதுபாழ்** = an old desolate *paalai* tract that can be pointed out.  
**கயிறு பிணிக் குழிசி** = a pot bound with rope.  
**பொறி** = seal.  
**ஆவணமாக்கள்** = officials called keepers of the records.  
**செஞ்செவி எருவை** = an eagle with fine ears.  
**குடந்தரீஇ இகுக்கும்** = to pull out and draw away the intestines.  
**கல் அதர் கவலை** = a pathway filled with stones.  
**இத்திப் புகர்படு நீழல்** = the mottled shade of the *itti* tree.  
**வானவன் மறவன்** = commander of the Chera king.  
**ஆனா நறவு** = liquor that does not spoil.  
**எஃகம்** = spear.  
**பனிவார்கண்** = eyes that shed water.
