## Explanation of meaning:

**Kaimmaan** = elephant. **Kanai-thuyil** = deep sleep.  
**Paarvai madappinai** = a young female deer.  
**Kalai** = male deer.  
**Il-vazhangaamaiyin** = remaining quietly to one side without moving about outside the house.  
**Thangaadhu** = without diminishing.  
**Vendhu-tharu vizhukkool** = what enemy kings gave him as tribute, together with the gifts given by his own king.

<div align="center">*</div>

**Note:** The scholar Avvai Duraisami Pillai, who wrote a commentary on *Purananuru*, says that Veerampattinam, the birthplace of Veeraiveliyanar, is near Puduvai.
