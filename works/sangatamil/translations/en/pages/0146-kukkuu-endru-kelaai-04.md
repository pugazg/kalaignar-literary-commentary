then the only higher path  
left for me

is to give up my life,”  
he said.

The friend smiled,  
deciding that she should no longer fight

with a man  
whose love was prepared even for death.

She told the heroine  
of his pure love

and brought her  
to nod in consent.

What followed?

Before elders,  
with parents and relatives rejoicing,

the entire wedding  
was completed!

The students of pleasure  
were ready

to learn  
inside the bedchamber school

all its wondrous arts.

Afterward,  
each became life  
to the other,

and they lived  
in intimacy.

One night,  
after the heroine

had floated and sunk  
in pleasure until dawn,

her friend saw her  
in the morning and asked:

“Was last night  
fruit-juice?

Was it honey-syrup?”

The heroine's face reddened  
with shyness

as she tried to hide  
the tooth-marks

on cheek and neck  
and the nail-marks below them.

Without telling  
the whole story  
of the previous night,

she said:

### (2)

“Listen to my pain, friend!

Thinking it was a night  
that would never dawn,

we went on playing.

Then the rooster cried  
‘Kukkoo!’—

and when it announced  
that day had come,

dawn arrived  
like a sword

to separate me  
from the lover  
who lay embracing my shoulders!”

with tears in her eyes.
