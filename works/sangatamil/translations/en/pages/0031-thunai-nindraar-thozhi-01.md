# The Friend Who Stood by Her!

Does not a tender shoot mean a new sprout?  
Does not a track mean a path?

In the same way, is it not customary  
for ancient Tamil works to call the male companion *thalaivan*  
and the female companion *thalaivi*?

Whenever the joyful love  
of the hero and heroine buds in secret courtship,

a dear male friend or female confidante  
gives the needed intelligence

and helps it blossom—  
Tamil literature tells us so;

and even grey-haired elders  
are known to savour it well!

In the fine Tamil work called *Ainkurunuru*,  
in neydhal, marudham, kurinji and mullai,

and in paalai where the rains have failed,  
poets offer the joyful experiences

reached by lovers bound together  
like flowers gathered in the evening!

A poet called Orampogiyar  
gives a marudham poem

sharp as mustard that never loses its pungency!

In the kurinji land,  
the unfailing love of the cuckoo-pair of love

is fashioned as a feast for this earth  
by the king of poets called Kapilar!

In the mullai land where the herdsman lives,  
the delight of lovers dear as life

is spoken by the poet Peyanar  
as testimony to Tamil greatness!
