# The Lizard's Call Has a Result!

(Sing to a suitable tune)

(Opening)

The lizard's call certainly has a result—even if
rationalists deny it,
the lizard's call certainly has a result!

(Verse 1)

Why should my heroine endure boundless worry any longer?
Her companion will come quickly to remove her distress!—for that,
remember this friend and bless her!
An ocean of joy is waiting for you!

(The lizard's call)

(Verse 2)

Travelling on and on, striving to seek wealth,
walking through the cruel, parched wilderness,
like a spirit that has left the body—he who
left you to waste away will come even now!

(The lizard's call)

(Verse 3)

Do not think that I am staging a drama
to drown you in superstition! Do not
wet your beautiful moon-face with tears
pouring from beneath your eyelids!

(The lizard's call)

(Verse 4)

They say there are many *kalli* thickets in the desert,
and many lizards leap and run there. Among them,
a male desired to join with a female!
