# The Colourful Bride Wedded by Vaanan!

A playful parrot, the doll of the household,  
a roaming breeze, a star at dawn—

a chieftain of the marudham land married that flower;  
at night the two forgot the world!

Upon a bed soft as ilavam cotton,  
they learned many arts, learned and learned,  
and learned anew each day!

What they had learned upon the bed  
was but a handful of earth;

what remained unlearned  
was as vast as the sand of the sea—

so he told his silk-skinned chaste wife  
and gave her countless pleasures.

Upon the family vine that twined in affection,  
a child appeared like a tender shoot.

Because the marriage bed rested for some days,  
the bridegroom suffered the sorrow of solitude!

Desire rose like an axe  
cutting down a tree—

and cut into his mind.

His wife, who did not forget virtue,  
raised their son like a tiger cub;

she fed him milk swelling in her heavy breasts  
through their dark tips.

Each time the husband thought of that,  
his mouth filled with saliva

for the sweetness he had lost—
