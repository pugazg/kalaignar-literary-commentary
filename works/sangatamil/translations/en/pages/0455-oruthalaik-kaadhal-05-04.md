If only I could remain immersed in that dream forever,
the two lovers would stay bound together in delight!
Through the fault my eyes committed, I awakened;
all the joy dissolved like dew touched by sunlight!”

*

Thus,
as Narkannai told Porkodi the dream she had seen
and sank in sorrow,
the victorious roar of the war-drum rose
as though it would grind the sky to powder.
“Victory for whom? For whom?” cried Narkannai,
flying out into the street!

*

> “The thorn-toothed bat of the great mango tree within the town,
> at the hour when it sleeps hanging from a lofty lovely branch,
> dreams of the sour taste of gooseberries
> in the great forest of Azhisi of the battle-winning Cholas—
> so too, friend, that has passed away……”

*

(Natrinai: poem 87  
Poet: Narkannaiyar)

## Explanation of meaning:

*Mul-eyitru vaaval* = a bat with teeth like thorns.  
*Maaththa ongal am-sinai* = the lofty, beautiful branch of a mango tree.  
*Thoongu-thuyil* = sleeping while hanging.
