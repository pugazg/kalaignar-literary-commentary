Holding honour alone to be the good life,
even if we embrace death, smiling in the field,

falling with a glorious wound borne upon the chest
and attaining great fame—that is a loftier greatness!

More than embracing a daughter of noble lineage
and delighting in the pleasure of union—

in the battle that keeps our daughter-land
from becoming a slave to the enemy, giving our life
and taking a long sleep upon her broad lap
is the path by which lasting joy endures!

So that the history of the Tamil people does not falter,
rising and falling like the waves of the roaring sea;

so that it spreads across the world like the fragrance of a scented grove,
honour alone is the life-principle that is nectar!
People who pawn that principle to someone else
in order to preserve ornaments and palaces—
we shall call them “parrot-men”!
We shall not accept them as Tamils!
Therefore,

even if eyes come and pierce us like monsoon rain,
even if spears spring upon us like kendai fish leaping in the sluices of the fields,

even if the savage tusks of elephants rushing in fury with face-armour
stab and tear us apart,

proclaim that death's embrace surpasses even a woman's embrace;
take up the war-conch and sound it!
Come forward to play our part upon the field!”

Thus,

turning even plantain trees
into diamond-hard teak,
giving a backbone even to cowards,
Chola Narkilli finished his speech.

What a sight it was: even worms seemed to become tigers
and march forth to cleanse the stain of the enemy!
