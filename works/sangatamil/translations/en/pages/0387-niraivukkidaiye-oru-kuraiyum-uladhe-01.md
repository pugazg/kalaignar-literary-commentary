# Amid Fulfilment, One Lack Remains!

Song  
Raga: Kharaharapriya  
Tala: Tisra Adi

(Opening)

Though mansions and halls surround me—and within them
fragrant gentle breezes drift and move,
there is no peace in my heart;
there is no way to dance in joy!

(No peace in my heart)

(Stanza 1)

Though mango groves and flower groves are there—and there
deer and peacocks graze and roam,
there is no peace in my heart;
there is no way to dance in joy!

(Stanza 2)

Though rubies, pearls and corals are there,
though emeralds and every green gem lie heaped,
there is no peace in my heart;
there is no way to dance in joy!

(Stanza 3)

Though jasmine is spread upon the bed,
though marukkozhundhu, civet and sandalwood give fragrance,
there is no peace in my heart;
there is no way to dance in joy!

(Stanza 4)

Though wealth has grown mountain-high—and our
mansion veranda is filled with guests,
there is no peace in my heart;
there is no way to dance in joy!
