## Explanation of meaning:

**Nonpagattu umanar** = salt-merchants who come with powerful bulls.  
**Ozhugai** = cart.  
**Magaar anna** = like children.  
**Nagaar** = tooth.  
**Nalineer muththam** = pearl from dense / abundant water.  
**Erundhu** = shell.  
**Nalgoor nusuppu** = slender waist.  
**Ular iyal aimpal** = fivefold braided hair with a moving quality.  
**Umattiyar** = women salt-merchants.
