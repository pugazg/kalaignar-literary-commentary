# A Scene in Nature That Shows One's “Kind”!

Ammuvanar,
who came to tell
a sweet love-story,
shows us a seashore scene
in honey-sweet *Natrinai*:

if one does not correctly recognise
one's own kind,
a thousand troubles may arise.

On the sandy shore
where surging armies of waves
strike again and again
and conches are washed up,
a fruit fell from a naval tree
and rolled along.

A swarm of bees,
wrongly thinking the fruit
belonged to their own kind,
crowded around it.

What folly!

Then a crab,
thinking it had found a fine fruit,
rushed forward
and caught one bee by the leg.

Because of the mistake
of failing to recognise kind,
the bee was trapped in danger.

Seeing it panic,
close to death,
the gathered swarm rose into the air,
crestfallen,
hearts distressed,
buzzing in turmoil.

The crab,
not knowing it was a bee
and thinking it had seized a naval fruit,
rejoiced greatly
and hurried away with it in its jaws.

The poor shining bee trembled:

“Because I too approached the naval fruit
without knowing its kind,
I have fallen into this danger
and am about to lose my precious life!”
