# Iron in the Forge and a Drop of Water!

Sivaganga country was ruled by Chinna Maruthu and Periya Maruthu—

two lion-hearted great warriors  
who blossomed as blood-red flowers  
in the history of that red soil.

At Kalaiyar Kovil, where statues stand for those two,  
there was in ancient Tamilakam a place called *Kaanapper*.

Even today it is a land of Maravar  
who rise to wage battles of honour.

There a minor chieftain named Vengai Marban  
built a fortress of towering fame

and issued a challenge:

“Who can conquer it?”

One might pierce an atom  
and enter into it—

but, warriors said in amazement,  
one could not enter that fortress!

At that time the Pandya country  
was ruled by a king

who was a lion in battle:  
Ukkirap Peruvazhuthi!

He possessed the strength  
to lift elephants and toss them like balls.

It was extremely difficult  
for an enemy struck by his sword  
to remain alive.

Along with overflowing strength  
he possessed an intelligent artistic heart;

he earned distinction  
by collecting the poems of *Akananuru*.

It is said that the ceremonial presentation  
of the *Tirukkural*—

that undying lamp of light—  
took place in his presence.

One day Avvai saw him seated  
with Chola Perunarkilli

and Cheraman Maari Venko,  
the three crowned kings together.

She wove and placed upon them  
a garland of rich Tamil verse

and blessed the unity  
of the three kings.

Ukkirap Peruvazhuthi,  
the king whose emblem was the fish,

was also the lover  
of a fish-eyed woman.
