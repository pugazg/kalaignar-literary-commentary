> “In ignorance,
> mistaking it for ‘possession,’
> the mother too
> suffered deep sorrow.
>
> Therefore it is cruel
> to leave it unknown:
>
> the pallor in her dark-lined eyes,
> like petals of a chosen flower,
> is the illness caused
> by the man of the nearby hill.”

<div align="center">*</div>

<div align="right">

(*Ainkurunuru* — Kurinji, Poem 242  
Poet: Kapilar)

</div>

## Explanation of meaning:

**Veriyena** = thinking that a deity has afflicted her.  
**Uzhandhanal** = suffered and was distressed.  
**Eyyaadhu viduthal** = leaving it without knowing.  
**Aaymalar** = a selected, excellent flower.  
**Seymalai** = a nearby hill.
