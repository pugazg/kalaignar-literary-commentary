> the *kaduvan* drank it unknowingly.
> Unable to climb the nearby sandalwood
> overgrown with pepper-vine,
> it rejoices and sleeps upon a bank of fragrant flowers.
> O lord, such unlooked-for pleasure is easily gained
> by the many creatures of your mountain!”

*

(*Akananuru*: poem 2  
*Kalitriyanai Nirai*  
Poet: Kapilar)

## Explanation of Meaning:

*kozhilai* = luxuriant leaf. *kolmuthir* = heavily fruited.  
*oozh uru* = fallen naturally.  
*oozhpadu theral* = the liquor called *theral*, formed naturally of itself.  
*karivalar saantham* = sandalwood tree overgrown with pepper-vine.  
*naruvee adukkam* = bank or layer of fragrant flowers.  
*kuriyaa inbam* = pleasure obtained naturally and unexpectedly.
