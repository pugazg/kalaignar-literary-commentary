(*Purananuru* — Poem 246  
Poet: Pandya queen Perungoppendu)

</div>

## Explanation of meaning:

**Melikol** = cruel / weakened sceptre.  
**Izhukkiyaan** = I shall become one who has lost.  
**Orii** = removing / leaving aside.  
**Vanpulam** = dry, harsh land.  
**Eemam** = funeral fire made from stacked wood.  
**Val** = abundance.  
**Nal** = dense / deep.  
**Orattru** = of the same nature / alike.
