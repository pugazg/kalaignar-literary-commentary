> —but if it comes
> to a distinguished man
>
> with strong feet
> that withstand
> surging great battle,
>
> then that royal fortune
> is very light indeed—
>
> like the white netti stalk
> in a low-water,
> drying pond
>
> that withers
> in the summer heat.
>
> Such is the splendour
> of kingship
>
> beneath the spotless
> white umbrella
>
> rising high
> to touch the sky,
>
> where war-drums thunder.”

<div align="center">*</div>

<div align="right">

(*Purananuru* — Poems 73 and 75  
Poet: Chola Nalangilli)

</div>

## Explanation of meaning:

**Madavon** = an ignorant man.  
**Thunjupuli** = sleeping tiger.  
**Sithadan** = blind man.  
**KazhAI** = bamboo.  
**Maindudai** = possessing strength.  
**Vandrinai** = possessing power and firmness.  
**Ollaa muyakku** = loveless union.  
**Paaldhara vandha pazhaviral thaayam** = royal inheritance that comes by proper succession.  
**Kooril aanmai** = lacking sharp / vigorous manliness.  
**Mandu amar** = great battle.  
**Madhan** = rising spirit / courage.  
**Parikkum** = to withstand opposition.  
**Arugayam** = dried-up pond.  
**Kidai** = netti stalk.  
**Noydhu** = light / easy.
