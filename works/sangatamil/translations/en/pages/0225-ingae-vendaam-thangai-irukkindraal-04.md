> “—O lord
> of the shore-rich land!
> If you grant me this,
>
> there are other shaded places
> where we may stay.”

<div align="center">*</div>

<div align="right">

(*Natrinai* — Poem 172  
Poet: name unknown)

</div>

## Explanation of meaning:

**Aayam** = group of female companions.  
**Kaazhmulai** = mature seed.  
**Agaiya** = to leave behind.  
**Nuvvai** = your younger sister.  
**Vilarisai** = soft music.  
**Kaduppa** = resembling / like.  
**Kodu** = conch.  
**Naral** = sound / resound.
