# One-Sided Love

## 11

Upon an ornate bed shaped
like a blue peacock spreading its tail,
the good maiden Aiyai lay,
her anichcha-flower body covered
with a garment like shed snakeskin.
Knowing nothing of her brother's coming,
she was sunk in deep sleep!

There had been no exaggeration
when Katti and Paanan,
before Kaavarp Penn,
had praised Aiyai's sculpted beauty in imagination.

Paranar had written a poem about a maiden,
as though undressing her in verse.
As a fitting example,
the Chola maiden appeared like a mould cast in gold,
endowed with milk-pots that could entrance bulls!

A breeze came in through the many-paned window
and mischievously shifted her upper garment.
The stars spread across every direction of the sky
started in shock,
blinked and stared.
The moon appeared between two mountains
and wondered how it could be pouring coolness
upon a bed inside the mansion.
What a marvel—
as though the stars themselves,
eager to understand the secret,
had convened a council of deliberation!

That night,
through the curtain swaying and flirting in the breeze,
a figure wrapped in a black cloak
came and stood beside Aiyai's bed,
holding a dagger.
Looking all around,
the figure stared closely
at that ten-and-a-half-touch golden doll.

At that very moment,
brother Killi too arrived there,
sword in hand,
to protect his sister!
