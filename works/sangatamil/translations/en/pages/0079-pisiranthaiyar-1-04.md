If you ordered that fish should live on land  
and be taken out of water,

your ministers and officials,  
lacking the strength to contradict you,

would simply nod  
like dolls.

Because such people have surrounded you,  
your own wisdom has grown weak.

If this tale continues without end,  
suffering too will spread endlessly,

and this ancient, splendid Sangam land, king,  
will sink low.

If the wealth of taxation  
wrung from people through torment

is taken as the mark of government,

then like a field entered by a rutting elephant,  
the country will become barren earth,  
benefiting no one.

Know this!” he said.

“You are unlike certain gift-seeking poets  
who come as guests, sing poems,

and push me deeper into amusement.

You have given me poetry like medicine  
and transformed my mind.

From today I am reformed,  
because your sacred feet  
have stepped into this palace!”

said the king.

The poet had won!

> * “If ripened paddy is harvested and fed by mouthfuls,  
> even less than a *maa* will last many days.  
> But if, though there be a hundred fields,  
> the elephant enters alone and eats,  
> its feet destroy more than its mouth consumes.  
> If a wise king knows the proper way of taking revenue,  
> wealth gathers by the crore and the country flourishes.  
> But if the ruler is weak in wisdom,  
> and every day, with a noisy circle that knows no measure,  
> greedily takes wealth without compassion,  
> then, like a field into which an elephant has entered,  
> he himself will not eat, and the world too will perish!”
