> —were pierced and spread by feeding bees;  
> torn from the stalk by the wind,  
> they scattered like hail from the sky  
> along the red-ridged road, coral-bright,  
> lying thick like bits of fat  
> upon a surface of blood—  
> near that beautiful little settlement.”

<div align="center">*</div>

<div align="right">

(*Akananuru* — Poem 9  
Poet: Kalladanar)

</div>

## Explanation of meaning:

**Puzhugu** = the socket at an arrow's tip. **Iruppai** = iluppai tree.  
**Senguzhai** = red new shoot.  
**Uzhudhu-kaan thulaiyavaagi** = petals spread after being disturbed by bees.  
**Kaalodu** = struck by the wind.  
**Aar kazhalpu** = breaking away from the stalk.  
**Aali vaanir paari** = scattered like a hailstorm.  
**Thuppu** = coral. **Neythor** = blood.  
**Ninaththin parikkum** = spread thickly like particles of fat.
