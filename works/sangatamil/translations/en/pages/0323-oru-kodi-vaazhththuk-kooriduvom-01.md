# Let Us Offer Ten Million Greetings!

When the greatly praised Thondaiman Ilanthiraiyan ruled,
with heart-captivating, artistic Kanchi as his capital,
Kadiyalur Uruthirankannanar rejoiced
by composing the *Perumpanarruppatai* for him
in precious Tamil.

This was the same poet who, for singing *Pattinappalai*
on Karikal Valavan,
received 1.6 million in gold as his reward.
These two long poems of the *Pattuppattu*
speak of the culture and way of life of the ancient Tamils.
They console and guide the mind, saying:
“If you go to such-and-such a person,
you will receive worthy gifts and find joy.”
They sing of generous and capable people
whose bounty feeds the world like a swelling spring,
and of the great prosperity of the lands
where those patrons live, rule and sit in honour.
To make all this an offering
through fruit-juice-sweet Tamil poetry—
that, the learned and the wise say,
is what an *arruppatai* is.

Kadiyalur Uruthirankannanar's *Perumpanarruppatai*
is a treasure containing
the distinction of the city ruled by Thondaiman,
that guardian of the arts of Kanchi;
the great distinction that men there knew no destitution
and the gentle women were likewise prosperous;
and the Tamil people's excellence of lineage and community.
