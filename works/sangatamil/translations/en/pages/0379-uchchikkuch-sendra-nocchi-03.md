regarded life as a trifle and fell in battle, even his nocchi garland had attained such a height. Praising and celebrating it so, the poet instils in all of us who read it a feeling of valour from crown to sole! Let us engrave this in our hearts! Let us stand praising it for the world to know!

*

> “........................................
> ............................ now,
> drenched in fearsome blood, its form transformed,
> the damaged garland, taken for flesh,
> we saw a bird of prey carry it aloft—
> because the young man who entered battle had worn it;”

*

(Purananuru — poem 271  
Poet: Veripaadiya Kamakkanniyar)

*

## Explanation of meaning:

*Iniye* = now. *Uruvu karandhu* = changing form.  
*Oru* = damaged. *Theriyal* = nocchi garland.  
*Oon seththu* = thinking it flesh.  
*Ugappa* = to carry aloft.  
*Malaindhamaaru* = because it had been worn.
