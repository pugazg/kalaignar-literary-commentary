# She Avenged the Wrong and Ended the Feud!

With leaping spear in hand,
with sword overflowing with fury,
even a young shoot
not yet of school-going age
would rise declaring,
“I shall pluck out the enemy and cast him away!”

Such was that age—
a golden age granted to the Tamil people,
an age in which many poets
nurtured the Sangam.

Warriors went to battlefields
like surging tigers,
while auspicious women
stood blessing them.

When the wind blew,
Tamil valour came striking through it.
When waves rose from the sea,
the stature of the Tamil rose with them.

The smoke of akil wood carried fragrance;
and within that fragrance
was the fame of Tamil.

The lovely moon appeared amid cloud;
and within it appeared
the treasury of Muthamizh epic wealth.

Even Emperor Ashoka,
who ruled a vast land famed across the earth,
spoke of the powerful people of the south
who were not subject to his rule.

Among those strong-shouldered peoples
of the south
was one division called the Kosars,
filled with courage.

They had the skill
to produce valour
and were famed
for purity of truthful speech.

Leaving Tulu country,
they ceased their wandering
as far as the eastern sea
and, history says,
settled in the regions
of Niyamam and Sellur.
