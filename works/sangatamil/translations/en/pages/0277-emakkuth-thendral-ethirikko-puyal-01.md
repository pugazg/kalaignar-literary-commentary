# A Breeze to Us; a Storm to the Enemy!

In the exalted land of Thagadoor,
where cool wind seems to squeeze water from clouds
to quench the thirst of mountains,
King Adiyaman Neduman Anji
slowly walked,
tasting victorious Tamil.

In the Aththani hall,
as usual,
learned elders,
men of wisdom,
generals with clustered moustaches,
and great ministers sat together.

They poured out rare counsel
for increasing the country's prosperity.

Adiyaman Neduman,
delighted in heart,
praised them,
commended them,
and blessed them all.

Seeing this,
Lady Avvai's eyes filled
with tears of joy.

She looked at the crowned king,
smiled,
and strung a poem
like a garland of mullai flowers.

She fixed it in *Purananuru*
as a pearl-necklace
that would never perish.

Feel and enjoy
the beauty of the comparison
in that strong verse.

Whoever may be around him,
Adiyaman shows affection,
attachment,
and compassion toward them.

And with that,
he has the sweetness
of moving among them with simplicity.

Is that not a quality
worthy of a leader?

Is that not a distinction
gained by this Tamil land?
