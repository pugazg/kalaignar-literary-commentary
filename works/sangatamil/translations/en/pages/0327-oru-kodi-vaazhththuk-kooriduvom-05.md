> with little earrings swinging at her ears,
> bamboo-like shoulders and hair gathered in short braids—
> she feeds her kin with food bought from the price of curd and buttermilk.
> Saving the price of ghee, she does not take bright gold;
> she obtains buffaloes, good cows and dark calves.
> If you stay among the broad-mouthed cowherds' settlement,
> you will receive green millet rice,
> like the tiny young of the broad-clawed crab,
> together with milk.”

*

(*Perumpanarruppatai*:  
lines 155–168  
Poet: Kadiyalur Uruthirankannanar)

*

## Explanation of Meaning:

*aambi* = mushroom. *poonchumattu* = soft head-pad.  
*kuzhisi* = pot. *alai* = curd, buttermilk. *kilai* = kin.  
*aruthi* = to feed. *nallaan* = good cow.  
*naagu* = calf. *njendu* = crab. *paarppu* = young one.  
*mooral* = rice.
