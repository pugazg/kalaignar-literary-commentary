# She Became Music!

One evening,
a friend eagerly ran

to the home
of the heroine
who had married

and was living
with the man
who had won her heart.

The house-door
stood slightly open.

She gently pushed it
a little farther.

At the sight
before her,

she covered her eyes
with one hand,

yet peeped
through the spaces
between her kantal-like fingers,

her head bent down!

Upon a mat
spread in the hall,

the heroine lay

like a dove
upon a temple tower,

without worrying
about arranging
her clothing.

Her husband
had buried his face
in the rise
of her breasts.

<div align="right">And for</div>

the many childlike
mischiefs he played there,

the heroine
was a willing companion.

Seeing her
dearest friend arrive,

she quickly covered
her jujube-fruit body
with cloth

and sprang up.

Yet he would not
let her go!

Intent on embracing her
until his thirst of desire
was fully quenched,

he seized
her lovely waist

and began
to pull away

even the little cloth
still clinging
to her body.

“Let me go!”

she cried,
her two fish-like eyes
leaping.

Pretending
to be fiercely angry,

she pushed
his hand away,

then embraced
her friend:

“Friend!
When did you come?”

“Live long!
For many years!”

the friend replied.

Then,
teasing,
she said:

“I came
when the hero,

having no pillow
on which to rest his head,

had buried his face
in the heroine's breasts!”

Still speaking
with mock seriousness,
she asked:

“Am I allowed
to sit here?”

the summer heat of desire,
