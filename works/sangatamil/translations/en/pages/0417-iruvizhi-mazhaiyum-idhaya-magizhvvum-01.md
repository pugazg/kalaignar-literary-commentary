# Rain from Two Eyes and Joy in the Heart!

Song  
Raga: Sindhu Bhairavi  
Tala: Adi

(Opening)

You see the rain from my two eyes;
will you also see the joy in my heart!

Bearing a battle-sword, my son Ponmudi
felled the base and gave up his life;
you see the rain from my two eyes;
will you also see the joy in my heart!

(Verse 1)

My son, who received a glorious wound upon his chest—
I praise him because he redeemed this soil; yet thinking
of how he crawled upon my lap and grew,
my heart grieves! My heart grieves!

(Opening)

You see the rain from my two eyes;
will you also see the joy in my heart!

(Thogaiyara)

I had borne as my son
a warrior who would pluck away
the heads of the enemy as they came leaping;
I had lived in immense joy!
“Mother, I am going to the battlefield and will return,”
he said that day, kissing me upon the cheek!
I clasped my brave treasure in an embrace,
patted him and said, “Go and return victorious, my son!”
