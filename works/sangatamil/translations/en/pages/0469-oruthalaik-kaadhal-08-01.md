# One-Sided Love

## 8

Like a hand-spear hurled across the battlefield,
Narkannai flew forward in haste!
Then, as though that spear had plunged into an elephant's body,
she fell to the earth, exhausted and spent!

When she opened her eyes,
she realized that her head lay at the poet's feet.
“I thought you would apply medicine to heal the wound in my heart;
yet, knowing that Killi would not think to rescue
a vessel sinking in the cool great sea,
why did you smash my honour in the middle of the assembly
like a pot made of clay?”
she asked through tears, her words breathing fire!

“Child, Narkannai! Do not kill me with your tears!
Though I acted in haste in the assembly,
you do not know what truly happened!
To learn the heart of Uraiyur's prince,
I spent a night speaking openly with him on your behalf
in the tireless battle-camp at the war-front.

‘Let the battle end; we shall speak afterwards.
Let tomorrow dawn with news of victory,’ he said,
and when Narkilli left with a smile upon his face,
I believed him!
I thought he was slipping away out of shyness!

Is there a grove that bars the southern breeze?
What mountain refuses the embrace of a honeyed waterfall?
I believed that this king too would marry you, maiden;
I even fixed a day for the wedding with your father,
and announced it in the hall with a heart full of satisfaction.
When Killi refused, I died inside!”
