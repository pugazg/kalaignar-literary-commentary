> * “You quickly know those who worship you;  
> you do not trust the words  
> of those who speak ill of others.
>
> If you yourself discover wrongdoing,  
> you examine it by what is just
>
> and punish it  
> with a fitting punishment.
>
> But if the offender comes,  
> bows at your feet,
>
> and stands before you  
> as in former days,
>
> even your punishment becomes gentler—  
> your grace grows greater than before.
>
> Fragrant rice cooked with nectar-like richness  
> is given without limit to all who come.
>
> O warrior with a bow-strapped chest—  
> only the women of your blameless household life
>
> can make you yield;  
> opposing warriors cannot.”

<div align="center">*</div>

<div align="right">

(*Purananuru* — Poem 10)

</div>

**Note:** Both poems were sung by the poet **Oonpozhi Pasungudaiyar**, as the source prints the name here.

## Explanation of meaning:

### Poem 378

**Midal** = strength. **Ilampaadu** = poverty. **Irumper** = relatives.  
**Seri-marabin** = things fit to be worn.  
**Thodakkunar** = they wore.  
**Araikkamai** = things meant for the waist. **Midatru** = neck.  
**Mathar-ani** = beautiful ornament. **Izhai** = ornament.  
**Araa arunagai** = rare joy that does not leave.

### Poem 10

**Val aridhi** = you know quickly.  
**Oppa naadi** = examine according to the path of justice.  
**Aththaga oruththi** = give punishment suited to it.  
**Malaiththal** = to waver / change. **Mallar** = warriors.
