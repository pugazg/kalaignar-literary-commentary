Are not even the harsh words  
of a maiden

sweet as rock candy  
to a young bull  
struck by love?

“She twists away my very life  
and carries it with her.

Make that sinner  
melt for me  
and show me grace,” he said.

“When my heroine makes a decision,  
she will not change it

even if her head must fall.

Let us see  
whether your penance bears fruit,”

said the friend,

then sprang away  
and joined the heroine.

<div align="center">*</div>

He stood there alone  
until evening.

After that  
he went again  
to the friend's house.

“Listen, squirrel  
that comes to eat guava fruit!

I tell you truly:

to touch my heroine's body  
is like finding a hare's horn—

impossible!”

she declared.

He did not fall  
at the feet  
of the fish-eyed woman;

instead he said:

“Friend!

Even if every pleasure  
in this ancient world

were gathered together  
and offered to me,

I would not touch them.

Even if I were permitted  
to rule the whole world  
surrounded by the ocean,

I would not accept.

A life without her  
is, to me,

iluppai flower—  
etti fruit!

Know this!”

“You torment me  
morning and evening,

through black night  
and blazing day,

without regard  
for any hour,

trying to conduct  
a ‘friend-as-messenger’ mission!

Have you no other work?

I tell you finally—  
go away!” she said.

He answered:

### (1)

“If lovers are able  
to distinguish

morning, day,  
the evening when rays decline,

the night when eyes sleep,  
and the dark hour before dawn,

I would call  
such love false love!

That is why  
I wander thinking of her,

without concern  
for the time of day.

If there is no way  
for me to attain her,

I shall mount  
a horse made  
from palmyra fronds—
