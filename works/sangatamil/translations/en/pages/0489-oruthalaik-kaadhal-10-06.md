With a smacking “ich” of a kiss
he made her senses reel,
drew her into his arms
and laid her upon the soft bed!

Two bodies—sacred bodies—
one dark-hued: his body.
The other mango-coloured:
the maiden's magical body.
Her two vessels of nectar pressed deep against his chest,
while her lily-eyes darted—
delight! delight!

Then the shower of dream-mist broke apart
and the sun of waking memory rose.
Killi opened his eyes,
his face spilling joy,
and rested one leg on the pillow.

As Killi lay there in rapture upon the bed,
a rolled palm-leaf flew in and fell upon him!
He picked it up and read it.
Trembling, he sprang to his feet!

“Tonight Princess Aiyai
will be taken captive from here.”

Seeing this message on the palm-leaf,
Killi laughed aloud.

“It seems the itch in the bodies
of Katti and Paanan, who ran and hid,
has still not subsided!
To protect Aiyai, my own sister,
this sword alone is enough!”

With a roar,
he set out like the fire of the cosmic end
for the mansion where his sister slept!
