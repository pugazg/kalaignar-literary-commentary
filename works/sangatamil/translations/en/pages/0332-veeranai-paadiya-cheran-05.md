> except for the shield, its rim gone,
> shattered and made something else,
> nothing of him remained upon the field.
> Establishing good fame that shines through long ages,
> he lives now in the mouths of poets whose tongues sing.”

*

(*Purananuru*: poem 282  
Poet: Perungadungko who sang the Paalai)

## Explanation of Meaning:

*arungkadan irutha perun seyaalan* = the warrior of action who performed a rare duty.  
*kilarthaar agalam* = the chest wearing a stirring, rising garland.  
*arungkadan irumaar vayavar* = enemy warriors who came to perform their own rare duty.  
*malaiyunar* = those who fight. *madangi* = turning back to attack.  
*alagai pogi sithainthu veraagiya palagai* = the shield that lost its protective state, shattered and became dust.  
*sen* = a long span of time.
