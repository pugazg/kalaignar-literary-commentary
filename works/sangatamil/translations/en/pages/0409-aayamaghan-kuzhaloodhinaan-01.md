# The Cowherd Youth Played His Flute!

Song  
Raga: Senjurutti  
Tala: Tisra Adi

(Opening)

The cowherd youth played his flute; that
cowherd maiden swayed and danced!
“If he embraces the bull and wins,
I shall accept him,” she declared!
With valour he sprang upon the bull—and after
winning a great victory, he leaned against her in love!

The cowherd youth played his flute; that
cowherd maiden swayed and danced!

(Verse)

The maiden of the *mullai* land, lovely as a bud,
would say of a man who fears the horns of a charging bull:
“Not only in this birth—even if there is
another birth after this,
I will not let him come near me even then!”
She who came in that tradition was a golden statue filled with beauty!
He subdued the bull that leapt like a tiger—and
rose after driving it off as though it were a mouse; crying,
“My lion!” she ran and embraced him—while he
scooped her up, saying, “My gold!”......

The cowherd youth played his flute; that
cowherd maiden swayed and danced!

(Conclusion)

To tend the wounds gashed by the two horns
of the bull that charged in fury,
her love overflowing all restraint, she pursed her lips; ah!
Seeing the wound heal in a single moment—

The cowherd youth played his flute; that
cowherd maiden swayed and danced!
