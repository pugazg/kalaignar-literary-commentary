“—and once that is done,
we shall forgive and forget our enmity toward Uraiyur,” said Paanan!

“You may encircle lands,
but if you think you can then bend a woman to your will,
the people of Uraiyur are not all trees standing helpless!
So forget your plan to obtain the good woman Aiyai,” she said!

3. “Is forgetting Aiyai, that unplucked fruit,
so easy a thing?
Paranar describes a maiden
with beautiful speckles like the lovely pollen
of vengai trees blooming in clusters,
with dark eyes,
and breasts rising like a hill as unopened buds.
I too must follow that poet's manner
and sing Aiyai's beauty until her face blushes with modesty!”
said King Katti.

Hearing him, Kaavarp Penn covered her ears!

“Enemies! Even if you kill me in this war-camp,
it does not matter.
Know that I shall not allow the life of green-plumed Aiyai
to be ruined!” she said.

“We shall see about that too! Ho! Who is there?
Shut her in a cage!” ordered King Katti.
