The meaning of the foreign word “Doctor” is teacher—“one who removes ignorance”—a scholar. But not merely a “scholar.” A Doctor is a scholar of ability who clarifies when doubt arises and removes a defect when it is pointed out. Therefore the Tamil expression *Mutharignar*—“elder scholar”—is the most fitting equivalent for that word. Rather than the terms *Munaivar* or *Pandaaragar*, I have continued to use *Mutharignar*.

What I enjoyed in reading Kalaignar's poems—the beauty of Tamil sound, the beauty of meaning, and the beauty of words—has been gathered and offered here so that others too may enjoy it. Even one who does not know the Tamil language can enjoy the pleasure its sound gives the ear. This book is an unfailing fruit-mixture in which many kinds of ripe fruit and fragrant ingredients mingle with honey in sweetness.
