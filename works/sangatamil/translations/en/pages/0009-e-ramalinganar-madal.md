# A Letter from Ki. Ramalinganar!

<div align="right">Date: 19-10-1985.</div>

To the gracious presence of the deeply beloved and revered Kalaignar,

Today I write my reply to your sweet letter dated 26.7.85, a letter pleasing as honey. As soon as I received it, I read it with pride and great delight. I felt grateful. I took the fulfilment of your loving instruction as my first duty.

I set myself to write, in the form of an essay, a foreword for *Sangatamil*, a work in which artistic beauty and poetic beauty are joined, and I have completed it. Professor Mr. Murugesan stood beside me and gave me firm assistance in that work. Though it may not have taken shape in a manner fully pleasing to my own heart, I gain satisfaction from having in some measure fulfilled your instruction.

Because of the unsteadiness brought on by infirmity, I lost my footing and fell. My left hip was struck, and there is pain so severe that I cannot move it. I am confined to bed. Even eating and attending to bodily needs have to take place in bed.

My friend Professor Murugesan came to see me. With his affectionate help, I was able to prepare this. I am unable to come in person and converse with you. I request that you yourself refine the foreword and send it to the press.

If, after knowing your views, there is anything further to be done, I am ready to complete it.

The greatness of your affection in considering even this humble self worthy and asking me to write the foreword is immense. I praise and cherish it.

<div align="right">

Thus, yours,

Ki. Ra.

</div>
