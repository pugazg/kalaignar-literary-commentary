“What is this water in your eyes?  
Did sea-sand get into them, my dear?”

<div align="right">—he asked,</div>

coming to blow it away,  
a caress mingled in his speech.

<div align="right">She stopped him:</div>

“You blow into my eyes—thank you! Thank you!  
But for many days I have been speaking into your ears!

Because you remain without listening to that,  
know that water pours from my eyes like a waterfall!”

“We must leave behind the phase of secret love  
and begin the phase of married love.

For your heart to rejoice,  
our marriage must soon take place  
with happiness overflowing.

Honey-dripping flower of lips! Jewel! Deer-like one!  
Is that not what you desire?” he asked.

She bowed her head and nodded, “Yes,”  
shy as her cheeks reddened like kumkum;

with her big toe she scratched a line  
and drew a little pattern, while overwhelming delight filled her heart.

The hero, filled with the heat of longing,  
patted the swelling curve of her rear,  
shaped like the bowl of a veena, and said:

“Do not grieve in vain, my dear!  
I swear by the moon travelling through the sky:

I shall soon take your hand and marry you.  
Cast off sorrow! Only a few days remain.

Without peering at the sorrowing side of this world,  
let us mingle in joy!

Household life is like walking a rope  
with a jackfruit balanced upon one's head.

Is it good to boil like rice in a pot?  
Or is it good to float like a boat riding a calm wave?

So, beauty of art! My delight! My precious one!  
Let us wait a little before becoming husband and wife.

Let us discover how far love extends—  
let us sound the depth of that sea!”

She, thrilled at the touch of her beloved's hand,  
felt bone, skin and every nerve grow warm;

<div align="right">until then—</div>
