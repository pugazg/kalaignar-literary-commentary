> “Bearing the unfailing,
> victorious sword,
>
> broad-armed and warrior-ankleted,
> you crossed the battlefield
> where enemies fell.
>
> Lord of the Atiyars,
> rich in roaring liquor,
>
> Anji of battle-winning fortune,
> wearing the shining golden garland!
>
> Like the One
> whose forehead shines
> with the milk-white crescent
>
> and whose throat
> bears the blue gem's colour,
>
> may you endure,
> great one!
>
> From a cleft
> in that ancient great mountain,
>
> you obtained
> the rare sweet fruit
> of the small-leaved gooseberry.
>
> Hiding within yourself
> the truth of what it could do,
>
> you gave it to us
> so that death might recede.”

<div align="right">

(*Purananuru* — Poems 87 and 91 — Avvaiyar)

</div>

## Explanation of meaning:

**Thevvir** = enemies.  
**Porunan** = warrior.  
**Vaikal** = one day.  
**Valiththa** = made / fashioned.  
**Valampadu vaay vaal** = an unfailing sword.  
**Onnaar** = enemies.  
**Aarkali naravu** = liquor that resounds with revelry.
