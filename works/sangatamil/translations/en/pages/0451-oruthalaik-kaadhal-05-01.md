# One-Sided Love

## 5

“I have been watching you for several days now!
You are in a state where even honey tastes bitter, girl!
If I see this as a disorder of youth and pretend not to see it,
you may stand at one pole and try to touch the other,
throwing yourself into it like a young calf that knows no fear.
That would be wrong—and it is a mother's duty
to stop it now!
Can a woman with a crippled, limping leg set out
to pluck a kurinji flower from a mountaintop?
When the flag shining upon the finial of the great rolling chariot sways,
and the Chola prince of Uraiyur sets out for the battle-front,
you ran, blocked his way and spoke with him.
If the town saw it, what would it say?
If disgrace comes upon the family of a renowned great merchant,
your father will have to stand before his kin and speak in shame!
We are of the merchant community—he belongs to a lineage born to rule the world!
Do not pine, jewel-bearing maiden, my daughter,
for a love unreachable even if ladders were stacked one upon another!
Your two eyes are swollen like viraal fish!
Go and sleep; they will become like sel-kendai fish again, as before!”

After pouring out anger and affection together
and giving this advice, the mother went away from her.
“If I am to forget her, I must give up my very life!” Narkannai cried,
