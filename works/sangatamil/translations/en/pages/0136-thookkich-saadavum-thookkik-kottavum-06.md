> * “As fierce fire spreads  
> and destroys the forest,
>
> through that beautiful woodland  
> where trees have burned,
>
> they have crossed  
> the cruel, scorching tract
>
> where even shade  
> is hard to find.
>
> I understood it  
> from the sign he gave.
>
> His spear was straightened;  
> its shining blade was wiped.
>
> The shield too  
> was fitted with peacock feathers  
> and bells.
>
> Unlike before,  
> he gave me love without measure.
>
> It seems the day has come, friend—
>
> the day when, grieving again and again,  
> the painted beauty of your eyes
>
> will swim  
> in a flood of tears.”

<div align="center">*</div>

<div align="right">

(*Natrinai* — Poem 177  
Poet: name not known)

</div>

## Explanation of meaning:

**Koor-eri** = intense fire.  
**Kaanam** = forest.  
**Naippa** = to be destroyed.  
**Odhukku arum venchuram** = a hot, harsh tract with no shade even to withdraw into.  
**Irandhanar** = they went / crossed.  
**Palagai** = shield.  
**Pandinum nani-pala alippa** = unlike before, showing me love without measure.
