—I joined them and gave them to you; I rejoiced!
This cluster of sweet Tamil fruit that I tasted,
I have given to you too in simple language.

*

> “From the short-growing *kuravu*,
> the fragrant flower matured from its little bud;
> the breeze mingled with and brought the scent carried by bees.
> In that lovely morning that gives delight to the eyes,
> the heart that, in distress, went
> to the man who loosened my shining bangles—
> does it now suffer, longing to return together with him,
> unable to rest from its task?
> Or, because he has shown no grace,
> has it come back here, ruined,
> looked upon my golden colour stripped of its former beauty,
> said, ‘This woman is a stranger,’
> and gone away again,
> joined now to its own affliction?”

*

(*Natrinai*: poem 56  
Poet: Ilam Peruvazhuthi)

## Explanation of Meaning:

*kuravu* = the *kura* tree. *sirunanai naruvee* = fragrant flower matured from a small bud.  
*vali* = gentle breeze. *elvalai* = shining bangle.  
*allal ureeyar sendra* = having suffered, went toward him.  
*usaa aay* = being a companion. *nasai* = desire.  
*thonnalan* = former well-being (beauty). *ethilaatti* = stranger woman.
