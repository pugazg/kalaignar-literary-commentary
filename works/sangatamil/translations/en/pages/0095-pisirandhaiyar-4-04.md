## Explanation of meaning:

**Thennam-poruppan** = the Pandya king who possesses Podhigai hill in the south.  
**En uyir-ompunan** = the friend who stands as support to my life.  
**Selvak-kaalai** = during prosperous times.  
**Allar-kaalai** = when hardship comes.  
**Nillalan** = he will come without stopping.

**Idaipada seyavaayinum** = though far apart from one another.  
**Thodai-punarndhu** = strung together as an ornament.  
**Aruvilai nankalam** = an ornament of rare value.  
**Paalar-aapa** = they become those beside one another.  
**Saalar** = those who are not worthy / not learned men of character.
