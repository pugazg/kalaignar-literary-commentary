“I do not know where my son has gone!
But he must have gone to some battle-front!
Though the exact place where the tiger has gone may be unknown,
this is the womb that bore the son
and sent him forth as a leader in battle,
like the mountain-cave where the tiger once dwelt, child!”

Hearing Kaavarp Penn's reply,
Narkannai set out toward the red battlefield.
But the mother stopped her
and asked her to stay at her house before setting out.
Narkannai did not refuse!

“Eat, rest and sleep.
Tomorrow, after sunrise,
I shall give you a palm-leaf and send you to Aiyai!
Even if Killi refuses his own sister's words,
Narkannai, I take responsibility
for making the marriage of Narkannai and Narkilli happen!
Killi too may come to Aiyai's palace.
Until then, do not pine so much in separation
that your arms grow thinner still
and your bangles slip away!”

Shy at Kaavarp Penn's teasing,
Narkannai pushed her bangles up as far as her elbows!
