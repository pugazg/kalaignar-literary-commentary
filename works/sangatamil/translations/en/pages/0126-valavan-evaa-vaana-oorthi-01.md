# A Sky-Vehicle Not Driven by a Charioteer!

Because science has grown,  
people of knowledge today travel the skies

and produce astonishing marvels.

The ancient age  
was not an age filled wholly with ignorance.

Even then, people studied  
the bodies of the heavens,

calculated the changes of time,  
charted the movement of planets,

observed stars flashing  
like bubbling lightning-smiles,

watched when they shifted  
from their places,

and told time  
by such observations.

Our ancestors even wrote works  
on the heavens

and explained without fail  
the very days

when the shadow of the earth  
would cover the sun or moon

and bring the phenomenon  
we call an eclipse.

That is no joke!

We who once lived in light  
suddenly fell into darkness,

while the scientific wealth  
of foreign lands

spread upward  
to the roof of the sky.

We called the waxing and waning moon  
a god.

We called the sun,  
which comes every day to perform its duty,  
a deity.

During our hours of idleness  
we reclined upon saltless imagination,

and allowed the astronomical discoveries  
our ancestors had examined and systematised

to become like crops  
withered by drought.

We disappeared  
into foolish tales.

When I immersed my eyes  
in a sweet Sangam poem

to drink the joy  
of literary Tamil—
