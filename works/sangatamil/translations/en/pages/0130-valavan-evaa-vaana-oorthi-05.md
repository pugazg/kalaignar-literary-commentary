## Explanation of meaning:

<div align="right">

(*Purananuru* — Poem 27  
Poet: Uraiyur Mudhukannan Sathanar)

</div>

**Payandha** = blossomed.  
**On-kezh** = a colour filled with radiance.  
**Vizhuththinai** = noble lineage.  
**Marai-ilai** = lotus leaf.  
**Visumbu** = sky.  
**Valavan** = one who drives; a driver; a charioteer.  
**Thingal puththel thiritharum ulagam** = the world in which the moon, as something new, moves about.
