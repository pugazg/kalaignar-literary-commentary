—mix it with the rice of ancient Tondi,  
filling seven large vessels,

even that would be only a tiny offering  
to the crow that removed the sorrow  
of the radiant heroine,” she said.

Even seven vessels of ghee-rice  
cannot equal the help the crow gave—

that is the meaning of this poem,  
preserved in beautiful Tamil *Kuruntokai*!

<div align="center">*</div>

> * “From Nalli's forest land, where hardy cowherds  
> obtain ghee from many cows, and from Tondi  
> where white paddy ripens in abundance—  
> even if seven vessels of hot rice were offered, friend,  
> it would still be little as payment to the crow  
> whose calling announced the coming guest  
> and eased the sorrow that loosened her broad shoulders.”

<div align="center">*</div>

<div align="right">

(*Kuruntokai* — Poem 210  
Poet: Kaakkaip Paadiniyar Nachellaiyar)

</div>

## Explanation of meaning:

**Nalli** = one of the seven great patrons. **Andar** = cowherds.

**Pallaa** = many cows. **Tondi** = the town called Tondi.

**Ezhukalam** = seven vessels. **Karaidhal** = the calling of a crow.

**Pali** = food offered as a share.
