# “Kukkoo!” Cried the Rooster!

## Left panel of the printed spread

Like thumbai flowers  
falling as rain,

like the white beards  
of ascetics

hanging down  
to the ground,

a pure waterfall  
flowed into a pool.

A peahen-like maiden  
bathed there,

splashed and sprang about.

Because her garment  
had become wet,

the full beauty  
of her body

appeared  
as though displayed

inside a glass case.

At that moment  
a young bull saw her  
and grew faint.

“Have two blooming lotuses  
been overturned

upon her chest?

Have their stems—

## Right panel of the printed spread

—been cut off  
at the base

and crouched there  
like bees?

Two dark stalks  
hold round fruits  
that sway—

she stands  
at the peak of attraction!

Her sari looks woven  
from strands of spider-web,

and as she wears it  
she casts spears from her eyes!

Who shaped her thighs  
from plantain stems

and brushed them  
with the colour  
of thaazhai flowers?

Even if a crore in gold  
were poured out

as a reward  
to nature

for creating  
this sandal-coloured sculpture,

could that reward equal  
the beauty

of the two eyelids  
she opens and closes?”

The youth reeled  
with desire.

But when she realised  
that someone hidden

was looking at her,  
she ran away.

He had seen  
the speed of a falling star;

now, from behind a rock  
beside the hill-waterfall,

he saw before his eyes  
the cool moon

suddenly leap away  
and disappear.

He left his life  
and memory there

and carried away  
only his body  
in the chariot!
