The poet Ammuvanar,  
in the wave-embraced neydhal land,

paints with beauty the scene  
in which those who practise the art of love rejoice!

And in the paalai land too,  
saying that there are birds that live by the ways of love,

the poet Othalanthaiyar  
speaks with compassion of the pain of separation.

Humans of sixfold awareness who live in the five kinds of land,  
creatures without such discerning knowledge that crawl, walk and fly,

forests, waterfalls, beautiful groves,  
mountain ranges rising one behind another,

the sea expanse rocked by the hands of waves,  
and everywhere the ways in which living beings live—

observing all of these closely,  
the poets gave five hundred pearl-like songs in Tamil!

They are springs of nectar from a rich inner life.  
Let us first plunge into one poem  
and rejoice within it!

In the neydhal land,  
on the shore where the sea roars,

a maiden of golden beauty,  
her form itself an adornment,

reclined upon a mound of sand  
and gazed at the fiery colour of the west.

From beside the flower-laden punnai tree  
came the physician who could cure her illness!

“You have come, physician!” she cried with delight,  
and, resting her head upon his shoulder, said,  
“Give me the medicine!”

“For this illness that buds in the morning,  
remains in bud through the day  
and blooms in the evening,

both of us are stricken and suffering.  
Therefore, with each as the other's medicine,  
we can drive the illness away,” he said,

embracing the golden statue of a woman!

Upon the mattress of the wide-spread seashore  
they fell, rolled, rose and rejoiced!
