# Mullai in Ollaiyur?

Ollaiyur,
an ancient town fragrant
with canopies of mullai,
is identified here with Oliyamangalam
in the Pudukkottai region.

Born to the notable
Oliyamangalam Kizhar of that place
was the famed great warrior
Perunchathan.

Whenever war arose
between the Pandyas and Cholas,
he gathered armies
and heaped victories
upon the Pandya side.

He had long shoulders
that could lift a hill,
and a hand that poured gifts.

Tamil scholars and elders
of good Tamil land
strung garlands of refined Tamil
and praised that noble giver.

Among them were
Kudavayil Keerathanar,
born under the Chola
whose tiger moved upon the flag,
and Thodithalai Vizhuthandinar—
two poets.

They heard the whole country praise
his courage that destroyed enemies in a moment
and his giving heart
that removed poverty.

Their own hearts filled with joy.
They sought his friendship
and became his friends.

Though Perunchathan stood
on the Pandya side
against the Chola,
Keerathanar did not fail
to praise his noble qualities.

The poet seems to have possessed
the great-minded spirit
that even jasmine
from an opponent's garden
still has fragrance.

Then one day,
news spread across the Tamil land
like a spear splitting a mountain:

“Great warrior Perunchathan
has fallen ill
and gone to sleep
in the lap of death.”
