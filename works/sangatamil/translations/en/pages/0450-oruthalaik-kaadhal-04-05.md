it is the thunder of the monsoon!
In such a state, if enemies are caught in his hands—
alas, who might those pitiable ones be?”

Disliking the poet's praise, he lowered his head.
Narkannai raised hers in swelling pride and delight.

Then a voice rang out:

“What is this, girl? Where have you come—to a place like this?
What business has a fly where iron is being forged?”

Perunthevi, her mother, appeared like a chariot of golden jewels.
Scolding her until her smile vanished,
she pulled her back into the house.

## Explanation of meaning:

*Kaarppeyal urumin* = the thunderous roar of the rainy season.  
*Naar-seriyath thoduttha aar-kanni* = an aathi garland strung tightly with fibre.  
*Aliyar* = those deserving pity.  
*Kavigai mallan* = a generous warrior.
