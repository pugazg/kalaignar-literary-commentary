# The Son Who Went in the Bell-Adorned Chariot!

Song  
Raga: Bhimpalas  
Tala: Tisra Adi

(Opening)

Her son who went in the bell-adorned chariot has still not returned;
he does not wish to return without driving the enemy away.
That is why, even after a thousand warriors of that town came back,
even after every one of their homes rang with rejoicing,
her son who went in the bell-adorned chariot has still not returned.

(Verse 1)

Many warriors bearing honourable wounds came home.
To tend their wounds with neem leaves,
their spear-eyed companions brought leafy branches
and fixed them at the fronts of their homes.

Her son who went in the bell-adorned chariot has still not returned;
he does not wish to return without driving the enemy away.

(Verse 2)

They sang the Kanchi *pann* to comfort the wounded;
they burned mustard in ghee and let its smoke mingle with the air.
With a resounding “kal” in every house,
they eased the weariness of those who had returned from the battlefield.

Her son who went in the bell-adorned chariot has still not returned;
he does not wish to return without driving the enemy away.

(Verse 3)

To return without completing the task entrusted to him—
that would diminish the principle he held;
and so he would not count what had been gained so far as victory—
that commander would not be content!
