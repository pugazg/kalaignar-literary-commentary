—forget the wrong
I committed without understanding,
woman!

Show me grace
and take me back,
my dear!”

He lamented and wept,
almost falling
at her feet.

She, however,
did not reduce her anger
even by an atom.

“Who am I, sir,
to show you grace?

All those beautiful quails

whom you once favoured
with overflowing love

and later abandoned
without grace

are still waiting
for you to return
and favour them again.

Do not forsake them.

Go back there!”

she said,
then went inside.

The poor man
remained standing there
like a statue!
