# O Valiant Bull—Will You Come Riding the Madal?

Song  
Raga: Kalyani  
Tala: Adi

(Opening)

Why did she say she would not marry me?
Shall I ride the *madal* and come ask in the great public assembly? Me—
why did she say she would not marry me? Shall I
ride the *madal* and come ask in the great public assembly?

(Why did she say she would not marry me?)

(Verse 1)

With wholehearted love I searched over a long distance,
falling into water-filled hollows, rising and running on;
I plucked and brought the *panchai* reeds!
I made and gave a doll just like that maiden!

(Why did she say she would not marry me?)

(Verse 2)

Shoulders like bamboo, a full-moon face,
teeth of *mullai*, words that shed pearls—
listen, friend, and I shall tell how I fashioned the doll
just like your heroine, my beloved!

(Why did she say she would not marry me?)

(Verse 3)

Sitting near the peacock in its season, I studied the beauty of her body;
I made a form just like her and finished it at once.
All day I praised the name of that doll-maiden;
I engraved only her as a picture within my heart.

(Why did she say she would not marry me?)
