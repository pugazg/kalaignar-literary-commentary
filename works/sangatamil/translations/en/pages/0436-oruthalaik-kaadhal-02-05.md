and if he laughs aloud and tells me,
‘Go back the way you came,’
would not my flower of love, before bearing fruit,
become a blossom that opened in the morning and withered at once?
My eyes have seen the sun and the moon,
the sea, sky, mountain and cloud,
peacock, dove, parrot and cuckoo,
lion, tiger, leopard and deer,
the beauty of nature, delightful groves,
sculptures shaped by art and distinguished works of art;
yet after seeing them my eyes
returned to my face in a moment!
Do you know my state today? The eyes
that saw that sweet man
have still not returned to me!
I have told you this truth.”

Hearing her speak, Porkodi, her friend,
understood that no physician of any land could cure
the illness that had come upon Narkannai.
She resolved to break this news
to Perungozhi Naaykan!

Just then,
“Daughter! Narkannai!”
called the great merchant as he entered the house.
“Listen, dear, to one piece of joyful news!
I have invited Perunarkilli, who defeated the wrestler,
to our house for the midday feast tomorrow!
With the consent of the poet Saathanthai,
he too has agreed,” he said.

The little toes of her gem-like feet began to thrill;
the two mango-fruits swelled still fuller;
the good maiden's whole body broke into gooseflesh,
and her cheeks turned henna-red.
With a leap that would shame a fawn, Narkannai
fell into her friend's lap without even fainting!
