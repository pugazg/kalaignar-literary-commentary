# The Verse Sung by Pari's Daughters

He bore a sword
in his hand;
he bore generosity
in his heart!

In Tamil history
he bore the name
Vel Pari!

Though the three crowned kings
joined together,
surrounded his Parambu hill,
and laid siege to it,

he did not tremble,
fold his knees,
bow in worship,
and think only
of saving his crown!

Many enemies together
might defeat
King Pari,

but no one
could defeat
his nature of giving
in abundance.

That is why,
though years upon years
have turned and changed,

Pari's name
has remained here
unchanged!

The world does not even know
which three kings
were those who
“defeated him,
brought him down,
and killed him.”

Yet even though
he fell in battle
and gave up his life,

why does Pari's name
still move among us,
undestroyed,
unhidden,
alive?

Being a warrior alone
does not earn fame
among humankind.

A man must also live
as a human being,

and every step
he measures out
without straying
even an atom
from that path of virtue

becomes the road
on which generation after generation
of society's descendants
will travel.

Like the linchpin
of a chariot,
Pari stood king
over many towns
around Parambu hill.

After calling him to war
and killing him,

the three kings
thought of seizing
his two daughters—
