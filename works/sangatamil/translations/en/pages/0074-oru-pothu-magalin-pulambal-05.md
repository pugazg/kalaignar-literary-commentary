> who speaks great words among us,  
> but at home raises hand and foot  
> only as his child's mother raises hers,  
> like a dancing doll in a mirror,  
> doing whatever pleases her!”

<div align="right">

(*Kuruntokai* — Poem 8; Poet: Alangudi Vanganar)

</div>

<div align="center">*</div>

## Explanation of meaning:

**Kazhanii maa-aththu vilaindhu ugu theempazham** = a sweet fruit ripening and falling from a mango tree near the cultivated field.

**Pazhana vaalai kathu-um** = a vaalai fish in the nearby pond bites and seizes it.

**Aadippaavai** = the figure seen in a mirror.
