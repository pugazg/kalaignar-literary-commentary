**Short man:**

Like a plough
fitted with its heavy share,

your back falls
and rises again—

that flawless beauty
bewilders me!

Why do you remain silent?
Tell me what you think.

Otherwise,
kill me yourself!

**Bent-backed woman:**

Do I look like a plough?

Did you even consider
the time
before mocking me?

You,
no taller than
a gaming board
stood upright—

are you the one
to argue about
my beauty?

You are a man;
should you not know
how one invites
a woman to union?

Young bull,
have you grown so proud
that even time and occasion
need not be considered?

**Short man:**

Woman whose body
has the beauty
of a plucked crane!

If I embrace you
against my chest,
your bent back
will press into me.

If, with desire,
I embrace you
from behind,

your curved back
will tickle me!

If you do not mind,
let us do one thing:

stand side by side
and embrace
until our hunger is satisfied!

**Bent-backed woman:**

Without even knowing
that you are half a man,
what a speech
you have made to me!

Many men come
asking as a boon
to embrace me
like a creeper
around a tree.

Not knowing that,
what defect
did you find in me

that you spoke
such hurtful words?

You who stand tall
as though a tortoise
had risen upright
to touch the sky…

Ah, ah!
And now a walk
like the god of love himself?

What beauty!
Go, go!…
