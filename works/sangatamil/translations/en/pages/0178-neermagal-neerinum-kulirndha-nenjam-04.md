—take hold of her forehead beneath its thick brows
and end her distress,
O king,”
the poet said.

“Leave the battlefront,
go embrace
the Chera queen
to your chest,

and order the horses
to be harnessed
to your chariot today!”

he said.

“As you set out
to seek your smiling,
beautiful companion,

look at the boundless joy
on every enemy face here!

Without the sound
of Ilanjeral Irumporai's
battle-drum,

their eardrums too
will finally rest
for a few days!

For that reason,
they will all agree at once:

go to ancient Vanji
and see your Vanji!”

the poet said.

“The very hours
you spend there
with the queen

will be sleeping-time
for the enemy kings here.

So, show mercy
and let them sleep
for a little while!”

he added.

The Chera heard
the poet's words

and nodded
to grant a brief rest

to the enemies
who scattered
from the battlefront!

He set out
to end the longing
of the queen,

who had left her very life
with him

and remained distressed
with streams
of dark-lined tears.

All the great warriors,
hearing his order,

ran and embraced
the tender bodies
of their gold-like beloveds!

“Long live the Tamil poet!”

they all thundered,
like waves
of the sounding sea.

Ilanjeral,
joining again
with the great Chera queen,

rose in astonishment:

“Has the night
already passed?”

And when the heroic king
pressed a thousand kisses

upon the beautiful breast
where necklaces rolled,

she too—
