## Source-fidelity repairs

SC9 made source-fidelity repairs in **5 English page files**:

1. scan **304** — restored `கட்டுக்குலையாப் பட்டுமேனி` as a **silken body whose form has not slackened**, replacing the inaccurate “unfailing silken form.”
2. scan **307** — corrected `வேங்கை` in the pavilion painting from a **tree** to a **tiger**, matching the immediately following fear that a living tiger would spring upon the female elephant.
3. scan **317** — restored the source relation in `அல்லல் உறீஇச் சென்ற நெஞ்சம்`: the **heart itself went in distress** toward the man who loosened her shining bangles; the first draft incorrectly made it share his distress.
4. scan **318** — removed the unsupported qualifier **“illicit”** from the opening `கள்ள மது`; the following prose itself separately develops the joke about stolen liquor and government permission.
5. scan **333** — corrected Kannagi's `தேரா மன்னா` from **“unseeing king”** to **“undiscerning king,”** aligning it with the immediately preceding description of judgment delivered without investigation.

These are English-layer fidelity repairs only. Canonical Tamil wording was not changed.
