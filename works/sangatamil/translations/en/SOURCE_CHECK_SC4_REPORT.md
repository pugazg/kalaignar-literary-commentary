## Source-fidelity repairs

SC4 made source-fidelity repairs in **6 English page files**:

1. scan **121** — restored the singular source title `வண்டு வந்தது ஏனடி?` as **“Why Did the Bee Come, Girl?”**, rather than pluralizing the title.
2. scan **129** — removed an unsupported negation from the classical line `வல்லார் ஆயினும், வல்லுநர் ஆயினும்`; the English now preserves the two affirmative source terms instead of saying “strong or not strong.”
3. scan **133** — replaced the conventionalized **“Book of Love”** label with the source-aligned **“section on pleasure”** for `இன்பத்துப்பால்`.
4. scan **135** — rendered `இருவிழியின் பாவைகள்` as the **pupils of the two eyes** disappearing beneath the flood of tears; the difficult `உற்றாரைத் ...` opening remains conservatively tied to the maintained Tamil without inventing a canonical repair.
5. scan **137** — corrected the cross-page subject at the scan-137→139 boundary: the Pandyan had **heard the Tamil verse of the learned poets**; the first draft had reversed the relationship and made the poets hear his verse.
6. scan **148** — removed an unsupported invented poet identity from the unusual maintained-Tamil wording `குளமுற்றொருவர்`; the English now renders it conservatively without fabricating a named poet.

These are English-layer fidelity repairs only. Canonical Tamil wording was not changed.
