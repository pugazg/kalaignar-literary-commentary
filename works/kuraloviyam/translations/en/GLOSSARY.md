# Controlled English Glossary — Kuraloviyam

This glossary records editorial defaults for the **project-created English translation** of `குறளோவியம்`.

Defaults are context-aware. If a later passage requires a different rendering to preserve meaning, record that decision during review rather than forcing one English equivalent mechanically.
