## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a farming family beneath a tree, with a small child and a food pot.

The beautiful woman opened her eyes as morning itself was budding. “There is so much work in the field. Wake up!” she said, touching him and pressing the bud of her lips upon his face before sending him off to labour. The worker who, with his shoulders, would lift the watery earth and make it yield gold and gems entered the field with the speed of a sword-bearing warrior entering battle. He began to pour out his sweat. “They alone truly live who live by ploughing and eating; all others follow behind, living by bowing to them,” he told himself with pride—
