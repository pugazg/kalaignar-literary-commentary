"He called my cheeks cups of wine.

"He called my breasts pomegranates.

"He said, 'Fine thread—that is what your lightning-waist is!'

"I lay with eyes closed upon his lap, intoxicated by his words.

"His hand combed through my hair.

"I cannot tell the end of that story.

"No one can even imagine it fully.

"Only through experience can that nectar-filled hour be understood.

"All of that is true, my heart!

"You rejoice in praising him as the one who poured out incomparable delight upon you.

"I respect your gratitude.

"But now he has gone away, plunging you into the pain of separation.

"You lie longing because he has not come back at once to show compassion and comfort you.

"And even in this condition you keep running after him, chasing behind him.

"Ayyo, my heart! You have no sense at all.

"You are a fool!"

She grieves:

"O heart, even while you yearn that the lover who left has not returned to show you affection, you run behind that very lover. What a great fool you are!"

This Kural identifies that woman:

> O my foolish heart! You long that the one who left gives no love,  
> yet you still follow behind the one who has gone.

Chapter 125 — Speaking with the Heart; Kural 1248

நல்கார் = will not show love
