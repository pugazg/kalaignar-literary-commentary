## Visual material

Large upper illustration of Kovalan, Madhavi and a child against a Puhar/port-city backdrop.

“I want no gold—I want no wealth—I want no fame granted by emperors who rule the earth. If the man of my eyes remains beside me always, I want no flesh, no life, no honey, not even the world where the gods dwell!”

Madhavi, the good woman, sang these words of love with her face buried against Kovalan's as she held him in her lap. The son of Masattuvan lay entranced, drinking again and again with his eyes the beauty of her moving lips.
