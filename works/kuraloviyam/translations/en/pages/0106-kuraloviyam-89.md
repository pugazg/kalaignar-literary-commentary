## Visual material

Colour illustration above the prose showing two uniformed men at a training-camp setting, one seated and one standing.

Kalingan and Kathiravan were close friends. After being apart for a few days, they met one day and spoke about several happenings in the town. With keen interest Kathiravan asked Kalingan:

“Friend, how is that anger of yours these days? Are you still flaring up in rage and pouncing as before? Or have you reformed yourself and become like a breeze?”

Smiling, Kalingan answered Kathiravan's question:

“My dear friend! Such a change has come over me that you will be astonished! I shall tell you about an incident—listen! It happened only yesterday. When you hear it, you will praise me!”

“What wonderful incident is that? Tell me that too; I am listening.”

“Yesterday I was sitting in the training camp. Many warriors were seated with me. At that time the commander came that way. I did not notice his arrival. The other warriors who saw him coming stood up and paid their respects. By the time he came near me—”
