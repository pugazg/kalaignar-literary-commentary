## Visual material

Large colour night illustration of Poonguzhali waiting by a tree while a distant figure approaches through the grove.

There was no moonlight that night. Only the stars blinked like Karthigai lamps. With an uncontrollable feeling of delight spreading through her firm young body, and with eyes that trembled in expectation, Poonguzhali waited in the mango grove. He had said that he would come there to meet her and unite with her. A week earlier, their meeting had taken place in that same grove. The mango trees, which had watched without appearing to notice the pictures of love and union the two had drawn there, now saw Poonguzhali standing alone and seemed to wait: when would he come? When would the next scene continue?

Remembering again and again the sweetness she had known that day, warming her lovely body with the memory, Poonguzhali leaned against a tree and performed a penance of waiting for the lover's shoulder that ought to have been there for her to lean upon. One naazhigai passed—then another hurried by.
