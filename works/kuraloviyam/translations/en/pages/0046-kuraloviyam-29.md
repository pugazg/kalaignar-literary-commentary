“O king of kings! Doesn't every undertaking require certain facilities and opportunities? I shall certainly carry out the deeds I announced to the people of the country and before Your Majesty. But there is one thing. I have travelled for many days and become exhausted. If it pleases you, I request that you receive me as a guest of the palace for a month or two. Once my body has regained great strength, I shall demonstrate my first plan—the feat of carrying a mountain on my head.”

The king gladly accepted his request. “Why merely a month or two? Take a full three months! During those three months every facility you require will be provided to you in the palace,” he said, patting him approvingly.

The public counted the days until they could witness the young man’s heroic feats.

He had a life of pleasure in the palace! Foods that gave strength to the body! Drinks that brought delight to the heart! Whatever he asked for was given to him!

The king kept telling his ministers that if the young man actually fulfilled the plans he had announced, he intended to appoint him commander-in-chief of his army.

Three months came to an end. The day was announced on which the first plan—the plan to walk carrying the mountain on his head—would be carried out. The whole country gathered at the foot of the mountain. The king, the distinguished ministers and the palace officials sat in their appointed places. The young man arrived in a colourful chariot and stepped down. His body shone with new splendour and strength. The effect of three months of palace life was clearly visible in his physique.

When they saw him, the king and the people welcomed him with applause. Drums thundered. Musical instruments sounded. The young man raised both hands. Every sound stopped and silence settled. He walked towards the foot of the mountain. Everyone watched without blinking, intent upon what was about to happen.

“I am ready to walk carrying the mountain on my head!” he shouted. A single roar of joy went up!

But what happened? He did not lift the mountain. Again and again he merely said, “I am ready to walk carrying the mountain on my head.” Having grown tired of hearing the same words repeated, everyone moved closer to him.

“Why do you keep saying the same thing? Show us by doing it!” the king shouted in a loud voice.
