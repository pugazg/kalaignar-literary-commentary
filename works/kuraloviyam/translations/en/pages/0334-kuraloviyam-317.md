## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a young woman carrying a bundle facing a man who has fallen in a muddy paddy field.

“It is not my fault, dear! It is not my fault! It is the fault of my eyes!

I am undergoing the punishment for that offence. Carrying a bundle of grass, I was coming along a narrow footpath through the fields. Did I expect my aunt's son Oliyan, who was working at the sluice with a hoe in his hand, to come toward me? To give me way, he moved aside along the edge of the narrow path. The bund slipped beneath him; he fell flat in the mud of the paddy field. I am a young woman of marriageable age! Could I run over and lift him up? Decorum stopped me! Modesty made me stand like a statue! Only my eyes did not fail to drink him in from head to foot as he fell in the field and rose again!

Dear! Those are wicked eyes! Criminals beyond forgiveness! Ah! One or two tongues would not be enough to describe the beauty of Oliyan's body!”
