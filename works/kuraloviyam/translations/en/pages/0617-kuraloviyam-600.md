There was a mischievous young man named Kunran.

His teacher often gave him one piece of advice:

"Read the Kural. Live by it."

That advice tasted bitter to him.

"The Kural is a treasury of wisdom that lays out a way of life. If men and women alike live according to it, they will win honour in whatever responsibility they undertake, become people of firm heart, and stand as examples of the grammar of life."

The teacher never failed to say this each day to his students, and especially to Kunran.

One day Kunran decided to turn his mischief upon the teacher himself.

And so he did.

The teacher sat beneath a tree in a chair reading a book.

Kunran came before him and, where the teacher could clearly see, began spinning himself around like a top.

The teacher watched.

Still spinning around himself, Kunran slowly began circling the very tree whose shade covered the teacher.

Surprised, the teacher asked:

"What are you doing?"

"I am following the way of the Kural, sir!" Kunran replied with a smile.
