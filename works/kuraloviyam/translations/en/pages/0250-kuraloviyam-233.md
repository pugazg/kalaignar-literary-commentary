## Visual material

Large upper illustration of lovers, with a woman also shown facing a mirror.

After saying that “lovers' quarrel is itself pleasure in love” and making us understand its greatness, Valluvar sharply declares in another Kural that not even a millet-seed's measure of quarrel should be allowed.

> When desire has grown to the measure of a palmyra,  
> one should not quarrel even to the measure of a millet seed.

When desire has grown as large as a palmyra, one must not think, “Let us try even a millet-seed's measure of quarrel.” Does he stop merely by saying it should not be done? No indeed!
