## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a married couple, with imagery of shared livelihood and intimacy.

Ah, what a maiden she was! Yes—Valluvar specially praises her beautiful *maamai* complexion. It seems to be a colour somewhere between the hue of a mango and that of an emerald. A fortunate young man receives that treasure-chest of new and wondrous beauty as the lady of his household.

The fountain of delight in domestic life, the honeycomb that removes the weariness of labour—is it not the stage called the “joy of union”? Two hearts become like milk and sugar candy; two bodies become like strands of braided hair. It is a time when, even if God himself were perhaps to appear before them and say—
