Large colour illustration showing two women in the foreground with a man's face represented in the sun behind them.

Thinking, “No woman in this world can be as shaken in heart as I am,” the beautiful maiden shed tears. Like drops of dew that gather upon mango leaves and then slide down over mango fruit, those tears ran along her cheeks.

“He said, ‘I will never leave you, just as the pulse never leaves the heart.’ Yet here he has left me. And still my heart has not stopped,” she sighed deeply.

“He said, ‘You are the breath of my life.’ Yet even after that breath has gone away from me, here I remain alive!” In the morning, after showering her with kisses, he had left saying, “I will come tomorrow.” Unable to wait even until the next morning, that smiling maiden suffered terribly.
