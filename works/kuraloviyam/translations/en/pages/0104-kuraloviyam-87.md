A warrior named Villavan stood at the entrance of his home. At that moment the war drums of the enemy forces were heard at the border of his country. At the same time came the call of his own king: “Let one warrior from every household set out to drive away the enemy entering our land.”

To be ever ready—always ready—to defend the soil of one's birth: is that not the mark of a warrior? Villavan was the very example of that ideal.
