"A fine, abundant name!"

"May your name be announced?"

"You ask though you already know! Still, let me tell you—my name is Karikalan. Kaveri and Karikalan have an inseparable bond, don't they?"

"Yes! Wasn't Kaveri born in Karnataka and did she not come seeking Karikalan all the way to the city of Poompuhar? Can history be hidden?"

"I came to ask for a boon, but you turn me aside toward the glory of history!"

"Is it not strange that someone should come asking a boon from one who is herself performing penance to obtain a boon?"

"What boon are you doing penance for? From whom?"

"And what is the boon you came to ask?"

"Do you still not understand? Can polished gold fail to shine?"

"There is also a proverb that all that glitters is not gold!"

"You speak beautifully!"

"Perhaps that is the benefit of seeing you!"

"Do you respect me that much?"

"Respecting one another is good culture!"

"Kaveri! I am longing to ask you openly!"

"Why only long for it? Ask boldly!"

"If I ask, you won't turn into a little tiger, will you?"

"Asking is your right. Giving or refusing is my position!"

"You are pushing me into a circle of doubt!"

"Do you not understand that I am suffering, caught in a circle of love?"

"Kaveri! The boon I wanted has already reached me! My silence? Ah, what shyness! Where... where...!"

We have seen two lovers speak so much in order to reveal the love surging in their hearts and become two lives made one—taking advantage, moreover, of a moment of solitude with no one else around.
