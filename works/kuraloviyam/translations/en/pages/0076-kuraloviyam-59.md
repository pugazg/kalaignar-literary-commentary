## Visual material

Large colour illustration above the prose showing a crowd of men in agitation and rising action, directly introducing the oppressive-rule vignette.

The citizens writhed like a mass of worms thrown into a furnace. They could no longer endure the demonic dance of the regime. They did not even have the right to cry, “Alas, we cannot bear this!” Locks on their mouths; shackles on their hands. Hunger for punishment was thrust upon the people every day through the gates of the prison. Cruel taxation broke and crushed the backbone of the poor. The frenzied dance of poverty raged on. If anyone raised a voice saying, “I am hungry,” those paupers were sent away to a place where they would no longer feel hunger.

In the name of establishing peace throughout the country, the government created the silence of darkness. While claiming that it revered discipline as the very eye of order, it imposed a scheme of control and slavery upon the citizens through repression. There is a saying: if one says “hmm,” prison; if one asks “why?”, exile. Under that rule, even if one did not say “hmm,” it was prison; even if one did not ask “why?”, it was exile. Putting dissenters to the stake and dragging advisers before courts in order to settle scores became the regime's amusements.

In that land of cruelty, no just sceptre was wielded; red-hot fire itself ruled. The people were being ruined like oxen bound to an oil press. Thinkers were crushed like tiny ants. Slanderers, meanwhile, received a daily feast under that dishonest regime.
