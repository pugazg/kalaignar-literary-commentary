In a pond where gentle little ripples sway, the surface water suddenly becomes disturbed even though no stone has been thrown from above.

How? How does it happen?

A clash among the fish playing beneath the water creates agitation upon the clear surface of the pond.

The man's face appears peaceful.

Touched by the breeze's hand, his smile seems to savour that delight. Light seems to dance across the face filled with calm.

His dearest friend sits beside him and, with feeling, recounts how Jain and Buddhist ascetics endured many hardships and laboured without rest to explain and spread their principles among the people of the world.

Listening to those noble accounts, the man becomes one with the events.

His face is like that quiet pond where clear, gentle ripples play.

Then a song is heard from somewhere.

Yes—the song he once heard.

A song he had heard many times:

> Sweet nectar of delight! Life of love!  
> Flower of my heart! Ray of dawn!  
> Forgetting myself, let me embrace you—  
> forgetting yourself, embrace me—  
> come near! Pour yourself over me!
