## Visual material

Large colour illustration above the prose showing bearers carrying a curtained palanquin along a road while two figures observe in the background.

Some men were carrying a palanquin along the road. Inside it, a man reclined and travelled while delighting in the natural scenery on either side of the road. With aching necks and blistered feet, the palanquin bearers continued their labour.

Two elderly men watched the scene from a pavilion. One of them had read many Tamil works. He said, “Did you see? This is the perfect example of a birth that committed sin and a birth that performed merit!” The other elder eagerly asked, “How? How?” The learned elder explained:

“Valluvar has portrayed this very finely in the Thirukkural. There is no need to investigate through books what benefit arises from virtue. If you simply look at the man travelling in the palanquin and the men carrying it, you can understand who among them practised virtue. In accordance with the virtue one has done, one rides in the palanquin. Those who did not practise virtue carry it.”
