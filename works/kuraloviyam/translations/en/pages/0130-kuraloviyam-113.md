## Visual material

Large colour illustration showing an elderly man and a younger companion beside a public gathering with a speaker platform and loudspeakers.

An elderly man whose eyesight had dimmed somewhat also had hearing that was slightly weak.

His grandson was walking with him as a companion along the road.

Because the light of his eyes had not failed completely, there was no need for the boy to lead him by the hand. Even so, the grandson walked close beside his grandfather as support.

A short distance away, hundreds of people had gathered. In the middle of the crowd was a platform. A man stood upon it. Though the old man's eyes could not see clearly, he could make out, like a play of shadows, that there was a man on the platform and a crowd around him. Looking toward the bright-eyed young man, the elder asked, “What is that gathering over there, son?” The young man did not give work only to his eyes; he gave work to his ears as well, listened sharply, and after a little while answered his grandfather.

“Grandfather! A man is standing in an orchard there, plucking and eating unripe fruit instead of ripe fruit. The people are standing around watching the sight for amusement.”
