It was a time wrapped in darkness. Lady Nature had turned away her radiant face and displayed the beauty of her black hair, enchanting living creatures, making them dance in delight, then tiring them into sleep, while now and then she smiled by showing her starry teeth. Cutting through the music of leaves stirring gently in the breeze, the clamorous cry of a bird could often be heard. Whenever that great sound rose, the other birds sleeping on the branches would open their eyes a little, lift their wings slightly, press their little feet more firmly into the branches, flutter for a moment, and then become quiet again.

Seeing the whole bird-kind tremble at the sound of its uproarious voice filled that owl with delight. With an arrogant heart that believed all the many-coloured little birds, the countless pigeons and every bird in that dense forest were its slaves—too weak and powerless to oppose it—the owl went on crying out.
