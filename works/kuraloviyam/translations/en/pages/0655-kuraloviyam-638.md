"So abandon the sulk and let me rise with you to the finial atop the tower of union.

"Take my hand and begin walking with me through the garden of pleasure.

"Is not meeting after separation a flood of supreme delight?

"Open the door and give way to that joy of love!"

After the lover bent his heart and pleaded with her in this way, she too closed the door of the bedchamber.

Opening lips blossomed with joy, fragrant with modesty and sweet honey, she said:

"Dear..."

Would he simply stand still after that?

"Life of my life! We are one life!"

He swept that spotted peahen into his arms.

Understanding everything she desired, he gave her pleasures neither of them had known before.

Then, like a flash of lightning, a thought entered the honey-tongued woman's mind.

What was it?

"All this—the great love he shows, joining me and giving me delight exactly as I wish—is it perhaps a sign that he will leave me again and reveal his lack of love?"

Here is the Kural for that thought:

> His great display of love, joining me exactly as I desire,  
> carries within it the thought of a hard parting and lovelessness.

Chapter 128 — Making the Sign Understood; Kural 1276

பெரிதாற்றி = showing great love  
பெட்ப = as desired
