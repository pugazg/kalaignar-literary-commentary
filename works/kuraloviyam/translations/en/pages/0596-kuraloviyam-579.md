No one came forward to stop his outrages and raise a sense of self-respect in Azhagarkudi.

Many miles from Azhagarkudi, a young ascetic who lived in a cave cut into a mountain rock—a man whose heart followed the way of the Kural—heard the news and grieved deeply.

To some people of Azhagarkudi who came to see him, he reproached them with the words: "One also comes to ruin by failing to do what ought to be done."

The people of Azhagarkudi did not understand what the young ascetic meant.

"One can be ruined by failing to do what must be done. All the people of Azhagarkudi could have united, opposed that cruel Sevathaan, and driven him out. It is because you failed to do so that so much harm occurred," he explained.

"Yes! We shall do exactly that!" cried the people of Azhagarkudi.

They gathered and marched toward Sevathaan's house. At that very moment he had suffered a heart attack and was pleading for his life.

As death approached, he spoke to the villagers:

"All of you forgive me. I have done you many wrongs. After I die, do not even bury my corpse in the burial ground of this village. Take this sinner's body and bury it in the next village's cemetery."

The people accepted his final request.

When he closed his eyes, they carried the body away and buried it in the neighbouring village's burial ground.

On hearing the news, the people of that village angrily gathered there.

"How dare you bury an outsider's corpse in our village?"

They erupted in fury. The result? The two sides clashed. Twenty bodies from the two villages fell in the burial ground. Hundreds were badly wounded.

The young ascetic heard the news, ran there, and brought about peace.

"First you came to grief by not doing what ought to have been done. Now, listening to Sevathaan, you have come to grief again by doing what ought not to have been done!"

he said sorrowfully.

The people of Azhagarkudi stood with bowed heads and exhausted hearts.

> One is ruined by doing what ought not to be done;  
> one is ruined also by failing to do what ought to be done.
