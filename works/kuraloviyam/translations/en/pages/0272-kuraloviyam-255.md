## Visual material

Large upper illustration of two women in the household setting connected with a Ramayana discourse.

A Ramayana kathakalakshepam was being conducted with elaborate arrangements. The Bhagavathar's manner of describing the scenes of Rama's coronation was remarkable. Devotees nodded their heads in appreciation. The hall overflowed with women as well. The men and women present were all from what was regarded as high social caste—and they were wealthy too.

The Bhagavathar's discourse and songs on the fourteen years Rama, Sita and Lakshmana spent in the forest, the Rama–Ravana war, and finally Rama's return and coronation sent all the listeners there floating in a flood of enthusiasm.

The kathakalakshepam ended at midnight. The Bhagavathar went to the house where he was staying. It was the home of a very wealthy man of a so-called high caste in that town. The lady of the house had also attended the Ramayana discourse. A maidservant from the household had accompanied her. Her caste, according to that social ranking, was considered somewhat lower.
