## Visual material

Large colour illustration of Gandhi walking with a staff, flanked by inset images of a spinning wheel and a handgun.

A figure carved from sandalwood seems to be walking toward us. In his hand is a walking stick. Even that is placed gently upon the ground, as though he fears the earth might hurt. A face that pours forth grace; eyes overflowing with compassion; flower-petals of a toothless mouth storing a smile. Though hundreds surround him, all of them appear to our eyes like stars in daylight, while he alone seems the sun. He is the embodiment of truth, the sacred form of love. He is that ocean of compassion, Gandhi.

The path of righteousness alone is his path. When confronting injustice, he will go toward the field of struggle ready even to sacrifice himself. To win freedom for the soil of India, he bathed in the fire of sacrifice. From that fire he emerged like refined gold. Wherever slavery existed, he hastened there to remove it. He raised the banner to uproot the cruelty of caste that declares one human being untouchable by another. He laboured throughout the country for the complete abolition of evils that bring society down—drinking, horse-racing and the like. Wherever uproar, riots and confusion...
