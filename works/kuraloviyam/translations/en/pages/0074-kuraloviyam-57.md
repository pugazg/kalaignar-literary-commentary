Large colour illustration above the prose showing women, children and a man gathered around a fruit basket, with food-serving imagery in the background.

The daughter of a close relative was about to be married. The village was a long distance away. Yet it was not possible simply to stay away.

So he set out with his wife and children to attend the wedding. Night interrupted the journey. They therefore found themselves in a situation where they would have to sleep in a village midway before continuing.

There was an old travellers' choultry there where people on the road could stay. He lay down there with his family and slept. He snored soundly. The fatigue of travel had brought deep sleep. It was only long after daybreak that the family awoke.

His wife opened her eyes first and cried out in alarm, “What is this! The bundles and baggage are all missing!”

“Alas! My money pouch is missing too!” he shouted.

The bundle of packed rice, the money pouch—everything had been stolen. What else could that whole family do but cry out in anguish? Nor did there seem to be anyone there who could come to their aid.
