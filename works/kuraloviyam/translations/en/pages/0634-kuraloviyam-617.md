Putting the gold coins into their bags, the ascetics rested their eyes for a while in the cave.

Aravaazhi slept peacefully.

Enaathi struggled without calm sleep.

A dream came to Enaathi.

He accepts the wealthy man's invitation and goes to his mansion.

A grand welcome is given.

His feet are ceremonially worshipped.

Silver coins are poured at his feet.

The wealthy man asks him to remain in the house permanently.

Smiling, Enaathi agrees and stays.

A private room is assigned to him.

Every day, the wealthy man bows to Enaathi and receives his blessing before going out.

Enaathi, who first sleeps on the floor of the private room, soon wants a cot and mattress.

After a few days he wants servants to hold and massage his feet.

Then a desire rises in him: would it not be better if those servants became his slaves?

The wealthy man hears of this.

The dream suddenly changes.

One night Enaathi stands upon the steps of the wealthy man's house.

The wealthy man looks toward him as though he does not see him, climbs into his palanquin carriage, and leaves.

Embarrassment runs across Enaathi's face.

The dream breaks and he awakens.

He wakes Aravaazhi, tells him the dream, and asks for medicine for the disturbance that has arisen in his mind.

Aravaazhi pats him and consoles him.

"I went to sleep without worrying about the value of the five gold coins the rich man gave me. I thought only that I could give them to poor people whom we meet on the road.

"Your state was not like that. You closed your eyes thinking greatly of the value of the five coins, of the magnificence of the man who gave them, and of the invitation he had extended.

"Have you forgotten the language of the Kural? Virtue lies in living with fear of desire. Do you know why? It is desire that deceives a person, surrounds him, makes him weaken and collapse."

Aravaazhi's counsel sends Kural lines surging through Enaathi's heart:

> What must be feared is desire; that fear itself is virtue,  
> for desire is what deceives a person.

Chapter 37 — Renouncing Desire; Kural 366
