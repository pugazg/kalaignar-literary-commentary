## Visual material

Large upper illustration of an anxious woman beside a man.

My sacred eyes!

Have you seen the result of the way you danced beyond all limits?

You forgot that everything must have a measure.

Now your punishment has arrived.

In the garden of the neighbouring house, a little sparrow leapt from branch to branch of a guava tree, dancing and showing its delight.

A cat came creeping silently and suddenly sprang.

Only the sparrow's “cheep! cheep!” mixed with the air.

Poor little sparrow.

I went to the pond to bathe.

There were two tiny sel fish.

My eyes—they were just like you!

Again and again they leapt from the water and frolicked.

How high can one keep leaping?

Should there not be a limit?

Those sel fish sprang high out of the water and suddenly fell upon the bank.

They thrashed.

They weakened.

I ran to save them.

But before I could—
