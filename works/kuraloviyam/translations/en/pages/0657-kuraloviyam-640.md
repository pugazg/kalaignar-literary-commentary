"If you describe them as I wish, I will write your greatness as you wish."

"Is that so? Wonderful! Then listen.

"I change continually between night and day.

"I send the cool breeze that gives comfort.

"I also give the sand-wind of the desert.

"I offer natural riches of beauty—lakes, ponds and rivers.

"Volcanoes and earthquakes too are effects that come through me.

"Fields spread like green silk garments.

"Fruit-groves cool the eye however long one looks.

"There are deer, honey-birds, peacocks, cuckoos, jewel-like pigeons, and many other creations.

"Opposite them are angry tigers, snarling leopards, lions, bears and other fierce animals.

"There are changing seasons, rain, lightning, thunder, waterfalls, cascades, and gardens singing songs of joy.

"There are pearls beneath the sea.

"Dig the earth and there is gold.

"And there is the human race that takes and uses all of these.

"Among that race are beautiful maidens and dishevelled forms, people with knowledge and people without it.

"Are not all these magnificent creations produced through my power?

"I can keep listing my greatness endlessly.

"Shall I say more?"

"Enough, mother! Enough!

"I do not regard anything you have listed so far as your true greatness.

"You possess one greatness far greater than all these."

"What is that, my son?"

"That is what I shall write as a Kural.

"One who was here yesterday disappears and is not here today.

"That, Mother World, is your greatness."

"Are you praising me—or showing anger toward me?"

"Take it however you wish.

"Nothing you listed seems properly to describe your greatness.

"You make the person who existed yesterday vanish today.

"That is the greatness that suits you."

Even while Valluvar was speaking, Nilamagal disappeared.

He opened his eyes.

As a result of thought that had flown like a flag, he took up stylus and palm leaf.

The Kural declaring the greatness of the world was born.

It is this:

> This world possesses the greatness of saying:  
> one who existed yesterday is not here today.

Chapter 34 — Impermanence; Kural 336
