I am not the only one praising Kalaignar; all of Tamil Nadu praises him today.

History has shown it to be true that one who possesses love for Tamil and love for the Tamil people is the truly wealthy person.

In that way, our Kalaignar has studied books for thirty years, attained a thoroughly clear scholarship, and written this work.

At one place in this book Kalaignar says:

The heroine serves a feast to the hero. Along with various vegetables, she serves bitter gourd. Of that bitter gourd he says: “It was bitter like rationalism!”

What elegance! This single expression is enough to understand Kalaignar.

Even if, in the future, many pages of *Kuraloviyam* were no longer available, this one page alone would be enough to know Kalaignar.

So far, thousands upon thousands of commentaries have been written... Has anyone, anywhere in them, mentioned the idea of rationalism? No!

In that respect, our Kalaignar is one who knows Tamil well—one who wants Tamil to live!

Kalaignar has given us *Kuraloviyam*, with eye-catching pictures on many pages, beyond Parimelazhagar's commentary, beyond Manakkudavar's commentary, beyond all the commentaries that have appeared so far.

I take pride in releasing, before this gathering of Tamil scholars and many thousands of members of the public, this book that will become the subject of major research in the future.

Bharathi Pathippagam has published this book in a highly distinguished manner.

As much esteem as there is for Valluvar who created the Kural, that much esteem belongs to this poet who created *Kuraloviyam*.
