Behind that rock, Paavai and Poonkundran had conducted so many games filled with the essence of delight. She was like a veena resting on Udayanan's lap!... When he began to play, the strings in both their bodies trembled and raised the music of love. Was that not the special glory of that veena concert!

Though a little time had passed, the musician arrived without failing the performance. The veena that had been waiting climbed into his hands and settled upon his lap.

“Paavai! Why are you looking at me like that? Are you furiously angry because I came a little late, my pearl set in a clasp?”

“It was anger I had, but the moment I saw you my longing drove it away, beloved!”

“O you whose guava-fruit cheek holds a dimple of vermilion! Why are those two lips trembling so?”

“You ask as though you do not know! Have you no fingers on your hands to count how many days they have gone hungry?”

“My fingers are already playing this veena!”

“That is exactly why my lips are trembling!”

There was no more talk! Only the sound “ich” kept echoing on that bed made by nature. The deer that had been running stopped to watch! Birds in flight settled on branches and looked on! The dancing peacock stood motionless!

A rain of kisses kept falling! Now there would only be the flood of pleasure!

“Beloved,” she said.

“Mm,” he answered.

“How does the fine blending of four lips taste?” she asked.

“My delight! As though adding yet another excellence to the sweetness of your gentle speech, the moisture that wells between your teeth turns to honey and gives me the taste of milk and honey mixed with the words you speak,” he said.

She embraced him! He embraced her! The breeze too embraced them both and satisfied its own frenzy.

> Like milk mingled with honey is the moisture  
> that wells between the white teeth of the soft-spoken maid.

Chapter 113 — Declaring Love's Excellence; Kural 1121
