## Visual material

Large upper illustration of an embracing young couple with a male portrait behind.

In the mango grove, in the fragrant flower garden, on the broad sandy shore, in the pavilion, in the wedding house, in the humble hut filled with happiness—in places such as these, evening watches and sighs as the man and woman of love, two bodies with one life, pull each other close, embrace, cover one another, join lips, and prepare a feast of pleasure without letting even a single moment come between them.

“Gold! Gem! My eye! My fruit!” Words of love drip like drops of honey. Beads of sweat bloom upon the rare and lovely brow. His fingers play a yaazh upon her soft body; her lap becomes a cradle of delight for him.

Using the evening hour, men and women partake of an incomparable feast and satisfy both the hunger of the heart and the hunger of the body.

But here is one man who, instead of dwelling on the feast he himself is about to enjoy, generously thinks that he ought to provide a feast for the evening itself.
