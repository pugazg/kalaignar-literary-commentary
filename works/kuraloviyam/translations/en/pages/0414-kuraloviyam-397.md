## Visual material

Large upper illustration accompanying a dialogue about a cow, calf and Chola king.

“They say the son of the king who ruled the land drove his chariot over a cow's calf. The mother cow cried out on seeing the calf dead. Understanding the cow's pain, the Chola king supposedly placed his own son beneath the chariot wheel and drove over him. Can anything like that really happen?”

A friend asked this with a laugh. Maraimudhalvan patted Nalangilli on the back and said:

“It may even be an imaginary story that never happened. But do not forget the deep idea the story explains.”

“What great idea is that?”

“It is an idea needed by a country, by society—why, by every household.”

“Enough preface! Write the conclusion.”

“I am going to! Do you know that, by looking at a man, it is possible to say that he will soon come to ruin?”

“Oh! Are you learning fortune-telling?”
