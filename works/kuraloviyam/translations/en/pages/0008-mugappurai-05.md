I have given these examples only to explain that I did not newly train myself in order to write Kuraloviyam, and that this book is simply a continuation of the work I have done for forty years, capturing the richness of the Tamil language from many different angles—not as the result of conceit! That evil quality has never found even the space of a sesame-tip in my heart!

There are those who have made the Kurals into flower garlands through prose.

There are those who have composed verse and explained the meaning of the Kural!

There are those who have offered concise and pleasing paraphrases of the Kural!

There are those who, through musical Tamil, send the sweet Kural flowing through the ear and cool the mind!

Amid all these, as my own contribution, I have turned events into scenes and let the Kurals move before you: in stream-like Tamil, dense with cool trees that give shade; in full-throated Tamil that kindles fire in the heart; and in deeply moving Tamil of feeling that brings a river into long eyes.

My congratulations to the highly skilled artists who have drawn graceful illustrations suited to these pictures of thought!

My thanks to my friend Chidambaram of Bharathi Pathippagam, who came forward to publish the first edition of this book, whose full rights belong to Tamilkani Pathippagam, Chennai!

The Foreword written for this book by Dr. V. Sub. Manickam—eminent scholar, fine Tamil scholar, and former Vice-Chancellor of Madurai Kamaraj University—has become a heartfelt blessing that urges me to engage still further and further in literary work. I shall always remain grateful to his heart, filled with culture, affection and love!

I joyfully dedicate this book to self-respecting Tamil scholars who carry on literary work with a sense of ethnic identity.
