...the waterfall clasped and held them all in playful delight.

Standing at a distance, he watched with eyes pouring jealousy and longed.

She finished bathing, wrapped herself in clothing so that the curves of her beauty were not too visible, and climbed to the bank beside the falls.

He too came and stood before her.

Though she met him there often, she had never looked at him deeply. She had never paused there even for a moment.

He, however, had stubbornly walked there day after day.

That day, perhaps she too had been waiting and longing for him to come.

That must be why, when she saw him, she did not leap away like a deer. She stood there, dazed.

Then she moved away.

As she left, a smile appeared upon her lips.

Even as he drank that smile with his eyes, she vanished.

He looked at a jackfruit ripening at the root.

He looked at a pomegranate hanging from its tree.

He looked at the buds filling the creepers nearby.

With his fingers he gently touched one bud clinging to its vine.

Just as sweetness lies hidden inside the jackfruit, and flavour hides within the pomegranate seed, fragrance is contained inside this bud that has not yet bloomed.

In the same way, he thought, within the bud called her smile—the smile she left behind as she went—there is hidden a delightful sign:

"She too has boundless love for him."

> As fragrance lies hidden within a bud,  
> there is something hidden within the maiden's budding smile.

Chapter 128 — Making the Sign Understood; Kural 1274
