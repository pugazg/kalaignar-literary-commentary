She turns the pages of literary works.

Every one of them only reminds her of separation from her lover and makes her wilt.

Not one extends a hand of comfort.

Whatever epic or puranic tale she opens, she sees Gopalan delighting with the gopis, Shiva embracing Mohini, or Gandhapan breathing in the fragrance of Alli.

Such scenes alone rise before her and make her burn.

She puts the books aside.

"At least let me close my eyes and forget my sorrow in sleep," she thinks, fastening her eyelids over her eyes.

But those troublesome eyes knock at the doors of the lids and peer out through the window of memory.

"In olden times, did they not send swans as messengers?

"Did they not send parrots carrying messages?

"Even pigeons have borne news and brought joy to lovers.
