Only if one looked closely were there two figures; otherwise one would have to say that they had become a single person.

Why elaborate further? For the illness that buds in the morning, grows through the day and opens into full flower by evening, those two little birds had made the boat's shadow into a soft bed so that each might give the other the needed medicine.

The bright chime of bangles and the bursts of youthful laughter that rose now and then were kept from the ears of anyone nearby by the helpful roar of the waves.

A cloud would embrace the full moon in the sky—and then, after seeing the festival of love these two were conducting upon the earth, would run far away from the moon in shyness.

There was no pearl necklace upon her neck or pearl ornament upon her hands. Why would she need one? She had sealed with her lips a casket containing a pure pearl-smile more beautiful than any jewel; again and again she opened it for him to see, only to close it before he could plunder it.

There were no costly jewels upon her body. What need had she of them? She herself lay against the gold of his body like a diamond set into it.

They were young doves from a simple family.

Love does not divide and value people by calling one poor and another rich. It is true that on a poor household's food plate there may be no sweet jackfruit segment. Yet here on the seashore, four lips showed through their actions that they could surpass the sweetness of jackfruit.

Are lovers' quarrel and lovers' union privileges only for those who live in upper-storeyed mansions? Here is this pair, showing that those who live in huts filled with the salt breeze too have quarrel and union. Let us listen as they speak like a yaazh making music.

“My dear! Did you have to go far out into the deep sea today before you could bring back fish?”

“Yes! I went a long way to catch leaping fish. But I found that the fish that steals my heart was right here, very near me.”

He stroked her eyes with his fingers.

“My dear! They say a great wind blew in the middle of the sea. What did you think of then?”

“What else would I think of, my love? I thought of you!”
