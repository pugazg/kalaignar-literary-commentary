## Visual material

Large colour illustration occupying the upper page: an older white-bearded man advising a younger man, with a sword-wielding figure and a reclining man depicted above them.

A small crowd had gathered in the middle of the street. Wanting others to praise him as a great warrior, a young man with no strength at all stood swinging a sword this way and that. His performance merely made everyone laugh. One elderly man in the crowd said:

“What is this? He stands twisting about like a woman! He does not even know how to hold a sword! If we set out for war trusting this clay horse, our country itself will be reduced to dust!”

“He is not even a woman; he is a coward! Could he not at least have made an effort and obtained some training? Without even knowing how a sword is to be wielded, he is wasting his time like this! Are we to stand here watching this coward's performance too?” Spitting out his irritation, a warrior left the crowd.

His feet turned toward the house of a physician.
