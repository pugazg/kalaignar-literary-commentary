"May I ask you something?"

"Please do! I too have something to ask you."

"What do you want to ask me?"

"My question will depend on what you want to ask me."

"Oh! So you too are going to ask a question?"

"Then what were you thinking of asking?"

"I too was thinking of asking a question. But some hesitation is holding me back."

"Is hesitation yours alone? I have it too!"

"First, may I know your name?"

"Kaveri!"
