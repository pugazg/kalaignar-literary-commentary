## Visual material

Large upper illustration accompanying the Mullai-kodi / Mukilan fisher-couple separation-and-dream vignette.

The waves of the sea struck the shore without clamour. The rising sun came up spreading brilliant light. Fishermen prepared their catamarans to go far out to sea and catch fish. The catamarans that had lain drawn up along the shore kissed the blue sea and danced upon the waves.

“Has Mukilan still not come?” someone asked among the fishermen.

“Only if Mullai-kodi lets him go can he come,” came the reply amid teasing laughter.

Not even two months had passed since their marriage. Those young hearts longed to spend every hour as food for one another. But only if work continued could the household go on.

“Let me go. I will fly back before dawn tomorrow,” Mukilan pleaded with Mullai-kodi, who clung to him and wept.
