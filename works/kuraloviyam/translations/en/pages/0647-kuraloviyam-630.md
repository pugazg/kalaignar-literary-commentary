He sighed as he looked at the marks her ornaments had left upon his body while they embraced tightly.

His lips moved:

"When the finely adorned woman joined me, she was like one who gave life to my own life.

When she left, she became like one who gave death to that life."

The thunder in the sky, the flashes of lightning, the sound of rain, and the roar of the waves did not stop.

He remained standing just as he was.

This Kural explains his grief:

> The jewelled maiden is life itself when she is with me;  
> when she leaves, she is death to that very life.

Chapter 113 — Declaring Love's Excellence; Kural 1124
