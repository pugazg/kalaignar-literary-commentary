A man who had never learned or heard the Kural properly knew only a few verses in fragments. Among many who knew even less, he presented himself as a learned poet and deceived them.

The people of his town came to regard him as a master of Tamil and praised and honoured him.

Eager for his fame to spread to neighbouring towns as well, he set out on a journey.

In one of the places he visited, a minor ruler who held authority there invited this "poet" to serve as a minister and spread Tamil literature.

The ruler considered giving a poet ministerial responsibility a great service he had rendered to Tamil.

He himself had studied good Tamil and learned literature properly. He wanted at least one poet among his ministers, and he fulfilled that wish.

But the result turned out very differently.

Suddenly news arrived that wild animals were entering the town at night, destroying crops and taking human lives. The report reached the ears of the minor ruler...
