If they immediately reached the summit of pleasure, she feared she would not be able to stay awake all night and drink every flavour she had imagined during their long separation. So she pretended to sulk, using lovers' quarrel as a strategy to remain in his embrace.

But, she feels, the emotion that surges over her while they embrace becomes a weapon that breaks her heart and will not allow the sulking to continue.

“Ah! I have to end the quarrel. Then the pleasure that could have been savoured for a long time without interruption will be spoiled!”

He too recognizes her struggle.

> Within the quarrel that lets me remain in his embrace  
> appears the weapon that breaks my heart and ends it.

Because within the very quarrel that gives her a reason to remain clasped to her lover there appears a weapon capable of overturning her heart, she cannot prolong it and wilts in distress.

He too wants her sulking to continue. What use is there in leaping once into the sky of pleasure, growing tired and falling back down? She must sulk. That radiant-jewelled woman must move to the edge of the bed. He must roll her back into his embrace.

A rain of kisses! Again and again! Another embrace! More embraces! Ah, what delight there is in her continuing to sulk and in his begging, teasing and coaxing her to end it. For her too. For him too.

But that impatient night seems ready to dawn already.

While he tries to end the sulking of the creeper of delight, asking for the boon of lip-honey and endless sweetness, he asks one boon of the night itself amid the dark penance of youth:

“May my beloved continue to sulk. And so that I may stand begging and embracing her to end it, may this night continue still longer!”

What a plea! She suffers because she cannot prolong her quarrel. He begs the night, “Do not dawn,” thinking that if her sulking grows, there is a special delight in calming it.

> May the radiant-jewelled one continue to sulk; and while I beg her,  
> may the night too continue and not end.

Chapter 133 — Joy of Lovers' Quarrel; Kurals 1324, 1329
