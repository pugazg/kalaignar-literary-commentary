## Visual material

Large upper illustration at a public tank with one young man in the water and another young man standing on the bank or steps.

Lice ran and played across his head.

Mucus flowed from his nose.

His pearl-white teeth stood coated yellow.

White dirt peeped from the corners of his eyes.

A silk shirt and silk veshti, stained in many places, clung to his body.

He was the son of a wealthy household. Yet not even the point of a sesame seed's worth of space in him was given to civility or cleanliness.

He would sit on the stone-paved front platform of his house.

There was even a beautiful puppy for him to play with. He never so much as turned to look at it.

Instead, he would chase piglets at the end of the street, piglets that had smeared mud upon themselves like sandal paste and ran grunting. He would catch one, clasp it to his chest and dance with delight.

He was, after all, the young man of a great house. Who in the village could question him?

The villagers themselves restrained the mocking words rising from their hearts and walked past pretending not to see him.

One day, without any change in his usual appearance, he came to the steps of the village tank holding a dead rat by its tail.

It was the public tank of the village.

Another young man was bathing in the water—
