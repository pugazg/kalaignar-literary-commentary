“What do you want?” Valluvar asked, kissing the crown of his head.

“I want to see a base man. You must show him to me.”

When Ilango said this, Valluvar laughed heartily.

“There are so many things in the world worth seeing. Why this strange desire, child?”

“Tomorrow in Kural class my teacher said he will show us who the base are. If I see one before then, when the teacher explains the lesson I can jump ahead and tell him what a base man looks like, and win his praise. That is why I ask, great Valluvar! Show him to me.”

“Is that so? Very well. Come with me.”

Valluvar led Ilango away. Coloured shapes filled the sky. Clouds gathered like waves of a foaming sea. Ilango followed Valluvar past them all.

“Look there, child,” Valluvar said, pointing.

Ilango eagerly looked in that direction.

A crowned king stood there. Another man knelt at his feet. Ilango could hear the kneeling man speak.

“King! Forgive me! I was a commander in the army defending our clan. You laid siege to the fort of our king, the leader of our people, and planned to raze it to the ground. I commanded the first force that came to resist you. But when I saw your enormous army, I became afraid. I cannot bear the suffering that battle may bring me!”

“Commander of the enemy army! What do you say now? What is your decision?” the king asks angrily.

The kneeling man answers:

“I have sold myself to you, O king!”

The king pats him approvingly and takes him into his own side.
