## Visual material

Large colour illustration showing a romantic couple embracing beneath coconut or palm trees at night.

A broad forehead.

Eyes that proclaim manliness; lofty shoulders.

A gait that reveals firmness.

It was that handsome warrior whom she loved. He too had surrendered himself to her in the same way. Many days had passed since her sidelong glance and his magnetic eyes signed that covenant. The summer heat called “separation” stood across the cool season called “embrace.” Both longed somehow to meet one day without anyone knowing.

Near the house was a beautiful coconut grove. Moonlight was pouring milk over Lady Earth! Mother, father, elder brother and younger brother were all in deep sleep. She alone lay sleepless, suffering as though stretched upon a plank bristling with nails; slowly she rose. “Everyone is asleep—go without fear!” the oil lamp seemed to say, moving its flame-tongue. The back door opened. Until she crossed the jasmine-vine bower, she walked without a sound, hardly even letting her breath be heard.

Then her feet carried her toward that coconut grove. The sign his eyes had given that evening, she had taken to mean: “I will wait in that grove—my dear, come.” In that grove where the coconut trees stood bearing their clusters like a potter carrying earthen pots upon his head on the way to market, that warrior who would hold her in his lap—
