Both the Kural and Kurunthogai use the mingling of earth and water as an image for the joining of the hearts of loving lovers.

In just such a state, with life, body and heart all becoming one and intertwined, we see a pair of lovers in the midst of that mango grove.

As the mango-cuckoos fill the air with music and the mango leaves dance to the breeze's rhythmic direction, the lovers take it all as the artistic performance in their own hall of love.

It is between just such a loving pair, the Kural declares with certainty, that lovers' quarrel should arise—and that itself is delight. What kind of delight is it? Valluvar makes the lovers themselves speak. Let us listen and understand.

“My dear! Even when I have done nothing wrong, you begin a lovers' quarrel and refuse union. At times—why is that itself a pleasure?”

“My lord! Even though you have committed no fault, I pretend that you have and quarrel with you; there is great pleasure for me in that too.”

“How, my dear? Explain where that pleasure lies!”

“When I quarrel and move away from you, your love for me grows stronger. Your beautiful arms ache to seize me. And my heart too throbs, wondering, ‘Will you not pull me close and wrap me in your embrace?’ Do you now understand the secret of why I quarrel?”

“My love! Even though you have revealed that secret, no matter how many times you quarrel hereafter, the delight that follows the quarrel will not diminish!”

“My lord! They speak of a world of the gods. Could that world contain a pleasure like the lovers' quarrel of us whose hearts have mingled like earth and water? It cannot! It cannot possibly be a world surpassing this one!”

The mango-cuckoos stopped their music! Those cuckoos flew off chasing one another! The lovers were responsible for that!

Here are the two Kurals for this Kuraloviyam:

> Even though he has done no wrong, lovers' quarrel has the power  
> to make his affection grow.

> Can even the realm of the gods offer a joy like lovers' quarrel  
> to those who have mingled like earth and water?

Chapter 133 — Joy of Lovers' Quarrel; Kurals 1321, 1323
