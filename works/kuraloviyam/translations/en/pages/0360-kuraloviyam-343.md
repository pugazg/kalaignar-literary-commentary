## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a woman embracing and kissing an ascetic inside a hut while another man walks away outside.

“What need have I for land?

What need have I for gold?

What need have I for a woman with intoxicating eyes?

I have received Mahesan's grace—all these attachments have fallen away and perished for me. The holy feet of the Lord are the one great attachment that has taken possession of me! Land, gold and the woman of illusion are snake-pits to this servant!

Water, penance and air alone shall be my food; the shade of trees alone the homes in which I stay and live; a deerskin alone the garment that guards my modesty—if I receive these, it is enough!

I would call fools those who go seeking little pleasures that perish, instead of bathing in the flood of supreme bliss at the feet of the Lord, our master!

The revelry and dalliance in which people roll together body to body, crying in pleasure that they have forgotten the world—all of it will lose even its form in this impermanent world!
