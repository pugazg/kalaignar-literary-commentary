“What can I do? I remembered how we chased one another through the grove and joined in delight. Without realizing it, I soaked my heart in pleasure.

“I even tried to control the mind by clearing my throat and steadying myself. But beyond all restraint, does not that feeling burst forth like a flood breaking through a dam?”

“Whatever else may be said, desire is a merciless lord of death. It has not even the slightest sense of propriety.

“Without thinking, ‘Poor things! These lovers lack the firmness to hold their hearts under control,’ or even taking pity on them, it exposes lovers like us right in the middle of a gathering surrounded by many people.”

There is a Kural perfectly suited to this conversation between the two lovers.

Here it is:

> Without asking whether they lack restraint or deserve pity,  
> desire breaks secrecy and appears in the public square.

Chapter 114 — Renouncing Modesty; Kural 1138

நிறையரியர் = those without restraint

அளியர் = those deserving pity
