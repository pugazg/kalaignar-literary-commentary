There was a man who had crossed the sea and become engaged in trade in a foreign country. He had a close friend as well. The two were partners, conducted their business successfully, and earned great wealth. Both began to long to return to their homeland. Taking a large share of what they had earned, they boarded a ship and returned to their country. It was midnight when they reached their own shore. The chest in their possession contained more than fifty thousand gold coins. So they decided to spend the night at a rest-house in that very port town and set out for their own village in the morning. Accordingly, they went in search of a rest-house.
