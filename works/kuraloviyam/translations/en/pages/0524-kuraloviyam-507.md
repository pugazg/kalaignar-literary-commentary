## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a ruler with attendants and advisers.

It was the period when the Pallavas ruled with Kanchi as their capital.

The later Pallavas, like Nandivarman, possessed an unquenchable love for Tamil.

During the reign of one such Pallava king, a man from the Chola country named Arulalan served among the army commanders.

The king had a special affection for him.

The Pallava ruler often discussed the condition of the country and affairs of government with his ministers and commanders before making decisions.

Yet he gave particular respect to Arulalan's views and often acted accordingly.

A decision was made to strengthen the army further by appointing several men to the posts of deputy commanders.

Many competed for those few posts.

At that time the Chola commander Arulalan was travelling from camp to camp, personally inspecting and organizing the Pallava forces.
