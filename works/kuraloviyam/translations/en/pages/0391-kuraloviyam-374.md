> A country is one where devastating hunger, unceasing disease  
> and crushing hostile invasion do not gather.

This Kural points to that excellence.

The teacher's explanations plunged the student into an ocean of happiness.

“Sir! You have spoken of the greatness a country must possess. Can a country endowed with every kind of wealth and with all the excellences Valluvar describes ever lose its distinction for some reason? If so, what might that reason be?”

Patting the student, the teacher answered:

“Dear boy! The Kural tells us that too. Listen! Even if every excellence, wealth and strength are present together, if there is no government to protect them and administer them properly, the people will gain no benefit from the country's greatness. Kuralar states this firmly as his concluding point.”

> Even when all such excellences are attained, there is no benefit  
> in a country that lacks proper rule.

The student stood amazed after hearing the meaning of the Kural. His eyes shone like sparks of thought. He pressed to his eyes the beautiful palm-leaf manuscript of Kurals the teacher had placed in his hands. He was overwhelmed again and again by the thought that, so many centuries ago, Valluvar had declared precisely when a country would flourish and when it could fall into decline.
