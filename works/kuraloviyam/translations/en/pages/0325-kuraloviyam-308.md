Thus days, weeks and months passed.

One day, while returning from the office, Iraaman turned toward the beach to get some air.

There, on the white sand, Amudham was walking.

Seeing Iraaman, she smiled and asked, “What brings you this way?” Though Amudham was not more beautiful than Annam, standing beside her Iraaman discovered that her figure and the movement of her body had a charm of their own.

Some feeling took possession of him.

Thinking she was only a tender flowering creeper who would yield if touched, he placed his hand on Amudham's shoulder.

She kept walking without voicing any objection. He followed beside her.

Near a boat, darkness was slowly spreading its rule.

“Shall we sit down?” asked Iraaman.

Smiling, Amudham sat there. She spoke about office matters. Iraaman, while talking with her, suddenly pulled her into a tight embrace. It lasted only an instant. Amudham freed herself from his hold and flared up: “Shame! What did you take me for? Just because I mix openly and cheerfully with everyone without deceit, did you imagine that my chastity was merchandise for sale?” With that, furious as a storm, she sped away from the place.

Embarrassment spread over Iraaman's face. He returned home with his head bowed in shame.

A shock awaited him at the doorway. The lock on the door had been broken. He stared wide-eyed. A rolled sheet of paper was fixed to the front of the door. He took it down, unfolded it and read the writing:

“To dear Iraaman, a notice from Kannan of the neighbouring house! You have failed in the plan you devised to imprison Annam and thereby guard her chastity. You did not understand that, rather than guarding a woman by locking her up, the finest protection is the quality by which the woman herself guards the fullness called chastity. Forgive us! Annam and I have gone far away from you.”

The written words seemed to laugh mockingly at Iraaman. Stunned, he held his head and sat down on the doorstep.

> What can the guarding of confinement achieve? For women,  
> the foremost guard is their own guarding of chastity.
