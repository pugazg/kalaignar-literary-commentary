## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a woman watching a man running toward her.

Stones pierced his feet!  
The two soles appeared  
as though set with rubies!

The summer sun blazed—  
not a single tree along the road  
was willing to hold an umbrella!

“Let my head split if it must,”  
he urged his legs onward  
without stopping the cruel journey!

Thirst curled his tongue—  
hunger, like a tool that cuts areca nut,  
cut into his stomach!

Half the distance still remained.

A plea rose in his heart:

“Walk on—  
defeat the suffering!”
