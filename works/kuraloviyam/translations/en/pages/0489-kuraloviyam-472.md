## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a man bending before a richly dressed woman.

“Never strays from the path! An honest man! The very form of justice! A pure heart!”

Those around him sang such praises.

They beat the drum of their tongues proclaiming that virtue itself had taken human form and was walking among them, and through such praise they obtained whatever help they wanted from him.

He, in turn, used them and began piling up a mountain of wealth.

The fortune he had already accumulated was enough for seven generations. Even those who had firmly declared that he had no reason now to desire more wealth were astonished as he used every evil means and made his treasury swell like a pregnant woman.

He presented himself to the poor as though he were a god. He lent them money, piled interest upon interest, tormented them, and even seized the vessels and household goods they possessed.

At the gambling table he had naked beauties serve intoxicating drinks that made the eyes of the men seated opposite him spin. At night he swept away all their possessions; by day he walked about gentle as a cow.
