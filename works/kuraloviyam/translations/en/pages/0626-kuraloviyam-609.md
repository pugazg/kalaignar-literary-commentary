A few days passed.

In the neighbouring village lived a friend of Pachaiyan.

When news came that the friend's wife had died, Pachaiyan rushed there to offer comfort.

He remembered the words Kunran had spoken to the son who had lost his mother.

His friend was crying aloud.

The villagers had gathered.

Pachaiyan placed a hand on his friend's shoulder and said:

"Do not cry! Was your wife a wife only to you? She was the wife of this whole village..."

He never finished the sentence.

His clothes were torn.

His body was covered in wounds.

He ran back to his father and, hiding nothing, told him exactly what had happened.

Kaniyan and Kunran hurried over. Seeing Pachaiyan's injuries, they were moved with pity.

"Look at the kind of son who has been born to me!" Pachai lamented to Kaniyan.

Kaniyan replied:

"Of all the blessings a man may receive, we know none higher than the blessing of children who possess understanding."

> Of all the blessings one may gain, we know none greater  
> than the blessing of children endowed with understanding.

Chapter 7 — The Blessing of Children; Kural 61
