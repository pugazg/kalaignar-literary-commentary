Love overflowed its banks like a wild river.

In the grove he touched the maiden and tasted delight.

The fear she had once shown like a startled deer disappeared, and she too came to rest in the young man's lap.

Cuckoos raised their chorus and sang the music of celebration.

Squirrels ran through the trees, shaking the leaves.

The tender woman moaned like a veena being played.

The broad-shouldered man, whose shoulders were like a mountain, conducted a hunt of pleasure.

The jewelled woman who seemed to add greatness itself to beauty devoured him with her eyes and delighted in making him an indelible painting upon the screen of her mind.

She did not know how to worship a god.

Within her heart, she worshipped her lover.

But if they lay forever in an embrace, who would provide for their expenses?

So her companion too went away to pursue his work.

Time would not pass for the flower-like woman.

She set out to visit the friend she had known before.
