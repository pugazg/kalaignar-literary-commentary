“—little goat! I am suffering from searching. Have you seen it?”

Even in her sorrow Enthizhai could not suppress a laugh.

“Boy, little brother! You are searching while carrying the goat on your own shoulder!”

she teased.

The youth suddenly remembered, cried, “Good heavens! Yes!” thanked her, and went away.

Then one of Enthizhai's friends came there, clearly planning some mischief.

“What brings you this way?” asked Enthizhai.

“Nothing much. I urgently need ghee, so I am going to the cowherd settlement,” said the friend.

“What are you carrying in that pot?” Enthizhai asked.

“Butter,” said the friend.

“A young man just now searched for a goat while carrying it on his shoulder. And you are wandering for ghee while holding butter in your hand. Fine spectacles indeed!”

Enthizhai kept laughing.

“All right, all right! Stop laughing. And what about you?

“You keep your lover inside your own heart and go searching for him somewhere else.

“I sent that young man looking for the goat precisely to make you understand this, and then I came myself.

“Is it funny only when I wander for ghee while holding butter?

“Funny only when that youth carries the goat on his shoulder and searches for it?

“But when you keep your lover in your heart and wander looking for him, is that alone not funny?”

Enthizhai understood the truth wrapped inside her friend's mischief.

Her face reddened with shyness.

> My heart, when my beloved already dwells within you,  
> where else are you going in search of him?
