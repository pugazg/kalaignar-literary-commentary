## Visual material

Large upper illustration of two women bathing beneath a waterfall, one crouched in the foreground and one standing behind.

Mocking speech, slander, harsh words, scorching abuse; committing evil deeds that make the town condemn them; joining eagerly in acts the world despises—such a group of petty character gained dominance and revelled in it.

Those opposed to them worked in many ways to preserve culture and honour ancient greatness.

Intent on disrupting that work, the small-minded faction displayed both its power and its boundless greed, drawing some people over to its side.

Is it surprising that hearts tossed by desire fell under their sway?

Among those who slipped away from the cultural group and joined the degraded faction were people of learning too.

But what use was that learning?

The moment they joined it, where did their scholarship, ability and fame go to hide?

Those who had once walked like tigers now wriggled like worms in the sewage tank of that degraded company.

Tongues on which beautiful Tamil had once danced now waved filthy words like ceremonial fans.

Their writings, once treasuries of elegant language, decayed and stank like corpses in a graveyard.

What could they do?

The place they had joined was like that.
