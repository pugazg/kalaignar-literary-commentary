## Visual material

Large colour illustration occupying most of the upper page: an idealized woman above two men facing one another beside a decorated ceremonial arch.

“Suppose a prize competition were announced and every woman in this world took part in it—even then she alone would win. In beauty, intelligence, character, gait and dress, she is beyond compare in every way. I would not describe her as a deer, a peacock, a dove, or a captivating parrot. My judgment is that there is nothing and no one in this world fit to be compared with her! She is the one I desire; I melt at the thought of her and worship her, enshrining her in my heart.”
