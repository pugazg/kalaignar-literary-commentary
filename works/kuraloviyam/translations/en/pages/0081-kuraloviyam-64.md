## Visual material

Large colour illustration above the prose showing a young woman seated on a bed beside a large mirror, resting her face in her hands.

When the lover departs, pallor spreads across the beloved's body, it is said. In the chapter called `பசப்புறு பருவரல்`—Suffering from Pallor—Valluvar has portrayed with great beauty the anguish of the maiden who becomes subject to that pallor.

On the bed, words of endearment fall; dance, song, sulking and union all compete with one another. In the morning she rises and looks into the mirror...
