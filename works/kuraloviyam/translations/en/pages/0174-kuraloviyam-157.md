## Visual material

Large colour battlefield illustration occupying the upper half of the page: a war elephant covered with arrows amid wrecked chariots and weapons.

A battlefield where all four divisions of the army clash. Swords flash like lightning. Arrows fall like rain. War drums roar like thunder. Chariots and horses charge at speed, striking and collapsing in the field. Herds of elephants coming like banks of cloud fight as though mountains were crashing into one another. To guard the honour of the soil in which they were born, warriors on both sides shed blood upon the battlefield. Final victory comes to only one of the two sides. Yet the warriors, sworn to fight without turning their backs and to die in battle, leap here and there across the field bearing bows, spears, swords and shields, performing their games of valour.

There, an elephant raises its trunk with a battle roar and begins attacking the warriors who come against it. When a huge rock rolls down from the summit of a mountain rising to the sky, the shrubs, creepers and small trees that stand in its path are all destroyed. In the same way, many warriors fall, unable to withstand the elephant's assault. Beneath its feet, the heads of warriors become like ripe coconuts.
