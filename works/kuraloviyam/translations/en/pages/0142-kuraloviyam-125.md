## Visual material

Large colour illustration showing a turbaned man framed within an eye-shaped motif, with crossed rod or spear-like forms and a smaller two-person inset scene.

Like a monkey leaping from branch to branch, there are people who leap from principle to principle; that Medical Sigamani was one of them. In truth, he was no crown-jewel of medicine. Sigamani was merely his name. That name became attached to his medical profession and he came to be called “Medical Sigamani.” He also had a business manufacturing and selling medicines. To show that the medicines he produced were superior, excellent and impeccable, he regularly used a cheap commercial trick. He went about proclaiming that every other medical trader except himself was dishonest—people who adulterated medicines and played with the lives of the public.

People, it is said, will think a person is pure gold without a flaw if he is the first to point out some fault in somebody or something! Medical Sigamani belonged to that class.

He had a desire somehow to obtain a government post and become famous. With that aim he deliberately befriended one of the palace loyalists and asked him to place his ambition before the government. That loyalist asked the government for a post not only for Sigamani—
