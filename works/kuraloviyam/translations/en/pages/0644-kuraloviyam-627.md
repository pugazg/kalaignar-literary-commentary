...would not let go. It kept chasing the calf and smeared upon its body the mud and filth clinging to itself.

The mother cow's patience ended.

With horns lowered, she charged at the pig.

Seeing the cow's speed, the pig let out a terrible squeal, ran far down the street, and finally leapt into a small pool of drainage water.

Already ten or fifteen pigs were soaking there among writhing worms.

The moment another of their own kind arrived, they surrounded it, rolled together, and celebrated with delight.

Valluvar, seeing this entire scene, fashioned for humankind a Kural expressing the truth that the lowly gather with the lowly:

"The greatness of the noble is that they fear and avoid base company.

The nature of the small-minded is that they embrace such company as their own circle."

> Greatness fears and avoids mean company;  
> littleness surrounds itself with it as kin.
