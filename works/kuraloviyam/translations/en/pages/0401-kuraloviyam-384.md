Seeing all this, the visitors from the other world are amazed. Cool wind blows along the direction in which they travel. Before long, rain falls. Water runs down the mountain streams carrying flower petals. Those tired from work, and those who have eaten their fill, scoop up that water again and again and drink.

The visitors ask the people of Earth about everything and collect details.

The time comes for them to return to their own planet. They board the scientific spacecraft they brought and depart.

Scientists of their world wait eagerly to welcome them.

“Listen—we will tell you the marvel we saw on Earth,” they begin joyfully. Everyone there watches their lips expectantly.

“There is something on Earth called rain. It is a wondrous thing! The foods suitable for the people there will not grow without it. And the people who live by eating the foods brought forth by rain also drink that very rainwater! The people of Earth do not even seem to think, ‘What! We live by drinking the very rain that gives us our food!’”

Hearing this, the inhabitants of that world praise rain. They lament that they have nothing like it. They marvel that the people of Earth drink the rain itself without showing gratitude for all the help it gives.

One of the travellers who returned from Earth says:

“A very great thinker there named Thiruvalluvar has written a Kural about the excellence of rain.”

“What did he write?”

“What did he write?”

Voices rise.

“He wrote that rain not only produces the food required by those who eat; it itself becomes drinkable nourishment for them.”

So he explains. The whole world begins talking about rain.

> Rain produces food for those who eat, and for those who drink,  
> rain itself becomes their drink.

Chapter 2 — The Excellence of Rain; Kural 12

துப்பு = food  
துப்பார் = those who eat  
துப்பாக்கி = making foodstuffs come into being
