## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a couple concealed in darkness near a lit lamp and patterned screen.

A beautiful lamp was spreading light inside that house. Outside, I saw someone waiting for the moment when the lamp would go out. Who was that person? Someone hesitant to enter the house while light remained. He must be a thief—or perhaps a secret lover yearning for the moment when, unseen by anyone, he could enter, touch, embrace and delight in his heart's beloved who lived there.

But the figure I saw belonged to neither of these kinds. He was no thief and no secret lover.

Then who stood outside the house with such eagerness, waiting for the lamp to be extinguished?

Shall I tell you his name? His name is “darkness.”

Darkness lies waiting for the lamp's light to vanish so that it may enter the house and rule there. Valluvar presents in an entirely fresh way the simple truth that when the lamp goes out, darkness surrounds the place.
