A new light entered Nallamma's eyes. Rapture spread across her lips. Her face blossomed.

“Elder! How happy I was when I gave birth to him! I feel an even greater joy now, hearing that he was a worthy man.”

Nallamma said this, and the flood of happiness seemed to sweep through her whole body as she curled up and lay down. The elder touched her body and tried to move her. That body had now reached a state in which it would never move again. Perhaps the joyful news had been the rare medicine that finally gave peace to her heart.

> Greater than the joy she felt when she gave birth  
> is a mother's joy on hearing her son called a worthy man.
