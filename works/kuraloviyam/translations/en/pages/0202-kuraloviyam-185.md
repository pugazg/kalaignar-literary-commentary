Large colour battlefield illustration with an elephant and a warrior amid fallen fighters.

The southern land is a field where valour grows. The man who blinks at the thrust of a spear is a coward—this is the Tamil war-cry.

Warriors of two great countries met and spoke.

One said proudly, “Our king has no habit of wearing armour on his back.”
