## Visual material

Large upper illustration associated with the Mankadu doctor vignette.

Mankadu was a beautiful little village. There was no one there who did not know the doctor. He had been born into a prominent family. Because of his wealth and prosperity, the people of the village praised him highly and treated him with respect.

No one was prepared to praise him for intelligence. Only a few poets who stood before him with outstretched hands hoping for gifts went about composing verses that compared his knowledge to the sea and his ability to the sky.

In a world where even a man who knows nothing begins acting as though he knows everything once he rises in status, it was no surprise that this doctor, who knew one or two things, strutted about thinking himself a mine of all knowledge.

A Pongal festival was held in Mankadu. As part of it, strong young men came forward to tame bulls.

Six victors were to receive purses of gold on behalf of the village. The villagers wanted the doctor to present them.
