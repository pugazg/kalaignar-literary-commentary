## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a puppet-like youth manipulated by others.

He praised an unlearned man as “O king of learning!”

He hailed the very embodiment of evil deeds as “O sacred flame!”

He wove a garland of praise for a revenge-minded man, calling him “Treasure-house of culture!”

He bowed before a crooked-minded man with neither policy nor goal, calling him “Mountain of principle!”

When he finished stringing together all these garlands of praise and placed them at the feet of the enemy king, his lips acted out a smile.

His heart, however, was weeping.

He could not bear the arrows of protest fired by his own conscience.

Even so, because he wanted at any cost to obtain a position that would let him sit in the royal assembly hall, he tried to forget the degraded state into which he had fallen.

He was educated.

He possessed talent.

He had the ability to use his intelligence and perform difficult deeds.

Yet in a war he had commanded as general, circumstances brought him to defeat by the enemy.

Rather than accept complete defeat and die on the battlefield or be taken prisoner, he surrendered to the enemy king.
