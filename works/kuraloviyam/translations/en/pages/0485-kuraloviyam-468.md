## Visual material

Large upper illustration of two modern men shaking hands, one beside a microphone.

> “Tamil is named nectar—sweet Tamil  
> is equal to our very life.”

Ah! With what a flood of feeling Revolutionary Poet Bharathidasan sang these lines.

Tamil and life are one. Even if it is called nectar, does it not stand equal to life itself?

Was it not to protect that living Tamil that Tamil Nadu put on the armour of battle?

Declaring, “Our brave shoulders are shoulders that play even in the cruel killing-field,” thousands upon thousands of Tamils shed blood and entered prison forts.

Eight Tamil lions created a history unknown in any other country, entered fire and died to protect the language.

Therefore, Tamilian! Turn the revolutionary poet's cry—“Look, the pride of Tamil lions has risen; look how it seeks the enemy host to tear it apart!”—into a war-song.

Sound the battle cry of the Namakkal poet's courage-filled line: “Say that you are Tamil—stand with your head held high!”

Echo the feeling of Bharathi's song: “When the words ‘Land of refined Tamil’ are spoken, sweet honey flows into the ear!” Rise with the sound of the trumpet.

The flag of Tamil glory once flew in radiant splendour across many lands.

The ships he sent out cut through the waves of the sea and reached foreign countries—
