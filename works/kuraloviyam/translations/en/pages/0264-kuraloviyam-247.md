## Visual material

Large upper illustration of two travellers confronted by a bear in a forest.

Two friends were travelling through a dense forest. One was named Nallaan; the other, Vallaan. To keep themselves from feeling afraid of the wild animals, they walked along singing folk songs with enthusiasm. They wanted to cross the forest and reach their village before dark, and so they moved quickly.

An unexpected great danger was waiting for them. After crossing a hill and turning into a narrow footpath, they were startled to find a bear standing there. The moment they saw it, they clasped each other's hands and began to run. The bear ran after them. Vallaan asked Nallaan, “Hey! Do you know how to climb a tree?” Nallaan replied, “No!” Vallaan trembled. “You wretch! It looks as though because of you I too must end up in the bear's mouth!”
