## Visual material

Large upper illustration of Veeran holding and examining a sword with Aravaazhi behind him.

“A day is a sword,” Aravaazhi said as he entered.

Looking at him in surprise, Veeran joked, “Come, come! Why are you shouting ‘sword! sword!’ the moment you arrive?”

Patting Veeran, Aravaazhi asked, “What are you doing?” Veeran was always one for mischievous talk.

“Aravaazhi! What you have just asked is what they call a blind question!” he said with a laugh.

At that moment Veeran was sharpening his sword and polishing it until it gleamed. Seeing this, Aravaazhi had asked, “What are you doing, Veera?”

“Look how brightly the sword shines!”

Veeran lifted the sword high with pride.

“Do you know how many enemies' heads, hands and legs this sword must have cut down?”
