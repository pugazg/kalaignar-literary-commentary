## Visual material

Large upper illustration of two young women reclining on a moonlit seashore.

On the seashore where the sand lay spread like a mattress, Maangili and Poonkodi reclined, each with a hand supporting her head, looking as though two moons had descended to the earth. A light of laughter played on Poonkodi's flower-petal lips. Maangili's face, however, was like a withered flower. The sea breeze tugged playfully at their clothes as though eager to enjoy the beauty of the limbs of those two golden statues.

The waves too seemed to have desires of their own. Suddenly one wave came and wet Maangili up to her knees with the saliva of its pearly foam. Another tried to touch Poonkodi's thigh. Neither the breeze's mischief nor the waves' play disturbed the tender conversation of the two women.

“Maangili! I have been watching you for a long time. Whenever I see you, your round moon-face is withered. What is the reason? Tell me!”
