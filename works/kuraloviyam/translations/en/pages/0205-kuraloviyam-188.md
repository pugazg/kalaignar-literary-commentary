## Visual material

Large colour illustration of two women, one reclining and one standing, beneath a small vessel/flame motif.

In the Kural, the word `alar` is explained as “being known by many.” In other words, it may be expanded as taking a piece of information one has received, attaching hands and feet to it, and spreading it so that many people come to know it. Particularly in the case of a man or woman who has fallen in love, there are always some people in a village who cannot keep the news buried in their own minds, but somehow tell others about it—in the public pavilion, by the tank, at temple entrances, at festivals or in the marketplace—and keep piling imagination upon it!
