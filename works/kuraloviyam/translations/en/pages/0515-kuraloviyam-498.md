—she had stayed alone in the hut,  
watching and waiting for him!

The man who arrived swept her into his arms.

That flower of the pleasure-garden  
drank deeply  
of the bee's kisses!

This is what the wise Valluvar says:

If one crosses suffering with resolve,  
pleasure will come humbly  
and stand with folded hands.

> Even when suffering comes, act with firmness  
> in the deed that will bring delight.

Chapter 67 — Firmness in Action; Kural 669
