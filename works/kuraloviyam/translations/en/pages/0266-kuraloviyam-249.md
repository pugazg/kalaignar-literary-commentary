## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a woman separated from her departing lover.

The lover who had lain embracing his tender beloved in a forest of jasmine flowers now parts from her. Both suffer at losing the chance to taste without interruption the feast of youth. Trade comes between them. And so they separate.

“When I am embracing my beloved, even if he moves away only a little, pallor spreads across my whole body and torments me. Is it right for him to leave someone like me for so many days—
