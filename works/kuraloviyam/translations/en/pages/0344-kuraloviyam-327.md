## Visual material

Large upper illustration of the distressed merchant Aththi seated with Valluvar.

A merchant named Aththi came to see Valluvar with both mind and face withered in distress. “What has caused sorrow to take possession of a face that normally blooms with laughter?” Kuralar asked with feeling.

Aththi's eyes became clouds shedding raindrops. In a trembling voice he spoke:

“Great one! I laboured at my trade, gathered wealth little by little and became a merchant praised by the town. You know that well. My relatives proudly said that Aththi would one day build even a palace and live in it. But do you know my condition today? Will I even find a place to stay in the shade beneath a banyan tree? Such misery has surrounded me!”

Valluvar was startled.

“Truly? What caused your trade to fail and the garden of your prosperity to wither? How did this change happen?”

Aththi could not explain at once. He remained silent for a while.

“Wanting to multiply many times the wealth I had gathered little by little, I bought a ship to trade with a foreign land. I loaded it with Korkai pearls, pepper and peacock feathers and sent it toward Yavana country! I put all the wealth I possessed into that ship and filled it with goods for export. Had it reached the Yavana land, the income I would have received from it would have been beyond measure! But... but...!”
