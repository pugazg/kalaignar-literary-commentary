## Visual material

Large upper illustration of an embracing couple.

He came and stood before the creeper-like woman who had kept her eyes fixed upon the road waiting for him.

Without even a trace of fear about who might be nearby, her beautiful eyes began grazing over his face.

Those in the house withdrew.

“Separation pierced through my heart and carried a piece away, my jewel,” he said.

“I do not know what happened to my heart,” she replied. “The reason is that when you left me, it went with you.”

He stretched out his arms and embraced the dark-haired woman.

She buried her head deeply in his chest and found bliss.

He dropped kisses upon the crown of her head.

She rained kisses across his chest.

“Why, my gold? Why do your honeyed lips refuse even to look back at mine?”

he asked with the bud of a smile.

“Beloved! Why hurry so soon? Those four petals have been fasting all day so that they may be drenched all night. Let them wait!
