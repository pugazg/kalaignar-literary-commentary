One minister came forward to answer both questions.

"Your Majesty! We tried calling him by proclaiming the greatness of the crown. We even knelt and pleaded with him to come. Neither method worked. Even if you threaten to behead him with the sword, this Tamil poet will not come. The reason? He is a poet of self-respect."

Hearing this, the king rose in fury like a charging bull.

"That wretched man! Shall a householder living in a palm-leaf hut ignore the summons of a lofty palace? Issue the order at once! Put that useless fellow in chains and drag him here!"

According to the royal council's order, they brought the lion of self-respect in fetters and made him stand before the king's court.

"Well then, poet! I am the king who rules this land. You are merely one man among the many households within my grasp. Why do you wander about with a stiff tongue, showing no concern for making your household life prosperous? When so many people sing my praise and cling to me for favour, why do you alone hesitate to cling to my feet? Answer me! Otherwise you will be punished by having your head crushed!"

Though the king threatened him in arrogance, the lion of self-respect did not lower his head.

"Even if the gifts from your hand came as heaps of gold and gems to kiss my feet, I would not touch wealth stained in that way. I know that the wealth you have amassed without fear of disgrace runs into many crores.

"I am a householder who has not forgotten Valluvar's teaching that the unfailing excellence of domestic life lies in fearing blame, gathering wealth rightly, and sharing what one has.

"You may rule this land, but my honour will not bow before you. I will not sing. I will not sing of you. I will not sing of one who gathers wealth without fearing disgrace."

The ruler flared up.

"Impale this poet!" he roared like a fierce tiger.

"Compared with receiving wealth from your stained hands, the sharp point of the impaling stake would be pleasure."

Saying this, he walked away without fear of punishment. The whole court remained motionless, eyes wide with astonishment.

> If a household fears blame and shares its food,  
> its way of life will never fail.

Chapter 5 — Domestic Life; Kural 44

பாத்தூண் = sharing and eating in due portions
