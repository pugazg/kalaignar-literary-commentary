## Visual material

Large upper illustration of an embracing couple.

He embraced her as the sweet breeze comes and clasps a grove, making its flowers thrill. A young warmth spread through both their bodies.

With a jackfruit-sweet tongue he stroked her gums, tasted the teeth within lips that opened in a lovely smile, and declared that he had discovered a brand-new mixture in which a stream of milk joined a stream of honey within her mouth. He showered hundreds of kisses upon her.

“My deer! My peacock! My honey! My nectar! What wrong is there in adding beauty to beauty? If you drew a line of kohl beside those eyes that dart like sel and kendai fish, would it not add still greater splendour, my slender-waisted treasure?”

He rested his head near her neck and waited for an answer.

With lotus hands she raised his head, placed his lips close to her own, and trembled like a veena, eager to reply.

“My beloved! You have decided that this maiden does not know how to line her eyes! I stand before the mirror looking only at you within my eyes because I fear that, while applying kohl, the tip of my finger may hide you—or that my eyelid may move and stand like a curtain before you. Every movement of yours is a feast of the three fruits to my eyes, beloved—a feast!”
