In the heroic soil celebrated as the Sivaganga country, even today one can see not only the statues that stand as memorials to the Maruthu Pandiyars but also the Kalaiyar Kovil associated with them.

Between the statues of those great warriors, Periya Maruthu and Chinna Maruthu, and to the right of the temple entrance, there is another statue: a man standing with a gun in his hand. Beside that statue stands the figure of a bear.

It is the statue of Karuthaan, who was once a close friend of Chinna Maruthu, the younger of the Maruthu Pandiyars.

Karuthaan was highly skilled in hunting. Perhaps because he trapped animals in his nets, he was also called "Valaiyan."

On one occasion, when the two Maruthu brothers went hunting in the forest, Karuthaan saved them from the attack of a bear and shot the bear dead.

Praising his bravery, courage and skill, the Maruthu Pandiyars, when statues of themselves were raised, had a statue erected for Karuthaan too as an expression of gratitude.
