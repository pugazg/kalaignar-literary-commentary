## Visual material

Large colour illustration showing the lovers in the foreground, with a silhouetted embracing couple against a bright moon in the background.

A great warrior returns after smashing to dust an enemy army that had surged against him like a mountain. As he looks at the blunted sharpness of his spear, he worries that, in just this way, the spear-like eyes of his beloved too must have grown dim while watching for his return. Even at the ceremony the king has arranged to honour him with gifts, he takes part without his heart being there. Without realizing it, he strokes his own cheek, waiting for the gifts his maiden will give him; then he runs homeward. Even when he comes before her—even when he calls, “Vanji!”—she does not notice. So completely has she lost herself, keeping her eyes fixed upon the road, sending out the waves of the sorrowful song, “I do not see my beloved coming!”
