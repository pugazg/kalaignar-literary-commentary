## Visual material

Large colour moonlit-seaside illustration occupying most of the page: a couple embrace beside a boat beneath the moon and stars.

A seashore curtained in blue! The moon pours nectar-like light, turning the white spread of sand into a bed strewn with jasmine and mullai blossoms. A large boat, exhausted after finishing its work among the waves near the shore, rests there. In the shelter of its shadow—two figures!
