## Visual material

Large upper illustration of an embracing couple.

Among white cloud-swans swimming in the lake of the sky, I saw a dark cloud move aside like lotus leaves and the moon reveal its radiant face.

I saw that scattered light leap and glitter as stars upon the waves of the sky.

From somewhere I heard the flute of a cowherd.

It seemed as though some woman in a beautiful upper chamber had taken up a yaazh and played it.

Mixed with the breeze, that honeyed nectar flowed into my ears.

I stood listening and then moved away.

At home my mother thought sweets made with jaggery were not enough.

She carefully prepared the three fruits—banana, mango and jackfruit—and fed me.

I ate and rejoiced.

Then I walked a little way toward a grove.

I surrendered my mind to the fragrance spread by mullai and jasmine and returned.

I stroked the cotton-soft body of the rabbit I raised and thrilled with delight.

When I caressed the peacock feathers pressing against my back, I knew the pleasure of softness.

Yes—

pleasure seen by the eye,  
pleasure heard by the ear,  
pleasure smelled by the nose,  
pleasure tasted by the tongue,  
pleasure known through touch.

Thus—
