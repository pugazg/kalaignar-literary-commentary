## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a young man speaking with a richly adorned woman.

“You are not a woman! You must be one of Rambha, Urvashi or Tilottama, the celestial women described in the puranas.”

“Oh! Is that so? Am I that beautiful?”

“I would say you are more beautiful than all three.”

“Have you seen them?”

“I have heard of them through the stories of the puranas.”

“Do you like me?”

“My dear! Is not your glance the hook that has caught this fish?”

“Oh! You are quite the connoisseur. And then?”

“Ah! Your soft lips look like the broad feet of rutting elephants that pierce my heart!”

“Mm! Go on!”
