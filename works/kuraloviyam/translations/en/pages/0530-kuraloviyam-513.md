—with a heart longing to know where all the pleasures separately experienced by the five senses might be found together, I walked along the bank of a lake.

Ah! How can I describe that delight?

All the pleasures that had come separately to the five senses gathered into one and ran to embrace me.

Seeing my beloved, who remains in my heart without leaving whether she is near or far—

hearing her words of love—

sinking into the fragrance that intoxicates me—

tasting the honey of her lips—

lying with our two bodies woven together as one—

how can I describe the pleasure the five senses experience all at once?

There is not even the smallest doubt in Valluvar's statement that all five kinds of delight are found in the radiant, bangled woman.

> Sight, sound, taste, scent and touch—  
> all five senses find their delight in the bright-bangled woman.

Chapter 111 — The Joy of Union; Kural 1101
