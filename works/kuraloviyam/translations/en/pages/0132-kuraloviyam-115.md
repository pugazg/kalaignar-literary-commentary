## Visual material

Large colour illustration showing a woman in the foreground and a man behind her in a wooded setting.

Why was a woman called a creeper? Because her body droops in tenderness?

Because she is delicate even in the way she bends as she walks? No—no! Just as a creeper needs a support around which to spread and cling, a woman too needs a companion upon whom she may rest and entwine herself. A tree over which a creeper has spread gains a beauty all its own! Trees without creepers are like naked human beings!
