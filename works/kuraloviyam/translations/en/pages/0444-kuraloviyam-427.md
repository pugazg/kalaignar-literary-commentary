## Visual material

Large upper illustration of two royal or court figures arguing before food stores.

“Did you commit the crime or not?”

The voice rang out in the royal audience hall.

Ministers, commanders and spear-bearing warriors stood all around as he thundered defiantly.

The king seated beneath the royal parasol sprang up, drawing his sword.

At that moment a great roar came from outside the palace.

Yes—it was a roar in support of the man who had raised his voice in the hall, and against the king.

The king stood still with his sword drawn. The entire hall fell silent.

“The story that someone broke into the granary where food grain had been stored for the people, looted it and fled is a fraud—a conspiracy!

“O king! You yourself joined hands with that band of thieves, carried all the grain from the granary into the neighbouring country during this famine, sold it there, and left the people of your own land to starve to death.

“Your treasury is now filled with bars of gold—but thousands of people in the country are dying every day.

“Now answer! Are you guilty or not?”

The king replied like thunder:

“No! No! I am not guilty!”

Nor did he stop there—
