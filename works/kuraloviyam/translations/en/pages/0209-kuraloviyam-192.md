## Visual material

Large colour illustration dominated by a tearful young woman's face, with silhouetted departing figures above.

A flood surges over its banks. At the sight of that flood, her eyes find a little consolation. Without her even realizing it, they leap into the flood and begin to swim.

“Where have you gone, my eyes?” she cries out. The eyes do not fail to answer her.

“We cannot bear to be without seeing him. We are swimming through this flood of tears in search of him,” the eyes say. At that, a smile flashes for a moment across her sorrow-covered face and disappears.
