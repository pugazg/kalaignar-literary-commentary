—they longed to drink each other in, taste nectar, and forget the whole world.

But at every such meeting, that harsh cry—“Hey, Azhagammai!”—never failed to rise like a wall between them.

Even so, there are many people in the world who consent to an unavoidable separation, cease tormenting themselves over the pain it brings, endure the separation, remain alive afterward, and live on, are there not?

The two lovers resolved that they too would live like such people, certain that one day they would finally join.

There are many people with hearts hard enough to bear separation. The lovers added themselves to that list and rebuked their own hearts.

The sound of the pain within those hearts is what this Kural explains:

> Many there are who, after enduring what is hard,  
> bear separation, put away the suffering, and live on afterward.

Chapter 116 — Inability to Bear Separation; Kural 1160
