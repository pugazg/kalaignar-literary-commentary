## Visual material

Large colour domestic illustration showing an older woman at left, a younger woman carrying stacked pots, and another woman holding a child.

“Little sister, where are you going?”

“What is this, akka—why ask such a question? I'm going to see our man, who has been working in the forest-fields since morning.”

“Oh! I'm babbling. I have cooked food like nectar especially for him, haven't I? Even though I know you are the one going to take it to him, in my confusion I asked such a question. I'm a madwoman!”
