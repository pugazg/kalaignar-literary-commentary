## Visual material

Large upper illustration of the hill-country ruler Seeralan kneeling before a sovereign.

Seeralan was the small ruler of a hill country. Its rich forests were the greatest treasury of his land. A portion of the annual revenue had to be paid as tribute to the emperor Pemman. With the remaining funds he was to provide the facilities needed by the people of the hill country.

Seeralan paid the tribute due to the empire regularly, but spent a large part of the wealth remaining in the hill country on his own luxurious life, the maintenance of the palace and the queen's jewels and ornaments.
