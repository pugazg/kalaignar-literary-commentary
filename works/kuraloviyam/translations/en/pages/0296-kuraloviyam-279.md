## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a victorious warrior advancing with troops.

A great war ends in defeat. The army's strength is reduced. The victor uses the situation to keep raining hardships upon the defeated soldiers.

A chapter of revenge!

An attempt to settle old spite!

Plans to wipe out the enemy altogether!

Every web of intrigue needed for that purpose is woven.

He has the warriors captured in battle beaten mercilessly. He orders that they be starved to death.
