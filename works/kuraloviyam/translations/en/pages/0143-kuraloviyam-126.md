Government officials had also been watching Sigamani's activities closely. One day a large crowd gathered in front of Sigamani's pharmacy. Government officers were inspecting the medicines there. The inspection had taken place because it had been caught red-handed that he was adulterating and selling his medicines. The people talked among themselves.

“How noble and disciplined he appeared until now. And now the truth has been exposed!”

“He lived by accusing the whole town in order to hide his own fault. That day he spoke like a floodgate bursting open about how evil it was to drink liquor, yet that very night I saw him drinking it with my own eyes!”

As many such views were being exchanged, an elderly man intervened and said:

“Have they forgotten Valluvar's words that even though some people may appear outwardly red and pure like the kunrimani seed, inwardly they are black like the nose of that very seed? This Medical Sigamani is an example of exactly that.”

> Though outwardly they may look red like the kunrimani seed,  
> there are those whose inner selves are black like its nose.
