—her tongue-coloured feet had reddened like hibiscus flowers. She stood with her waist resting against the parapet of the outer terrace adjoining the bedchamber, her red-lily fingers weary from propping up her moon-face. Staring into the sky, she asked angrily with her eyes, “O cool moon that burns, why do you scorch only me?”

Then a sound rose near the outer terrace and stopped.

The golden maiden's heart beat fast to know what it was.

She looked toward the direction from which the sound had come.

He had leapt onto a tree branch beside the terrace and was moving toward where she stood. Yes—it was the same man she had met by the waterfall that evening.

She had gone there to bathe. He too had come. As she bathed with closed eyes beneath the rush of water, the cool waterfall played many mischievous tricks upon her clothes and body. He stood at a distance blessing the waterfall. After bathing, she came away combing out her wet hair and carelessly failing to straighten the clothes that had slipped, thinking no one was there. She collided with him. Only then did he emerge from behind the flowering bush.

Without her knowing it, her clothes corrected themselves. Is that not true womanhood?

She looked at him as though facing an army. “Is this what becomes a man?” she hurled at him harshly. He stood speechless and dazed. She too stood hesitating. Then she gave speed to her feet and disappeared from there. She had seen him in many places before, not only by the waterfall; he too had seen her. He knew which household's green vine she was. She knew who he was as well. But the encounter beside the waterfall that evening had been unexpected by both. In what a state had such a close meeting occurred!

It was that same man who now leapt from the branch onto the terrace. Slowly mastering her shock, she let anger show and asked in a voice that reached only his ears, “Who is it? What business do you have here at this hour?”

“Don't you recognize me? It is I,” he said, coming to stand beside her.

“What audacity you have! You showed such ‘good manners’ beside the waterfall this evening. Is this the same continuing story now?”
