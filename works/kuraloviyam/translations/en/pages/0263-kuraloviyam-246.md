Moon! I stand motionless in the pond, marking the rhythm for your dance upon the waves with the beat of my heart. Come, moon, come! White flower! Silver peacock! Come! Come!

Have you come, great beauty? Yes—what is that on your face? Wait, wait! Let me look closely. Oh! Is that not the mark of the seal I placed the other day? Did I place a seal on you? No, no—I have poured out to you the story between me and my beloved. How many days have passed since she left me! When she returns, will the mark I pressed upon her on the day of parting appear on her cheek just like this?

Moon! Stop your Bharatanatyam for a moment and look a little at my misery. Without you the sky has no beauty; without her I have no life.

It is wrong even to compare her with you! Her face possesses a radiance beyond yours. You are a white moon; she is a golden moon. The face of my Tamil maiden is a picture painted by joining two fish for eyes. Her smile is like pearls packed into a jewelled casket, peeping out because there is no room left inside! I told you that even if you performed penance for a thousand years, no lord could grant you such a boon.

If I try to console myself by looking at you in her place, even that gives me no relief—you pour the fire of love upon me in abundance! You shower me with delightful memories of that colourful parrot-maiden!

Who is standing together on the bank and laughing? Ah, the young men of the village? Seeing me suffer from separation in love and speak to you without knowing myself, they stand on the bank clapping and laughing. Do you see?

Let them laugh. They may even laugh thinking me mad!

Did Valluvar speak without reason? Because they have not suffered the pain I have suffered, those ignorant men stand before my eyes and laugh at me. Only if they too had suffered as I have would they understand!

This is a lover's cry of anguish.

This Kural gives it expression:

> The unknowing laugh before our very eyes,  
> because they have not suffered what we have suffered.

Chapter 114 — Renouncing Modesty; Kural 1140
