## Visual material

Large upper illustration of an armed crowd in uprising.

Murders had become everyday events.

Robbery and theft had become forms of entertainment.

Such was the country.

The very people belonging to those who ruled built towers for injustice and held daily consecration ceremonies for the finials of violence.

Apart from those already sacrificed to murder and plunder, the remaining citizens were continually ruined by the cruel hands of the rulers.

Poverty struck them.

They struggled and suffered in destitution.

Even in that condition, the ruling group girded themselves to squeeze out taxes, surrounded the people, and tormented them.

Death itself seemed the only thing capable of giving joy to the poor and humble.

Those who suffered could cry out, but there was no chance their cries would reach the ears of those who had come to rule.

What those rulers savoured was only the music of the pleasure-creepers standing around them and shooting arrows of desire—the sound of anklets ringing in dance.

Those who asked “Why?” were crushed.

Those who advised them, “Can this injustice continue?” were thrown into pits of destruction.

There was no longer any limit to vulgarity or degrading speech.

Whoever came forward to raise a powerful voice for the people crying in deprivation—
