## Visual material

Large night-time illustration of a woodcutter couple embracing beneath a tree.

Cutting firewood was his trade. Carrying it on her head and selling it was her work. Every day he would take his axe and go into the forest. He felled trees, cut branches and returned home with a load of firewood.

The firewood he brought one day would be taken by her the next morning to sell; from morning until evening she wandered beneath the fierce sun.

Those two lovebirds who parted in the morning had made a life of returning to their nest only at night.

Her lover would point with concern to how her body had darkened from wandering in the burning summer heat selling firewood.

“Wasn't I dark already?” she would ask, leaning into his lap. “Black grapes are sweeter,” he would say, delighting in her taste.
