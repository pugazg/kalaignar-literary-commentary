## Visual material

Large colour battlefield illustration of a commander cradling a wounded or fallen warrior.

The war drum had sounded, calling him to the battlefield—a terrible mire where armies become foliage, red blood pools like water, elephants trample, and the heads of enemies are to be planted. The call had come for that warrior.

“See how the army roars, my dear—give me leave without standing in my way,” pleads the warrior-son who regards every day without an honourable wound as a wasted day. The beautiful woman, whose eyes themselves have grace and whose form is lovely, gathers her pearl-smiling lips and kisses him. “Return with victory, Atthaan,” she says, embracing him, wiping her tears on his shoulder without letting him see them.
