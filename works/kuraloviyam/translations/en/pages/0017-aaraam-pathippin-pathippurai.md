# Publisher's Note for the Sixth Edition

When we read Kalaignar’s book *Kuraloviyam*, every page is clear nectar that never cloys: descriptions that sweep up the heart, stories that give pleasure the more one reads them.

In this manner he has written beautiful explanations for Kurals in all three divisions of the Thirukkural—the Book of Aram, the Book of Porul and the Book of Love.

Five editions of this book have been issued through our publishing house, and it now appears as the sixth edition. We convey our heartfelt thanks to our leader Kalaignar for granting us permission to print and publish, in this sixth edition, *Kuraloviyam*—a prestigious publication of Bharathi Pathippagam and an incomparable book of the Tamil people.

We express our thanks to the artists Mr. Jeyaraj and Mr. Padmavasan, who beautifully drew the pictures required for each Kural in this book.

We also thank Mr. Ko. Shanmuganathan, assistant to our leader Kalaignar, and Tamilkani Pathippagam for providing every assistance necessary for the publication of this book.

We further offer our thanks to all those who stood by us and helped in every way to bring out this sixth edition: Mr. G. Sivanesapandian (Pinnacle Software (P) Ltd.), Mr. T.S.P. Muthuraman Chettiar (Muthuraman Enterprises), Mr. G. Devarajan (Retd. Chief Telephone Supervisor), Mr. A. Muthuraman Chettiar (Visalatchi Paper Mart), Mr. R. Muthukkumarasamy (Managing Director, Saiva Siddhanta Works Publishing Society), Mr. Akilan Kannan (Thaagam), Mr. A. Ramanathan Chettiar, and Mr. M. Tirupati Chettiar (Thirumagal Nilayam).

With the thought that this excellent book should serve as a guide for every family, we have brought out this sixth edition with special care. Our thanks to the readers who purchase it and benefit from it!

Chennai-17  
1.1.2004

— Bharathi Pathippagam
