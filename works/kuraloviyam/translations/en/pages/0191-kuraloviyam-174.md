## Visual material

Large colour illustration showing a young couple beneath a flowering tree.

Clouds gather in the sky. The maiden Earth looks upward, her face bright with delight. It has been many days since she met her lover, the rain. She has longed for the mischievous embraces of that lover; now, seeing signs that he is about to arrive, her joy overflows. Driven by his desire to touch the beautiful body of the maiden Earth, the rain-lover comes rushing down. Mother Earth gathers him into her embrace! When earth and water join and mingle, can they easily be separated? The Thirukkural says they cannot. Kurunthogai polishes this idea and gives it still greater breadth. When rain falls upon red soil and gathers there, Kurunthogai shows how the water too takes on the colour of that soil until the two mingle without distinction.
