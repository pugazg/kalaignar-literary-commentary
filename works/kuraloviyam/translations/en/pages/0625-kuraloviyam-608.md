Kaniyan, who rose with the sun, laboured in the paddy fields and never failed in his duty of gathering the ripened grain.

He had a son named Kunran, the lamp of his lineage.

In the house opposite lived an old man named Pachai, who raised his son Pachaiyan with great affection.

Kunran learned all the good education his father Kaniyan provided and rose like a lamp upon a temple tower.

Pachai, on the other hand, decided that it was enough merely to keep Pachaiyan close to him; he arranged matters so that rational understanding never came near the boy.

One day news arrived that the mother of a close relative had died.

Pachaiyan and Kunran went to that village to offer condolences.

The relative was sunk in grief over the loss of his mother.

Kunran consoled him:

"Was your mother a mother only to you? She was like a mother to this whole village."

His eyes filled with tears.

After the funeral rites were completed, the two returned home.
