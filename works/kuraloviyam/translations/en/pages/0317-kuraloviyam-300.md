## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a boastful man atop a steep rock before villagers.

A man proclaimed heroically that there was no one who could defeat him. Nor did he fail to announce that nothing in the world was beyond his power. Whether there was firmness in his heart or not, the villagers heard the volcanic words bursting from his lips and showered flowers upon him, praising him as a hero, a man of mighty shoulders, and a lion with a fearless heart. His speeches were such that the people of the village could not realize that inwardly he was a coward.

A little distance outside the village stood a steep rock. Many had tried to reach its summit and slipped and fallen halfway. One day, among the villagers, he vowed, “See my strength. I will climb to the top of that rock.” A day was fixed for him to fulfil his words.

The villagers gathered around the steep rock. He too arrived there.

“People of the village, listen to one thing! I told you that I would reach the summit of the rock. I did not explain then how I would get there. My goal is simply to reach the summit. For that, not only courage but a little wisdom must be used. Let four men here stand one upon another's shoulders. There is no need to fear; stand leaning against the rock!”
