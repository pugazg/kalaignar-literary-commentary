## Visual material

Large colour illustration showing a separated woman looking up toward the moon beneath a giant pair of eyes.

“The moon burns like fire—alas! Cruel moon! You are called the cool moon; why then do you pour embers upon me?” There are women who flare up in anger at the full moon like this when they are separated from the husband they once embraced against their breast in joy.

“Wicked moon! Do not stand before me! Go! Go! Hide yourself!” There are women who stand firing arrows of words and shedding tears—
