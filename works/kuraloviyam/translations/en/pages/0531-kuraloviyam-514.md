## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a man shielding a child beneath a cloth in heavy rain, with several figures crossing the embankment behind.

The sea roars in fury, its waves raised by storm winds, like a thousand tigers roaring together.

The spinning cyclone uproots trees and playfully plants them upside down.

Lightning slashes before the face like many-edged swords hurled straight at one.

Thunder crashes as though the roof of the sky has collapsed and shattered in every direction.

Rain lashes down like a hard-hearted employer whipping a labourer who arrived late.

Darkness is like the heart of one without mercy.

Through this scene a man walks alone.

In his arms he holds something wrapped in a woollen cloth.

From within comes the thin sound of a child crying.

Yes—a tiny infant.

The mother who gave birth to that tender shoot has closed her eyes forever.

Nature has swallowed the father too.

The man is the child's elder brother.

If he can carry the infant safely to the place it must reach, many people are waiting there to cherish and raise it.
