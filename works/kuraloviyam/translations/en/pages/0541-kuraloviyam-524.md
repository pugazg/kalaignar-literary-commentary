## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a man holding a child before a wealthy older man.

A footpath where dogs and pigs roam.

Piles of rubbish lie here and there.

Dew fallen through the night has left the uneven path damp.

An old cow, still unable to bear the cold, lies hunched by the roadside with its head resting upon the earth.

There is a hut on the path without even a roof.

Its door too has fallen inward.

Though full daylight has arrived, inside that open house lies a middle-aged woman with her breasts uncovered.

A child rolls beside her, trying first one breast and then the other, crying sharply.

Yes—unable to bear the cruelty of poverty, the woman died at dawn.

The little infant, knowing nothing of life or death, sucks at its dead mother's dried breasts and cries in disappointment and hunger.

At that moment the scene does not escape the notice of the philanthropist Vayalooran, who happens to pass that way.

He stops and looks carefully.

“What are you staring at, sir?

“You are the one who carries the title of philanthropist, are you not?

“What you see now is a mother dead of hunger and a child struggling toward death!”
