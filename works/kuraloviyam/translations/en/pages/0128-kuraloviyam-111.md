## Visual material

Large colour illustration of two lovers seated among trees and flowers.

From ancient times there has been a belief among Tamils that one sneezes when someone is thinking of us, and that when a person sneezes those nearby offer a blessing. Using that custom, Valluvar paints a rare scene of love.

Upon the green grass of a grove, dressed in milk-white garments, the young woman and young man of love are playing upon a rain of delight. As they embrace closely and rejoice inwardly, perhaps a small lock of the dark hair of the moon-faced woman pressed against him unknowingly touches his nose. Whatever the cause, he sneezes...
