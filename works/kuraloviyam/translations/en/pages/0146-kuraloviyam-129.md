## Visual material

Large colour illustration showing an elderly physician facing a tiger in dense forest.

A dense forest! Ground crowded with thorny scrub! Though it was daytime, the trees cast such deep shade that light could scarcely enter. A physician who had to cross that forest to reach the next village had no other route and so walked along that very path. He was pleased to think that the journey was proving useful in one way as well. The reason was that the forest abounded in leaves and medicinal roots needed for preparing his remedies. With great enthusiasm he filled his medicine bag with them all. One might even say that in that whole region there was no physician equal to him. If poor people fell ill, he treated them without taking even a coin from them.

His feet were always circling through the places where ordinary people lived. With the fees he received for treating the wealthy and the households of landed proprietors, he was able to carry on medical service for people suffering in poverty. For that very reason the people of the region had gathered together and joyfully awarded him even the title “Master Physician.” Praises such as “There is no illness that will not be cured if he gives the medicine” kept piling up as well. Not only human beings: those who kept animals and birds at home also sought out this same physician when those creatures fell ill.
