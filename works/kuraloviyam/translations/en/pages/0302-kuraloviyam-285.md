“I have already shown you the places where both of them live!” I said.

“When?” he asked.

“Just now!” I said.

“Where?” he asked.

I explained it to him. “Those men at the forge worked without even thinking of weariness, pouring out sweat. Thirumagal makes their hearts her palace and lives there. And that watchman lies idly by, turning a beautiful pavilion into a filthy one; Moodevi makes his heart her house and lives in that dark dwelling.”

“Ah! What a wonderful thought!” said my friend.

“This is neither my thought nor my imagination. It is Valluvar's Kural,” I said.

Then I recited the Kural.

It is this:

> They say great Moodevi dwells in the sluggard;  
> the lotus-dame dwells at the feet of the man without sloth.
