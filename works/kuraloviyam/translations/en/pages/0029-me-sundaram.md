# Dr. Me. Sundaram

In the entire Indian subcontinent, the one distinguished person whose creative literature has been studied to the extent of producing, for the first time, six M.Phil. degrees and five doctoral degrees is Kalaignar.

That is because Mother Tamil gave me the good opportunity to supervise and award all eleven of them.

The six M.Phil. degrees obtained through research on Kalaignar's creative literature range from *Silappathikaram* to *Kuraloviyam*.

I also had the opportunity to award five doctoral degrees based on Kalaignar's speeches, writings, thoughts, minor literary works, major literary works, poems and plays taken together.

Kalaignar has created a body of creative literature capable of generating eleven research degrees in this way. I take pride in that.

Rather than speak only of my own praise for Kalaignar, I wish to mention here the praise offered by two American professors.

In a letter written to me on 11-2-85, Professor Phillips Segalman of the University of Minnesota in the United States greatly praised Kalaignar's sense of ethnic identity, sense of language and literary scholarship.

In another letter to me, Professor Hardgrave praised Kalaignar, saying that Kalaignar's work is work that crowns Mother Tamil with a jewelled crown.

These are people who did not know Kalaignar personally; they read his writing and placed this garland of praise upon him.
