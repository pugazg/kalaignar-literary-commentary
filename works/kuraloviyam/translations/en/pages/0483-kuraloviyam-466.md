## Visual material

Large upper illustration of embracing lovers beside a lamp.

He must keep reading newer and newer books.

He reads ancient literature again and again and savours it.

Whatever renaissance literature appears, it climbs into his hands, passes through his eyes and settles in the chamber of thought.

Geography, history, any field of knowledge, essays, poems, novels, plays—only after he has read everything does even a drop of his desire feel satisfied.

After finishing what he has read, he longs for what remains unread.

Such is his hunger for knowledge.

To feed that hunger burning in his heart, libraries become banquet halls.

The stomach, after eating and eating, warns that it needs rest.
