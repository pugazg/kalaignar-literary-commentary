## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a wounded man supported by another man.

The young man had been languishing in prison under a seven-year sentence. He had been sent to prison for a crime he had not committed. Though he raged inwardly whenever he thought of the gift the court had given an innocent man, he adjusted himself to prison life and strengthened his heart to endure those seven years.

He took great care both to perform the work assigned to prisoners properly and to make use of the prison library. For reasons no one knew, one of the officers there bore an unnecessary hatred toward the young man. Yet the young man obeyed prison rules and behaved respectfully toward every officer.

He had a special desire to help solve the other prisoners' problems and to run to their aid whenever illness struck them. For some reason, that good conduct displeased that particular officer. The young man repeatedly suffered the consequences inside the prison.

The officer would deliberately summon him in a harsh voice and speak to him contemptuously. On some days he would beat the young man with his cane—
