“You go mad even if you are separated from him for just a little while, akka!”

“Go on, you little tease! Look at you joking and mocking without even remembering that you're speaking to your elder sister!”

“I'm only a little girl, akka! Can't I joke? You never dislike it when I talk about your man. You only pretend to be shy. Isn't that so, akka?”

“All right, all right! The rice and curry are getting cold. Take them and leave. Tell him to come quickly when his work is done.”

“Shall I tell him to fly here? Akka, if you have two wings, give them to me! I'll tie them to him and tell him to fly back.”

Saying this, the little girl lifted the food basket and set out. As her elder sister stood by the front platform watching her walk away, she heard a child crying inside the house. Calling “Appa! Appa!” and toddling along, he came out to the platform. Even when she lifted him, he would not stop crying. “Appa! Appa! I want Appa!” he began shouting loudly.

The elder sister called out to the younger girl who was going down the street. “What? Why did you call me?” the girl asked as she ran back.

“Here! Take him to his father for a little while! He's tormenting me because he wants to see him!” she said, handing the child over to her sister. The child's face blossomed. Carrying that little bud, the girl took leave of her sister and ran off.

A woman sitting by the window of the house opposite has been watching this scene and listening to the sisters' conversation. She is a fish-eyed woman bewildered and suffering in separation from her lover.

Just as the little girl from the opposite house carries food in search of her brother-in-law, that lover's heart, it seems, goes in search of her beloved! And just like the child crying stubbornly because he wants to see his father, her eyes torment her because they want to see her lover. So she pleads with her own heart:

“My heart! When you go to him, take my eyes with you too! My eyes are devouring me because they long to see him.”

> My heart, when you go, take these eyes with you too;  
> longing to see him, they are devouring me.

Chapter 125 — Speaking with the Heart; Kural 1244
