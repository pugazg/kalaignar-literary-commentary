## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a lover addressing the moon and its reflection on the water.

Beautiful moon, I delight in watching you leap upon the pond and dance your feet among the waves. I do not wish to raise my eyes and look at the sky. If I do, you seem to be at an unreachable distance from me. Yes—like my beloved!

That is why, seeing you appear so near and move beautifully among the waves, I have stepped into the pond itself to gather you into my arms. Ah! Why does your body of light tremble like this? The moment I embrace you, where have you slipped away?

My treasure with a golden body, tender form and honeyed face, fountain of delight, my incomparable living portrait—she too is like this! When I embrace her and tremble with desire, she darts away like a fish, leaps and runs like a deer!
