## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a couple seated closely on a bed.

Valluvar refers to “sneezing” in three or four places in the third division of the Kural!

It was believed in those days that a person would sneeze because a close relative bound by affection was thinking of them. Even today there are people who hold that belief. Can anything equal the marvellous Kurals Valluvar composed with that belief as their basis?

A lover who is lying in an embrace on the bed sneezes. The moment she hears the sneeze, the beloved blesses him: “May you live a hundred years!” But after blessing him, a sudden doubt flashes through her mind. “Who was thinking of you at this hour and made you sneeze? Who is she?” she asks, abandons her blessing and begins to weep. After setting out that scene, Valluvar brings several more delightful scenes before us.

A beautiful woman sits in the moonlit courtyard, suffering the pain of separation from her man. She longs, wondering, “Amid all his unending duties, will he think of me?” That longing widens into a question. Just then she feels the sign of a sneeze coming. Yes—
