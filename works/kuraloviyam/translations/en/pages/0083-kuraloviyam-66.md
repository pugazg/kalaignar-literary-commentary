## Visual material

Large colour illustration above the prose showing a spear-bearing victorious king or warrior in conversation with a bearded Valluvar figure; chariot and army imagery appear behind them.

A king who had entered enemy territory and won victory was returning towards his capital surrounded by his soldiers. On the way he met the great Valluvar. As soon as he saw him, the king got down from his chariot, came forward and offered his respects. Valluvar too stood blessing him.

“O king! How was the war you fought against your enemies?”

Before Valluvar had even finished asking, the king announced the news of his victory and began explaining how he and his warriors had fought on the battlefield and secured that triumph.
