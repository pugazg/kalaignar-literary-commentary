## Visual material

Large colour illustration showing two men facing a third young man in a mountain landscape with a waterfall in the background.

A sculptor who turns stone into art and a painter who makes pictures speak were walking along a mountainside, delighting in the sights of nature. The pleasurable embrace of the gentle breeze was nectar to their imaginative minds. Across the mountain peaks, clouds lay spread in many forms, as though black elephants and white horses were learning to walk there. The two talked happily with one another about the wondrous works of art they had created, and rejoiced that this luxuriant landscape was so fitting a place for their conversation. In the distance, a waterfall appeared like the silver hair of a grey old woman tossing rapidly in a strong wind. Looking at his friend, who proudly said that he had painted a picture like that, the sculptor replied with delight: “See there—a deer is standing and watching a peacock dance. Only yesterday I finished carving just such a sculpture in stone.”

In the midst of their conversation, the painter suddenly opened his eyes wide and stood astonished. “Ah! Who is that? It looks as though beauty itself has taken form and is coming toward us!”

The sculptor too looked in the direction the painter was gazing. He knew who was approaching. The painter had already begun describing the beautiful living picture coming toward them.

“My friend! Never until now have I created such a flowering of beauty with my brush.”
