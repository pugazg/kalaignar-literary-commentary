## Visual material

Large colour illustration occupying the upper half of the page: a young woman in a yellow dress seated by an opening, with two shadowed figures in the background.

At the edge of the hall window sat that young Tamil woman, with a crescent-curved forehead, lips like the kovai fruit, sharp spear-like eyes, and cheeks radiant with kumkum light.

She would have been sixteen. The richness of youth flew like a banner on a mountain peak. Her gaze seemed to swallow the wide expanse of sky. In the softly blowing breeze, her silk garment stirred.

She could not help hearing her mother speaking rather loudly from the street-side veranda. Her mother was scolding her ten-year-old son. He was the younger brother visible from the window.

“Hey, Marudha! Where have you been playing?”

“I went to my friend Thenappan's house in the next street and played with him, Amma!”
