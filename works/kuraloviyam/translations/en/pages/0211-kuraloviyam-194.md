## Visual material

Large colour illustration of an armed warrior confronting two men in a wooded setting.

The armies of the enemy king, massed like a surging sea, suddenly surrounded and attacked Thennavan's fortress. Stunned by the unexpected shock, Thennavan's forces were thrown into confusion. King Thennavan himself was deeply astonished. Though he had powerful spies, how had the enemy ruler laid siege to his fortress without even their knowing? He could find no answer to that question. Yet the answer did not remain hidden for long.

The fortress fell into the enemy's hands; the enemy flag was raised at its entrance. As Thennavan advanced and struck at the attackers, three masked men surrounded him and seized him. The enemy king came and stood before Thennavan with a victorious smile. Wondering, “Those who captured me came from within my own ranks... who are they?”, Thennavan turned and looked. The three men removed the cloths covering their faces. They were none other than the commander-in-chief whom Thennavan had trusted most, together with Thennavan's confidential spies!

Looking at them, the captive king burst into laughter.

“No matter! I was worried that my country and I had been defeated because my army lacked ability. No! No! They have made it clear that this defeat came through traitors, and so they have relieved me of that worry!”

Thennavan spoke with complete calm.
