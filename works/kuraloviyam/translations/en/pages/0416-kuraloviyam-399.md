## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a turbaned young man carrying a basket overhead beside a young woman.

It was a beautiful street lined with mansions. From morning through half the night, men and women passed along it, eating food and throwing away the leaves on which it had been served. Litter lay scattered across the street. Morning wind leapt and played, moving the leaves and refuse that had lain still.

In the evening, people drove oxen home from the fields; dung lay here and there. Herdsmen brought cattle from the grazing grounds, and because the animals went on without looking back at the grass that fell from their mouths, green patches appeared along the road.

When morning bloomed, Vaigai came as usual with a long broomstick in her hand to perform the duty of beautifying the street. Vadivazhagan had waited until she came; the moment she arrived, he too came to sweep the street.

From one end Vaigai began cleaning, scooping the rubbish into a basket.

From the other end Vadivazhagan removed what had accumulated and gradually drew nearer to her.

Every day the two of them worked to clean that street. Yet beyond their control, their eyes began to speak to one another.
