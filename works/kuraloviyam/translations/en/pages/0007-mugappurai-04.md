> “Fame! You are a courtesan. From those who wander in search of you until their legs ache, you take payment and bestow love!  
> 
> Fame! You are a lunar realm. In their effort to reach you in full, many great minds have still been unable to succeed!  
> 
> Fame! You are the summit of the Himalayas. Through unceasing effort, a few will seize you!  
> 
> Fame! You are a block of ice. Even when you are held within the hand and gripped tightly, you melt into water and disappear!  
> 
> Fame! You are a jar of liquor. The flies that fell for you never rose again!  
> 
> Fame! You are a shadow. You will keep following those who do not concern themselves about you!”

This is one example showing that, nearly forty years ago, I had already tried to create the word-pictures that today have multiplied in great numbers under the name “free verse”! Here is another:

> “Mountain! I would call you  
> a jeweled crown wrought in gold and green  
> to adorn the head of Mother Earth,  
> seated upon her throne  
> beneath the celestial canopy  
> where diamond lamps shine!  
> I marvel whether all the white clouds of the sky  
> have fallen below and grown  
> to a height beyond the reach of the eye!  
> When you have wrapped your body in snow!”

There are many such sculptures that I shaped in prison.

Tamil Nadu knows, through the book collection titled *Thenalaigal*, that many years ago I also had the practice of writing short stories in a poetic style.

Therefore, in taking graceful Tamil, mixing thought into the colours of letters, and painting pictures of ideas for the Kurals, I have felt no hesitation; nor has any obstacle stood across my path.

I ask you humbly and kindly not to think that I say this out of arrogance, measuring and proclaiming my own ability.
