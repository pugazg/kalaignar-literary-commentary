## Visual material

Large upper political illustration of Lenin, Stalin and Soviet figures.

After Lenin, the great warrior who created Soviet Russia, the honour of building and safeguarding that communist land belongs to Stalin. Stalin defended the country from Hitler's massive invasion and protected the communist doctrine as well.

After Stalin's death, Malenkov assumed leadership, and then Khrushchev overthrew him and took charge. Khrushchev spent a great deal of time criticizing Stalin.

It is said that once, at a meeting in the Kremlin, Khrushchev was sharply criticizing the authoritarian excesses of the late Stalin. Someone interrupted and asked, “When Stalin's dictatorial abuses were taking place, what were you doing without challenging them?”

Khrushchev reportedly looked angrily at the man who asked. The questioner fell silent and sat down.

Then Khrushchev calmly replied, “That is exactly what I too was doing.”

Such reports used to come during the Khrushchev period.
