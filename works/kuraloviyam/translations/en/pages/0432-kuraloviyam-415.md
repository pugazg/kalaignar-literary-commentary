—realized that the child had moved near the water and tried to leap toward it. Before she could reach it, she shouted to the beautiful woman:

“Catch the child! Catch it!”

The beauty heard the cry, but with a darkened face climbed onto the bank and moved aside to adjust her wet clothes.

Before the mother could seize the child, it slipped into the water.

Even so, she immediately lifted it out and saved it from the mouth of death.

Her whole body trembling, she kissed the child and embraced it.

“My life! You survived!”

Then she poured her anger upon the beautiful woman:

“What use is beauty in every limb if there is no love within? Outward beauty in one without inward love is only pretence. The beauty of love is absent in your heart. A body of bone covered by skin has no worth by itself—go!”

> What use are all the outward limbs of the body  
> to one who lacks the inward limb called love?

Chapter 8 — Possession of Love; Kural 79
