## Visual material

Large colour illustration above the prose showing a bound young man/captive in the presence of armed or royal figures, with an elderly observer at left.

There are those who marvel at and praise the turning point by which a man who had roamed the forests committing highway robbery and murder became Valmiki, the singer of the Ramayana; we have seen such praise.

Here is a warrior. He shines as the dawn-star of the younger generation. He radiates like a flame of liberation. But now he is a caged bird. Soon a scaffold of death is being prepared to drink the life of that bird of freedom.

He went into the enemy's country and was caught. He had longed to enter that very fortress because he wanted to establish his camp inside the enemy stronghold itself. When suggestions were made that the force could be gathered from afar, he brushed them aside and asked his comrades to send him into the enemy fort itself. He was sent in according to his wish.
