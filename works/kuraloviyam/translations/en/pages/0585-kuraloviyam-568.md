The old man, wrinkled like a shrivelled mango, sat on a platform at the edge of the village. A few children with faces like the young sun stood around him, smiling brightly. In their hands were a sheaf of paddy, a mango, a bunch of flowers, and several other things.

"Grandfather! Tell us what kind of soil this paddy crop grew in!" asked one boy.

The old man took it, examined it closely, and said:

"Since the plant is thick and healthy and the ears are clustered heavily, it must have grown in soil enriched by river silt."

"Grandfather! Where is the mango tree that bore this fruit?"

Another boy asked.

"Ah! This is a grafted mango! The soil suited to bearing fruit of this quality is found in the Salem region."
