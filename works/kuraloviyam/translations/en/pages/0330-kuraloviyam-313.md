## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a bare-chested man walking away while an elder speaks to a child behind him.

“Grandfather! That man there is staggering as he walks away. Who is he? From his appearance he looks like someone who has lived in the forest. But why is his walk so unsteady?” asked the grandson.

“Him? He is not only physically strong; he has also studied books of knowledge. Yet there is a reason why he walks with such a stumble. He is carrying a burden too heavy for him to bear. That is why he staggers,” the old man replied.

The boy was young, and the answer did not satisfy him. “What is this, Grandfather? I cannot see any load on that man's head! Yet you say he is struggling because he cannot carry the weight.”

“You are still in the tender season of youth. That is why you cannot see that the man has strayed from the right path. I know why he leaves the way of virtue when it is there before him and goes by another route. The pressure of the bundles he carries makes his legs falter. So, leaving the path of virtue, he walks wherever his feet take him, not knowing where he is going.”
