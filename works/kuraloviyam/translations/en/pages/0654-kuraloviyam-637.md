"Did you suffer so much because we were apart, my dear?

"Do you think only you know how to quote Paavendhar's line, 'I remain alive; I have not died'?

"Did I not write back, 'What is living here at all?'

"What do you know of the pain of separation that wrung my heart dry?

"Ah! Why do you look at me like that?

"Your eyes shoot toward me like glass marbles children send racing through the street.

"You said your body had grown pale.

"You said your bangles had slipped loose.

"You even said, 'I will never look at this merciless man again.'

"I know all of it, my dear. I know how anger placed you in the middle of its forest.

"My beauty! My nectar!

"How could I forget the sugar-rich jackfruit kisses we exchanged beside the roaring sea?

"How could I forget the way our sweet words ended and, in the chamber of the bed, we silently sang the climactic poem of our own epic?

"Is it not the sweetness of those memories that lets us chase away seconds that feel like ages?

"That truth applies to you too, my dear, does it not?

"Then come.

"Let us embrace the dam of love that has grieved night and day without cooling and soothe it by wrapping ourselves around it.

"We know that the heat born in an embrace can feel like cool rain from the moon!"
