—outside. Othiyan and Iniyan, each unaware of the other, stood in different places and listened carefully to that conversation. The minister and his wife were speaking. At that moment the minister's bodyguard noticed two men hiding outside the mansion in disguise. Raising his drawn sword, he charged at them. The spies tore through the air and fled.

The next morning Othiyan met the king. “What happened? What is happening?” the king asked.

“I heard with my own ears the minister telling his wife that he has decided to oppose you, and that nothing can equal doing so,” said Othiyan.

“Tell me exactly. What words did he use?”

“The minister told his wife, ‘There is nothing equal to advancing upon it.’ That means there is nothing equal to drawing near and opposing you, does it not, Your Majesty?” Othiyan said with great feeling. The king immediately burst into loud laughter. Before the sound of that laughter had died away, Iniyan too entered.

“Well, Iniya? What have you learned?”

“Your Majesty! The minister's wife looked at him and lamented: ‘Such suffering has come upon you! A situation has arisen in which the king himself suspects you and puts you to the test!’ To console her, he quoted this Kural:

> When adversity comes, smile at it;  
> nothing equals advancing upon it to overcome it.

The king began laughing again. Then, addressing Othiyan with authority, he said:

“Othiya! The minister was explaining the Kural's meaning—that when suffering comes, one must remain unshaken, confront it and overcome it. Because of your hatred for him, you used half a Kural to try to create enmity between him and me! Knowing that spies like you too may exist, Valluvar said that information brought by one spy must be investigated through another spy and compared before the truth is established.

> Whatever information one spy brings,  
> have another spy investigate it and ascertain the truth.

Was it not by heeding the advice of this Kural that I came to understand your wrongdoing? Go! Do not stand before me any longer! Iniya! Go at once, tell the minister that I have lovingly summoned him, and bring him here!”

Iniyan too went to bring the minister. The king sat down with a smiling face.
