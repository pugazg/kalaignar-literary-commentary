At the entrance to a Pillaiyar temple, five or six young men were playing sadugudu. Just then a devotee arrived carrying a coconut. The young men at play crowded around him, realizing that the coconut in his hand was meant to be broken as an offering.

After worshipping Pillaiyar, the devotee lifted the coconut high and struck it against the black stone there. It shattered into many pieces. The boys fell over one another trying to pick up the scattered coconut. Some got pieces of flesh; one or two got only broken shell. Only one youth managed to seize a large half. He took it and ran all the way to a ruined pavilion in the distance, climbed up, and ate the whole thing himself.

The youths who had not received even a tiny piece stood disappointed. Every one of them was a tender shoot from a poor household, worn down by poverty. Having eaten the large half-coconut, the youth mocked those who had received nothing. The devotee went over to him.
