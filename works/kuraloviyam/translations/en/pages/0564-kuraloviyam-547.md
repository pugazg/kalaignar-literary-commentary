People of a certain village wondered what would happen if, instead of building towers with a broad base and a narrowing top, they made the base narrow and widened the structure as it rose. They tried to give practical form to that thought. Towers normally resemble the English letter "A", do they not? But those villagers began building one like the English letter "V". They stacked the black stones and bricks for it. The attempt ended in failure: many construction workers were injured, their legs broken, and were left disabled and unable to move about.

An intelligent man of those times watched the tears shed by dark clouds run down the cheeks of mountains as waterfalls, become little streams, join together into a great river, and rush toward the sea. He grieved at the sight of so much water being wasted into the sea. A question arose in his heart: what if a dam were built across the river and the water stored? He did exactly that and raised a dam.

"Good heavens! Why are you stopping the river water?" some people asked.
