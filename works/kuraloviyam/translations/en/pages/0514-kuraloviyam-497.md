Evening came; the fiery day withdrew!

Darkness too came quickly  
and squatted down.

The owl's music—  
the cruel tiger's roar—  
the forest elephant's trumpet—  
the jackals' howl!

That was the forest festival  
held there every day!

A man unused to seeing and hearing it  
would die of fear.

But he—

through military training itself  
had cast away fear.

So he walked—walked—  
kept on walking!

A snake wound itself around his leg.

He lifted it away  
as though it were a tender creeper,  
kept his eyes upon the path,  
and travelled through the desolate forest.

The sky began to spit rain.

Lightning laughed like death.

Clouds massed together  
and thundered behind him.

Rain and storm came  
to offer their welcome!

He did not wilt.  
He did not grieve.  
His eyes did not leave the road!

He walked—walked—  
kept on walking!

All the sufferings that had seemed like poison  
flew away like cotton.

The journey too came to an end...

The beloved he had sought, his very ideal, was waiting.

Even if others had poured out millions,  
she had touched none of it—
