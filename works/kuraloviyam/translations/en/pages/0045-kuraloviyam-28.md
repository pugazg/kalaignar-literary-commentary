## Visual material

Large colour illustration above the prose: a young man raises both hands before a seated ruler and a large gathered crowd.

“I shall carry a mountain on my head and go in procession throughout the country! This is my first plan! I shall bend the sky into a bow and twist sand into a rope to serve as the string for that bow! This is my second plan! I have many more wondrous plans one after another. Later I shall carry them out before the people of this country and show them.”

Thus a young man appeared in the midst of the public assembly and issued a proclamation of his plans. The people listened to his words open-mouthed and became spellbound. News that such a young man was roaming about making heroic boasts reached the king’s ears as well. The king ordered that he be brought to the royal audience hall. The young man entered the hall surrounded on all sides, and the king did not dismiss him lightly; he asked whether the plans the youth had announced to the people were truly possible.

“There is nothing in this world that is impossible, Your Majesty! I shall certainly carry out the plans I announced,” the young man declared, standing upright.

“If so, valiant youth, carry out your first plan here before me! Are you ready?”

To this eager question from the king, he replied very calmly.
