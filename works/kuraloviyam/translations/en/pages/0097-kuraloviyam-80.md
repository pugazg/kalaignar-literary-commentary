## Visual material

Large colour illustration above the prose showing a couple embracing in a flower-filled grove.

It was his habit to come yearning into the midst of that beauty-laden grove. Whenever she arrived, every flower there would turn away in shyness. The reason was simple: no flower in that place could equal her flower-like eyes. So when the flowers of light set in her face appeared, was it not natural that the flowers of the grove should bow their heads in shame?

For some reason, that flower-eyed maiden had not come near the garden for two or three days.

Today, there she comes. But there is no vigour in her walk. Usually her gait is like a creeper that has learned Bharatanatyam. Today, however, something seems to hinder that walk. The flowers that would normally grow shy at the sight of her now stand with their heads held high. Ashamed to look at those flowers, her eyes turn away. The whole situation has been reversed. Where once the garden flowers bowed before the beauty of her flower-like eyes, today it is her eyes that turn away from the flowers, ashamed like a warrior showing his back in battle.
