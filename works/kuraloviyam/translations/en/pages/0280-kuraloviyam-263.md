## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a mother, child and father in a domestic setting.

It was a simple family, yet a family in which refinement showed itself in everything.

The lady of the house was like a golden statue, an auspicious lamp. She wanted her family, like herself, to be filled with goodness and to flourish as a flower garden admired by others; she worked toward that end.

The house they lived in was small. Yet, to her eyes, not even a speck of dust should remain. She repeatedly swept away rubbish and refuse and kept increasing the brightness of the home.

Whether it was clothing or the bed that helped them sleep, she wanted everything to appear with a sense of beauty. She was particular that no stain should be upon them.

But one of her children behaved as he pleased, in a reckless and unruly manner. He was far removed from his mother's nature.
