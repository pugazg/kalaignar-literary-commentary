"...you utter them! You are in a position where you ought to deal gently even with younger people, yet you twist your moustache at elders themselves!"

"That is my nature, Vengai. I cannot change it. I sit on the governing council; others are the ones who must bend before me."

"People may bow before wisdom. If arrogance wants slaves, they may stoop—but the noble will never bend their heads to it."

"Vengaimarba! My path will always be a path of my own."

"Boasting as though you can knock anyone down is no good path. It may be your own path—but it is a path to ruin."

"Shouldn't my thoughts match my status?"

"That is exactly what I am saying. One must remember what position one occupies and how one ought to behave."

"You have become a great preacher! The trouble with travelling beside you is that I can never escape your sermons."

Saying this, Vezhamugan struck his horse toward another side. It began galloping along a different track.

"Hey! Hey! The way you are taking is dangerous!"

"Didn't I tell you already? My path is a path of my own!"

Vezhamugan sped away by another route and vanished from Vengaimarban's sight.

Vengai smiled, parted his lips gently, and said:

> The stature of one who does not swerve from his position  
> and conducts himself with self-restraint is greater than a mountain.

The quality of one who understands his station and never departs from behaving with the restraint suited to it is greater than the height of a mountain.

Before he had finished reciting the Kural and its meaning, a cry of "Ayyo!" sounded in the distance and fell silent.

Alarmed, Vengaimarban turned his horse and flew in the direction Vezhamugan had taken.

He saw Vezhamugan and his horse falling together into a terrifying abyss, so deep that they could no longer be pulled out and saved.

Vengaimarban let out a long sigh.

The man who ought to have remained steady in his position and risen mountain-high had, because he lost his footing, descended into the depths of the earth.

Thinking this, Vengaimarban mounted his horse and continued his journey.
