## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a woman walking away from a man.

Protecting honour is greater than protecting life.

Indeed, one may even give up life in order to protect honour.

But what a fate befalls that incomparable and exalted thing called honour when it gets caught among lovers!

Whether man or woman, once they fall under the common name “lover,” and reach the point where each can no longer bear separation from the other, the state into which they fall is rendered by the Kural with extraordinary flavour.

Eye with eye. Cheek against cheek. Sugarcane-sweet lip against lip. Pomegranate breast against chest.

Forgetting the whole world: body joined to body, life joined to life.

Did the lovers hate only the suffering called “separation” that cut apart those who had been joined like this?

No.

The separated love-birds grew angry even with one another.

“Why did you leave me?” the woman poured out in her heart.

“Is it fair that you stay away and punish my life?” the man raged within himself.
