“Urkkavalan” was not merely his name; he was also the guardian of that village. The responsibility of protecting and looking after ten thousand people had been entrusted to him.

One day hundreds of people—men and women—ran anxiously towards his house. The household guard seated the visitors near the entrance and carried the news to Urkkavalan. He had just gone to the bedroom thinking he might finally sleep. Hearing the message, he muttered, “It seems they will not even let me sleep...”
