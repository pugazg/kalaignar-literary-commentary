—noble man.

Knowing that mere survival would be of no use, the old man walked into death's belly with a smile blooming upon his face.

Even a cow caught in a snake's mouth would struggle to escape.

But the heroic sage Vinoba refused to touch any medicine and welcomed death.

Did death conquer him?

Or did he conquer death?

Valluvar the great answered that question two thousand years ago.

Here is that Kural:

> Even death itself can be overcome  
> by those who attain the strength born of disciplined austerity.

The meaning is that those who gain the light of resolve through disciplined fasting or austerity will face death without fear and win over it.

Chapter 27 — Austerity; Kural 269

கூற்றம் = death
