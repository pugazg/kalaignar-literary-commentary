“What are you saying, daughter?

“How many men are performing penance for the chance to win you, the very form of beauty!

“If you reject all of them, whom do you want to marry?”

“Mother, must not everything we think be a thought of what is high?

“Then I will tell you.

“There is a Tamil man of sacrifice named Arima.

“He joined the struggle to protect Mother Tamil, lost one arm, and even now remains locked in prison.

“It is for him that I am waiting.”

“What? Him?

“That one-armed prison bird?

“Is this what you have chosen as the very highest?

“Your wish can never be fulfilled. It will not be fulfilled!”

“Mother, please forgive me.

“Can we forget the next line of Valluvar's Kural?

“He says firmly: ‘Let every thought be directed toward what is high. Even if that high aim is pushed away and does not come to pass, do not abandon the thought itself.’”

The mother sat with her hand upon her cheek, grief made visible.

The young woman rose, went to the window and stood looking out into the vast sky.

> Let every thought be of what is high;  
> even if it is pushed away, do not push that thought away.
