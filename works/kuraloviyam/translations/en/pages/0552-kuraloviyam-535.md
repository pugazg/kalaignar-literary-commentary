## Visual material

Large upper illustration of an embracing couple.

“The moment she appeared on the dance stage, every face in the audience lit up and shone.

“Her name was Malarvizhi.

“They called her simply Malar—Flower.

“Her eyes were flowers. Her body was a flowering creeper.

“Perhaps her parents, delighted by the beauty of her eyes at birth, named her Malarvizhi precisely for that reason.

“At that performance the flautist who was supposed to accompany her dance did not arrive.

“So the opportunity came to me.

“My flute music seems to have attracted her.

“In the changing expressions of her face, a smile flashed and seemed to rest upon me for a few moments as though delivering some message of appreciation.

“From the feet whose anklets sounded without missing the rhythm—not wavering by even a sesame-seed's breadth—to the changing radiance upon her milk-moon face, everything kept pulling me toward her.

“With love of art and purity of heart, I lost myself in the wonder of that beautiful dancer's performance.

“No other dangerous thought sprouted in the field of my mind.”
