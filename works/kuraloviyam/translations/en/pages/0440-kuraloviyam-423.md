## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a young couple embracing while a woman calls from nearby.

A drop of honey lay upon both their lips, exchanged from one to the other. They had no time even to savour with the tongue the honey that touched their mouths before that harsh voice sounded from the garden:

“Hey, Azhagammai! What are you doing there?”

The owner of that voice was Azhagammai's mother. The only thing she had not yet done was lock her daughter in a chest for safekeeping.

Azhagammai and Anbumathi, embracing with their hearts already bound together, separated in shock.

“The cart is ready in the street to leave for our town. Your father is waiting. I am wandering everywhere looking for you. Come here!”

The mother dragged her away in haste and seated her in the cart.

Anbumathi stood on the veranda of his house, watching the moving cart that carried Azhagammai away.

She lived in another town. From time to time she came with her parents to visit relatives. It was then that she took up residence in the heart of Anbumathi, who lived next door, and enthroned him in her own heart.

The two had already made plans for marriage. Meanwhile, once or twice a month, whenever they received a chance to meet—
