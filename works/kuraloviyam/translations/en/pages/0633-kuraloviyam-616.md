Two ascetics had taken a vow to cut off desire and cultivate virtue.

They promised their teacher that they would make service to other lives the aim of their existence, and then set out on a journey.

One was named Aravaazhi. The other was Enaathi.

A wealthy man of the village heard that the two had stopped to rest in a mountain cave and went to see them.

He placed fruit upon two trays, offered them, and bowed to the ascetics.

The two kept only two fruits to satisfy their hunger and joyfully gave all the rest to the poor and needy.

The wealthy man invited both ascetics to his house.

They refused.

He then took ten gold coins, gave five to Aravaazhi and five to Enaathi, and said:

"Use these on your journey to help others."

Then he took leave and went away.
