## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a displaced king and military companions.

At a time when the people were deceived, one man rose to power. He stained the reputation of the reigning king, won the people over to his side, launched a sudden attack and brought the country under his own rule.

The former king, wounded gloriously in battle, swore that he would recover the country that had fallen beneath the enemy's feet. He now lived in a mountain fort.

The enemy, meanwhile, showed no concern for devising plans that would benefit the people who had come under his rule. Instead he carried out many acts that caused decline. His entire thought was fixed on seizing the mountain fort where the old king lived and destroying that king and the steadfast soldiers who remained with him beyond recognition.

The people trembled as they realized the new king's rule was jungle-rule. The atrocities committed day after day by his commanders knew no limit. Except for the few who clung to him, everyone with clear understanding recognized his falsehood.

Using the confused among the people and those who lived by bowing before him, the new king went on spending the treasury's wealth on increasing weapons and soldiers.

The commanders and warriors surrounding the king in the mountain fort appealed to him:

“Our country is becoming a wilderness, O king! Let us care nothing for our lives and wage war now upon the new king's fort to save the land and the people. Give the order!”

The king smiled and began to explain.
