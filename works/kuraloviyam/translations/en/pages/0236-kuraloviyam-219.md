Anbumani said that he had joined the police headquarters in the capital, was earning his living there and carrying out his duties. Inbamani described how the business he had started in the port city had made him a wealthy man. Standing on the side of the market street, the two inquired after one another's welfare.

Anbumani said he had come to the port city on important official business and that before returning, once his work was finished, he would visit Inbamani's house. He obtained the address of the house and parted from his old friend. With a smiling face Inbamani bade Anbumani farewell and returned home.

After completing all his inquiries about the day's factory work, Inbamani called his wife in a pleasant voice and asked her to serve the meal.

She spread the leaf, served the food and invited her husband to eat.

Just then Anbumani entered, calling, “Inbamani!” “Come, come!” said Inbamani, his voice slightly drawn.

“I've come at exactly the right time. Tell her to lay a leaf for me too!” Anbumani said with a smile.

The moment those words left Anbumani's tongue, Inbamani's face changed without his even realizing it. Seeing the involuntary change on the face of a man who knew nothing of welcoming and honouring guests, Anbumani's heart withered like a tender shoot fallen into fire. He decided that somehow he must leave at once.

“Oh! I forgot. On the way here I promised to meet a friend. Let me go see him before he goes elsewhere. Don't wait for me. You eat!” Saying this, without waiting for a reply, Anbumani quickly left.

Inbamani looked at his wife's face. She knew very well that her husband disliked hospitality. Looking at him with restraint, yet in a way that would make the lesson sink into his understanding, she recited a Kural. Here it is:

> The anicham flower withers when merely smelled;  
> a guest withers when received with a face turned away.

That is, the anicham flower withers the moment it is smelled. In the same way, if a guest is received without a blossoming face, the guest too will wither.
