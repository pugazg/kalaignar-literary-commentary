If we listen to two flowers speaking about this, the truth will come out. Let us listen closely to the words shed by the movement of their petals.

“Why do her eyes, whose beauty once made us ashamed, look today even more faded than a withered flower?”

Another flower answers with an explanation:

“Have you not heard the news? Her lover went to another town two days ago and has not yet returned home. Thinking of that separation, she has wept and wept until her eyes have withered. That is why today we have defeated her eyes.”

Perhaps she too heard the flowers laughing and teasing her. At once a Thirukkural verse sounds softly within her heart. Since her lover has gone away, leaving sorrow with her, the eyes that wept have lost their beauty and now stand ashamed before fragrant flowers.

> Thinking of him who went far away, leaving sorrow with me,  
> my eyes have grown ashamed before fragrant flowers.

While this Kural is still sounding, the voice of her lover calls, “My jewel!” A flash like lightning! They embrace. They become like veena and its music. Seeing this, the flowers break into a sweat. What can they do? Their victory did not last even a little while! In that shock, they perspire. At once the flowering creepers and plants draw in the breeze and fan themselves. The cool air that struck the flowers then slips between the embracing lovers.

She, who wished to embrace him so tightly that even air could not pass between them, feels even that tiny gap as separation. Her eyes, restored to beauty by her lover's return, now become pale again! She must hold him so tightly that even the wind cannot enter. Otherwise, her eyes cannot bear even that smallest separation.

> When cool air slips between the embrace, pallor comes  
> upon the great rain-filled eyes of the innocent maiden.

Can there be in the world any painting equal to these images Valluvar has drawn to show how even the slightest separation destroys the body's beauty? The answer is only: “No! No!”

Chapter 124 — Loss of Bodily Beauty; verses 1231, 1239
