After many days, when her dear friend saw the slender-waisted woman arrive, she almost danced with delight. The friend, whose affection never cooled and who was precious as gold, had nothing sweet to offer her to eat.

There was only milk. She heated it carefully, mixed in sugar, and held it out to the young woman.

The lotus-eyed maiden touched the cup of milk and at once set it down.

"Why is it so hot?" she asked.

"You used to say that milk loses its taste once it cools. How have you changed now?" asked the bewildered friend.

Could she know what the divine young woman was thinking?

"My lover, who bathes me in caressing Tamil, lives filling my heart, heart joined to heart.

"Friend, without understanding even a little, you give me boiling milk.

"If I drink milk that sends up such heat, will not my beloved who lives within my heart writhe, unable to bear that heat?

"How can I drink it? How can I make my dear one burn?

"Would it not be wrong for me to drink the milk and make him suffer?"

As these thoughts ran through her mind, the swan-gaited beauty waited until the milk cooled.

How richly, like ripe fruit, Valluvar gives us the excellence of love!

> My beloved dwells within my heart; knowing hot food would burn him,  
> I fear to eat anything hot.

Chapter 113 — Declaring Love's Excellence; Kural 1128
