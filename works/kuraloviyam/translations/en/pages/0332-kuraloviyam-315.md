## Visual material

Large upper palace illustration of a ruler receiving a tied money bag from a woman while a labourer with a hoe stands at the left.

“O ruler of the land! More than a thousand people under our rule are standing at the fort gate, groaning in distress.”

“Guard! What is the reason? Why did you allow those donkeys to come as far as the fort gate?”

“They say they are hungry. Starving. For many days.”

“Who has stopped them from eating?”

“There is no food. That is why they are starving.”

“Oh! Is that so? Then what is the reason there is no food?”

“There is no work. So there is no way to earn wages. They say hunger is piercing their lives...”

“They howl, and you have come here to tell me about it! None of you likes to let me live in peace!”

“The crowd says they will return only after seeing you and placing their grievance before you. They are protesting.”
