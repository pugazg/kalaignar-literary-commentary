## Visual material

Large upper illustration of two women beside a tree.

Enthizhai was a painting no brush had drawn, an epic no pen had composed.

In a few moments of delight her parents had shaped her, polished her for ten months, and for twenty years nature itself had kept adding beauty to that golden lotus and sent her walking through the world.

Her graceful form and honey-sweet speech poured continuous joy upon the man who gained her as his companion.

That handsome man was Aadarko.

Like palm leaves woven tightly together, they remained joined all day.

Then one day the great storm called separation arrived.

“My treasure, only for a few days. I will go and return,” he said.

Making her shed tears she had never shed until then, he set out for another town.

That flower which had bloomed every day drenched in his loving care now withered in loneliness.

Her eyes wore themselves away watching the road.

Her heart wandered to the waterfall seeking him.

She went to the riverbank and yearned, “Where is he?”

At that moment a young cowherd came running here and there in agitation with a little goat slung across his shoulder.
