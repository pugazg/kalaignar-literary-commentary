## Visual material

Large market illustration of a woman carrying a basket, with a young man behind her.

“What is it, Ezhini? Have you only now returned from the Monday market? I see nothing in your hands! Why—did you buy nothing? You set off so eagerly saying you had to go to the market. Why do you return looking exhausted?”

Instead of answering Iniyan's questions, Ezhini stood with his thoughts wandering somewhere else.

Grasping his mountain-like shoulders and shaking him, Iniyan piled on more questions: “You are standing dazed, letting your thoughts fly away somewhere like cotton scattered by the wind when an ilavu pod bursts. Why? What happened?”

“Nothing, Iniya! I have just met Yama,” Ezhini replied quietly.

“Yama? Where did you meet him? What did he look like? Did you really meet Yama, or are you speaking that way because you escaped from some accident?”

“No! No! I really saw Yama.”
