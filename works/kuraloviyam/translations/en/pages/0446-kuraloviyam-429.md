## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a woman looking into a mirror.

She stood before the blue mirror. Neither the hair she had combed with a straight part, nor the glowing kumkum upon her forehead, nor her flawless cool-moon face, nor lips showing the red of the hibiscus, nor the beauty of two ripe pomegranates came into view for her in that mirror. All she saw there were her eyes.

Tears stood caught upon their lashes.

“Are you crying?” she asked, smiling faintly. Pain and anger rose by turns within that smile.

As her eyes gazed at those very eyes, an event entered through them and stirred her memory. Yes—a scene from the past.

Young men had come to tame a bull. Some budding young women were bewitched by their powerful bodies. Her own beloved had been among the victors that day. With a scrape from the bull's horn across his chest, he came toward the slender maiden.

Then her eyes whirled a ceremonial fan for him. Her eyes asked whether the wound from the bull's horn hurt. His eyes replied that the pressure of a deer's horn would heal it.

What followed also appeared before her eyes. But a curtain of tears fell across those long eyes and at once hid the climactic scene.
