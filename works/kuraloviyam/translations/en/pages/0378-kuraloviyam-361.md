## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a young couple facing one another.

Bidding farewell to the night that had given them delight, she gave a hundred kisses on the cheek of her lover, that treasure chest of great affection, and received them back at once with interest and without delay. He pulled her toward him saying, “Wait, wait,” while she resisted with an “Uh-huh.” With that they brought that day's epic of love to a close, saluted the morning sun, and both set off to perform their duties.

She came to the beautiful front yard to draw a rice-flour kolam. The swan-shaped design stirred the wings of the bird in her thoughts. She remembered how, when her beloved had said, “You are like a swan,” she had teased, “Then scoop me up and eat me,” and leaned upon his shoulder; and how, tasting the sweetness of that delightful reply, he had embraced her tightly. Those memories made patterns within the pattern.

Leaving the threshold and entering the kitchen, when she took up the milk to boil, all the pleasure of the previous night—when they had taken the threefold text apart letter by letter and discovered its meaning—rose before her and filled her with delight. The good woman swooned in remembrance.
