## Visual material

Large illustration of a pottery yard with stacked earthen pots, women and a young man.

Pots taken from the kiln are stacked on one side. On another are pots and cooking vessels shaped from green clay and set carefully to dry. The potter's wheel is at rest. She is sitting beside it. An indefinable sorrow lies upon her face. What else could it be but the longing that she still has not seen her lover—
