"The way of the Kural? What kind of performance is this?" the teacher asked, now slightly angry.

"Sir! Doesn't the Kural say that one must live in harmony with the world? You taught that lesson only yesterday.

"I thought about how one should live in harmony with the world. The world turns around itself and also goes around the sun, doesn't it? So, like the world, I am turning around myself and also circling this tree."

That was Kunran's answer.

"This is a mischievous explanation—or ignorance from not understanding the Kural properly.

"When Valluvar says 'the world,' he means the people who live in the world, not this globe called the Earth.

"One must live in harmony with the ways of the people of the world. Those who have not learned to live in such harmony belong among the 'unlearned,' even if they have studied many other books. That is the meaning of the Kural."

The teacher spoke.

Kunran continued his questioning:

"Then suppose most people in the world are fools. Suppose they still live as though in a savage age. Must we too adjust ourselves to their ways and live like them?"

"Brother! The people of the world whom Valluvar means are people of high culture and knowledge, not people who live like beasts.

"Understand one thing. Light comes from the lamp burning inside a house. Light also comes from a corpse burning in a cremation ground. Would you say both are light and therefore try to use the light of the cremation ground? You would not.

"In the same way, when we say 'people,' we must mean those who practise the higher qualities proper to human beings. Not everyone with a human form is truly among such people.

"So Valluvar insists that one must live in harmony with people of good character; and he states decisively that those who do not, however much they may have learned, remain ignorant and unlearned."

Hearing this explanation, mischievous Kunran bowed his head.

He asked the teacher's forgiveness, promised, "From now on I will live by the way of the Kural," and saluted him.

> Those who have not learned to live in harmony with the world,  
> though they may have learned many things, remain unlearned and ignorant.
