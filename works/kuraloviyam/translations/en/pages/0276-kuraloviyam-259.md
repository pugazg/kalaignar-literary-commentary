## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a man climbing high in a nettilinga tree.

The nettilinga tree grows tall like teak, though it is not as sturdy. Its foliage, leaves and branches grow thick and lush. During festivals it is customary to tie this foliage to the pillars of ceremonial arches as decoration.

In my youth, I had gathered a student group and arranged a festival to protect Tamil. I joined my friends in all the constructive work for the event. From carrying festival announcements among the people to taking the lead in decorating the pavilion so that the celebration would be splendid, I involved myself in every task. One of those jobs was to cut and bring nettilinga foliage. My friends and I went at night in search of nettilinga trees along the riverbank.

We took with us a man to climb the trees and break off the branches. He had evidently been drinking heavily. He climbed rapidly and kept breaking off leafy branches. We gathered the branches that fell below and piled them together in one place—
