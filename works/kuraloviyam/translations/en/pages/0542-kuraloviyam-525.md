Hearing that voice, Vayalooran turned.

A man with no legs, sitting in the neighbouring hut, was giving him this explanation.

Anger rose in the philanthropist.

He glared at the disabled man and hurried away.

A young man came to the man in the hut and asked:

“Well, sir! Did you see how the philanthropist's story turned out?”

The young man's name was Semmal.

“What can we do, son? This is the age of publicity-seeking philanthropists!

“If a hundred people had been gathered here, that man would immediately have lifted and embraced this orphaned child and personally conducted the funeral rites of the dead woman.”

the man muttered.

The philanthropist who had hurried away went and sat at a music festival held in a temple.

Many musicians poured down showers of music.

At last one man appeared on stage to sing.

He had barely begun when Vayalooran shouted:

“Enough! Stop! Your song and your music do not fit together at all!”

“Yes! Just as your eyes and the quality of compassion do not fit together, this song and its music do not fit together!”

said Semmal, placing his hand upon the philanthropist's shoulder.

Vayalooran turned in shock.

The motherless infant was in Semmal's arms.

“Sir, philanthropist! You saw that scene of misery there.

“The mother lay as a corpse and the child writhed in hunger.

“What greater scene of sorrow could there be?

“If seeing it did not give birth to compassion in you, what use are those eyes?

“Just as song without harmony with music is useless, eyes without harmony with compassion are useless.

“Understand that!”

Semmal said this and left carrying the little child.

Vayalooran stood stunned.

> What use is melody if it does not accord with song?  
> What use are eyes that possess no compassionate regard?
