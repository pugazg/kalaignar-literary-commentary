## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a ruler above two learned men in conversation.

Two eminent poets from two different countries met at a Tamil festival. Their meeting did not remain merely a meeting on the festival stage. Had it been so, their conversation might have ended after exchanging enquiries about one another's welfare. Both delivered extended addresses at the festival.

One poet explained that the name of the language “Tamil” is formed by joining the letter `த்` from *vallinam*, the letter `ம்` from *mellinam*, and the letter `ழ்` from *idaiyinam*. Another poet explained that among the three celebrated fruits, `பலா` (jackfruit) is *vallinam*, `வாழை` (banana) is *mellinam*, and `மா` (mango) is *idaiyinam*—
