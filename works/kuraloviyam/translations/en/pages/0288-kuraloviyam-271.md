## Visual material

Large upper illustration of Valluvar speaking with a man who seeks guidance on the ascetic life.

“I have decided to undertake a life of renunciation. What advice would you give me for it?”

A man of middle age, with a strong body and an undimmed light in his eyes, stood bowing before Valluvar as he asked this question.

“Only those who have renounced completely are worthy of the higher state. I will not call those who merely act like renunciants ascetics. People who deceive others by presenting themselves as renunciants through their gait, clothing and manner are increasing. I despise them. I feel pity for the people who entrust their understanding to them. Renunciation is not in a costume. First, the mind must ripen for it.”

Valluvar thus drew a clear line around his view of renunciation.
