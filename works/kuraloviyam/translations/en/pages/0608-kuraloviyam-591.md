"Oh, my friend! Let me ask you something. Will you explain it to me?"

"Ask without hesitation. Whatever the matter, I will try to explain as far as I can."

This conversation was taking place between two young men riding side by side on horseback along a forest road.

The one seeking explanation was named Vezhamugan. The one answering was Vengaimarban. The first carried a heavy body; the second had a compact, well-built frame.

Vezhamugan asked:

"Like you, I am one of those on the council responsible for governing this town. Yet many among our people seem to dislike me. Have you noticed that, Vengai?"

Vengai laughed and answered:

"Even I dislike you at many times, Vezham. Do you know why? You are one of those who fail to behave with an awareness of the responsibility they hold."

"You dislike me too? What have I done so irresponsibly? Don't talk like a fool. Measure your words!"

"See! See! You turn your mouth into a sword and hurl it even at me. People like you must think about the higher position life has given them and behave accordingly. You grow angry, act in haste, and utter arrogant words without restraint..."
