“That is the Kural he recited and used to strike me before leaving,” Nallithayan muttered sadly.

The woman piled sweet-cake kisses upon his muttering lips, turned his thoughts elsewhere and pulled him back into her lap.

Porko returned home wounded in spirit. His wife learned from him what had happened and consoled him.

“I cannot even imagine that my old friend, who possesses such enormous wealth, behaved like this!”

Porko's eyes filled with tears. Wiping them, his wife said:

“My dear! Can he become a truly elevated man merely because he possesses great wealth? If delicious fresh cow's milk is poured into a vessel coated with impurity, the milk itself spoils. In just that way, great wealth in the hands of a person without character loses its worth.”

“Yes, my dear,” said Porko.

The beautiful Kural embodying the comparison she had expressed came to his mind:

> Great wealth gained by a man without character  
> is like good milk spoiled by the impurity of its vessel.
