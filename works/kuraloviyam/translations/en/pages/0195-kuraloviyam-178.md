## Visual material

Large colour illustration of a returning warrior approaching a young woman in a grove.

Having hurled spears and felled elephants on the battlefield, driven away cavalry with the force of his arrows, cut down the wheels of enemy chariots with his sword, and raised cries of victory, a warrior returns home. No sweet wife waits there to welcome him, place a garland upon him, embrace him until her breasts press against his chest, and soothe his honourable wounds with her lips. The reason: he is a young warrior who has not yet married. The heroic mother who bore him waits on the raised veranda at the entrance of the house, eyes fixed upon the road, ready to gather her warrior-son into her arms and shower him with flowers of blessing.

But on his way home, the warrior unexpectedly finds himself facing another great army. In the midst of a grove where flowers bloom upon trees, plants and creepers and fallen blossoms beautify the ground as well, that force suddenly attacks and startles him as he walks alone.

Before him stood only a woman! The instant he sees her, that hero who has seen many battlefields stops in bewilderment.

> My strength, before which enemies tremble on the battlefield,  
> has broken before this radiant forehead.

This Kural comes to his mind.
