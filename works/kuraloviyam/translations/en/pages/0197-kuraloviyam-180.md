## Visual material

Large colour court/praise illustration showing two bearded men in the foreground with a gathered group behind them.

“My dear sir! Until now I have never seen in anyone the qualifications, ability, intelligence and learning that you possess; nor will I ever see them again. I would say that your entire head is brain, and that the rays of sound rising from that brain have the power to dispel the darkness of this world itself! You are smiling—but is that merely a smile? No! It is a garland of pearls of thought drawn from the depths of the ocean of your heart! Your writing is honey syrup, the juice of red sugarcane, cow's milk, a spring of nectar!

☆ Your speech—the music of the yaazh, the breeze's lullaby, the song of a waterfall!  
☆ To converse with you is itself a unique pleasure!  
☆ Is a tongue that does not praise you even a tongue?  
☆ Is a hand that does not bow to you even a hand?
