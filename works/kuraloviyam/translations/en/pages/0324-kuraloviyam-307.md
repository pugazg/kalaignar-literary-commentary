## Visual material

Large illustration of a seated man and woman.

A man named Iraaman married a beautiful woman and began family life. Her name was Annam.

Iraaman held a high post in a large office.

One evening, when he returned from work, he saw Annam standing near the entrance of the house and flew into a rage. She had been waiting only for his arrival.

But he slapped her on the cheek and poured out fiery words: “Which man passing along the street were you standing here admiring?”

Annam wept. Though Iraaman then calmed down, he began to think that no other man should even be allowed to look at her.

“You do not understand, Annam! The mouth of this world is very wicked. There is a woman called Amudham who works in my office. In the evenings she walks on the beach; she roams through the market streets; she mixes freely with men, laughing and talking. The whole town speaks contemptuously of her. A woman must have restraint.”

Thus he began advising Annam. Every day when he left for the office, he made it his habit to lock her inside the house and, when he returned from work, to open the locked door himself.
