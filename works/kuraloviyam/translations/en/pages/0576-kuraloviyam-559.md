...words, there are marvellous instruments capable of making two hearts speak to one another in a single instant.

What are those rare instruments?

In a bathing tank, many women appear like lotuses as they bathe. On another side, young men are bathing. Let us imagine that among them there is a Kaveri and a Karikalan.

Kaveri's gaze suddenly launches itself into Karikalan's. Karikalan's gaze likewise collides with Kaveri's. The love that hesitation, confusion, trembling and a faltering tongue had slowly struggled to express through the mouth is revealed by those looks in a single second; they speak and finish a thousand stories.

If eyes meet eyes in equal love and their gazes join as one, spoken words become so unnecessary as to have no use at all.

Here is the Kural:

> When eye meets eye and their looks accord,  
> what use is there in spoken words?
