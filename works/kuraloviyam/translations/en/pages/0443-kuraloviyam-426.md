“Enough, enough, my friend. I am only doing the duty I owe a friend. You need not praise me in return. If our friendship alone continues without perishing under any condition, that itself gives me courage and joy.”

Nallaan would say this to Nagan with tears shining in his eyes.

Then one year came a sudden season of violent storm and rain. Floodwater covered the village. Rain fell for ten or fifteen days without stopping, and the storm changed the river's course. No one could even tell where the riverbanks had been.

As a result, every one of Nallaan's fields beside the riverbed was buried beneath earth and sand. No other landowner in the village suffered a loss like his.

When the rain and storm ceased, Nallaan went to see his lands. Only mounds of earth were visible.

It would take a year to remove the accumulated soil and sand and restore the buried fields. Nallaan's heart broke. Even so, he firmly believed the rich land could be made fertile again.

He longed to share his grief with Nagan.

For four or five days Nagan had not come to his house. Worried that some accident might have happened to him in the flood, Nallaan sent a man to bring him.

Nagan told the messenger, “I will come tomorrow.”

The same answer came from him every day.

Then Nallaan learned that Nagan was now spending his time at the house of another landowner in the village named Vallaan.

Vallaan's lands lay on higher ground and had not been badly damaged. He had not suffered a catastrophe like Nallaan's.

For Nagan, bending his tongue to praise and bless Vallaan was easy.

Nallaan worried less about his fields being buried than about what kind of friendship Nagan had offered him.

His heart groaned with the words of the Kural:

“What does it matter whether one gains or loses the unworthy friendship of those who draw near when there is benefit for them and withdraw when there is none?”

> What does it matter whether one gains or loses  
> the unequal friendship that joins when useful and leaves when gain is gone?

Chapter 82 — Evil Friendship; Kural 812

அறின் = when not useful
