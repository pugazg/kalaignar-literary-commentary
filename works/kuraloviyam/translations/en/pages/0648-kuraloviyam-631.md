The commander struck his broad shoulders proudly.

"King! From my youth I have grown up eating the food of this palace.

"You brought me from nowhere into this royal house, made me a man of ability, raised me to stand among the great warriors at the front of the army, and last year appointed me commander to subdue the enemy king's fortress.

"You even gave me an award for the victory I won in that battle.

"Have I not regarded that as the greatest honour of my life?

"Now a new opportunity has come seeking me.

"I will not let it go.

"The very enemy who was defeated by us and lost part of his country is gathering new strength.

"He means to fight us and recover the lands he lost.

"Give the order.

"I myself will take command of a vast army and conduct the war!"

His voice thundered like a war-drum.

The king embraced the commander to his chest.

"The victory you will win this time will bring you even greater fame than the victory you won before.
