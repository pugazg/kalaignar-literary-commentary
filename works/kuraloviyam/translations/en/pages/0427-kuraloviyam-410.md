## Visual material

Large upper illustration of an embracing warrior couple.

A young couple overflowing with youth and delight, conducting household life so that virtue is honoured and fame spreads its fragrance.

He—the great warrior, embodiment of the martial theme, who guards the land.

She—the good woman, lamp of a warrior clan, guarding the home and blessing her husband.

He returned after sounding the victory drum, destroying the enemy, while the noise of triumph poured through the city like a rain of music.

“My forehead-kumkum has survived, my beloved,” she said, clinging to his shoulder.

He embraced her. All the honourable wounds on his chest lost their pain in an instant under the pressure of that ecstatic embrace.

“The work of the battlefield is done! The enemy is gone! Now let us end our sorrow on the field of pleasure and feast together, my nectar-fruit. Let us drink the honey of love and dance in joy. Every day we were apart moved like an age. From now on days and time are our slaves. What do you say?”

“That is exactly what I too was thinking, my sugar-candy!”

The warrior embraced her.
