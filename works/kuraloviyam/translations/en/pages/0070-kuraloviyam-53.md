## Visual material

Large colour illustration above the prose showing a woman in red walking before a pair of stylised eyes, with white flowers in the lower background.

> If the kuvalai flowers could see her, they would bow and look to the ground,  
> saying, “We cannot equal the eyes of this jewel-adorned maiden.”

If only the kuvalai flowers had eyes, on seeing the eyes of that delicate creeper-like maiden they would lower their heads and look at the earth, saying, “Alas! We are nowhere near as beautiful as her eyes.” Such is the beauty of her gaze.
