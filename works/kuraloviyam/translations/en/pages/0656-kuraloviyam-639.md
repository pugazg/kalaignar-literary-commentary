We sometimes personify the world as a maiden called Nilamagal, the Lady Earth.

One day that Nilamagal came to see Valluvar.

When the whole world takes the form of a woman, do not ask where Valluvar was sitting.

We must simply treat this as an extravagant imagination.

Arguments and counterarguments arise only when such imaginings are presented through myths and then demanded to be accepted as literal truth.

Let that be.

Let us watch the meeting between Valluvar and Nilamagal and attend to the thought in their conversation.

"Come, mother, come! What brings you suddenly?"

"May a mother not come to see her beloved son? What is surprising in that?"

"You would not have come without a reason, mother."

"You are piling up this rare epic of Kurals. I came because I thought I might ask you to clear one doubt."

"What doubt is that, mother?"

"What do you think of my greatness?

"Write it into your Kural and show me.

"I long to know it."

"The mother of the world has such a doubt and such a desire? How amusing!

"No matter.

"Tell me now: what are all the things people say are your greatnesses?"

"My son! Must I myself tell you those things?"
