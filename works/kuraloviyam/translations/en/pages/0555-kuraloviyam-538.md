The ascetic was astonished when Poonkundran entered.

“Is this really the same Poonkundran who once lived in a house crowned with a golden finial?”

he asked in shock.

Poonkundran had fallen into such poverty.

“Do you see my condition?”

he asked with tears in his eyes, clasping the ascetic's hands.

“I expected that your condition might become like this.

“I had heard that the people surrounding you would reduce you to this state.

“It seems that has now happened.”

The ascetic answered calmly.

“Master! The relatives around me were not cruel people.

“They treated me with affection.

“My friends were sweet-natured.

“You cannot say they carried poisonous thoughts.”

The ascetic laughed after hearing Poonkundran's explanation.

“Poonkundran, it appears you still have not changed.

“The people around you did not look like enemies to your eyes.

“But you did not understand that they carried inner enmity.

“Such a thing is not easy to recognize.

“Listen to one thing I will tell you.

“If you lie and rest beneath a tamarind tree merely because it gives pleasant shade, the body may heat up and become ill.

“If, thinking only that water is cool, you draw and drink from a poisoned well, narambu-silandhi disease may take hold and kill you little by little.

“The relatives and friends who surrounded you were like the shade of the tamarind tree and the cool water of the poisoned well.

“Without showing their inner hostility, they gradually reduced you to poverty.

“Have you never read the Kural Valluvar wrote?”

“Which Kural, Master?”

“Even shade and water that seem pleasant are evil if they cause illness.

“In the same way, the qualities of relatives too are evil if they cause suffering.”

Hearing the meaning of that Kural, Poonkundran—who had lost his wealth—fell at the ascetic's feet and cried aloud:

“True! True!”

> Shade and water are evil when they bring pain;  
> so too are the ways of one's own people when they cause harm.

Chapter 89 — Internal Enmity; Kural 881

தமர்நீரும் = the nature of one's own circle and relatives
