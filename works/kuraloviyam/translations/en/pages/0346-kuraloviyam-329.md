## Visual material

Large upper illustration of lovers.

“The sun that disappeared in the west—when it disappeared in the west again the next day, we drew aside the curtain of the bed and looked east in unbearable sorrow, thinking dawn had already come. Then, seeing not sunrise but realizing that night was only now arriving, did we not embrace each other tightly once more, count two nights and one day as a single day, and lose ourselves in unending delight? Have you forgotten all that, beloved?” she asked, soaking his mountain-like shoulders with the rain pouring from her eyes.

“How could I forget, my dear? My very eye, how can you have such a doubt? Will we ourselves even be here tomorrow morning? Or what would we do if this world suddenly broke apart? That is why I said: before that happens, let us drink deep of every drop of the nectar of love we must enjoy. I drew your waist close; you wilted like the stem of a water-lily and gave yourself to me like a flower to this bee. Must I tell you that those sweet moments stand in my heart like a gallery of sculptures carved in stone for you to know it?” he asked, wiping away her tears and pressing his lips to the crown of her head.
