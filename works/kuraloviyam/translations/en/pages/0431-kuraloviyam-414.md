## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a beautiful woman bathing while a mother and child are nearby.

Her lightning eyes take life. The taste of her speech intoxicates. Her cheeks draw one in. Below her neck rises a mound of delight. Who could deny that her waist alone deserves the name “waist”? Before her fingers, the kantal flower would bow its head. Magnetism fills her whole body. Upon this earth, only refined fresh gold could possess such colour.

Unable to stand before that beauty, even Urvashi, Rambha and Tilottama of mythical imagination would run away admitting defeat.

One day that summit of beauty came to bathe in the cool water of a stream.

While she was bathing, another woman came carrying a child on her hip and a pot on her head. She set the child down on the bank and began scrubbing the pot clean.

A beautiful child! Bound for trouble! It sat on the stream bank. Then its thought began to crawl—and the child crawled too, crossing the edge and moving toward the flowing water.

It came from the side where the beauty was bathing. The mother, still cleaning the pot—
