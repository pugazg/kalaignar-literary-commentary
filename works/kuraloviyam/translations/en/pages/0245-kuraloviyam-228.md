## Visual material

Large upper illustration of women in domestic and friendship scenes.

Maangani and Poonguzhali were inseparable friends. Born in neighbouring houses, they had grown together from the age before they wore clothes, eating and sleeping together, sharing a true friendship like one life in two bodies, praised by the people of the village and blessed by their kin, until they reached the age of sixteen.

Through the efforts of her parents, Poonguzhali's wedding was arranged and completed. Because the husband who came to her was a spring of affection—
