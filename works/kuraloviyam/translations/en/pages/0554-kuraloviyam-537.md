## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a wealthy man before an ascetic.

Poonkundran was born into a wealthy household.

He had many relatives and friends.

Every one of them behaved sweetly toward him.

And why would they not?

He possessed such immense wealth that chests filled with gold coins could be stacked in the very front hall of his house.

He accepted their opinions as though they were golden sayings uttered by wise elders.

Those surrounding him praised and celebrated his every movement, continually cooling and pleasing his heart.

Years rolled on like this.

One day, in a small settlement near that town, a disciple came running into the hut of an ascetic who lived without attachment.

He announced that Poonkundran had come to see the ascetic.

The ascetic told him to bring Poonkundran in.
