—the moment you left me, the bangles slipped from my wrists. Pallor spread all over my body. My eyes remain in penance, fixed only upon the direction in which you went.

I lie down on the soft bed. I try closing my eyelids. I wonder whether my eyelids alone are made of glass, for through those closed lids it is you who stand before me, gathering your lips for a kiss. My lips gather too. I even hear the sound of a kiss. Only afterwards do I realize, Atthaan, that I have been pressing my lips against the corner of the pillow.

Would it not feel soothing to lie face down upon the bed? Such a thought comes. Alas! There is not even an atom of the pleasure I feel when my breasts press against your broad chest. My forehead, neck and waist all burn like fire. You would encircle my waist with your powerful arms. Then I would leap, yield and cling to your shoulder. The more I remember it, the more only fiery breath comes from me now.

In the torment of desire that I endure, do you not suffer even a mustard-seed's measure of pain? Is that why your heart has allowed you to forget me and remain away for so many days? I have poured my suffering into this letter. The silken body you touched with delight and gave pleasure to now looks like a dead tree. Let shoots of pleasure cluster upon it, let flowers of love bloom, let the juice of desire's fruit swell—my beloved, if only you had wings, bind them on and come through the sky in a single moment! Quench my longing. With love, Alli.”

The slender maiden read the letter once, then again. She placed it in an envelope to send to her lover. Then she took it out again. She read it once more. Then she felt ashamed at the sight of the letter. Shyness swung in her eyes. Suddenly she tore the letter to pieces and threw herself upon the soft bed. Her mouth murmured:

“My heart! Give up one thing—either give up desire, or give up modesty. I cannot bear both. I poured out my feelings and wrote the letter, but modesty has stopped me from sending it to him!”

With tears in her eyes Alli rolled upon the bed.

> Give up desire, or give up modesty, my good heart;  
> I cannot endure these two together.
