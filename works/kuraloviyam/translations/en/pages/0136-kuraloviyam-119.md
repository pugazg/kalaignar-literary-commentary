## Visual material

Large colour illustration showing a royal or warrior couple with spears and weapons in the background.

She was a princess, a dwelling-place of beauty. The king of a neighbouring country wished to marry her. But she had already given away her heart to the commander of her own land. Learning his daughter's mind, her father sent a letter to the neighbouring king explaining the truth.

That king, his eyes blazing like fire, ordered the war drums to be beaten.

The princess's father too made his soldiers ready. They could not withstand the siege by the neighbouring country's forces for long. The enemies broke through the fortress gates and entered. A fierce battle took place. The princess herself took up a sword and fought. Before her very eyes, her father's severed head fell. In the end she too was taken prisoner—
