★ A fort is a place that possesses warriors and weapons strong enough to march out and attack the enemy, while at the same time serving as a place of protection against the enemy.

★ A region with crystal-clear water, firm and fertile land, mountains fit to serve as walls, and dense forests giving deep shade can itself provide protection like a fort.

★ Height that reaches toward the sky, breadth suited to that height, strength that prevents enemy entry, and firmness—learned authorities say these four belong to a fort.

★ A fort in which the area needing direct defence is small while the remaining protected space is broad can destroy the confidence of a hostile force that comes against it.

★ A fort is one that cannot be seized by the enemy, contains abundant food supplies, and allows those inside to remain secure however many days it is besieged.
