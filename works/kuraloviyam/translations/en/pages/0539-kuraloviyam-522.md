## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a village man moving or running with a dwelling behind him.

Vazhithunaiyan was a young man from a middle-class family.

He lived with his wife in a simple tiled house.

He found a special joy in working for the village and serving its people without rest.

At all hours many villagers surrounded him and received help.

Serving the poor—

building huts for those without shelter—

digging ponds and wells for those who wandered without drinking water—

with the support of friends, his good work continued in this way.

Opposite his simple house, foundations were laid for a seven-storey mansion.

A few poisonous-minded people loudly spread the claim throughout the village that Vazhithunaiyan himself was building it.

At first some villagers believed them; others doubted and wondered.

But within a few days the truth came out.

A highway robber had spent all the money he had accumulated to build that mansion.

Only after the robber held a great celebration and moved into it did the people fully understand Vazhithunaiyan's innocence.
