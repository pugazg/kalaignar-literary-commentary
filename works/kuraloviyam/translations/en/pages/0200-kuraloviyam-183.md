## Visual material

Large colour illustration of an embracing reclining couple beneath a dark sky.

Cheek pressed to cheek, they held each other tight.

Seeing on his chest the wound of honour he had received in battle, she embraced him hard as though saying, “Here, let me soothe it,” her two mango-like breasts pressed deep against him. Her red-lily fingers, which plucked the yaazh and raised music, intertwined with his fingers, which lifted the sword and fought. With the fingers he freed again, he stroked her silken body and tasted delight. That pleasure, without his even realizing it, made him embrace her as though squeezing and wringing her. Her navel seemed like a blossoming maghizham flower!
