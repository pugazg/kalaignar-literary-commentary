## Visual material

Large upper illustration of Karmegam in the foreground with another man and a large house behind him.

Karmegam has a paddy field. He has land where sugarcane grows. He has a coconut grove as well. He also has a large house in that village. He possesses neither limitless wealth nor troubling poverty; his life is somewhat above the ordinary middle-class level. Karmegam had been happy in that life.

But for the past few months, suddenly, whenever anyone saw him there were lines of disgust on his face. Some weariness in his eyes. Weakness in his walk. Seeing the change in him, talk arose in the village public hall.

“Why is a man who was always cheerful changing like this? Has some illness seized him? Is that the cause of his worry?”

To the man who raised this question, the doctor who regularly treated Karmegam replied.
