A cow was walking along the street with its calf.

The sight of the calf running round and round its mother and then walking beside her attracted everyone.

The mother cow watched carefully so that the calf would not wander far away.

The calf would leap like a deer into the middle of the street.

The cow would stop, stare at it, show her sharp horns, and threaten as though she might butt it.

Understanding its mother's anger, the young calf would immediately bound back toward her.

The cow would lick and caress the calf's body.

Along that street came a dark pig, growling, its mouth soiled from eating filth.

It came up and began rubbing itself against the calf.

Perhaps thinking it had found a companion, the calf ran back and forth along the street with the pig.

The cow's anger blazed.

She glared furiously at the calf and bellowed, calling it back to her.
