## Visual material

Large upper illustration of lovers beneath a tree.

“My love! You are terrible! How could you keep looking at me like that in a place where so many people had gathered? My parents were standing nearby. Others were watching you watching me.

“Could your eyes not show at least a little restraint? Why did those lips have to tremble like that and call me? Go away, my love! Everyone must have understood us perfectly.”

“Oh! So you accuse me? How did you see that I was looking at you?

“Was it not because you yourself were looking only at me that you can describe so accurately my unruly eyes and my lips sending invitations?

“Yes, my dear! When you saw me, your cheek became like kumkum scattered in a bowl of sandal paste. Why was that?

“And when your beautiful nose could not give voice to your long breath and the longing mixed within it, were not the buds of your breast swelling again and again?

“Love exposed you just as it exposed me. Did it not?”
