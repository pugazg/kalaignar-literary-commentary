## Visual material

Large upper illustration of two men near a river, with a swimmer in the water.

“They speak so highly of lovers' quarrel, quarrel, quarrel! They say there is no delight equal to the union that follows it. I too felt a desire to experience it with my wife!”

So said a man named Killi to his friend Nalli. Nalli laughed and asked, “Did you try? Did you succeed?”

“I intended one thing, but what happened ended up being something else entirely!” Killi replied sadly.

“Tell me in detail. Let me enjoy your story a little.”

“The moment I entered the house I deliberately wore an angry face. I expected my wife to ask, ‘Why do you look so troubled, my dear?’ She paid no attention at all. She brought the plate for my meal and dropped it down in the hall with a thud! I am someone who usually eats while talking cheerfully. That day I sat and ate like a mute.”
