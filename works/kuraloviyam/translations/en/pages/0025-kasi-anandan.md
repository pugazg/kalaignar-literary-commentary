# Kasi Anandan

In this *Kuraloviyam*, Kalaignar has turned all the warriors—the ancient warriors of the Purananuru—into pictures.

But in our land, long ago, this same Kalaignar—he himself—yes, he himself—turned all those who had remained as pictures into warriors! On this Tamil soil he has turned warriors into pictures; in Eelam, he turned pictures into warriors.

That is why today we stand in the field and continue to struggle.

We are duty-bound to come here and take part in this celebration as a way of expressing our debt of gratitude to Kalaignar; and we do take part.

Anna once placed a *kanaiyazhi* ring on Kalaignar's finger. If Anna were alive today, he would have placed *kanaiyazhi* rings on the other four fingers of the Kalaignar who wrote this *Kuraloviyam*.

At this moment, Kalaignar's *Kuraloviyam* is being released on what is a very great and exalted day in Tamil Nadu.

One who is created by the people is not a leader; the one who creates people is the leader. Leader Kalaignar is a “leader”: among the Tamil people, in the world, on the soil of Eelam, in Malaysia, Singapore, Fiji, South Africa... wherever a Tamil lives, in this land, among this people, in this age, Kalaignar is the one leader who creates people.

Among all those who have appeared on this soil, Kalaignar alone has repaid the greatest debt of gratitude to Thiruvalluvar.
