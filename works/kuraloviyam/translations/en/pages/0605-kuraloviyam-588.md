Valluvar did not merely sit in one place and "preach," or write down some bundle of advice. His greatness lay in travelling through many regions of the country, seeing, hearing and understanding the ways in which people had arranged their lives, and from those observations giving the world a lofty way of life through the Kural.

On one such journey, an ancient Tamil town called Palur received the good fortune of bearing his footsteps.

There lived in that town a man named Azhagiyan, a lover of Tamil. He invited Valluvar to his house and requested him to stay for a meal. Valluvar agreed.

Azhagiyan's wife was making careful preparations in the kitchen.

Azhagiyan sat in the hall conversing with Valluvar, whose face was absorbed in thought. Valluvar marvelled at and praised Azhagiyan's love of Tamil, his ability and all his qualities.
