## Visual material

Large colour pastoral illustration of a woman carrying a basket in the foreground while an ayar youth with a staff stands behind.

The maiden Earth shone in beauty, dressed in green silk. Even after the cows, calves and bulls had eaten grass until their bellies were full, the wealth of the earth-maiden's beauty had not diminished.

As though saying, “Enough for today; we shall see to tomorrow later,” the cattle and calves ignored the grass growing richly across that broad grazing land and stood with heads raised.

The son of the ayar community who had driven those domestic animals out to graze prepared to walk back toward the village. The sharp-horned bulls and the bounding calves all obeyed the sound of his voice and formed into a line toward home.

A veshti folded and tied at the waist! A cloth wound as a turban upon his head! A long staff in his hand! Around his neck, a chain strung with a tiger claw! A face bearing the light of calm and valour together, with a budding moustache!
