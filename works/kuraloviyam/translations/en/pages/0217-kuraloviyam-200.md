## Visual material

Large colour domestic illustration of a husband eating while his wife sits nearby.

The wife stood at the entrance until her legs ached, waiting for her husband to return from the office. In keeping with Bharathidasan's line, “Before her legs could break, the husband too arrived,” the moment he stepped onto the threshold she welcomed him with a smile and brought water for him to wash his hands, feet and face. She also stood holding a towel for him to dry himself. After looking into the mirror and putting his appearance in order, he sat down at the dining place.

As she spread a banana leaf before him, she asked in her sweet parrot-like voice, “Why so late today, Atthaan? Was there too much work at the office?”

“No. On the way home there was a seminar at a Kural festival. I thought I might drink in a little of that pleasure too, so I spent some time there,” he said.
