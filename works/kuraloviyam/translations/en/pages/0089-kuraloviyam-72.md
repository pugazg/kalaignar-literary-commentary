## Visual material

Large colour illustration above the prose showing two women close together, one placing her hands on the other's shoulders, with a large male profile in the background.

“He gave me a smile—I gave one back; he touched me—I rebuked him; he embraced me—I slipped away; he pursued me—I yielded; he left me—and here I remain!”

She stands explaining her pain through her eyes. Another woman, trying not to become like this, holds on to her friend's shoulder. Love-sickness and modesty hang on either side, with life itself as the carrying pole—what am I to do? That peahen droops in anguish.

> Love and modesty hang on either side of life,  
> like burdens from a carrying pole, within my suffering body.
