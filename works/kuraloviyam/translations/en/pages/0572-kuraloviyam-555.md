There was a great merchant who crossed the rolling sea to earn wealth. His name was Uthiyan. The ships floating upon the sea revealed the trading links Tamil Nadu maintained with many foreign lands. Uthiyan owned some of those ships.

He had a friend named Nannan. The world praised the two as inseparable companions, as lives bound to one another.

One day, while Uthiyan sat on the seashore in pleasant conversation with his friend Nannan, one of his servants brought news that struck him like thunder.

Because the sea had turned violent and a great cyclone had arisen, all his ships had sunk. Those ships, carrying pepper, pearls, peacock feathers, rice and other goods for overseas trade, had shattered to pieces in mid-ocean.

Hearing the news, Uthiyan writhed with chest pain and collapsed upon the sand.
