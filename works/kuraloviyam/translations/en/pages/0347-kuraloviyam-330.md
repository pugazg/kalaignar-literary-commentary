“You who forget nothing—how can you speak of forgetting only me and going away to the Yavana land? Think for yourself: is it fair?”

“Should not our trade flourish in foreign lands too, so that the wealth of Tamilakam may be praised, my delight? I am undertaking this sea voyage only to fulfil a duty, my fruit-sweet nectar! Give me leave without distress and send me on my way!”

“It is news that will split my heart, beloved—even your leaving me for a little while!”

“And what of me? Is my heart stone? Just watch how quickly I return, clasp you in my arms and lie beside you without knowing the passage of time!”

“Why these deceiving words? Once you have become hard-hearted enough to speak of parting, the hope that you will return and drown me in affection is, for me, useless. I cannot believe it!”

Weeping and sobbing, the two embraced and fell upon the bed. The seagoing vessel, loaded with peacocks, pepper and pearls for the Yavana land, waited in the sea for his arrival.

> If he is hard-hearted enough to speak of separation,  
> vain is the hope that he will return and bestow his love.

Chapter 116 — Inability to Bear Separation; Kural 1156
