## Visual material

Large colour illustration above the text showing an elderly man beside a tree and a woman within a circular inset, visually accompanying the Kural discussion.

> She worships no god; she rises worshipping her husband—  
> Rain will fall when she says, “Rain.”

Many who wrote commentaries on this Kural have given mistaken interpretations.

“A woman who believes there is no god above her husband and worships him—if she tells the rain to fall, it will rain.”
