## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a woman before an oval mirror, with the returning prince pictured in the mirror.

“Like a lotus closed because it has not seen the sun, like a water-lily folded because it has not seen the moon, your face has been lying faded. Now it is going to break into a springtime smile! The prince pursued the ruler of the enemy land who had carried off Aathirai, fought him and defeated him; not only that, the whole capital has put on a festival appearance to welcome the prince who is bringing that enemy bound to the axle of his chariot. You suffered wondering whether victory would come. Did I not tell you then? Our prince may be defeated only when you bend your brows like bows and shower arrows from your eyes; he is a Tamil warrior of the Purananuru who will never show his back to an enemy's spear or lance!

“Could that prove false? Having entered the enemy's stronghold stealthily in the darkness, he is now returning with the flag of victory rising to touch the sky—
