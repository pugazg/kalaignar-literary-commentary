Large colour illustration above the prose showing two men conversing in the foreground while a third man walks away in the background.

“Malaiya! Whenever our village hunchback and dwarf are seen, they are always speaking contemptuously about you. Last night I went to a wedding feast in the neighbouring little village. The hunchback and the dwarf were there too. During the feast, talk turned to you. The two of them took turns attacking you with their words.”

As Silamban told this news to his friend Malaiyan, another of Malaiyan's friends, Kalingan, was standing nearby. With fire flashing in his eyes, Kalingan looked at Silamban and asked:

“When they were speaking so insultingly about Malaiyan, did you simply stand there and listen?”

Silamban replied, “What else could I do but listen? Could I start a fight with them? The hunchback is an ignoramus! The dwarf is a fool! Both of them know only how to lash out with their mouths. If we talk with them, it is only our time that will be wasted.”
