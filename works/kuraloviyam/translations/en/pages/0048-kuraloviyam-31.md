## Visual material

Surreal colour illustration above the prose: a rounded heart-like, human-faced figure with thin arm/leg forms, set against blocks of blue sky/sea colour.

One day I was walking along the seashore. A strange figure was moving towards the sea. I was overcome with wonder.

The figure did not look like a man or a woman. Nor did it seem like a bird. It did not appear to be an animal either. Driven by the desire that I must somehow find an answer to this riddle, I walked quickly towards it. The closer I came, the more fully I could see the figure’s appearance. Its body was shaped like a tortoise. There was no separate head. In that tortoise-like form itself were the organs of the face—eyes, ears, nose and mouth. Two vine-like legs; two arms like vines as well! I stood bewildered. The figure looked at me, but it did not stop. I stood there quietly, thinking I would see what it was doing.

Suddenly the figure stretched out its hands and scooped up sand from the shore! It carried the sand to the sea and threw it in! Again and again it kept doing the same thing. At first I thought it might simply be playing by scooping sand, but the figure’s activity soon became a great puzzle to me. It scooped the sand! Threw it into the sea! Stood for a while in weariness! Scooped sand again! Scattered it into the sea.

Summoning courage, I went a little closer still. The figure looked me up and down and then bent to scoop up more sand.
