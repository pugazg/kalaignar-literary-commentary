He had sung it. He had joined in and sung it too.

Now she does not sing. He does not sing either.

Someone else, somewhere, is singing the song they once sang.

He has no concern for the singer now.

The beautiful queen who sang it then is no longer here. Nature has carried her away.

Many months have flown past.

Yet now the same song—again that same song.

The fish beneath the pond of his heart begin colliding.

So many memories. So many events. They strike against one another.

The upper surface of the inner self is the face, is it not?

The calm that had filled it so richly is gone.

Agitation. Great waves of grief.

The result of the struggle taking place at the bottom of the heart!

By seeing that result, one can understand what is happening within.

Can we not easily learn the nature of a change in the heart through the face?

Like a mirror that shows whatever stands before it, the face displays through itself the emotions arising within, says Valluvar.

What a storm of sorrow a song heard from somewhere has created upon a face that had shone with the polish and brightness of peace!

Is this not a mirror reflecting his heart exactly?

Then the face is indeed the mirror of the inner self.

> Like a crystal that shows what stands beside it,  
> the face reveals what has grown strong within the heart.

However much one tries to hide the storm of the mind or conceal one's feelings, the face will reveal them like a mirror showing what stands before it.

His close friend, seated nearby, notices the change in his face.

He also hears the music.

By subtle understanding he is able to grasp why his friend's face has changed.

If we ask what instrument can measure with such subtle intelligence another person's thought—the feeling moving like a wave through that person's heart—the Kural speaks decisively of the greatness of the eyes.

The change within appears upon the face.

And the eye becomes the measuring rod that reads what has appeared there.

> If one seeks the measuring rod of subtle understanding,  
> there is none other than the eye.

Chapter 71 — Reading the Sign; Kurals 706, 710
