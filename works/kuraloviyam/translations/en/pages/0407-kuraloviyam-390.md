“—and I came to ask whether that is fair!”

“I never knew you befriended me expecting something like that. I chose and appointed whoever was qualified to manage the trading house. What is wrong with that?”

“Enough of your friendship! I am leaving.”

“Go freely! Valluvar was right when he said that courtesans who seek wealth rather than love, thieves, and friends who form ties for advantage and weigh what profit they may obtain are all alike. Go, friend. Go!”

The man who had called himself a friend left quickly. The door of the house was bolted.

> Friendship that weighs what it may gain, courtesans who take payment,  
> and thieves—these are alike.

Chapter 82 — Evil Friendship; Kural 813
