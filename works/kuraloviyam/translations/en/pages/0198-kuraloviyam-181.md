☆ Never before has such a scholar been born into this world.

Thus one man went on praising the president of the celebration who sat upon the stage.

The president remained seated with a smile, but his face itself made clear that he had not sunk into the intoxication of that praise. The man who had been praising him finished his speech, took a beautiful golden shawl and draped it around the president. He placed a jasmine garland upon him. He fell at his feet and bowed. Everyone seated in the hall raised cries of congratulation. The man who had praised him then began to sing, with melody, a song he had composed about the president. A friend seated nearby asked the president:

“How much greatness is yours! How much fame! Just seeing this drenches my own heart in happiness. Yet there is not even a trace of delight upon your face. Why?”

The president whispered his answer into his friend's ear:

“My friend! It is true that some greatness and fame have come to me. But look at this man praising and extolling me beyond all bounds, raising me to the very sky! Am I truly worthy of so much? No! I have been appointed chief poet of the king's court. I must understand the responsibilities that belong to that position and conduct myself accordingly. If instead I act thinking, ‘Ah! Who else receives such greatness? Is this not a day of delight?’ then tomorrow this responsibility may be taken away. Would I not then have to grieve, ‘Alas! My greatness is gone’? That is why I say that when happiness comes, one must not desire it and exalt it.”

His friend could not accept this explanation. He argued against it. At last the poet who presided over the celebration said one thing to his friend:

“My friend! You will not believe me merely because I say it. I shall show you through what happens.”

When the man who had praised him finished singing, the poet stood, spoke briefly, offered his thanks, and then made an announcement.

“You praised me so greatly because I received the post of court poet in the king's assembly. But I have not been able to enjoy this happiness for very long. An official palm-leaf message has only now arrived from the palace. It says that the order appointing me to that responsibility has been withdrawn!”
