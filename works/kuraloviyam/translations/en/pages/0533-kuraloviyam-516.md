## Visual material

Large upper illustration of a reclining man with a woman leaning over him beside a vessel or table.

Valluvar says, “What one truly possesses is inner drive.”

Lands and fields spreading as far as the eye can see—

gardens and groves—

a mansion rising like a temple tower crowned with a finial that seems eager to kiss the sky—

heaps of gold coins in the granary instead of grains of paddy—

diamonds, pearls and other gems winking in many colours from walls fitted with sheets of gold—

a breeze entering through windows barred with silver rods, carrying sandalwood, javvadu perfume, civet and other fragrances—

the upper garment of a beautiful woman dancing again and again in the mischief of that breeze—

the pleasure of embracing that woman, resting one's head upon her lap and lying there with eyes closed—

the soft vibration of a yaazh increasing that pleasure—

and a sound sweeter than the yaazh—
