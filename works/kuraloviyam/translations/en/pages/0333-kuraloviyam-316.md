“They will protest! Then they will sing songs of war! Today I have a ceremony to present a prize to the court dancer. Tell those useless fellows I have no chance to see them and drive them away!”

“Think before you say that. If our guards carry out your order exactly, the figures standing at the fort gate shedding tears may turn into burning embers.”

“Are you warning me and insulting my courage? No one can even enjoy writing a story in which a pack of rats defeats a tiger! Their wandering without work is the fruit of fate! Their destiny! The command of God!”

“They say their wages were inadequate. Those who employed them drove them out, saying there was no place for the voice of rights. If you intervene, you can change their fate and remove their poverty.”

“What arrogant creatures! Dogs who raise the voice of rights against the very man who gave them food to live have no place in our country. I cannot see those unruly people! I will not! This is my final and firm decision. Announce it to them. Go at once!”

The crowd at the fort gate refused to leave. Guards surrounded them and attacked them brutally. Murder charges were filed against the six young men who had led the thousand people at the front—charges claiming that they had come to kill the ruler himself.

All six were sentenced to death. The news spread like wildfire.

The condemned men were taken to the gallows. A sea of people surged up. The prison fortress wall was smashed. The six were freed.

The next target of that tiger-like crowd, now enraged after storming the prison, was the palace itself. The flood of people entered there too. The king was not there; he had fled somewhere. The people's drum of victory split the sky.

Valluvar warns that the strength of a ruler who makes harsh speech and excessive punishment his policy will be worn away, just as even iron is worn down by a file.

Here is the Kural:

> Harsh words and punishment that exceeds the proper measure  
> are the file that wears down a king's destroying might.

Chapter 57 — Avoiding Tyrannical Severity; Kural 567
