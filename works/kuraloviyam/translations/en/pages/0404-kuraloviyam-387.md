Where were those boats?

Ah! One boat was named Modesty.

The other boat was named Manly Virtue.

Alas! Both boats had been swept away in the fierce flood of desire that whirled around and seized him.

“The violent flood called desire sweeps away a man's good manliness and his modesty—that is, those two boats.”

So says Valluvar. Here is the Kural:

> The fierce flood of desire carries away  
> the two rafts called modesty and good manliness.

Chapter 114 — Renouncing Modesty; Kural 1134
