Large colour illustration above the prose showing embracing lovers in a moonlit/night setting.

“My dearest friend, listen to what I am going to tell you. You are eager to know what happened last night. First I will tell you what happened the night before that.

“He had said he would come when the village was asleep. ‘Girl, come and wait beside the little waterfall where moonlight falls upon the water and makes it shimmer like sheets of silver, near the small rounded rocks that lie like elephant heads. Whatever happens, I will come, my dear,’ he said, leaving even a bite-mark below my neck.

“If such redness appeared where others could see, there would be a battle at home. Father would leap up saying he would split my head with the firewood-cutting tool! He too knew that. So, as though touching me only lightly and softly, he touched me without causing trouble and poured pleasure over me. Yet the marks he left behind became mirrors that showed me his face and enchanted me.

“I lay in penance waiting for the appointed hour to arrive, then went and stood beside the little waterfall near the rocks. I simply kept standing there. Perhaps even the moon grew tired from kissing and kissing the waterfall! It pulled a blanket of cloud over itself and went to sleep. My eyes, leaning upon a nochi tree there, too...”
